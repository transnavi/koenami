# 伪声（萌娘百科）

## What it says
- Defines the practice: 伪声是将自己声音伪装成别人的声音的一项技能 ("disguising one's own voice as someone else's voice"), framed as an ACG-subculture 萌属性 (moe attribute); 伪 = disguise/imitate, 声 = voice. 这项技能可以伪装性别…ACG亚文化中的"伪声"大多数都指跨越性别的伪声 — in the ACG subculture 伪声 mostly means cross-gender 伪声.
- 发声原理 (production principle): 改变发声腔体与共鸣位置。同时改变声音波长 ("change the resonating cavities and resonance position, and change the sound wavelength") — wavelength here is the community's word for frequency.
- Cavity scheme: 胸腔 (chest: trachea, bronchi, lungs), 口腔 (oral: 喉腔 laryngeal cavity, 咽腔 pharyngeal cavity, mouth), 头腔 (head: 鼻腔 nasal, sinuses). The entry's gender directions: 男伪女，主要改变咽腔共鸣，减少喉腔共鸣，去除胸腔共鸣 ("male-to-female: mainly shift to pharyngeal resonance, reduce laryngeal, remove chest"); 女伪男 the reverse (increase laryngeal).
- Breathing typology: 胸腔呼吸法 (female: upper-chest breathing, shoulders rise), 腹式呼吸法 (male: abdominal breathing, larger intake), 胸腹式 (both).
- Four named methods with star ratings (推荐/难度/伤嗓 — recommendation, difficulty, throat-harm): 音阶法 (scale method with a breathy 哈 then a solid 啊; 5★/3★/3★), 气泡音 (vocal fry to "find the feeling"; 5★/5★/1★ — the least harmful), 抬软腭 (raising the soft palate; 2★/2★/5★ — most harmful), 学猫叫 (imitating a cat; ratings unknown).
- Safety advice: 每天最多练1小时，天赋党也不可以多 (at most 1 hour a day); 变完声再练…变声时不能练了，且每天不宜超过20分钟 (train only after voice change; never during 变声期, then ≤20 min/day); 循序渐进 — go neutral (中性音) before cross-gender; 蜂蜜水 after practice. On 速成 (quick methods): 所有伪声速成都是针对天赋党的，普通人练完秒变公鸭嗓 ("all quick courses are for the gifted; ordinary people end up with a duck voice").
- Cheating routes it names: real-time voice-changer software (MorphVOX Pro) and post-processing (Melodyne, Sequoia).
- Performance genealogy: the 演唱型 ("singing-type") practitioners are explicitly called 两声类（歌手）, glossed in the entry with the Japanese 両声類（りょうせいるい） and the pun on 両生類 (amphibians) — the direct link to the niconico concept [[nicodic-ryoseirui]]. Chinese singers listed include 李玉刚, 周深, 吾恩, 排骨教主 (a bilibili singer).

## Bearing on voice training
- The central document of the 伪声 vocabulary the Chinese MtF community inherits or reacts against: 咽腔/喉腔/胸腔共鸣 directions, 气泡音 as a safe starting tool, 中性音 as an intermediate target. [[aogi2021]] criticises exactly this genre (模糊的表述和单一的训练方式), and [[digital-transchinese-mtf-voice]] distances its method from 假声-oriented 伪声 teaching.
- The cavity scheme is a folk anatomical model of what [[formants]] measures: shifting resonance "up" out of the chest toward the pharyngeal/oral cavities is, in acoustic terms, shortening the effective tract and raising formants.
- Its harm ratings (抬软腭 5★ 伤嗓) and practice caps are the community's own injury folklore, to be read against [[voice-health]] sources rather than as clinical fact.
