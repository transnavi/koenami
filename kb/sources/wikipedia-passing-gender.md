# Passing (gender) (English Wikipedia)

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17: "In the context of gender, passing is someone being perceived as a gender they identify as or are attempting to be seen as, rather than their sex assigned at birth."
- For transgender people: "a term used for a person being perceived as a cisgender person of their gender instead of the sex they were assigned at birth."
- "The appropriateness of the term passing, and the desirability of blending into society, are debated within the transgender community." Being perceived as cisgender "may face less prejudice, harassment, and risk of violence, as well as better employment opportunities. This is sometimes termed passing privilege."

## Bearing on voice training
