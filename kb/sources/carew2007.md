# The effectiveness of oral resonance therapy on the perception of femininity of voice in male-to-female transsexuals

## What it says
- Ten trans women had five sessions of oral resonance therapy targeting lip spreading and forward tongue carriage.
- F1, F2 and F3 (for /a/, /i/, /ɔ/) and F0 generally increased after therapy; F3 increased significantly.
- Listener ratings trended toward more feminine for most participants; self-ratings of femininity and satisfaction improved.
- A spontaneous increase in F0 occurred during resonance therapy.

## Bearing on voice training
- Concrete, cheap resonance exercises with measured formant change; also evidence that pitch rises without being forced.

## Notes
- n = 10, no control group.
