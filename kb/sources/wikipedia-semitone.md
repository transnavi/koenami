# Semitone (English Wikipedia)

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17: "In Western music theory, a semitone is one of two parts into which a whole tone is divided. Semitones are the smallest intervals commonly harmonically and melodically used in Western music…"
- Extracts API, same day, on equal temperament: "The modern tuning system of 12-tone equal temperament divides the octave into twelve equal semitones, each with a frequency ratio of the twelfth root of two (100 cents)."
- Two notational types are distinguished: a diatonic semitone (minor second, different staff positions, e.g. C to D♭) and a chromatic semitone (augmented unison, same staff position, e.g. D♭ to D♮).

## Bearing on voice training
- Why voice apps report pitch in semitones as well as hertz: a 12 Hz rise is a large step at 100 Hz and a small one at 250 Hz, while one semitone is the same ratio everywhere (the twelfth root of two). Comparing a trans woman's F0 change in Hz without converting to semitones overstates progress at the low end of the range.

## Limits
- Community-edited music-theory article. It does not discuss speaking-voice F0 or just-noticeable differences in speech.
