# Adaptação transcultural para o português Brasileiro do Vocal Congruence Scale e Transgender Congruence Scale

## What it says
- Two instruments were adapted at once: the Vocal Congruence Scale (VCS) and the Transgender Congruence Scale (TCS). The method had two stages — cross-cultural adaptation and pre-test (abstract, "Método").
- The adaptation team: 「foi composta por uma equipe de dois fonoaudiólogos e dois não-fonoaudiólogos」, who translated to Brazilian Portuguese independently, then back-translated to English with native English speakers, with a committee (a translator, a methodologist and three speech-language pathologists) approving the final version.
- In the VCS adaptation five items needed adjustment — 「quatro deles quanto à forma e um quanto ao conteúdo」; the TCS needed form adjustments in five items.
- Pre-test: 「os instrumentos foram aplicados em 38 indivíduos transgêneros (29 mulheres trans, 2 travestis e 7 homens trans)」, with a "não aplicável" option added to the answer key. That option was chosen significantly less often than the regular responses, which the authors read as evidence the items fit.
- The resulting instruments are named 「Escala de Congruência Vocal」 and 「Escala de Congruência da Pessoa Transgênero」.

## Bearing on voice training
- Vocal congruence — the felt match between voice and identity — becomes measurable in Portuguese here, and the population tested (women, travestis and men together) is the one Brazilian services actually see ([[transgender-care-context]], [[patient-reported-outcomes]]).
- Worth pairing with [[martinho2025]], which found that F0 measures barely predicted TWVQ scores: the congruence instruments ask the question the acoustic measures miss ([[voice-quality]]).

## Notes
- Instrument-adaptation work with 38 participants in the pre-test; no clinical outcomes and no comparison group, hence low.
- Claims taken from the Portuguese abstract; the item-level changes and the instrument itself were not read.
