# Autopercepção vocal de mulheres e homens trans

## What it says
- 69 trans people attending an outpatient clinic for the transexualisation process answered an anamnesis, the Qualidade de Vida em Voz (QVV) instrument and the Transgender Woman Voice Questionnaire (TWVQ) (abstract, "Métodos"). Ethics approval 4.333.500 / CAAE 34437720.1.0000.5546; observational, cross-sectional and descriptive.
- Results: a positive correlation between the QVV socio-emotional domain and the voice rating, and a negative correlation of the TWVQ with the variables 「percepção sobre a voz」, 「percepção da impressão dos outros sobre sua voz」 and 「voz atual」.
- 「Homens e mulheres trans diferiram sobre a percepção da voz ideal」 — trans men and trans women differed on what an ideal voice is.
- The overall finding is dissatisfaction: 「Constatou-se uma insatisfação quanto à percepção de pessoas trans sobre suas vozes e uma baixa qualidade de vida em voz, sem diferença entre mulheres e homens trans」.
- The sex difference the authors do draw out: trans women's daily-life vocal impact was 「influenciado, principalmente, pela percepção do outro, na busca por aceitação social e minimização dos riscos que o preconceito de gênero impõe」 — driven mainly by how others hear them.

## Bearing on voice training
- The asymmetry is the finding worth keeping: both groups are dissatisfied, but only the trans women's dissatisfaction is oriented toward the listener ([[gender-perception]], [[voice-health]]). That is the same structure [[barros2018]] finds from the trans-men side, where voice mattered mainly through how it affected other people's reading of them.
- 69 people at one clinic with low voice-related quality of life is the baseline against which the vault's outcome studies should be read: therapy that changes acoustics without changing this score has not done the thing ([[patient-reported-outcomes]]).

## Notes
- Self-report only, no acoustic measures, no listeners, no therapy; published in a broad-scope journal with light peer review, hence low.
- Claims taken from the Portuguese abstract; the item-level QVV and TWVQ results and the anamnesis categories were not read.
