# 药娘（百度百科）

## What it says
- Defines the community term: 药娘，指通过服用或注射药物（包括但不限于雌性激素、抗雄性激素）等手段（不包括物理疗法如整形），使其生理状态（主要为体貌特征）接近女性的男性或双性别者…通常属于跨性别群体 — people feminizing their bodies through hormones (not surgery); usually, but not only, trans women.
- The entry's own caution about the word's register: mtf.wiki's glossary (not this entry) marks 药娘 as possibly offensive especially when used by outsiders ([[mtfwiki-slang]]); the Baike entry itself uses the term neutrally and lists it as equivalent to 外文名 Transsexual, 别名 跨性别女性 — an equivalence mtf.wiki explicitly rejects (MtF不都是药娘，药娘不一定是MtF).
- On self-medication culture the entry states: 药娘圈内的"服药经"却往往是通过经验传播。从药的种类、效果到服用剂量、购买渠道都是按个人经验口口相传…常常出现乱吃药、用药过度的现象 — knowledge passed on person-to-person, with misuse and overdose common.
- It carries unsourced scare statistics (平均寿命只有40岁…变性人平均寿命也只有50岁) and a harm list for unsupervised hormone use (depression/mania, hyperkalaemia, cardiovascular events, liver and kidney effects, irreversible fertility loss), ending with 不要随便入坑吃药，请遵医嘱，科学变性 ("don't start pills casually; follow medical guidance").

## Bearing on voice training
- Context for the audience of Chinese feminization material: a population that often reaches hormones without clinical supervision, and for whom voice is unaffected by oestrogen ([[hormones-and-voice]]) — the practical reason training guides like [[aogi2021]] and surgery wikis like [[mtfwiki-vocal-surgery]] circulate in this community.
- The entry documents that 药娘 explicitly excludes surgical routes (不包括物理疗法如整形), so voice surgery patients are a different, overlapping group.

## Limits
- Community-edited encyclopedia with weak sourcing: the life-expectancy figures appear without citations and contradict each other's framing; they are recorded here as the entry's claims, not as facts. No dosing detail should be drawn from this or any note here.
