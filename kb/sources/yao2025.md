# 嗓音训练在男声女调转换中的应用及挑战分析（姚瑷娟 2025）

## What it says
- Author affiliation 柳州市人民医院 (Liuzhou People's Hospital); published 2025-05-31, CC BY 4.0 (journal landing page, 2026-09-17).
- From the abstract: 男声女调是一种普遍现象 ("male voice with female pitch is a common phenomenon"), and more male voice-clinic patients now hope to improve it through 嗓音训练. The proposed workflow: first 主观评估 (subjective assessment), then 嗓音障碍指数 (voice handicap index) scoring; those scoring high proceed to a graded training plan (制定训练方案、调整发声方法，循序渐进) to raise the 转换成功率.
- It argues 不同程度的男声女调患者…需要采用不同的策略和方法 and that 男声女声训练不能仅依赖单一方法 — multiple methods must be combined; keywords: 男声女调, 嗓音训练, 嗓音主观评估, 嗓音障碍指数.
- Its reference list is short, Chinese-language, and partly non-verifiable (journals like 中国民族声乐, 中国物理治疗 for items dated 2018–2022); none of the references is indexed in the databases checked here.

## Bearing on voice training
- 男声女调 is the Chinese clinical term for a persistently high-pitched (falsetto-like) voice in a cisgender man — the same population the international literature calls mutational falsetto/puberphonia, treated in China by therapy ([[liang2017]]) and relaxation laryngoplasty ([[wang2009]]).
- The term matters for this vault because mainland voice medicine discusses pitch feminization adjacent to this diagnosis rather than under a transgender heading; a reader searching 中华 journals for 女性化 finds this vocabulary instead ([[chinese-voice]]).

## Limits
- A 3-page discussion paper in a low-tier open-access nursing journal (evidence: low): no data, no case numbers, and its references could not be verified; the claims are a single author's practice narrative.
- Not a study of transgender voice; included for the clinical vocabulary and workflow only.
