## What it says
- Narrative review (literature search focused on articles evaluating vocal outcomes, patient satisfaction, and complications) covering Wendler glottoplasty (WG), laser-assisted voice adjustment, and type IV thyroplasty as vocal feminization techniques for transgender women.
- WG produces fundamental frequency (F0) increases ranging from 20 to 112 Hz, with particularly good results in patients under 40 years of age.
- States postoperative voice therapy is crucial for optimal outcomes, despite potential complications such as reduced vocal power and long-term dysphonia.
- Recommended postoperative care includes appropriate diet, physical activity limitations, and absolute vocal rest for 15 days.
- Synthesizes key studies to argue for integrated surgical-plus-therapeutic approaches to optimize voice feminization results and quality of life.

## Bearing on voice training
- Gives a wide F0-gain range (20–112 Hz) for Wendler glottoplasty specifically, useful for bracketing expected outcomes alongside the narrower point estimates in individual cohort studies (e.g., yilmaz2021, mora2018).
- Reinforces, from a surgical-review perspective, the same "surgery + therapy > surgery alone" pattern documented in park2024 and chowdhury2025, plus concrete postoperative care recommendations (15-day vocal rest).

## Notes
- Narrative (not systematic) review — no stated search strategy, inclusion criteria, or risk-of-bias assessment, so findings should be treated as an expert synthesis rather than a quantitatively pooled result.
