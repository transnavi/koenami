# Efecto inmediato de la terapia de tracto vocal semiocluido en los parámetros acústicos en personas transexuales entre 13 a 24 años

## What it says
- Eight transsexual participants aged 13 to 24 from Chillán, Chile, 「who have not received any treatment that can modify their vocal characteristics」 — no hormones, no surgery, no prior therapy (abstract, "Método"). Pre-test/post-test pre-experimental design, one session; Praat used for the acoustic measures.
- Result: although the acoustic parameter values moved, 「there is only an immediate effect in the acoustic parameters Jitter Local and Jitter Rap」, which showed statistically significant differences between the measurements before and after therapy.
- On F0: 「According to the perturbation of the fundamendal frequency, the transsexuals' voices became more deep and the harmonics-to-noise ratio approached the normal value after the therapy.」
- Conclusion: semi-occluded vocal tract therapy has an immediate effect on the Jitter parameter in MtF transsexuals aged 13–24 and 「can be considered a viable and complementary alternative in the process of masculinization of the voice」.

## Bearing on voice training
- The population is the distinctive part: adolescents and young adults with no hormonal or surgical treatment, i.e. the state of the voice before any medical intervention ([[puberty-voice-change]], [[voice-therapy-techniques]]). Most of the vault's clinical samples are adults already on hormones.
- Note the direction confusion in the abstract — it reports voices becoming "more deep" and then concludes about masculinisation, while the sample is described as MtF. The acoustic claim that survives is the jitter reduction and the harmonics-to-noise ratio moving toward normal, which is a voice-quality effect rather than a pitch one ([[voice-quality]]).

## Notes
- n = 8, no control group, one session, and the abstract's framing of effect direction is internally inconsistent; graded medium only for the peer-reviewed pre-post design.
- The same group's larger follow-up study is [[fuenzalida2021]].
- Claims taken from the English abstract as deposited; the Spanish full text was not read.
