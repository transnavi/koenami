## Bearing on voice training
- The "everyone's method differs" position is the community's standard defence, and it is also why the layer has no outcome data: if methods are personal, no method can be assessed. The 2021 handbook makes the opposite argument — that feminization is explicable in 科学、准确的语言 and that vague claims are the reason learners fail [[aogi2021]] — and the 2021 article course's advice to compare voice lines rather than copy one teacher is the middle position [[bili-cv6240136]].
- The 女伪男 target here is a male voice in the ordinary range; the durable route to one is testosterone, and the self-taught route is the 压音 drill whose injury risk the community itself warns about [[bili-weiyinjiaolianershu]], [[voice-masculinization]].
