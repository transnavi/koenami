# Gender-Affirming Feminization Voice Surgery: A Scoping Review of Preoperative Assessments

## What it says
- Ovid MEDLINE, Ovid Embase, APA PsycINFO, Cochrane Library, CINAHL and PubMed searched on 11 February 2025, with no language or date limits, for assessments and surgical criteria used to choose transfeminine patients for voice feminisation surgery (abstract, "Data Sources", "Study Selection").
- "Of 520 studies screened, 42 met inclusion." Most were retrospective case series or case reports (2000–2025) with sample sizes from 1 to 506.
- What surgeons assess: "Acoustic parameters, especially fundamental frequency, were commonly assessed. Perceptual tools included GRBAS, CAPE-V, and clinician judgment by speech-language pathologists." Patient-reported outcomes included the Voice Handicap Index and Trans Woman Voice Questionnaire.
- "Endoscopic evaluation was frequently performed, and CT imaging was often used for cricothyroid approximation cases."
- Conclusion: "There is significant variability in preoperative criteria across studies, with limited reporting on key factors like prior voice training or psychological evaluation."

## Bearing on voice training
- Answers a practical question for anyone weighing surgery: what a surgeon will actually measure first — F0 above all, then perceptual rating, then a voice questionnaire and an endoscopy [[clinical-guidelines]] [[voice-surgery]].
- The named omission is what the training route supplies: whether the patient has already had voice therapy is rarely reported, so the two paths are not being compared in the literature [[speech-language-pathology]] [[patient-reported-outcomes]].

## Notes
- PRISMA-ScR, two independent screeners. The extracted criteria are what studies *report*, which the authors explicitly note is variable and incomplete — this is a description of practice as published, not a recommendation.
- Claims taken from the PubMed abstract.
