# Cricothyroid muscle (English Wikipedia)

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17: "The cricothyroid muscle is the only tensor muscle of the larynx aiding with phonation. It is innervated by the superior laryngeal nerve. Its action tilts the thyroid cartilage forward to help tense the vocal cords, thus increasing the pitch of the voice."
- Two facts a learner needs from this: the mechanism is a *tilt* of one cartilage against another, not a squeeze; and the nerve is the superior laryngeal, distinct from the recurrent laryngeal that serves the rest of the intrinsic muscles ([[wikipedia-vocal-cords]]).

## Bearing on voice training
- This is the muscle a pitch exercise is training, and the joint a cricothyroid approximation surgery fixes in place ([[voice-surgery]]). Korean uses the native-word form 반지방패근 ("ring-shield muscle") rather than a Sino-Korean calque, which matters when searching Korean-language material.
