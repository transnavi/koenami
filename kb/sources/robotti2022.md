## What it says
- Cross-sectional, non-randomized survey study performing cross-cultural adaptation and translation of the TVQMtF into Italian (I-TVQMtF) following standard guidelines, then assessing reliability and validity in consecutively recruited transgender women.
- Confirmatory factor analysis found a two-factor model fit better than a unidimensional one; both internal consistency and test–retest reliability were satisfactory.
- Convergent validity: negative correlations between I-TVQMtF scores and two additional self-perception/aspiration-of-voice-femininity items; positive correlations between I-TVQMtF and the Italian Voice Handicap Index.
- Negative correlations were also found between I-TVQMtF scores and time spent living in the female gender role (longer time living as female associated with lower/better scores).
- Concludes the I-TVQMtF is a reliable and valid instrument for assessing voice-related quality of life in Italian-speaking transgender women.

## Bearing on voice training
- Adds another validated language version of the TVQ(MtF) instrument family to the cross-linguistic PROM evidence base (alongside dacakis2013, schwarz2017, salm2020), again confirming a two-factor structure and strong VHI convergent validity.
- The association between longer time living in the affirmed gender role and lower distress scores is a relevant longitudinal-adjustment data point, distinct from treatment effect per se.

## Notes
- Cross-sectional, non-randomized convenience sample; single-country (Italy) validation study, test-retest interval and sample size not specified in the abstract retrieved.
