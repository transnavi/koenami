# Percepção auditiva de juízes leigos quanto ao gênero de mulheres com edema de Reinke

Not a transgender study, and filed here because it supplies something the trans literature assumes without testing: how naïve listeners assign gender when a cis woman's voice has dropped and roughened.

## What it says
- 46 naïve listeners judged 48 dysphonic voices — 24 from women with Reinke's edema (GER) and 24 from women and men with other dysphonias (control, GC) — on a count from 1 to 10, labelling each voice male or female and stating whether they were certain or in doubt (abstract, "Método").
- Gender errors were more frequent in the edema group than in the control group, and certainty was higher in the control group.
- Within the edema group, grade 1 edema produced fewer errors than grades 2 and 3: the more tissue, the more often the voice reads as male.
- Mean F0 of the women whose voices were judged masculine was 141 Hz, against 149 Hz for those correctly identified — an 8 Hz separation.
- Conclusion: 「as vozes das mulheres com edema de Reinke são identificadas como vozes masculinas」, and increasing edema grade together with falling F0 is related to more errors and more doubt.

## Bearing on voice training
- A natural experiment in the other direction: cis women whose voice quality and F0 moved toward the masculine range became unreliable gender cues to listeners, and the listeners' uncertainty rose with the acoustic distance ([[gender-perception]], [[female-voice]]). Roughness and a low F0 together, not F0 alone, did the work — consistent with the perceptual weighting of quality alongside pitch ([[voice-quality]], [[leung2018]]).
- The 8 Hz F0 difference between misjudged and correctly judged voices is small compared to the 30–50 Hz gaps trans-women studies report between groups, and it appears in a population where the listeners had no reason to expect anything but a woman. Useful context for how much of gender perception is prior expectation ([[santos2020a]]).

## Notes
- Listeners heard only a numbers count; the speakers' smoking history, edema aetiology and any treatment were not part of the analysis read here. Graded medium as a controlled perceptual study with a clear comparison group.
- Claims taken from the Portuguese abstract.
