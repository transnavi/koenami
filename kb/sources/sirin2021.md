# Psychometric Evaluation of Adapted Transsexual Voice Questionnaire for Turkish Trans Male Individuals

## What it says
- Psychometric evaluation of the a-TVQFtM — the trans-woman TVQ reworded for trans men — in 50 Turkish trans men at the Kocaeli University Gender Dysphoria and Voice Clinics; 27 hormone-naïve, 23 on testosterone (abstract, "METHODS").
- Internal consistency α = 0.975 overall; 0.948 for the anxiety-and-avoidance factor, 0.940 for vocal identity, 0.923 for vocal function (abstract, "RESULTS").
- Strong inverse correlations with voice-related quality of life (r = −0.863) and with self-perception of voice masculinity (r = −0.715), both P < 0.001.
- Scores were significantly lower (better) in the participants undergoing hormone treatment than in the hormone-naïve group.
- The authors' own caveat: "Although not specifically designed for trans male individuals, the current results provide confidence as to the psychometric properties of a-TVQFtM" (abstract, "CONCLUSION").

## Bearing on voice training
- The Turkish counterpart of the Brazilian trans-male adaptation noted in [[schwarz2024]]: every "trans male" questionnaire so far is a reworked trans-woman instrument, so its items carry female-directed assumptions.
- The hormone-group difference is a patient-reported echo of the acoustic finding that testosterone lowers F0 within the first year [[nygren2016]] [[ziegler2018]].

## Notes
- Single tertiary centre (Kocaeli, near Istanbul); cross-sectional, so the hormone-naïve versus treated comparison is between people, not within.
- Factor names are the authors' own translation from the instrument's structure.
