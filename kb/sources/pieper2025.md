# Chirurgische Feminisierung und Maskulinisierung der Stimme bei Geschlechtsinkongruenz（Pieper et al. 2025）

## What it says
- The premise: «Eine Identifikation mit der Stimme bzw. dem eigenen Stimmklang spielt für viele Transgender-Personen eine wesentliche Rolle» — identifying with one's voice and its timbre matters substantially for many trans people (abstract).
- The non-surgical route it puts first: «Eine Annährung an den angestrebten Stimmklang kann bereits durch nichtoperative Maßnahmen wie funktionelle Stimmtherapie und Hormonbehandlungen erzielt werden» — the target timbre can already be approached through functional voice therapy and hormone treatment.
- The surgical route, conditional on the first: «Zudem können in spezialisierten phonochirurgischen Zentren Geschlechts-„bekräftigende" chirurgische Eingriffe im Larynxbereich durchgeführt werden» — in specialised phonosurgical centres, gender-"affirming" surgical procedures on the larynx can additionally be performed (quotation marks the author's).
- Authors: Pieper, Mürbe, Mainka and Caffier — the Charité Berlin voice group whose patient-facing pages this vault already holds.

## Bearing on voice training
- The German-language article title names both directions in one phrase, «Chirurgische Feminisierung und Maskulinisierung der Stimme» — which is how the Charité page frames its service too [[charite-stimme-trans]]: type III thyroplasty downwards and glottoplasty upwards inside one programme ([[voice-masculinization]], [[voice-surgery]]).
- Its ordering of the three means is the standard one in this vault's German sources: functional voice therapy and hormones first, phonosurgery in a specialised centre as the addition [[dgti-stimmanpassung]], [[meister2016]].
- The abstract's own quotation marks around «bekräftigende» are worth noting: the German professional literature was still marking the word as borrowed terminology, the same period in which [[xu2025a]] was marking the Chinese field's standardisation and [[scarpa2025]] was reporting a national surgical cohort.
