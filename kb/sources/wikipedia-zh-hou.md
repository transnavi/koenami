# 喉（中文维基百科）

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17 (extract in traditional Chinese): 喉頭是哺乳類頸部的一個器官，用於保護氣管，或是作為發聲構造。同時也是氣管和食道分開的位置。 — a mammalian neck organ that protects the trachea and serves as the sound-producing structure, and the point where trachea and oesophagus separate.
- Interlanguage titles: en Larynx · ja 喉頭 · ko 후두.
- The zh article is very short; 喉頭 (traditional/Wikipedia usage) and 喉 are the same article, and mainland clinical writing uses 喉部 (as in [[aogi2021]]'s anatomy section).

## Bearing on voice training
- The one-line orientation for Chinese readers: airway protection first, phonation second — the same framing the English larynx article gives ([[wikipedia-larynx]]) and that [[larynx-anatomy]] follows.
