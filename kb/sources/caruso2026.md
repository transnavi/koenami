# Evaluation of Vocal Changes and Quality of Life in Transgender Patients Undergoing Hormonal Treatment（Caruso et al. 2026）

## What it says
- Premise: "Voice constitutes a crucial marker of gender identity, and its congruence with an individual's identified gender can exert a profound influence on psychological well-being" (abstract, Purpose).
- What the study is: a protocol for assessing vocal changes and quality of life "during the first six months of hormone therapy in transgender individuals" — the first six months, not a long-term follow-up (Methods).
- Instruments named: acoustic parameters analysed with PRAAT and the Multi-Dimensional Voice Program, plus "a dedicated questionnaire, the Transgender Self-Evaluation Questionnaire", for quality of life in relation to voice perception.
- The reported finding, as the abstract words it: "The findings suggest that the integration of objective acoustic measures and subjective self-assessment tools enables a comprehensive evaluation of the vocal transition in transgender individuals and underscores the effectiveness of hormone therapy. The results of the self-assessment questionnaire are consistent with the effectiveness (or lack thereof) of hormone therapy."
- The observation about trans men that the abstract adds: "comparison of objective parameters between transgender men and cisgender women suggests that differences in articulation of the mouth and tongue may be observed even prior hormone therapy in assigned female at birth individuals."
- Setting: Department of Sensory Organs and Department of Experimental Medicine, Sapienza University of Rome, with INAIL's Department of Technological Innovations.

## Bearing on voice training
- A recent Italian protocol statement for the hormone-only window — the six months in which a trans woman's voice does not change and a trans man's does — with the acoustic battery (Praat and MDVP) and a self-report instrument (the TSEQ) tied to it ([[hormones-and-voice]], [[patient-reported-outcomes]]).
- The articulation remark is the one finding with training relevance: differences in mouth and tongue articulation between trans men and cisgender women were observed *before* hormone therapy, which is a reason to treat articulation as a variable that arrives with the speaker rather than as a product of the hormonal transition ([[articulation]], [[formants]]).
- It is the Italian companion to the surveying and surgical Italian work in this vault [[scarpa2025]] and the instrument base [[robotti2022]] — the same country's research moving from validating questionnaires to prospective monitoring over the first months of care.

## Notes
- Prospective cohort and protocol report in the Journal of Voice; graded medium. The abstract describes findings in the summary register ("the findings suggest…"), without numbers, sample size or effect sizes.
- Only the PubMed abstract was read; the full text is paywalled and was not accessed.
- The article is in English; included under the Italian heading for the Italian cohort and institutions. The Transgender Self-Evaluation Questionnaire is named as the abstract names it; no further bibliographic detail is given there.
