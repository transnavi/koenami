# In-Site Spectrogram (Voice Resource Project wiki)

## What it says
- What it is: 「There is a js based spectrogram made by Sumi included in the site. It can be found below. This has been updated, and you can find the old version at: oldspec.sumianvoice.com」, with a link to 「Open the spectrogram」.
- The one feature it documents, under the heading Pitch floor: 「You can set a pitch floor by specifying a minimum pitch (in Hz) which will cause the program to beep annoyingly whenever the sound drops below the set value.」
- It points to the wiki's own reading guide for the display ([[sumi-spectrograms]]).

## Bearing on voice training
- This is the wiki's own instrument, and the only measurement it advertises is a floor alarm: a threshold below which the voice must not fall, rather than a score of how feminine the voice is ([[tools]], [[fundamental-frequency]]).
- That choice is the same position the spectrogram page argues — a display to train the ear, not a verdict — and it is the opposite of the app-based scoring the community discusses elsewhere ([[machine-gender-scoring]], [[transvoice-app]], [[sumi-spectrograms]]).
- A pitch floor is the one thing a training session needs when the goal is a higher habitual pitch without force, which is what the naturalisation method trains ([[sumi-pipm]], [[sumi-pitch]]).

## Limits
- The spectrogram itself was not opened or run during this fetch, so nothing is claimed here about its display, accuracy or outputs. Pitch floor is the only feature the page describes; the alarm is the page's own word for it.
