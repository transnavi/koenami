# The surgical management of sex reassignment surgery and its options

The 2001 description of the Okayama University gender team — one of the first in Japan — and the place voice surgery appears inside it: as one line in a list.

## What it says
- The team's founding, in the abstract's words: 「A gender treatment team composing staff members from the Psychiatric, Plastic Surgery, Urology, Obstetric/Gynecological Surgery, and the Social Service, was established at the Hospital of Okayama University Medical schools in 1999.」
- The framing of the condition: GID 「is a unique human condition that is classified behaviorally and treated medically with hormones and surgery in the severe form」, and it 「has been and still somewhat remains controversial by religious beliefs, social institutions and health care delivery systems」.
- The FtM procedures listed: 「mastectomy, phalloplasty, Phalloplasty are completion and an appurtenance」. The MtF programme: 「the genital surgery. The genital change surgery consists of the penile inversion, orchiectomy, vaginoplasty.」
- Where voice sits: 「Other procedures include reduction thyroid chondroplasty, hair transplant, voice change, laryngeal surgery, epilation with the laser.」 Voice change and laryngeal surgery appear as two entries among the ancillary procedures, next to tracheal shave, hair transplant and laser epilation — with no further detail in the abstract.
- The overall judgement: 「SRS is the only effective treatment available today in the management of GID. The aesthetic and functional results achievable from various procedures are generally satisfactory and are acceptable to the patients」, with a caution that 「numerous steps of information for SRS will be required」.

## Bearing on voice training
- Voice is not a category here; it is an item in a list of optional procedures, delivered by a plastic-surgery department within a multidisciplinary team ([[transgender-care-context]], [[voice-surgery]]). The vault's Japanese surgical material from the same years comes from ENT clinics that specialised in laryngeal framework surgery ([[tanaka1981]], [[sanuki2007]]), not from these university teams.
- The co-author 難波祐三郎 (Namba Y) is the author of the vault's 2014 Okayama review [[namba2014]], which is the later, fuller statement of the same programme's comprehensive-treatment model ([[clinical-guidelines]]).
