## Bearing on voice training
- The video that fixed "R1" as English-community jargon for the first tract resonance (F1 / pharyngeal resonance). That is the community name for the tract-length work in [[formants]], not for vocal weight.
- The "gender control knob" claim is the instructor's; listener research ranks F0 first and formants second ([[leung2018]], [[gelfer2013]]), not R1 alone.
