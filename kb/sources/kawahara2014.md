# 音声の実時間表示とモーフィングで探る声の多様性

## What it says
- The abstract, in full: 「An extended morphing which enables time varying multi-aspect morphing of arbitrary many voices was recently formulated. This article introduces speech research tools based on this extended morphing to invite researchers to an exciting research field of para- and non-linguistic aspects of speech communications. This article also introduces a set of realtime visualization tools of sounds using the realtime functionality of Matlab to facilitate understanding of underlying signal processing and human auditory perception basics.」
- Keywords listed with the article: 音声分析合成, VOCODER, 音声変換, モーフィング, パラ言語情報, 実時間表示.

## Bearing on voice training
- A review of the Japanese morphing-and-visualisation toolkit line by its author, written for 音声研究; the morphing of voices along para- and non-linguistic axes is the technique on which the listener-perception experiments in this vault rest, and the same technique is what community spectrogram tools show in real time ([[skuk2014]], [[praat]], [[sumi-in-site-spectrogram]]).
- The stated purpose — making the signal processing and the auditory perception legible together — is the didactic problem the Japanese community tools also address, and it is the reason this note sits under tools rather than under a clinical concept.

## Notes
- A 解説 in the Japanese phonetics journal; the morphing tools described are general speech technology, not aimed at gendered voice, and none of the vault's gender-perception findings come from it. Only the English abstract was read.
