# Voice self-perception and acoustic parameters in transgirls adolescent related to pubertal stage blockage

## What it says
- Cádiz (Puerta del Mar University Hospital, paediatric endocrinology with ENT) cross-sectional study of Spanish trans girls on puberty blockers, split into early-block and late-block groups; Voice Handicap Index (VHI-30) and Trans Women Voice Questionnaire (TWVQ) plus anthropometric, clinical and vocal data including F0 (abstract, "METHOD").
- F0 was 71.5 Hz higher in the early-block group (P < 0.001); TWVQ and VHI-30 scores were higher (worse) in the late-block group (P < 0.001); self-perception of voice was more feminine with early blocking (P = 0.001).
- Negative correlations of F0 with age at blocking (r = 0.669 as printed) and with TWVQ score (r = 0.755).
- Conclusion: "Early puberty blockers seem to be related to lower score in TWVQ and VHI-30. It could represent less vocal discomfort in trans girls."

## Bearing on voice training
- The Spanish-language counterpart of the Dutch/BE adolescent cohort findings [[ciancia2026]] [[boogers2025]]: blocking before the voice breaks preserves a higher F0 and prevents voice dysphoria — stated with the sources' own hedging ("seem", "could").
- Explains why some Spanish-speaking young adults arrive at voice work with a feminine F0 and a masculine filter, and others with both to change [[formants]].

## Notes
- Cross-sectional between-group comparison, not longitudinal within-person; the abstract's correlation signs are reported as printed, and the direction stated in words (earlier block, higher F0) is the interpretable finding.
- Sample size is not stated in the abstract; adolescents, so no bearing on adult hormone decisions.
