# Autopercepção vocal e psiquismo em pessoas transexuais

## What it says
- Multiple case study with three trans adults aged 18–40, recruited by the snowball method from the researcher's social network, in São Paulo (abstract, "Método").
- Two procedures per participant: a recorded free account (「depoimento livre」), analysed by Bardin's content analysis, and two questionnaires — the TWVQ (Questionário Autoavaliação Vocal para Transexuais) and the Beck Anxiety Inventory (Escala de Ansiedade de Beck).
- Four themes came out of the free accounts: 「voz, narrativas sobre a infância, trajetória e família」 — voice, narratives about childhood, trajectory, family.
- TWVQ results: 「autopercepção vocal positiva somente em 01 sujeito e negativa nos demais」 — positive in one participant, negative in the other two. On the anxiety scale, two were moderate and one high.

## Bearing on voice training
- A very small study, but it records something the instruments cannot: the voice complaint is embedded in a life narrative that starts in childhood and runs through family ([[patient-reported-outcomes]], [[transgender-care-context]]). Two of three participants with negative voice self-perception and raised anxiety scores is the kind of pairing that makes a pure acoustic target look thin.
- Methodologically it belongs next to [[sebastiao2022]]: Brazilian qualitative work using the TWVQ alongside interviews, in a field where almost everything else is acoustic measurement.

## Notes
- n = 3, recruited from the researcher's own network, no acoustic measures and no therapy; graded low.
- Claims taken from the Portuguese abstract; the interview transcripts and the coding frame were not read.
