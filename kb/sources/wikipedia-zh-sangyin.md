# 嗓音（中文维基百科）

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17 (extract in simplified Chinese): 嗓音是指有动物呼吸系统的动作产生的用以传达信息的声音。嗓音只限于蛙类、鳄类、壁虎类、鸟类和兽类… — the article defines 嗓音 broadly as animal voice produced by the respiratory system, and frames calls in birds and non-human primates as its main examples.
- For humans the zh Wikipedia tree puts the anatomical detail in separate articles (人聲 human voice, 聲帶 vocal cords, 喉 larynx); 嗓音 itself stays at the zoological level. The article holds no interlanguage links (langlinks returned none), so it is not the counterpart of the English "Human voice" article.
- 嗓音 is the everyday and clinical word for (human) voice in Chinese: hospital pages write 嗓音女性化 ([[voicedoctor-tw]], [[mtfwiki-vocal-therapy]]), journals write 嗓音训练 ([[yao2025]]).

## Bearing on voice training
- Terminology anchor: when Chinese sources say 嗓音 they mean the voice as produced by the respiratory system and larynx, matching the source–filter framing of [[source-filter-model]] without using that term.
