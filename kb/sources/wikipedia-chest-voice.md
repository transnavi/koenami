## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17: "Chest voice is a term used within vocal music. The use of this term varies widely within vocal pedagogical circles. ## Bearing on voice training
- "Chest" as a feeling of vibration in the thorax is not the laryngeal mechanism ([[herbst2020]] quotes a Collegium Medicorum Theatri consensus to that effect). A trans-feminine speaker told to "get out of chest" may drop TA activity (lighter source) or raise the larynx (shorter tract), which are different tasks.
