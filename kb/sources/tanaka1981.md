# 声の高さの異常に対する手術

The earliest item in this pass, and the one that establishes the two operations everything later in Japanese voice surgery refers back to.

## What it says
- Two groups, both operated on and both described in the abstract: 「男性で声の高すぎる症例5例に対して, 前筋切断術と甲状軟骨形成術III型を施行し, 著明なピッチの低下を得た」 — five men with too-high voices given anterior cricothyroid muscle section plus thyroplasty type III, with a marked pitch drop.
- 「これらの症例は何れも心理的機能的治療が無効に終った変声障害である」 — all of them mutational dysphonia (変声障害) where psychological and functional treatment had failed.
- The authors' position on waiting: 「こうした変声障害に対しては, いたずらに時間を費すことは得策とは言えず, 積極的に手術を行なうのがよいと考える」 — for this kind of mutational dysphonia, spending time is not advisable; operate actively.
- The other group: 「女性で声の低すぎる症例9例に対して, 甲状軟骨形成術IV型を施行し, 良好なピッチの上昇を得た」 — nine women with too-low voices given thyroplasty type IV, with a good pitch rise.
- And the claim the type IV operation was built on: 「特に, 不可逆的と考えられている男性化音声の例に対しては, 甲状輪状軟骨接近術は安全で確実な手術法であることが示された」 — for masculinised voice, considered irreversible, cricothyroid approximation is safe and reliable.

## Bearing on voice training
- The founding statement of the Japanese surgical pair: type III with anterior muscle section to lower pitch, type IV (cricothyroid approximation) to raise it ([[voice-surgery]], [[pitch-and-scales]]). Both operations recur throughout the vault's Japanese material — [[sato2007]] for therapy targets that might avoid them, [[sanuki2007]] and [[nakamura2022]] for type IV in trans women, [[otsu2010]] and [[nakamura2011]] for type III in men.
- Two things here are dated: "masculinised voice is irreversible" was written before the modern trans-surgery literature, and the recommendation to operate early in mutational dysphonia is the opposite of the voice-therapy-first sequence every later Japanese source in this vault follows ([[sato2007]], [[jspn-guideline2018]], [[clinical-guidelines]]).

## Notes
- A 1981 case series, 14 patients across two indications, no control group and no blinding; graded low on design and year.
- The operation for the too-high voice involves cutting the anterior cricothyroid muscle, which no later source in this vault reports in this form.
- Claims taken from the Japanese abstract of the J-STAGE article page.
