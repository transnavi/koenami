## Bearing on voice training
- The "omnibus" format — an existing creator folding their scattered clips into one canonical long video — is the dominant pattern of the 2017–2021 period in this dataset: the same move produces [[bili-laxiaojiao2021]] (139 min), [[bili-jingjupan]]'s follow-along versions, and the re-issue pattern at [[bili-qiancangmonster2025]]. It is how a channel establishes a single reference video that later tutorials can build on.
- The description's framing of method as something to 了解…学习 in one sitting, with 简化 and 分类 as the value added, is the 伪声 layer's alternative to the structured curriculum the 2021 handbook wrote [[aogi2021]].
