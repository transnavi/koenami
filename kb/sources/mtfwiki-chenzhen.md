# 上海市长宁区精神卫生中心｜陈臻（MtF.wiki）

## What it says
- Community-written protocol page for the most-referenced voice trainer on the Chinese mainland: 陈臻, 上海市长宁区精神卫生中心, 800 CNY/次, phone booking, no 易性症证明 (transsexualism certificate) required.
- The page states his knowledge base: 陈臻的理论知识和民间伪声教程有较大出入，主要是康复学和嗓音学相关知识，基本与声乐和配音无关 — rehabilitation science and voice science, explicitly at odds with folk 伪声 tutorials and with singing/dubbing pedagogy. First sessions include a body and psychological baseline survey; assessment uses a professional sound card and software (no report handed out).
- Training emphasis: 主要目标是在喉部放松的状态下提高声音的基频，陈臻的训练以提升基频为主，修饰音色为辅 — raise F0 with a relaxed larynx first, timbre second. Timbre work listed: 气流发声训练 (breathy phonation, "使嗓音听起来比较柔和"), 展唇发音 (lip spreading) and 舌位前移 (forward tongue) for a fronted resonance.
- On resonance and anatomy, the page attributes to him: 共振峰对音色有影响，而共振峰结构主要受制于声道的解剖结构，男女声道解剖结构（如长度，体积等）有一定差异，所以很难通过训练使共振峰结构完全女性化，只能通过训练微调共振峰结构 — formant structure is anatomy-bound (tract length, volume), so training can only fine-tune it, never fully feminize it.
- Mandarin-specific technique it records: training a non-standard tone contour (与标准普通话不太一致的语调，主要体现在三、四声) to keep F0 from falling into the male range — a lexical-tone workaround unique to tonal languages.
- Reported outcomes: 多数人的训练结果是基频提高了50Hz-60Hz，达到一个中性的声音，目前笔者没有听到过240Hz左右的完美女声的案例 — most trainees gain 50–60 Hz and reach a neutral voice; the page author knows of no case of a "perfect female voice around 240 Hz".
- Practical warnings: bring laryngoscopy/stroboscopy reports if available (he otherwise assumes a normal male larynx); be able to hum in tune or pitch work eats sessions; he is blunt about improper phonation; not recommended if severe psychological distress is present; first course does not cover English-language voice.

## Bearing on voice training
- The clearest Chinese-language description of what clinical 嗓音女性化训练 at one mainland provider actually contains: F0-first with relaxation, breathiness/lip-spreading/forward-tongue as secondary timbre tools, and an anatomy-limits view of formants — converging with [[leung2018]]'s weighting (F0 dominant) while more pessimistic on formant trainability than [[carew2007]] and [[kawitzky2020]] suggest.
- The tone-contour trick is a genuinely Mandarin-specific training device, absent from English-language protocols; it belongs beside [[intonation]].
- The "neutral voice at +50–60 Hz" outcome claim, though community-observed, sets expectations far below typical cis-female F0 ([[fundamental-frequency]]).

## Limits
- Community-edited page describing one clinician; outcome numbers and quoted views are page-author recollections ("笔者"), not published data; the clinician has reportedly edited/visited the page himself (it carries a 巡回 banner), which is both corroboration and conflict of interest.
- Fees and booking details drift; recorded as of the 2026-07-09 page update.
