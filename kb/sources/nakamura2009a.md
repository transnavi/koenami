# 女性ホルモン異常による低音化音声障害に対する甲状軟骨形成術4型

Two cis women whose voices dropped with falling oestrogen and who were operated on for it — the feminisation operation applied outside trans care.

## What it says
- What the operation is and does, as stated in the abstract: 「甲状軟骨形成術4型（4型）は声帯の緊張を高め，話声位（SFF）のピッチを上昇させる術式である」, usually combined with arytenoid adduction or type 1 thyroplasty in unilateral fold paralysis, but 「ピッチ上昇を目的に単独で施行されることもある」.
- Case 1: a 72-year-old woman in customer-facing work, several years of falling female hormone, four years of hormone replacement therapy. 「更年期症状は良くなったが，SFFは徐々に低下し相手に伝わらず日常生活に支障をきたし」 — menopausal symptoms improved, but SFF fell gradually until she could not be understood and daily life suffered. First-visit SFF 186.1 Hz, lowest tone 157.8 Hz.
- Case 2: a 50-year-old woman in care work, whose SFF fell gradually over two years with work consequences, alongside a menopause diagnosis and HRT. 「更年期症状は改善したが，声は良くならず」 — symptoms improved, voice did not. First-visit SFF 178.3 Hz, lowest tone 130.8 Hz.
- Both had type 4 under local anaesthesia, with SFF measured intra-operatively using analysis software and the pitch tuned to what each patient was satisfied with: 「術中より解析ソフトを用いSFFを測定し，患者の満足するピッチに調節した」.
- Results: 「術後のSFFは症例1：323.7 Hzに，症例2：251.1 Hzに上昇した」. Conclusion: 「女性ホルモン異常による低音化音声障害には4型は有用な術式であった。」

## Bearing on voice training
- Documents that the same operation is offered for age- and hormone-related pitch loss in cis women, with the same intra-operative tuning method the trans-surgery literature uses ([[voice-surgery]], [[hormones-and-voice]]). The vault's Japanese clinic pages list the same broadening of indications ([[kyoto-ent-clinic]]).
- The intra-operative satisfaction-guided adjustment is the point: the target is the patient's ear, not a normative F0 ([[fundamental-frequency]], [[patient-reported-outcomes]]).

## Notes
- n = 2, no control group, short follow-up not stated in the abstract; graded low.
- The SFF figures are single-visit means, not averaged over a sample; case 1 ends at 323.7 Hz, well above any cis female mean, and no quality or effort measure accompanies it.
- Claims taken from the Japanese abstract on the J-STAGE article page.
