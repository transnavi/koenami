# Sequential Wendler Glottoplasty and Laser Reduction Glottoplasty for Voice Feminization

## What it says
- Retrospective cohort at a university hospital: 18 trans patients had Wendler glottoplasty (WG) first and then laser reduction glottoplasty (LRG), 17 had LRG first then WG; 35 cases in total over a 15-year period, each followed at least 1 year (abstract, "Methods").
- Outcome measures: Voice Handicap Index (VHI-30), Transsexual Voice Questionnaire (TVQ), and acoustic analysis of /a/ and running speech, pre- and postoperatively.
- "Their preoperative, first, and second postoperative mean sF0 were 146, 175, and 215 Hz, respectively; these differences were statistically significant (p < 0.001)" — i.e. roughly +29 Hz from the first operation and +40 Hz from the second.
- "Their postoperative mean jitter percent, shimmer percent, noise to harmonic ratio (NHR), cepstral peak prominence (CPP), and cepstral spectral index of dysphonia (CSID) worsened significantly compared to preop values (p < 0.05); however, mean postoperative acoustic results were still within normal limits."
- VHI and TVQ improved significantly (p < 0.05); "Patients' self-ratings of their postsurgery voices revealed all feminine, leading to a patient gratification score of 100%."
- The authors' conclusion: "If transgender female patients are unsatisfied with their voice after WG or LRG, the addition of the alternative procedure may significantly feminize their voice."

## Bearing on voice training
- Quantifies what a second, different operation adds when the first leaves the patient unsatisfied: about +40 Hz of speaking F0 on top of the first procedure's +29 Hz, at the cost of measurably worse perturbation and cepstral measures that nonetheless stay inside normal limits [[voice-surgery]] [[fundamental-frequency]].
- Read against the single-procedure series in this vault, it establishes that a failed glottoplasty is answered by the other glottoplasty rather than by the same one again, which is the surgical analogue of changing strategy after a training plateau [[yilmaz2021]] [[birkent2026]].

## Notes
- Level of evidence 4 (the authors' own label), single centre and a single author, no control group and no randomisation of which operation came first.
- The 100% gratification figure comes from patient self-rating in a series where the operations were chosen sequentially because the earlier one had not satisfied the patient; the acoustic gains are reported in means without dispersion.
