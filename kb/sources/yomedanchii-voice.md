# 俺の嫁ちゃん、元男子。「声パス・ボイトレ」カテゴリ

The document [[nicodic-ryoseirui]] pointed at with the slug `yomedanchii-voice`: a long-running Japanese trans-woman 4-koma and essay blog by ちぃ (handle yomedan_chii, X @chii_gid_mtf), author of the print comic 『花嫁は元男子。』 (飛鳥新社, 2016). Its 「声パス・ボイトレ」 category holds 37 posts, running from the 2018 start of the blog to 2025.

## What it says
- Post of 2025-08-27, 「【声の女性化】声の手術する予定でもボイトレは必要？」, answering a reader question: 「術法にもよるかと思いますが、基本的にはボイトレは必要だとされています。ただし声を女性化するボイトレとはちょっと違うらしい」.
- What the surgery is, in her words: 「まず「声の女性化手術」は声帯を調整することで、声を女性的にする手術です。私はそこまで詳しくないのですが、タイや日本でもやっていますが、今は韓国での手術が有名ですかねー…」 — an explicitly non-clinical voice, and the geography it gives (Thailand, Japan, and currently Korea) is the community's current picture, not a clinical referral.
- Timing: 「術法によっては声帯を傷める可能性があるので、術前はボイトレを推奨してないこともあるそうですが、逆に手術後は一時的に声が出しづらくなるので、声がある程度出せるようにするためにボイストレーニングをするのが一般的なようです」, and then the distinction she draws about what that training is for: 「ここのボイトレは女性ボイスを習得するためのボイトレではなく、声を安定して出せるためのボイトレらしいので、根本が違うかもしれませんね。」
- She records the combination case: 「中には「声は高くなったけど女性的な発声じゃなかったので、声の女性化ボイトレも併用しました」って人もいる」 — surgery and behavioural training as complements, reported second-hand.
- The post's single reader comment (2025-08-27) is a blunt statement of the pitch/naturalness split the vault's research also finds: 「声を高くするだけなら半日でもできるけど女性的な声を出すのは完全に才能」.

## Bearing on voice training
- The clearest Japanese community statement of the surgery/training division of labour: pre-operative training may be withheld because of fold-injury risk, post-operative training is routine and aimed at stability, and neither replaces the other. Read against [[dhaeseleer2023]], [[park2024]] and [[aires2023]] for the outcome side; the vault has no Japanese outcome study of the same question ([[japanese-voice]]).
- Her geography of where people currently travel (Korea prominent) and her refusal to give clinical detail are representative of how this material circulates: experience and second-hand report, with the technical layer left to clinics ([[kyoto-ent-clinic]], [[met-ent-tokyo]]).

## Limits
- A 4-koma and personal-essay blog, not a clinician's account: the author says plainly 「私はそこまで詳しくない」. The 37 posts are experience-led; the single comment quoted is one anonymous reader's opinion. Posts are undated in the category index and were read from the live site; only the 2025-08-27 post was read in full for this note.
- No acoustic measurements, no protocol, no outcome data.
