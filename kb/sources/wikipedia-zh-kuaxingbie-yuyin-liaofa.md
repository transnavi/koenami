# 跨性别语音疗法（中文维基百科）

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17 (extract in simplified Chinese): 语音治疗、语音疗法或语音训练指任何用于改善或修改人声的非手术技术。因为声音是一个人的生物性别与社会性别的社会线索，跨性别者可能会以声音训练或治疗作为其性别转换的一部分，以求其声音与其性别认同一致，减少其遭到性别错称的可能性。
- The extract states the goal set explicitly: 不论其目标是更加女性化、中性化还是男性化 (more feminine, neutral, or masculine goals all count), and frames 语音疗法 as one form of 性别肯定治疗 aimed at reducing 性别不安 and the chance of being read as trans.
- Interlanguage titles: en Transgender voice therapy · ja メラニー法 (the ja article is named after one community method) · no ko link held.

## Bearing on voice training
- The Chinese-language umbrella term for what this vault splits into [[voice-feminization-therapy]] and [[voice-masculinization]]; the ja interlanguage title shows each language names the field after its own dominant method.
- Its "reduce misgendering" framing is the same outcome measure the clinical literature uses (TWVQ items, [[wang2022]]).

## Limits
- Community-edited article; the effectiveness claims are carried by [[leyns2021]] and the other studies behind [[voice-feminization-therapy]].
- Note the term choice: the article says 语音疗法, while clinicians on both sides of the strait write 嗓音治疗/嗓音训练 (Taiwan [[lin2024]], [[voicedoctor-tw]]); mtf.wiki notes that medically 嗓音女性化训练属于言语治疗的范畴 ([[mtfwiki-vocal-therapy]]).
