# Type III Thyroplasty for Patients With High-Pitched Voice Disorders

## What it says
- "High-pitched voice disorders can present notable social and professional obstacles for cisgender and transgender men." PRISMA review to April 2024 across Medline/PubMed, Embase, Cochrane, ScienceDirect, SciELO, LILACS and Google Scholar.
- Nine studies, 69 unique patients, all level 4 evidence. "Most patients were cisgender men with primary mutational dysphonia (66.7%, n = 46)."
- "The meta-analysis showed a significant reduction in fundamental frequency after surgery, with a mean difference of -75.9 Hz (95% confidence interval [CI]: -91.5 to -60.3)."
- "Cisgender men and patients submitted to unilateral resection had higher preoperative fundamental frequency and showed a more pronounced reduction"; primary and secondary mutational dysphonia did not differ.
- Conclusion: "Type III thyroplasty effectively lowers vocal pitch in patients with high-pitched voice disorders. The procedure is viable for both cisgender and transgender men."

## Bearing on voice training
- The surgical endpoint for puberphonia that does not resolve with therapy, and the same operation used for voice masculinization. Two-thirds of the pooled patients were cis men with mutational dysphonia, so the trans-specific evidence inside this estimate is thin.

## Notes
- 69 patients across nine level-4 studies; no randomised comparison with voice therapy.
