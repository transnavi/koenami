# Thyroid cartilage and vocal fold reduction: a new phonosurgical method for male-to-female transsexuals

## What it says
- Ramathibodi Hospital, Mahidol University, Bangkok: a new pitch-raising procedure in 6 male-to-female trans women — composite resection of a vertical strip of anterior thyroid cartilage together with a segment of vocal fold, shortening both folds and increasing their tension (abstract).
- Mean pitch changed from 147 Hz before operation to 315 Hz afterward; all patients "were particularly pleased with the softness of the voice and the simultaneous loss of the prominentia laryngea (Adam's apple)"; longest follow-up 6 years (abstract).
- The authors open by stating that available pitch-raising techniques of the time "yield neither a good long-term result nor a high enough pitch to simulate a female voice" (abstract).

## Bearing on voice training
- The early Thai entry in the voice-feminization surgery literature, from the country that later became a destination for it ([[yanhee-voice-surgery]], [[kamol-hospital]]); its external, cartilage-plus-fold-resection design is the ancestor of the reduction approaches that later reviews group with thyroplasty-type methods [[song2017]] [[catani2024]].
- The 315 Hz mean sits well above the ~200 Hz Korean VFSRAC outcomes [[kim2017]] [[kim2024]] — an early demonstration of how much pitch an aggressive shortening can buy.

## Notes
- Six patients, surgeon-reported satisfaction, pre-2001 outcome measurement; read as a historical case series, not comparable to modern series with validated PROMs [[patient-reported-outcomes]].
