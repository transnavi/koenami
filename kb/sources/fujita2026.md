# Investigation for relative voice impression estimation

## What it says
- Predicts the perceptual difference between two utterances of the same speaker along antonymic axes (e.g. Dark–Bright, Cold–Warm) using recordings of one professional speaker reading in various styles.
- Self-supervised speech representations outperform classical acoustic features, especially for complex impressions; current multimodal LLMs proved unreliable for the pairwise task.

## Bearing on voice training
- Suggests self-supervised embeddings can capture impression shifts within a speaker — relevant to Koenami's 聴こえ方 estimate, but single-speaker evidence.
