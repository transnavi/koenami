# The role of f0 and formant frequencies in distinguishing the voices of men and women

## What it says
- Sentences by men were resynthesised with f0 shifted toward female values, formants shifted, or both; and vice versa for women.
- Shifting both f0 and formants changed the perceived sex in about 82% of cases; shifting either alone was usually ineffective.
- Results suggested f0 is somewhat more important than formants; with isolated /hVd/ syllables, f0 shifts alone were more often effective.

## Bearing on voice training
- Pitch alone or resonance alone rarely flips the listener; the tutorial's "both together" advice rests on this.
