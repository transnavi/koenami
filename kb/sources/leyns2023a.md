# Logopedie voor transgender vrouwen - Clara Leyns (UGent)

## What it says
- A 90-second Dutch-language pitch of a doctoral project on speech-language therapy for transgender women, presented at the Flemish PhD Cup 2023. The description of this upload states: "Bekijk de pitch waarmee Clara Leyns het brons behaalde bij de PhD Cup 2023" — the pitch took bronze.
- The nine-day-earlier trailer for the same final describes the format: "Clara Leyns (UGent) is één van de 8 onderzoekers die op 19 oktober strijden voor de Vlaamse PhD Cup 🏆 In aanloop naar die finale proberen de 8 finalisten jou alvast in 90 seconden te overtuigen."
- The clip is therefore the researcher's own public summary of the Ghent speech-feminization programme in Dutch, pitched to a general Flemish audience rather than to clinicians.

## Bearing on voice training
- The Dutch-language public face of the Ghent line whose clinical results this vault holds in English: the systematic review [[leyns2021]], the short-term programme results [[leyns2023]], the long-term follow-up [[leyns2024]] and the intonation trial [[papeleu2026]]. It is the same research group speaking Dutch, in the register a Dutch-speaking trans viewer would meet it in.
- Together with the Transvisie pages [[transvisie]] it gives the Dutch-language community a route into the field — one from the clinic side, one from the university side — where the vault previously held only Dutch-language clinical papers.

## Limits
- A competition pitch, not a publication: 90 seconds, no methods, no numbers. The video was not transcribed for this note, so only the occasion and the presenter's affiliation and result are recorded. Graded community.
- Her thesis itself is not the subject of a note here; the vault cites it through the peer-reviewed papers [[leyns2021]] [[leyns2023]] [[leyns2024]].
