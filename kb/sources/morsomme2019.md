# Translation, Adaptation, and Preliminary Validation of Dacakis and Davies' "Transsexual Voice Questionnaire (Male to Female)" in French

## What it says
- French adaptation of the TVQ MtF by the Voice Unit of the University of Liège (Belgium) with the Laboratoire Parole et Langage, Aix-en-Provence (France), following WHO translation recommendations; 36 Belgian and French trans women took part (abstract, "METHODS").
- The abstract notes the starting problem: assessment "originally constructed for individuals with dysphonia" and "no French-language questionnaire" for people seeking voice feminization.
- Results: "excellent repeatability and reliability"; items correlate with the six areas of concern Davies and Johnson identified — social interaction, emotions, gender identity, effort and concentration, physical aspects of voice production, and pitch.
- Concurrent validity could not be measured "owing to lack of sufficiently detailed stories"; the psychometric properties are judged acceptable and the questionnaire usable "as is in daily clinical practice".

## Bearing on voice training
- The French-language member of the TVQ family: [[dacakis2013]] original, [[mora2018a]] Spanish, [[salm2020]] German, [[robotti2022]] Italian, [[schwarz2017]] Brazilian Portuguese, [[taskin2022]] Turkish, [[wang2022]] simplified Chinese.
- Its six concern areas map onto the domains a French-speaking client and orthophoniste can now discuss in a scored instrument [[loret2019]].

## Notes
- Belgian–French mix, so metropolitan French; n = 36 with no test–retest interval or coefficients quoted in the abstract beyond the summary adjectives.
- Concurrent validity unmeasured — the authors' own stated limitation.
