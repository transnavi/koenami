# Parâmetros vocais e prosódicos na fala de influenciadores digitais transgênero da mídia social brasileira

## What it says
- Corpus: audio from twelve Instagram videos, selected on themes and view counts, by Brazilian trans and cis digital influencers (abstract). Perceptual-auditory assessment was blind: judges stated what gender they took the speaker to be and marked the prosodic boundaries and prominences used expressively.
- Acoustic analysis measured F0 for speaker characterisation, f0 variation and pause production as markers of prosodic boundaries, and f0 and intensity variation as markers of prominence.
- The central negative finding: 「a fala dos sujeitos trans, seja por sua expressão vocal, seja por suas características prosódicas, não é avaliada exclusivamente por meio da recuperação de padrões acústicos」 — trans speech is not evaluated solely by recovering acoustic patterns.
- And the formulation the authors settle on: trans speakers' speech 「é dialeticamente constituída, pois existem pontos de aproximação e distanciamento」 — there are points of both convergence with and distance from the speech of the declared gender.

## Bearing on voice training
- A rare Brazilian study that measures **prosody** — boundaries, prominences, pauses — rather than sustained vowels, and it finds listeners reading gender from something other than the acoustic values ([[intonation]], [[gender-perception]]). This is the perceptual finding that the automatic-scoring tools in the vault sidestep ([[machine-gender-scoring]]).
- Instagram audio is uncontrolled (compression, editing, microphones), which is why the "convergence and distance" reading is the defensible one and per-speaker numbers are not. Methodologically it sits with [[santos2020a]], which used YouTube video the same way.

## Notes
- Twelve videos, convenience selection, no clinical population and no treatment information about the speakers; graded low.
- Claims taken from the Portuguese abstract; the judge panel size and the measured values were not read.
