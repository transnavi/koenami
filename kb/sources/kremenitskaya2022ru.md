# Суицидальная активность и коморбидная психическая патология у лиц с гендерной дисфорией

## What it says
- Retrospective analysis of records of 926 patients who applied with gender-related complaints in Russia between January 2014 and April 2021: "FtM 515 (55.7%), MtF 411 (44.3%)", median age 24 years (13–65) (abstract).
- «В группе трансгендерных пациентов у 75,2% отсутствуют какие-либо психические заболевания, кроме гендерных нарушений» — 75.2% had no mental illness other than the gender-related one (Russian abstract); comorbid mental pathology was present in 24.8% on average.
- "The suicide rate in transgender group was 12.5%"; for one patient with suicidal experience there were 1.3 attempts, from 1 to 5 (abstract).
- Suicide activity and comorbid pathology depended on the age at which the gender-related condition began, and the authors name modifiable risk factors: support of relatives, external correspondence to the associated gender, early steps to "change gender", earlier application for medical care (abstract). The Russian wording is «внешнее соответствие ассоциируемому гендеру» — outward correspondence to the associated gender.

## Bearing on voice training
- The paper's own risk list includes outward correspondence to the associated gender among the modifiable factors, which is the ground voice work stands on: in this Russian cohort, the population seeking care was young (median 24), and the authors tie better outcomes to earlier steps and to external gender congruence [[transgender-care-context]].
- Baseline data for a language sphere where the vault otherwise holds no clinical sample; it says nothing about voice directly and should not be cited for voice outcomes [[voice-across-languages]].

## Notes
- The Russian title recorded here is the one CyberLeninka holds (…у лиц с гендерной дисфорией); the English translation in PubMed renders it "gender dystrophia", which is a translation of the same title, not a different condition.
- Retrospective chart review at one Russian centre; the authors themselves note that Russian data on this population were previously absent.
- Only the abstracts were read.
