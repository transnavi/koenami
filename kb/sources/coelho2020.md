# Self-perception of voice symptoms and vocal health and hygiene knowledge in popular and classical singers

## What it says
- 186 singers (104 popular, 82 classical), aged 17–60, completed the Brazilian validated Voice Symptom Scale (VoiSS / Escala de Sintomas Vocais) and the Questionário de Saúde e Higiene Vocal (QSHV).
- "Classical singers presented greater perception of vocal symptoms when compared to the popular ones for total and emotional scores" of the VoiSS.
- "The popular and classical groups do not make any difference regarding health and vocal hygiene, even though the groups obtained values above the QSHV normality score."
- "There was no correlation between knowledge about vocal health and hygiene and vocal symptoms in singers."

## Bearing on voice training
- Knowing the vocal-hygiene rules did not predict having fewer symptoms. Hygiene education is worth giving, but it is not a substitute for changing how the voice is actually used — the same point [[vanhoutte2011]] makes by classing hygiene as *indirect* therapy.

## Notes
- Cross-sectional self-report from Brazilian singers; no measurement of whether the knowledge was acted on. Published bilingually (English/Portuguese) in CoDAS; the abstract read was the English one.
