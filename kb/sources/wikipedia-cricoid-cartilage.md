# Cricoid cartilage (English Wikipedia)

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17: "The cricoid cartilage, or simply cricoid or cricoid ring, is the only complete ring of cartilage around the trachea. It forms the back part of the voice box and functions as an attachment site for muscles, cartilages, and ligaments involved in opening and closing the airway and in producing speech."
- Short description: "Complete ring of cartilage around the trachea."

## Bearing on voice training
- The ring the thyroid cartilage tilts on, and the one the arytenoids sit on. "Crico-" in cricothyroid and cricoarytenoid names this cartilage.
