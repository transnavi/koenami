# 性同一性障害における音声外科手術―Female-to-Male症例に対する甲状軟骨形成III型について―

A single FTM case in which surgery was chosen for the voice alone — no hormones, no genital surgery.

## What it says
- The case as the English abstract states it: 「We report a case of GID (gender identity disorder) of the female-to-male type, where her vocal pitch was lowered to her satisfaction by Thyroplasty type 3.」
- The framing about variation in the population: 「The distress of GID patients derives from the contradiction between their sexual physical features and gender self-consciousness. As to the levels of distress and the means patients select to resolve the problems they face, individual variations are great.」
- What made this case unusual: 「The present patient did not want either transsexual surgery or hormonal treatment, but merely her vocal pitch lowered.」 The operation was offered on that basis, and 「Thyroplasty type 3 successfully lowered her vocal pitch as desired without any complication.」 The abstract notes that surgical details and key points are described in the paper.

## Bearing on voice training
- In Japan's own surgical sequence, type III is the pitch-*lowering* operation and type IV the pitch-raising one ([[tanaka1981]], [[sanuki2007]]). This case applies the lowering operation to a trans man who wanted nothing else done ([[voice-masculinization]], [[voice-surgery]]).
- It is also a reminder that testosterone is not a precondition for a lowered voice — in the standard route the pitch drop comes from hormone treatment ([[nimura2015]], [[ziegler2018]]) and therapy is offered when that proves insufficient ([[barros2018]], [[santos2020]]). Here the order was reversed entirely.
