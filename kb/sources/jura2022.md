# 100万回再生された両声類の過去最短の女声講座

At 5.5 million views on the 2026-09-17 check, one of the most-watched Japanese 女声 lessons anywhere. Its appeal is stated in its own title: it is the *shortest* one.

## What it says
- Platform metadata, 2026-09-17: title 「100万回再生された両声類の過去最短の女声講座」, channel じゅら (@jura1124), published 2022-06-17, 5,495,516 views.
- The channel's own description (fetched 2026-09-17) is a content schedule rather than a manifesto: 「毎日19時~shorts投稿！　毎週月、金曜日新作動画投稿中！　女声釣り配信不定期！　笑ってもらえたらいいな！」 — daily shorts, twice-weekly videos, and 「女声釣り配信」, streams in which viewers are invited to be caught out by the voice. The tone is entertainment.
- The title's 「100万回再生された」 refers to an earlier video of the same kind and is the uploader's own framing, not a fact about this one's reach. The channel is represented by 株式会社モカ.

## Bearing on voice training
- A data point about how Japanese learners actually encounter this material: the highest-reach lesson is marketed as the shortest, through an entertainment channel, and sold on the voice catching listeners out. That is the same criterion the 両声類 corpus has used since 2007 — whether the 声質 convinces a listener ([[nicodic-ryoseirui]], [[nicovideo-ryoseirui-tag]]) — rather than any measured target.
- The register vocabulary such a lesson has to work in is the Japanese one: 地声 against 裏声, with ミックスボイス in between ([[wikipedia-ja-uragoe]], [[wikipedia-ja-seiku]], [[brushvoice-joseigoe]]).

## Limits
- Verified from platform metadata only: oEmbed for author and title, the watch page for date and view count. The lesson's content, length and method were not examined, and nothing here should be read as a claim about what it teaches.
- View counts are as of the 2026-09-17 check.
