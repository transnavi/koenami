# Equal temperament (English Wikipedia)

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17: "Equal temperament is a musical temperament that divides the octave into 12 identical parts. It is a logarithmic scale with a ratio equal to the 12th root of 2. The scale unit is known as a semitone or half step."
- Short description: "Musical tuning system."

## Interlanguage titles
ja: 平均律 · ko: 평균율 · es: Temperamento igual · de: Gleichstufige Stimmung · fr: Gamme tempérée

## Bearing on voice training
- The explicit statement that the musical pitch scale is logarithmic: equal steps are equal *ratios*, not equal hertz. That is why a pitch tracker that reports semitones is using a log scale, and why "how many Hz did I raise" is the wrong comparison across different starting pitches.
