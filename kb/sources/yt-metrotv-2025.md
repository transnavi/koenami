# Terapi Bicara untuk Transgender

## What it says
- An Indonesian national television segment (METRO TV, published 2025-09-24) on speech therapy for transgender people. The broadcaster's own description states the premise: «Terapi bicara dapat membantu transgender menyesuaikan suara sesuai identitas mereka. Program ini menjadi dukungan penting untuk kepercayaan diri dan kesejahteraan mereka» — speech therapy can help transgender people adjust their voice to match their identity, and such a programme is important support for their confidence and wellbeing (video description, 2026-09-18).
- The segment is tagged by the broadcaster with #Transgender, #TerapiBicara and #Inclusive.
- The channel is the verified account of METRO TV, an Indonesian national news network; the description read carries the network's social links and no clinical detail.

## Bearing on voice training
- Evidence that the Indonesian-language public framing of trans voice work runs through «terapi bicara» (speech therapy) delivered by clinicians, a different starting point from the community peer-teaching that dominates the other languages in this batch [[speech-language-pathology]] [[community-training]].
- It is the only Indonesian-language item in this pass that names voice adjustment for trans people as a health service rather than as a surgical procedure, which is the counterpart of the surgical framing in the Indonesian Wikipedia article [[wikipedia-id-transgender]].
- For the vault's language coverage it establishes the Indonesian term «terapi bicara» alongside «feminisasi suara» [[voice-across-languages]].
