# 甲状軟骨形成術3型の検討

The fullest type III series found in this pass: 19 cases with pre- and post-operative speaking F0, the indications, and a unilateral-versus-bilateral contrast.

## What it says
- What type 3 does, as stated: 「甲状軟骨形成術3型（3型）は甲状軟骨の前後径を縮める術式である。その結果，声帯は弛緩し話声位（speaking fundamental frequency：SFF）のピッチは下がり，喉頭効率も改善する。」
- Indications: 「主な適応疾患は，声帯萎縮や変声障害でのSFF高音化に対する音声治療の無効例，声帯の過緊張が気息性嗄声の要因となっている症例など」 — vocal fold atrophy and mutational dysphonia where voice therapy has failed, and cases where hypertonicity of the folds drives a breathy dysphonia.
- The series: 19 patients operated at 一色クリニック・京都ボイスサージセンター between 2001 and 2006 — 17 men and 2 women, aged 18 to 73 (mean 32.6). Diagnoses: vocal fold atrophy 8, mutational dysphonia 4, sulcus vocalis 3, atrophy with sulcus 2, and 「声を低くしたいという強い本人の希望と性同一性障害が各1例ずつ」 — one case each of a strong patient request to lower the voice, and gender identity disorder.
- Fine detail on the operation: all under local anaesthesia; 「局所麻酔で施行しているため，全例において患者の満足のいく音声が得られた」; 29 sides treated in total, with 「face to face」 cartilage-edge management in 24 sides and 「overlapping」 in 5, and an average resected cartilage width of 2.4 mm for the face-to-face group.
- Outcomes: bilateral operations lowered SFF from a mean 43.0 semitones (196.0 Hz) to 35.4 semitones (127.8 Hz); unilateral from 42.9 semitones (198.4 Hz) to 37.7 semitones (149.3 Hz). The AC/DC ratio rose from a mean 36.7 % to 52.1 %.
- Conclusions: type 3 lowers SFF satisfactorily, and 「両側施行症例は片側施行症例よりもSFFの低下効果が大きかった」 — bilateral produced a bigger drop than unilateral.

## Bearing on voice training
- The GID indication is one case out of 19, and it is listed last among the diagnoses — a snapshot of how marginal trans voice surgery still was in Japan's main framework-surgery series in 2008, next to atrophy and mutational dysphonia ([[voice-surgery]], [[transgender-care-context]]).
- The efficiency result, AC/DC ratio rising from 36.7 % to 52.1 %, means the operation changes something beyond the pitch number, which is the ground under [[nakamura2022]]'s claim that type IV raises quality of life as well as speaking pitch ([[voice-quality]], [[patient-reported-outcomes]]).
- The paper states the change both in semitones and in Hz ([[pitch-and-scales]]); by its own scale the bilateral drop is 43.0 → 35.4 semitones, about 7.5 semitones, against 196.0 → 127.8 Hz.

## Notes
- Retrospective series, no control group, single centre and single surgical lineage; graded low. Only the Japanese abstract was read.
- The two female patients are not separated from the 17 men in the outcome figures reported here.
