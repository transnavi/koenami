# 音声障害診療ガイドライン2018年版のエッセンスと活用

How the Japanese voice-disorders clinical practice guideline is built and how it is meant to be used — including the classification codes that make multi-centre work possible.

## What it says
- What the guideline contains: 「音声障害の定義と分類, 疫学, 検査と診断法, 治療を示し, 本邦の音声障害診療の現状を考慮したエビデンスに基づいて, ガイドライン作成委員会のコンセンサスが得られた診療が推奨されている」(abstract).
- The classification is taken from abroad and fitted to Japanese practice: 「音声障害の分類は American speech-language hearing association (ASHA) の Classification manual for voice disorders-I を基にして, 本邦の診療に即した音声障害の分類表が掲載されている」. The point of the codes is administrative as well as clinical: 「分類表に示したコード番号による音声障害を来す疾患の患者データ管理を行うと, 多施設との共同研究を行う際にも患者の疾患抽出が楽に行える」.
- Epidemiology as reported: voice disorders are said to be more common 「逆流性食道炎を合併している場合や, 女性, プロフェッショナルボイスユーザー, 高齢者に多い」 — in people with reflux oesophagitis, and among women, professional voice users and older people.
- The diagnostic methods covered: 「問診, 聴覚心理的評価, 自覚的評価, 内視鏡検査, 空気力学的検査, ボイスプロファイル, 音響分析, 喉頭筋電図」 — interview, auditory-perceptual assessment, self-assessment, endoscopy, aerodynamic testing, voice profile, acoustic analysis and laryngeal electromyography.
- Treatment is covered under three headings — 「薬物療法, 音声治療, 手術治療」 — and the guideline closes with 「12の Clinical Question (CQ)」, each with a recommendation grade based on a literature search.

## Bearing on voice training
- The framework the vault's Japanese clinical material sits inside: auditory-perceptual and self-assessment ratings, acoustics and aerodynamic measures as the standard assessment battery, and therapy alongside surgery as the treatment categories ([[clinical-guidelines]], [[voice-health]]). The instruments the vault tracks — TVQ/TWVQ, Vocal Congruence Scale — are self-assessment tools inside exactly this battery ([[patient-reported-outcomes]]).
- The ASHA-derived classification is why a Japanese paper can label a case by code; it is also why a gender-related voice complaint has no obvious slot in the scheme, which is the gap [[mesuda2005]] described in 2005 and [[nakamura2024]] is still teaching around ([[transgender-care-context]], [[speech-language-pathology]]).
- This is the document a Spanish-language clinic blog cites as "Umeno 2019" ([[logopediaymas-feminizacion-voz]]); the guideline itself is the 2018 edition summarised here in 2022.

## Notes
- A summary article rather than the guideline: it reports the guideline's structure, not its 12 recommendations or their grades. Graded low for that reason; the guideline document itself is the citable source for any recommendation.
