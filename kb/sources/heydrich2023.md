# [Schnittstelle Logopädie im Prozess der Geschlechtsangleichung von Mann zu Frau]

## What it says
- German-language paper from a Cologne Logopädie practice and the SRH Hochschule für Gesundheit, Düsseldorf, on where Stimmfeminisierung fits in the MzF ("Mann-zu-Frau") transition pathway; four expert interviews across otolaryngology, endocrinology and plastic surgery (abstract, "MATERIAL UND METHODEN").
- The German abstract's framing: "Die Stimme ist für die Geschlechtsidentifikation während des Transitionsprozesses entscheidend, da die Stimmfeminisierung einen bedeutenden Anteil zum Gelingen des Passing hat."
- Uniform expert recommendation that "voice treatment should be considered early in the transition", with conservative therapy preferred "to avoid e.g. irreversible surgery of the larynx" (English abstract, "RESULTS").
- The focus of voice treatment "is centrally on the adaption of the speaking fundamental frequency"; postoperatively, Logopädie adapts the speech pattern to the new anatomy and prevents "uneconomic voice and speech".
- The authors call for consultation with professional societies and "more randomized trials of specific voice treatments in MzF-trans*" before a guideline consensus.

## Bearing on voice training
- The German-language statement of the therapy-first principle this vault holds from [[nhse1719]] and [[leyns2021]]; the vocabulary is Germany's clinical register — Logopädie, Stimmfeminisierung, Passing, Transition.
- Notes explicitly that no German-language guideline existed; the expert-interview consensus is the stopgap.

## Notes
- Four interviews is a small expert panel; graded low. Terminology "MzF-Trans*" carries the era's starred usage.
- Complements the German TVQ validation [[salm2020]]: Germany has the instrument and the practitioners, and — per this paper — not yet the guideline.
