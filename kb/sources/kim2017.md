# A New Conceptual Approach for Voice Feminization: 12 Years of Experience

## What it says
- Single-author retrospective series from the Yeson Voice Center, Seoul: 362 patients treated with vocal fold shortening and retrodisplacement of the anterior commissure (VFSRAC) plus phonatory-pattern rehabilitation between 2003 and 2014 — 313 male-to-female trans women, the rest androphonia (34), androgenital syndrome (8), aplastic anemia treated with androgens (4), pseudohermaphroditism (3) (abstract).
- Mean F0 rose from 144.1 Hz preoperatively to 207.4 Hz at 6 months; the mean increase was 53.0 Hz in the female androphonia group and 73.6 Hz in the MTF group after postoperative phonatory-pattern retraining (abstract).
- Acoustic and aerodynamic assessments stayed within the normal range and mucosal-wave regularity was maintained; the author reads this as patients achieving a natural phonation pattern (abstract).
- Conclusion: VFSRAC plus phonatory-pattern retraining "was effective for patients who wanted a natural feminine voice"; level of evidence 4 (abstract).

## Bearing on voice training
- The largest single-surgeon VFSRAC series, and the anchor for the Korean surgical ecosystem the community describes in Korean ([[yeson-voice-center]]) and Chinese ([[mtfwiki-vocal-surgery]], [[bili-luna2023]]).
- The paper's own framing — surgery plus retraining of the phonatory pattern, with the MTF group gaining more F0 after the retraining program — treats postoperative voice work as part of the method, not an extra ([[voice-feminization-therapy]]).

## Notes
- Retrospective, uncontrolled, single center, surgeon-reported outcomes; the femininity increase is a subjective postoperative assessment by the same clinic (abstract).
