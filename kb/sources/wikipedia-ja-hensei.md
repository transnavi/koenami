# 変声

The article the brief lists as 声変わり is titled 変声 and the everyday form is the redirect; the MediaWiki API resolves 「声変わり」 to this page (redirects=1, 2026-09-17). Interlanguage links include **Voice change** (en), 변성기 (ko), 變聲 (zh), Mue (voix) (fr) (langlinks API 2026-09-17).

## What it says
- Intro: 「変声（へんせい）とは、ヒトの発声器官の成長によって声帯振動、発声の様式が変わり声の音域や音色が変化すること。一般的に声変わり（こえがわり）とも言う。人によって個人差がある。」
- The definition is production-side: it is the larynx growing, and the change shows up in both 音域 and 音色. The article ends the intro by flagging individual variation rather than giving ages.

## Bearing on voice training
- The Japanese scene's trans-man material assumes 変声 has already happened or has been produced by testosterone, and asks what behavioural work remains; the 声 page gives the correlating number, 男性のほとんどは第二次性徴で急に低い声に変わる ([[wikipedia-ja-koe]]). For evidence, the vault holds the longitudinal data ([[hollien1994]], [[harries1997]], [[harries1998]], [[hacki1999]], [[pedersen1990]]) and the Japanese clinical case reports ([[yanagi2015]], [[nimura2015]]).
