# 性同一性障害に関する診断と治療のガイドライン（第4版改）

The Japanese national clinical guideline as it stood when the insurance decision below was made. It is the document that the later editions and the whole Japanese clinical literature cite.

## What it says
- Title page: 「性同一性障害に関する診断と治療のガイドライン（第4版改）／日本精神神経学会 性同一性障害に関する委員会」. The committee list on the title page names its members by clinic, and a separate 外部委員 list includes 岡山大学病院ジェンダーセンター's 難波祐三郎 and 中塚幹也.
- 目次 as printed (pp. ii–iii) runs: Ⅰ はじめに; Ⅱ 我国における性同一性障害の医療の歴史（ブルーボーイ事件と第1版、治療開始例と第2版、特例法と第3版）; Ⅲ 性同一性障害の医療の現状とその問題点; Ⅳ 第4版ガイドライン改訂の理念とその要点（二次性徴抑制ホルモンの使用、ホルモン療法開始年齢の引き下げ、医療を担当する医師の要件と研修環境の整備）; Ⅴ 診断と治療のガイドライン; Ⅵ すでに治療を開始している症例への対応; Ⅶ 診療に当たる精神科医師に関する暫定措置; Ⅷ おわりに.
- Under Ⅴ-4 治療のガイドライン the subsections are 1) 精神科領域の治療, 2) 身体的治療 — and 身体的治療 contains exactly three items: (1) ホルモン療法（二次性徴抑制療法を含む）, (2) FTMに対する乳房切除術, (3) 性別適合手術. **There is no voice section in the 目次.**
- The edition history printed on the guideline index at jspn.or.jp (2026-09-17): 第3版 2011-05改訂; 第4版 2011-11-19, 2014-05-17一部改訂, 2017-05-20一部改訂, 第4版改 2018-01-20.

## Bearing on voice training
- The negative result is the finding: in the Japanese national guideline, voice is not a treatment category. It appears in the history section (Ⅱ) only as part of the record, not in the treatment chapter's list of physical interventions ([[jspn-guideline2024]] confirms the same for the 5th edition).
- Consequences for the vault's Japanese notes: the surgical and therapeutic work on voice in Japan is published in ENT and laryngology venues — 喉頭, 音声言語医学, 気管食道科学会会報 — rather than in gender-medicine guidance ([[matsumoto2022]], [[nakamura2021]], [[nakamura2022]], [[sakuraba2009]]), and voice training is offered by ENT clinics and commercial schools, not by the guideline's framework ([[kyoto-ent-clinic]], [[brushvoice-joseigoe]]).

## Limits
- Read from the published PDF: the title page, the committee list and the 目次. The treatment chapter's body text was not read, so this note claims only what the table of contents and the index page show. The absence of a voice section is asserted for the 目次, not for every sentence of the body.
- Superseded for current practice by the 5th edition ([[jspn-guideline2024]]); kept here because sources still cite it.
