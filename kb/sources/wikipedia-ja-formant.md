# フォルマント

The Japanese article for *formant*; interlanguage links include **Formant** (en), 포먼트 (ko), 共振峰 (zh), ความสั่นพ้องเด่น (th) (langlinks API 2026-09-17).

## What it says
- Summary endpoint 2026-09-17, in full: 「フォルマント（英: formant）は音声の周波数スペクトル上で周囲よりも強度が大きい周波数帯域である。ホルマントとも。」
- The definition is spectral-only: a band of greater intensity in the spectrum, with no mention of the vocal tract or of resonance; the alternative spelling ホルマント is recorded.

## Bearing on voice training
- The Japanese method page for メラニー法 says the method works by 「具体的には、フォルマントを調整する」 ([[wikipedia-melanie-ho]]), so a reader arriving from the Japanese community meets the word as a training lever rather than a measurement. The measurement side is in [[formants]] and [[tanaka2022]].
- Community tooling for Japanese speakers scores formants directly; a note.com author published a formant-based voice-judging app ([[note-formant-app]]).
