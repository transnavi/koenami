# 男伪女御姐音基础教学教程（幻音学院）

A transcript of an instructor's lesson, opening "hello 大家好 这里是幻音的寒衣". Unlike the tutorial videos it is a continuous explanation spoken out loud, so its teaching moves and its corrections can be followed in text.

## What it says
- The central correction is against falsetto: 我们经常会犯的一个错：假声伪音 ("a mistake we often make: falsetto 伪音"). 假声 is defined as 通过挤压我们的声带和喉咙，发出来的一个比较尖锐的一个声音 ("a shrill sound produced by pressing the vocal folds and the throat"). The target is distinguished by shape rather than by pitch: the female voice's thinness is 相较于我们的本音来说是一个比较圆润的细, where falsetto is 非常扁然后非常尖锐的一个细 — rounded thinness against flat shrillness.
- The fix is 咽腔共鸣, taught with a feel test: 把一只手放在我们的喉结上面，然后舌头的话微微吐出一点，放在我们的下嘴唇上面，去发一个「诶」这个音 — the hand is on the larynx, the tongue tip forward on the lower lip, and on 「诶」 你会感受到你的喉结有被往上提就是往里缩的一个感觉. That is described as 咽腔共鸣.
- 咽腔 and 喉腔 are distinguished by what the larynx does under the hand: with 咽腔 the 喉结会往上提; with 喉腔 我们的喉结是不会动的, and 喉腔和咽腔比起来的话，声音的话会比咽腔发的声音更粗.
- Having found 咽腔共鸣, the next step is to 抬我们的软腭, with one correction: 我们的软腭不是一个半拱形的嘛，我们只有中间拱起来的那个中间那个地方一个用力点，其实你只需要记住我们只有中间一个用力点，其他地方用力那就是错的.
- The prosody tip is the final-syllable one: on the same sentence, male speech ends 非常干净利落, while 他们女生的话，就是每一句话的最后一个字，尾音的地方…他们会往后，有一点拖一下的感觉, demonstrated on 今天你吃饭了嘛 — the trailing 「嘛」. The stated value is that this detail 能够更加的生动, and the homework is 平时的话我们也可以就是经常的观察一下，就是女生讲话是一个什么样的.

## Bearing on voice training
- The larynx-under-the-hand test is a usable check for the larynx-height control the rest of the corpus describes abstractly: raising the larynx shortens the vocal tract and lifts F2 and above, which is what the vault's [[formants]] and [[vocal-tract-anatomy]] notes treat as the main formant lever, and what the handbook formalises as 高喉位 raising F2 [[aogi2021]].
- The falsetto/rounded-thin distinction restates the register problem in perceptual terms — [[vocal-registers]] describes the M1/M2 boundary the community keeps crossing — and the 软腭 correction (one contact point in the middle, not a broad press) is the practical version of the error list at [[bili-cv9623302]] and of the handbook's warning that 抬软腭 practice done hard is the harmful variant [[moegirl-weisheng]].
- The 尾音 observation is a segmental-prosodic cue: final lengthening and a falling-off release rather than a clipped stop. It is the same cue the article course makes into a rule (字尾 returns to 女虚音 with 低-高-低 pitch, [[bili-cv9417414]]), and the same intuition behind training intonation range, which the meta-analytic evidence associates with perceived gender alongside pitch and resonance [[intonation]], [[leung2018]].
