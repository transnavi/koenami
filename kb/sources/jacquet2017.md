# Apport du biofeedback dans le cadre d'un traitement de féminisation vocale chez une participante transgenre (M-t-F) : étude de cas（Jacquet 2017）

## What it says
- Setting, in the thesis's own words: trans people in a gender-reassignment pathway « expriment le désir de féminiser leur voix », and some « se tournent vers les vocologistes afin de réaliser une thérapie de féminisation vocale » (abstract). It notes that « de nombreux paramètres vocaux influencent la féminité d'une voix », citing Davies et al. 2015.
- The stated difficulty of the work: it « est complexe de par la nécessité de réaliser de nombreux ajustements pour la personne tant au niveau de sa technique que de son usage vocal » — many adjustments in technique *and* in daily usage (abstract).
- The rationale for the tool: to help learn the new behaviour and transfer it into daily life, « l'utilisation d'un traitement par biofeedback semble pertinente », a technique it says has proved itself in medicine for about ten years (citing Maryn, De Bodt & Van Cauwenberg 2006).
- Design: a single case, comparing a biofeedback treatment against classic logopaedic treatment with the aim of raising F0; the question posed is whether two weeks of biofeedback raise F0 and whether such a treatment is relevant within voice feminization therapy (abstract).
- Result: « un effet positif du traitement par biofeedback » — the participant raised her F0 and reduced the percentage of biofeedback activation over the treatment, which the author reads as learning of the targeted motor behaviour (abstract).
- The two forward-looking conclusions: biofeedback is worth proposing « en complément d'un traitement logopédique » (as an addition to, not a replacement for, logopaedic treatment), and « la réalisation d'un coaching de la participante pendant le traitement par biofeedback semble primordiale » (abstract).
- Its own limitation is stated in the abstract: « Les résultats sont toutefois à considérer avec modérations étant donné qu'il s'agit d'une étude de cas exploratoire », with larger samples and methodological changes wanted.

## Bearing on voice training
- The Francophone articulation of the carryover problem: the therapy session teaches the new motor behaviour, and what is being tested is whether a device worn outside the clinic generalises it. That is the same question the published, peer-reviewed version of this line of work answers with a vibrotactile dosimeter [[morsomme2022]] and the earlier conference report posed [[remacle2018]].
- Its position that biofeedback complements rather than replaces logopaedic work matches how the technique literature treats instrumentation generally: as feedback on a target already taught, not as the teaching ([[voice-therapy-techniques]]).
- Records the name of the external tool that measures vocal load and pitch in this line of work: the VoxLog dosimeter (named in the 2018 conference abstract, [[remacle2018]]).
