# 【女声】たった今、喉仏を上げる感覚がわかった気がするので一応。

A beginner's two-paragraph note on day three of 女声 practice, recording how they arrived at the larynx-raising sensation that every Japanese guide starts with.

## What it says
- The premise as the writer states it: 「女声を出すまでの第一関門であろう、喉仏を上でキープさせること」, with the common routes being 「水や唾液を飲み込んだ瞬間に息を止めたり、舌根を上げたり、舌骨を上げたり」.
- What failed first: 「何度も水を飲み、何回もキープしようとしても声をだそうとしたり、呼吸をしようとするとすぐに下へ下がってしまったり」.
- What worked: 「舌根が上顎にくっついてる感じもしてて、それっぽい声が出た」 — the writer describes the result as possibly エッジボイス and summarises the cue as 「自分的には吐こうとする感じに近かった」.

## Bearing on voice training
- A clear statement of the first hurdle in Japanese practice — holding the larynx high — and of the swallowing-based method learners use to find it [[larynx-anatomy]] [[japanese-community]].
- It is the community practice the English-language wiki singles out as dangerous when held: swallowing is fine for locating the larynx, holding the posture is not [[sumi-questionable-exercises]]. The writer's report that the position collapses the moment they try to phonate or breathe is the same difficulty, from the learner's side [[voice-therapy-techniques]].

## Notes
- A beginner's note, day three of practice, containing no measurement and no sustained result; the writer marks the whole observation as provisional (「一応」). Recorded because it documents the technique's actual first step in Japanese practice, not because it establishes anything about outcomes.
