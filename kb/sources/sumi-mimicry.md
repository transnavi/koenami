# Mimicry (Voice Resource Project wiki)

## What it says
- Why it is claimed to be necessary: 「Some parts of the voice are so small and hard to consciously modify that mimicry is the only way we know of. Mimicry is a good way to learn new microbehaviours, speech patterns and can also eventually modify your voiceprint. It breaks down the barrier of the subconscious wanting to keep the voice confined to what it's used to and allows for new tweaks to happen.」
- What it is claimed to help with: 「microbehaviours / unconscious speech aspects」, 「speech patterns intonation」, 「sibilants」, 「tempo fluctuation」, 「consistency」.
- What it is claimed not to help with: 「vocal weight」, 「pitch range」, 「resonance」, 「clarity」 — with the caveat 「This is assuming we're not going to spend years doing it, since it is technically possible to learn these somewhat through mimicry at an extremely slow rate unless we've been predisposed to learning them already.」
- Choosing a clip: 「identify an aspect of voice you want to change or a voice you want to have」; 「find an example of that voice quality that is at a similar pitch and not too far out of your resonance range」; 「make sure it's not too fast, or it will be harder to mimic」; 「get a 2-5 second clip, just a short phrase of about 5-10 words, cut it from the clip and add a beep at the beginning」.
- The method, under the heading Mimicry 101: 「the reason mimicry works is that it lets your subconscious figure stuff out intuitively so don't overanalyse」; 「try to copy all speech patterns - intonation, tempo, resonance drops, volume」; 「get into the character, expect their voice to come out of your mouth and believe it 100%, don't just hope, believe.」; 「play the voice in your head before and during your attempt」; and two delivery methods — 「dub over the clip as it's playing in real time」 and 「listen to a clip, and while listening mouth the words but don't speak, then once it's finished try to do your best impression」.
- Stated benefits: learning speech patterns; 「if you find your self perception is negative, it can help you get a voice that sounds "less like you" and overcome self bias」; and learning 「microbehaviours and other unconscious aspects of voice that are difficult to change normally」.

## Bearing on voice training
- The wiki's own limit is the important half: mimicry is offered for the layer that isolation exercises cannot reach, and explicitly not as the route to weight, resonance, clarity or range — those are to be trained directly first ([[sumi-getting-started]], [[sumi-vocal-weight]], [[sumi-resonance]]).
- The step in the curriculum is therefore ordered: get to the pitch and resonance of the target voice first, then mimic ([[sumi-voice-examples]] supplies the clips, [[sumi-microbehaviour-list]] the things to listen for).
- The claims about the subconscious and about self-perception are the wiki's own theory; no source is cited, and the "voiceprint" it says mimicry can modify is not given an acoustic definition ([[voice-impressions]]).
