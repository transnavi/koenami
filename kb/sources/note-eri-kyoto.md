# 京都耳鼻咽喉音聲手術医院の女性声にする手術１

A short first-person report of Wendler glottoplasty at a Kyoto clinic two years earlier, and of the year of living with the result.

## What it says
- Operation and recovery: 「私は約2年前に、京都にある京都耳鼻咽喉音聲手術医院でウェンドラー声帯短縮術を受けました。」 The operation was under general anaesthesia and 「術後の痛みはほとんどなく、想像していたよりもずっと楽だったことを覚えています」.
- The hard part named explicitly: 「手術そのものよりも大変だったのは、その後の生活でした」 and 「特に辛かったのは、約1か月間ほとんど声を出してはいけないという制限です」 — no voice for about a month, communicating by writing (筆談).
- Travel from Aichi prefecture (新幹線 to Kyoto, then an express bus), described as both a physical and mental burden, with relief that the operation went well tempered by having to make the same journey back.
- Outcome after two years: 「今は、２年たって声が２３０hz平均出ますので完全女声です」, and 「集まりとか声が女の子なので女の子扱いされてめちゃめちゃうれしいです」.
- The caveat the writer adds: 「それでも男っぽいトーンで話すと男声になってしまうので注意です」 — pitch is high but a masculine-sounding delivery still reads as male.

## Bearing on voice training
- A Japanese patient's account of the same procedure the clinical literature describes, with the recovery period named in weeks rather than sessions: about a month of near-total voice rest [[voice-surgery]] [[voice-health]].
- The final caveat is the one worth keeping: with a post-glottoplasty pitch around 230 Hz the writer still says a masculine tone of voice reverts to being heard as male, which is the perceptual point the surgical literature also makes about intonation and delivery [[fundamental-frequency]] [[gender-perception]] [[japanese-voice]].
- Gives the domestic Japanese option (Kyoto) alongside the Korean and Thai clinics that dominate the community's surgery discussion [[transgender-care-context]].
