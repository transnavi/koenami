# SOVT training with a free-end tube in dysphonic adults: a systematic review

## What it says
- PRISMA systematic review of semi-occluded vocal tract training with a free-end straw in adults with dysphonia; outcomes were acoustic parameters (jitter, shimmer, HNR, CPP, F0) and contact quotient.
- "This section outlines the inclusion of three studies with 127 participants from an initial pool of 5752 records." One used electroglottography, two used acoustic assessment, and training durations differed.
- "The Cochrane Risk of Bias-2 tool indicated a high risk of bias across the studies, primarily due to missing outcome data and randomization issues."
- Conclusion: the review "highlights the beneficial effects of SVOT using a free-end tube in improving acoustic characteristics and glottal performance in dysphonic adults. The technique enhances vocal fold coordination, reduces tension, and promotes efficient phonation. Further research is recommended."

## Bearing on voice training
- The most-repeated exercise in trans voice practice rests on three small studies with high risk of bias. The physiological rationale ([[titze2006]]) is much stronger than the outcome evidence.

## Notes
- "Free-end tube" means a straw in air, not the water-resistance variant ([[kissel2023]], [[saldias2020]]). Only three trials met inclusion; none involved trans speakers.
