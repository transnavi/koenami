# なぜトランスジェンダー当事者はジェンダークリニックを受診するのか？

The opening paper of a panel discussion on voice in gender-incongruence care at the Japan Laryngological Association — the venue where Japanese ENT specialists discuss the question this vault is about. Author affiliation as printed: 岡山大学病院ジェンダーセンター.

## What it says
- The clinic model: 「The Departments of Psychiatry Neurology, Gynecology, Urology, and Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery play a central role in the operation of the Okayama University Gender Clinic.」 Treatment follows 「“The diagnostic and therapeutic guidelines for patients with gender identity disorder (The Japanese Society of Psychiatry and Neurology)”」 — the guideline whose later editions are in [[jspn-guideline2024]].
- Why patients come: 「The motivation for seeking these treatments is the strong discomfort arising from having physical characteristics opposite to one’s own experienced gender.」 The dysphoria is described as two-sided — 「both a subjective sense of discomfort and a suspicion that others may be uncomfortable with an appearance that does not resemble the desired gender」 — and the paper ties that to social cost: certainty about how one is perceived is what makes acting in society possible, and its absence 「leads to disadvantages in social life」.
- Where voice sits in that: 「Mastectomy, facial feminization, masculinization or feminization of appearance through hormone therapy, etc., and female voice through vocal cord surgery are thought to improve subjective gender discrepancy and at the same time improve social discomfort and reduce disadvantages in daily life.」
- The caveat the paper ends on: 「Of course, we must not forget that the essential solution is to create a tolerant society that accepts a person in the gender that they desire, no matter what their appearance may be.」
- The panel's Japanese title names the topic directly: 「性同一性障害の治療：望む性へ向けての音声改善」. The same issue of 喉頭 carries 中村's type-4 thyroplasty paper ([[nakamura2022]]), 二村's testosterone paper ([[nimura2022]]) and 小林's rehabilitation paper ([[kobayashi2022]]).

## Bearing on voice training
- States the clinical rationale for voice work in Japanese practice: voice change is sought not for its own sake but because being read wrongly carries social cost, and the outcome aimed at is reduced disadvantage in daily life. That is the same framing as the English-language quality-of-life literature in the vault ([[hancock2011]], [[hancock2017]], [[parchem2025]]).
- The panel is the closest thing to a Japanese clinical consensus statement on voice: none of the papers in it is a guideline, and the national guideline itself has no voice chapter ([[jspn-guideline2024]], [[wikipedia-ja-gi-society]]).
