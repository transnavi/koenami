# 伪声怎么入门（男伪女）（Bilibili 专栏）

A nine-lesson introductory course posted 2018-05-08, the most-read of the collected articles. It is written as a spoken script with the demonstration syllables and the ear-training remarks left in (好了，下面我做下示范), and its technical vocabulary is small: 气音 (breathy onset), 换声区 (the register break), 共鸣 (resonance) and 语气 (manner).

## What it says
- 第一课 伪声准备: buy a decent microphone, record yourself, drink honey water, sleep; 练习要适度，不可太急的求成, no smoking, no iced drinks or chilli before or after practice. The warning is explicit: 嗓子高度负担了还继续练习。可能会造成永久不能练伪声的，还有可能导致毁声 ("continuing to practise on an over-loaded throat may permanently prevent learning 伪声 and may ruin the voice").
- 第二课 基础知识 gives the method in one line: 男伪女在于要提高喉软骨,去掉胸腔共鸣，提高软腭，共鸣方式变成口腔共鸣 ("male-to-female is about raising the laryngeal cartilage, removing chest resonance, raising the soft palate, and switching resonance to the oral cavity"). The diagnostic for chest resonance is a hand on the chest while speaking (嘿-嘿-嘿); the highest male falsetto (Ha) is labelled 太监音 — 很尖锐。很刺耳 — and noted to raise the 喉软骨 automatically. A warning is attached to the obvious model: 新贵妃醉酒 can be listened to for reference 但是不要去练这个歌曲，因为这个是京腔，要紧嗓子才能发出。紧嗓子是伪声的大忌 ("do not practise that song, it is 京腔 and requires squeezing the throat; throat-squeezing is the cardinal sin of 伪声"). The claim: 伪声是完全放松嗓子发出的声音 ("伪声 is produced with the throat fully relaxed").
- 第三课 基础练习: the 气音 drills (Ha-Ho-He) are to erase the throat tightness, first producing the tight high 太监音, then adding breath until it relaxes. Breath control by breathing in deeply and letting the air out as slowly as possible; male and female breath are contrasted coarsely (男生的气息…很粗。很硬 / 女生的气息很柔弱，很细。很软).
- 第四课 基础进阶: 爬音节 (scale climbing) from the base voice to the top, to map one's own range.
- 第五课 真声伪声: the target is the 换声区 (register-change zone) between 真声 and 假声 — 真声过多甚至会爆出本音，假声过多会有尖着嗓子的感觉，甚至直接爆太监 ("too much modal voice and the base voice breaks through; too much falsetto and the voice goes shrill, or straight into 太监"). The zone is found by ascending or descending scales, with the warning that it 即使第一天找到了。第二天又消失了 (may be there one day and gone the next).
- 第六课 真声强化: strengthen the zone using the 气音 and then 全部的汉语拼音, then words, then sentences — the same 音→词→句 ladder as [[bili-cv9417414]] and [[bili-shaojinalitao]].
- 第七课 真声进阶: 心态决定了伪声的成败。伪声先伪心 ("mindset decides success; fake the mind before the voice"), then names the styles: 妖孽音-御姐音-少女音, and advises copying real women's 语气 over an imagined one — the same correction [[bili-cv9417414]] makes.
- 第八课 音域扩展: songs chosen by register for practice (低音: 相思引, 星月神话, 荷塘月色, 青媚狐; 中音: 隐形的翅膀, 爱啦啦, 爸爸妈妈, 要抱抱; 高音: 爱的供养), plus the claim 一直用伪声说话的话。本音会变质 ("speaking in 伪声 constantly will change the base voice").
- 第九课 伪声总结 answers six questions: throat tightness (sustained Ha for three days — 我就这样解决的), shakiness and running out of air (狗喘气, lip trills, jogging, or pushing fists into the waist while humming), the 换声区 not found (combine the highest falsetto with low vocal fry to produce a 哑音, then add breath), nasality (practise the nasal 哼-嗯 and feel the vibration at the brow), a resulting "受音" (climb scales or sing), and a damaged base voice: 凉拌！歇着吧。慢慢找回来 ("what to do? rest. find it back slowly.").
- A closing 注意事项 list specifies delivery rather than voice: appropriate volume, lower pitch, moderate speed with good pauses, rounded timbre, clear articulation without regional accent, sincere emotion, easy projection, flowing breath, standard and polite wording.

## Bearing on voice training
- The course is the folk layer's complete method in miniature and in its own words. The four moves it teaches — raise the laryngeal cartilage, remove chest resonance, raise the soft palate, move resonance oral — are a cavity-shift description of what shortening the effective tract does to formants ([[formants]], [[vocal-tract-anatomy]]), and it agrees with the handbook's ranking that a raised larynx drives F2 [[aogi2021]].
- 换声区 and 真声伪声 are register language: the target is the modal–falsetto boundary rather than falsetto itself, which is the same point the vault's [[vocal-registers]] note makes and the one [[bili-cv9417414]] states as 说话用的伪音一般是真音. The 太监音 stage is the community's name for the failure of staying in M2; it recurs across the dataset [[bili-tonghuaqiuye]], [[bili-cv10585868]].
- Its harm content is more concrete than most: a permanent bar to learning 伪声 (永久不能练伪声) and 毁声 as outcomes of practising on an over-loaded throat, and the 本音会变质 warning in 第八课. Both are the community's own versions of the risk the pitched-up-voice literature describes, and both are consistent with [[voice-health]] and [[vocal-fatigue]]. The course also endorses 金嗓子喉宝 with the caveat 记住 这货不是医生 ("remember, this is not a doctor") — a rare instance of the layer bounding its own folk remedy.
- 紧嗓子是伪声的大忌 ("throat-squeezing is the cardinal sin") is the same position as the handbook's argument that throat tightness, not high larynx height, is what injures [[aogi2021]] — the community's one durable safety consensus.
