# La féminisation vocale proposée aux femmes transgenres - Analyse des pratiques orthophoniques

## What it says
- French orthophonie study published in Glossa, the journal of the French speech-language pathology federation's publishing house: the authors built a treatment protocol "à partir de la littérature et avec l'aide d'orthophonistes expérimentés", then trialled it with five trans women using a single-case experimental design with repeated measures, plus a subjective satisfaction scale (abstract).
- The protocol's targets were élévation de la fréquence fondamentale (F0), valeurs formantiques (formant values) and l'étendue vocale (vocal range).
- Results: increases in F0, formant values and vocal range "survenaient parfois suite à l'intervention orthophonique. Cependant, la grande variabilité interindividuelle ne permettait pas de confirmer les tendances observées."
- Patient satisfaction rose after therapy; the authors' conclusion is that despite inconclusive acoustics, "l'augmentation de la satisfaction des patientes nous permet de suggérer que ce protocole de prise en charge est une réponse pertinente à la plainte spécifique des femmes transgenres".

## Bearing on voice training
- The French-language account of the same triad — pitch, formants, range — that [[leung2018]] ranks by evidence weight; note the register vocabulary: orthophonie/orthophoniste (France), prise en charge (management/care), plainte (the presenting complaint).
- A rare description of French clinical practice; the satisfaction-before-acoustics finding echoes [[quinn2021]].

## Notes
- Five patients, single-case design, no control group — graded low; the "variabilité interindividuelle" the authors name is the standing obstacle in small-series behavioural work.
- Published 2019, before a French TVQ existed; the satisfaction scale used is not the [[morsomme2019]] questionnaire.
