# Translation and preliminary evaluation of the Brazilian Portuguese version of the Transgender Voice Questionnaire for male-to-female transsexuals

## What it says
- Step one was translation and cross-cultural adaptation of the TVQ:MtF and its demographic form — "translation, reconciliation, back-translation, harmonization, and pilot test"; step two a preliminary psychometric check (abstract, Methods).
- 13 male-to-female transsexual participants aged 21–47 answered the questionnaire twice, test and retest, at a planned interval of about 30 days.
- Internal consistency was high in both conditions (Cronbach α = 0.911 test, 0.952 retest) and test–retest reliability averaged ICC = 0.957 (95% CI 0.916–0.984).
- Conclusion: the TVQ:MtF "is an appropriate instrument for vocal self-assessment of male-to-female transsexual patients, which can be recommended for clinical and research goals".

## Bearing on voice training
- This is the Brazilian branch of the TVQ family the vault follows through the Dutch original and the Spanish abbreviated version ([[mora2018a]], [[casadomorente2021]]): the same instrument that lets a speaker rate how their voice bears on daily life, rather than measuring it ([[patient-reported-outcomes]]).
- The later Brazilian work uses it as the standard outcome here — see [[martinho2025]] (TWVQ), [[santana2022]] (TWVQ plus QVV) and [[irineu2024]] (the newer Vocal Congruence Scale).

## Notes
- n = 13, no control group, single centre (Universidade Federal Fluminense / Itaguaí); the paper itself calls the psychometric check preliminary, hence the low grade.
- Abstract read; the adapted item wording, the reconciliation committee and the pilot-test report were not read.
