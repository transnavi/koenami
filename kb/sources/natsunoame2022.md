## What it says
- Platform metadata, 2026-09-17: title 「【男声】自分流！イケボの簡単な出し方・作り方【男声講座】」, channel 夏の雨が好き。, published 2022/05/10, 134,252 views.
- 「イケボ」 is the Japanese performance word for an attractive male voice — the counterpart of カワボ and the target of a whole sub-genre of lessons [[japanese-community]]. The title marks the method as 「自分流」, the uploader's own.
- The 男声 side of the same search is largely this vocabulary: イケボ, ショタボ, ダミ声, and lessons addressed at people who can already do a female voice rather than at trans men.

## Bearing on voice training
- Records what the Japanese 男声 material is mostly for: character voices for performance, mirrored across the gender line from the 女声 lessons, with 「自分流」 as the standard hedge about provenance [[community-training]] [[male-voice]].
- The behavioural masculinisation route without testosterone is the one the clinical literature has least on [[papeleu2025a]] [[voice-masculinization]]; the Japanese corpus is a large body of practice for it, aimed at a different population.
