## Bearing on voice training
- The 从认识到运用 framing splits recognition from application, which is the distinction the vault's own note structure uses: understanding what the acoustic variables are ([[formants]], [[fundamental-frequency]]) is separate from being able to produce them in connected speech.
- The announced 咽音 (twang) topic is the technique the 2021 handbook recommends for laryngeal health alongside high-larynx work [[aogi2021]], and which the singing literature in the dataset treats as a separate register skill [[singing-and-voice]]. Its appearance here as the "重点" suggests the channel had arrived at the same conclusion.
- The claim that a non-feminine starting voice can still learn 伪声 is the standard reassurance; the 2021 handbook's answer to the same worry is that the F0 target is set from the speaker's own range rather than from an absolute figure [[aogi2021]].
