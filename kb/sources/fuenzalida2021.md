# Efecto inmediato de la terapia de tracto vocal semiocluido en los parámetros acústicos en los procesos de masculinización y feminización de la voz

## What it says
- 40 transsexual participants aged 18–40, pre-test/post-test pre-experimental design of a single dimension, cross-sectional in time, descriptive and comparative in scope (abstract). The instrument for acoustic measurement was Praat (Boersma and Weenink, 2018).
- Semi-occluded vocal tract therapy (TVSO) produced only limited immediate effects in the trans women: 「although in the female transsexuals the values of the acoustic parameters decreased; there was only immediate effect in Local Jitter, Jitter APQ5, Harmonic-noise ratio and Fundamental frequency (F0)」 — and it was the F0 change that made the voice higher.
- In the trans men, F0 was modified after therapy, so 「the voice became more deep」.
- Conclusion: TVSO 「has an immediate effect mainly on Jitter and F0 and can be considered an alternative, viable and complementary therapy in the process of masculinization and feminization of voice」.

## Bearing on voice training
- A cheap, single-session exercise that moves F0 measurably in both directions, which is why semi-occluded work appears in warm-up and voice-quality practice across the vault ([[voice-therapy-techniques]], [[voice-quality]]).
- It is an *immediate* effect only, measured pre/post in one session; nothing here shows it transfers to connected speech or to how listeners read the voice ([[voice-feminization-therapy]]). Reading it as a feminisation method would overreach what the design supports.

## Notes
- Pre-experimental with no control group and no randomisation; 40 participants but no per-group n or dropout reported in the abstract. Graded medium for the peer-reviewed design and the acoustic instrumentation, weak for causal inference.
- The earlier, smaller study from the same group on 13–24-year-olds is [[sandoval2019]].
- Claims taken from the abstract.
