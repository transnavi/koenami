# 両声類（ニコニコ大百科）

The community's own definition of the word that names the whole Japanese scene. Article page number 154523; first written 2008-05-26, last revised 2015-01-06 (revision 2141245), both printed on the page.

## What it says
- Definition, first line: 「両声類（りょうせいるい）とは、性別を逆転させたような声で歌唱する人達への敬称である。」 — "an honorific for people who sing in a voice that sounds like their sex reversed". The article calls it a 敬称 (term of respect), not a diagnosis or an identity.
- Scope: most often applied to videos where 「男性にも関わらず、女性のような高い(若しくは、質の)声で歌う」 (a man singing in a female-like high — or female-like in quality — voice), but the article says the reverse case and spoken (non-singing) videos also get the tag.
- 「主に"声質"だけをタグの対象とするため、(ある程度なら)音痴でもタグがつく」 — the tag targets 声質 (voice quality) alone, so being off-pitch does not disqualify a video. Pitch accuracy and voice-gender are treated as separate things by the tagging community.
- Second sense: 「男声、女声両方を使いこなせる人の事を指して両声類タグをつける事もある」 — someone who commands both a 男声 and a 女声. The article notes that some singers therefore keep their own sex undisclosed and present as 性別不明.
- Judgement is explicitly subjective: 「このタグがつくかどうかは、個人の価値観による部分が大きい。（ex:ある人には女声に聞こえるが、別の人は男の裏声にしか聞こえない）」 — one listener hears a 女声, another hears only a man's 裏声 (falsetto). The article also records an unsettled question: whether a singer who only uses the sex-reversed voice counts as 両声類 「議論が必要な所である」.
- Etymology as the article reconstructs it: the word grew out of a comment on りゃく's video 朝と夜のニコニコ全員俺β版 (sm946483). A 07/09/22 comment read 「両生類？」 (amphibian?), the next 「両性類か両声類でしょｗ」; the article dates tag formation before 07/11/05, when a 「両声類タグわろた」 comment appears. It labels this 「有力な説」 (the leading account), not a certainty.
- Scene note: on ニコニコ動画 the 両声類 are overwhelmingly 歌い手 (cover singers), whereas on ニコニコ生放送 they are more often 声真似練習者 (voice-mimicry practisers) or chat streamers.
- Related tags the article lists: 男声, 女声, ショタ声; related articles include 多声類, 闇の両声類, 性別が迷子, 男の娘, 両声類修行中, 両声類オーディション, 両声類歌い手一覧.

## Bearing on voice training
- Gives the Japanese scene's vocabulary its base layer: 女声 / 男声 as producible objects, 声質 as the tagged property, 裏声 as the failure mode a listener names when the 女声 does not convince.
- The 2008 article predates and is independent of trans voice care; the same words later carry into trans-community guides ([[brushvoice-joseigoe]], [[yomedanchii-voice]]), which is why the two literatures share terms.
