## Bearing on voice training
- Names a target the clinical literature does not: Japanese practice distinguishes types of 女声 by character and age impression, which is the same axis [[hayashi2026]] studies in female voice actors' performance speech and that [[yasuda2023]] extracted descriptors for. A learner in this tradition is choosing between voices, not converging on one.
