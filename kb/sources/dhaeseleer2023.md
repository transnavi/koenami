# Voice Outcome of Glottoplasty in Trans Women

## What it says
- Prospective longitudinal non-controlled trial; 35 trans women undergoing glottoplasty, or glottoplasty plus chondrolaryngoplasty, assessed before surgery and at 1 week, 1 month and 6 months after.
- Significant change over time in all f0 and intensity parameters, Dysphonia Severity Index, Acoustic Voice Quality Index, Voice Handicap Index and Trans Woman Voice Questionnaire; listener- and self-perceived femininity both rose.
- "After glottoplasty an immediate and short-term decrease in voice quality, vocal capacity and frequency range was measured with a progressive recovery on the longer term."
- "Long term side effects of glottoplasty are a reduction in speaking intensity and intensity range."
- "Significant differences in evolution of listener perceptions were found between the groups with and without voice therapy… Voice therapy seems to improve the outcomes of glottoplasty, but should be further investigated."

## Bearing on voice training
- Register and range are part of the cost of pitch surgery: frequency range narrows before it recovers, and loudness range stays reduced. Anyone who sings should weigh that against the pitch gain.
- Voice therapy alongside surgery changed how listeners heard the result — the behavioural and surgical routes are not alternatives.

## Notes
- Non-controlled; group allocation to voice therapy was not randomised, so the therapy comparison is observational. Six-month follow-up only.
