# Féminiser sa voix en 30 MINUTES - Femme Transgenre

## What it says
- A French-language peer tutorial uploaded 2024-02-06 by a singer-creator (channel then at 1,260 subscribers; also publishes music under the page elisabethdhyberie.com). The description states the demand it answers: "lorsqu'on est une femme transgenre il est crucial de travailler sa voix et féminiser sa voix afin d'obtenir un bon passing en société et éviter beaucoup de déconvenues."
- It states the source of the exercises rather than presenting them as her own: "J'ai eu la chance de travailler avec une orthophoniste parisienne qui m'a conseillé quelques exercices très utile d'orthophonie pour féminiser sa voix", offered as "des exercices d'orthophonie à réaliser quotidiennement" that "peuvent être réaliser avant même la prise d'un traitement hormonal féminisant".
- The description links three things: downloadable exercise sheets on the creator's own site, a directory — "BDD TRANS ORTHOPHONISTES: https://bddtrans.fr/voix/" — and the Voice Tools app for iOS and Android.

## Bearing on voice training
- The French instance of the peer-tutorial genre this vault holds for Spanish [[longi2021]], German [[montugai2023]] and Chinese communities, with one difference worth recording: the exercises are attributed to a named professional's advice, and a directory of trans-friendly orthophonistes is handed on with them.
- That directory, bddtrans.fr, has since gone dark: its /voix/ path returned 404 on 2026-09-17 and the homepage read "Le site bddtrans a été suspendu suite à une demande de l'hébergeur après une dénonciation de médecin" — so a resource French community videos still point to is currently unavailable.
- The claim that the exercises can be started before hormone therapy matches what the French clinical and professional pages say about estrogens not acting on the voice [[alloortho-voix]] [[wikitrans-voix]].

## Limits
- One creator's video; the exercise sheets live on her own site and the video was not transcribed, so this note attests the framing, the attribution to an orthophoniste and the linked resources, not the exercises themselves. No measures, no follow-up. Graded community.
- "Féminiser sa voix en 30 minutes" is the title's promise, not a duration claim made about therapy; the description describes daily practice.
