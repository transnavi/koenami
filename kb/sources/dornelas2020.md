## What it says
- 27 users aged 18–49 of the Ambulatório Trans de Sergipe — "Portas abertas – Saúde integral das pessoas trans: cuidar e acolher", Universidade Federal de Sergipe — completed the Brazilian QVV (Qualidade de Vida em Voz), the Transgender Voice Questionnaire (TVQ MtF) and the ITDV voice-disorder screening index.
- The sample was 63.0% trans men and 37.0% trans women, mean age 26.7 (±9.1); 96.3% had a specific voice complaint.
- Mean scores: QVV total 26.1, ITDV 3.9, TVQ MtF 70.6. No significant relationship was found between the results and gender identity or age; a significant relationship was found between QVV and TVQ MtF.
- Concludes that voice-related quality of life was low, and that "quanto maior a percepção de suas alterações vocais, pior a sua qualidade de vida, independente do gênero com o qual se identificam".

## Bearing on voice training
- Shows the TVQ MtF used, in Portuguese, on a sample that was mostly trans men — a use the instrument was not designed for; [[schwarz2024]] later adapted it for that population.
- Distress tracked self-perceived vocal change rather than the direction of transition, so a training tool should not assume trans men need less support ([[voice-masculinization]]).

## Notes
- n = 27 at one northeastern Brazilian public clinic; cross-sectional, no acoustic measures, no listener ratings.
- Mixing trans men and trans women in a single TVQ MtF score limits how the 70.6 mean can be read.
