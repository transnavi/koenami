# 變聲（中文维基百科）

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17 (extract in traditional Chinese): 變聲是指人類在青春期所發生的一種生理變化…男性約在12∼16歲；女性約在13∼15歲，也有可能延到十六歲左右。成年後聲音基本上不太會改變。 — voice change is the pubertal change, boys roughly 12–16, girls roughly 13–15 (sometimes to about 16); after adulthood the voice basically changes little.
- Interlanguage titles: en Voice change · ja 変声 · ko 변성기.
- The "adult voice barely changes" clause is the sentence every feminization method has to work around: no oestrogen-driven change ([[hormones-and-voice]]), hence training or surgery.

## Bearing on voice training
- The Chinese orientation for [[puberty-voice-change]]; the ages it gives are consistent with the longitudinal sources behind that note ([[harries1997]], [[hollien1994]]).
- 變聲期 is also the community's safety boundary: the moegirl 伪声 entry warns 变声时不能练 and caps practice at 20 minutes a day during that period ([[moegirl-weisheng]]).

## Limits
- Community-edited article; age ranges quoted for orientation, with the sourced numbers carried by the studies behind [[puberty-voice-change]].
