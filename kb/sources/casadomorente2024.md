# Comparison Between two Surgical Techniques for Increasing Vocal Pitch by Endoscopic Shortening of the Vocal Folds

## What it says
- Marbella Quironsalud with Madrid (Complutense) and Navarra collaborators: 22 Spanish trans women aged 20–62 — 12 Wendler glottoplasty (GP), 10 vocal fold shortening and retrodisplacement of the anterior commissure with laser-assisted voice adjustment cordotomy (VFSRAC+LAVA), all followed by postoperative speech therapy; assessed preoperatively and at six months (abstract, "METHODS").
- F0 rose significantly in both groups, more with VFSRAC+LAVA: +47.75 Hz (GP) versus +69.70 Hz (VFSRAC+LAVA).
- TWVQ scores fell significantly in both, again more with VFSRAC+LAVA; perceptual VAS ratings likewise.
- Conclusion: both techniques shorten the folds endoscopically and raise pitch, VFSRAC+LAVA producing the better results — but "when compared with previous studies it seems that the LAVA technique may not significantly contribute to the postoperative results", so "the VFSRAC technique followed by postoperative speech therapy could be recommended".

## Bearing on voice training
- The Spanish surgical series' current endpoint: VFSRAC — the technique the Beijing group also reports in Mandarin speakers [[sun2025]] — plus therapy as the standard package.
- Uses the Spanish TWVQ as the patient-reported arm, closing the loop the validation studies [[mora2018a]] [[casadomorente2021]] opened.

## Notes
- Retrospective, 22 patients unevenly split (12 vs 10); the LAVA question is settled by comparison with other studies rather than a within-trial arm.
- Six-month follow-up only; F0 gains at 12 months in this group's earlier series were the yardstick of [[casado2017]].
