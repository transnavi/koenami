# 友善醫師與諮商資源整理（伴盟跨性別資訊平台）

## What it says
- An advocacy-organisation directory of trans-friendly providers across Taiwan's cities, in five sections — 身心科, 荷爾蒙補充, 性別肯認手術, 嗓音/語言治療 (voice/speech therapy), 諮商師/所 — with the standing caution to phone ahead (前往之前，可先致電或上網查詢).
- The 嗓音/語言治療 section lists: 台北榮總 耳鼻喉部 王怡芬 (可轉介語言治療師); 振興醫院 耳鼻喉部嗓音醫學中心 張學逸 and 張智惠; 新北 亞東醫院 耳鼻喉科 王棨德 (聲帶手術，可轉介語言治療師 — links the team's site, [[voicedoctor-tw]]); 博淘語言治療所 復健科（語言治療）鄧敦弘 ([[protalk-gabby2024]]); 新竹國泰 復健科 洪薏婷 (rehabilitation referral); 台北榮總新竹分院 陳螢萩; 台中 中國醫 耳鼻喉科 賴俊毓, 鄒永恩, 邱建仁.
- The gender-affirming-surgery section (性別肯認手術) covers SRS surgeons by city separately from voice; the endocrinology section carries per-clinic notes (e.g. one clinic 僅為FtM，無提供MtF).

## Bearing on voice training
- The Taiwan counterpart of mtf.wiki's mainland directory ([[mtfwiki-vocal-confirmed]]): same crowdsourced epistemology (自行甄别), but maintained by an incorporated advocacy organisation and spanning a national-health-system context where 語言治療 sits in rehabilitation and ENT departments ([[transgender-care-context]]).
- Together the two directories show where Chinese-speaking trans people can find voice care: a handful of named ENT and SLP providers island-wide on the Taiwan side, hospital departments plus one psychiatric-centre trainer on the mainland side.

## Limits
- An advocacy-organisation list with no stated vetting beyond community reporting; entries are recorded as listed; individual providers' current availability, and whether they serve trans men as well as trans women, is not stated for every row.
- Fetched 2026-09-17; the list's own compile date is 2024/12, so entries age.
