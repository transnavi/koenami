# Vocal cord nodule (English Wikipedia)

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17: "Vocal cord nodules are bilaterally symmetrical benign white masses (nodules) that form at the midpoint of the vocal folds. Although diagnosis involves a physical examination of the head and neck, as well as perceptual voice measures, visualization of the vocal nodules via laryngeal endoscopy remains the primary diagnostic method."
- Bilateral and at the midpoint: that is how they are distinguished, on this account, from unilateral polyps and from other midfold swellings that have not been seen.

## Bearing on voice training
- The reason a hoarse pitch-raising programme is sent to a laryngologist rather than given more exercises: nodules are a visual diagnosis ([[young2021]] makes the same point for trans patients). The trial of resonant and confidential voice for nodules is [[verdolinimarston1995]].
