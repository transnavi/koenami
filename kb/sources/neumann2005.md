# Die operative Stimmerhöhung bei Mann-zu-Frau-Transsexualismus（Neumann 2005）

## What it says
- The problem as the German abstract frames it: Mann-zu-Frau-Transsexuelle leiden nach erfolgreicher geschlechtsangleichender Operation sehr häufig unter der unverändert männlichen Stimme, die in Diskrepanz zum weiblichen äußeren Erscheinungsbild steht und somit die soziale Integration als Frau erheblich stört — after successful gender-affirming surgery, patients very often still have an unchanged male voice, out of keeping with their female appearance and seriously disturbing social integration as a woman.
- The treatment limit it states: «Hormonbehandlung bzw. eine alleinige logopädische Therapie führt nicht zu einer dauerhaften Stimmerhöhung» — hormone treatment or speech therapy alone does not produce a lasting pitch rise; hence the interest in a phonosurgical technique for permanent elevation.
- The technique and its lineage: the unit's Cricothyroidopexie mit Miniplatten is based on Isshiki's type IV thyroplasty; the earlier results were «durch häufiges Ausreißen der Nähte unzureichend und nicht dauerhaft», so they modified it by fixing wire sutures over miniplates after maximal approximation of cricoid and thyroid, which avoids manipulating the inside of the larynx and prevents suture pull-out.
- Case volume and follow-up: since 1993, 67 Mann-zu-Frau-Transsexuelle underwent Cricothyroidopexie nach Berghaus/Neumann; voice assessment was pre-operative, post-operative and after one year.
- Outcome figures from the abstract: the higher vocal-fold tension «bewirkt eine ständige Anhebung der mittleren Sprechstimmlage um durchschnittlich 5-6 Halbtöne» (a permanent rise of the mean speaking pitch by an average of 5–6 semitones). While pre-operatively no patient had a female speaking voice, post-operatively «bewegten sich 28% im weiblichen Bereich und 38% erreichten zumindest eine indifferente Stimmlage» — 28% in the female range and 38% at least in an indeterminate one; long-term observation showed the results to be durable.
- The social measure: a questionnaire on how the voice treatment affected patients' social integration found «eine bessere gesamte Verweiblichung der Transsexuellen und eine bessere soziale Integration».
- Conclusion: the technique has proved «insgesamt erfolgreich und wenig risikoträchtig», and — on the strength of the pre/post parameters and the patients' mostly subjective satisfaction — «dass die Phonochirurgie als fester Bestandteil mit in das therapeutische Gesamtkonzept bei Transsexuellen einbezogen werden muss».

## Bearing on voice training
- The 67-patient German cricothyroidopexy series gives the concrete numbers behind the "pitch moves, quality and completeness do not" pattern: a mean 5–6 semitone rise, and yet only 28% landed inside the female range and 38% only in an indeterminate one. That is the same shortfall the Würzburg glottoplasty series reports fourteen years later [[meister2017a]] and the telephone test quantifies as 7 of 18 passing [[meister2017]].
- Its claim that therapy alone does not hold a raised pitch is the German side of the argument the training literature answers with maintenance and carryover work ([[voice-therapy-techniques]], [[vocal-fatigue]]); the vault's community sources report the opposite emphasis — that trained voices are maintained without surgery ([[community-training]]).
- The social-integration questionnaire is an early instance of measuring the outcome the voice is meant to serve rather than the acoustic parameter ([[patient-reported-outcomes]], [[transgender-care-context]]).
- Names the technique family — Berghaus/Neumann cricothyroidopexy with miniplates, derived from Isshiki type IV — alongside the same group's 2002 report on the procedure's effect on gender-specific voice qualities [[welzel2002]].
