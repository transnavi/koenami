# Vocal Traditions: Estill Voice Training®

## What it says
- Semantic Scholar abstract, 2026-09-17: "Vocal Traditions is a series in the Voice and Speech Review that highlights historically important voice teachers and schools of thought in the world of vocal pedagogy. In this essay, Estill Voice training offers its overview, history, key features, goals, and details on teacher training."
- The authors are Kimberly Steinhauer and Mary McDonald Klimek, writing from inside the organisation (Estill Voice International). The essay is the programme describing itself.
