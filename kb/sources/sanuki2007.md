## What it says
- Reports two separate laryngeal-framework-surgery case series performed under local anesthesia at the same institution: type IV thyroplasty for 31 MTF/GID patients (1999–2006), and type II thyroplasty with a titanium bridge for 41 patients with adductor spasmodic dysphonia (AdSD, 2002–2005).
- MTF/GID group: F0 rose in all 31 patients; mean preoperative F0 was 135 Hz, mean postoperative F0 was 236 Hz. Authors state type IV thyroplasty requires specialized skill but is effective for pitch elevation in MTF/GID.
- AdSD group (unrelated to gendered voice, included for comparison of the framework-surgery program): followed up at 1 year via questionnaire; 70% rated their voice "excellent," the remainder "good" or "fair" — none rated it worse.

## Bearing on voice training
- A second, closely matching data point for surgical pitch elevation in trans women (135→236 Hz), essentially replicating nakamura2007's figures (133.8→237.8 Hz) — likely an overlapping/related cohort from the same surgical group, which strengthens confidence in the ~100 Hz surgical pitch-elevation benchmark for type IV thyroplasty.
- The AdSD arm is not about gendered voice and is noted only for context; not relevant to feminization/masculinization claims.

## Notes
- n=31 (MTF/GID) + n=41 (AdSD, off-topic), single Japanese institution, local anesthesia framework surgery.
- High overlap with nakamura2007 in surgical technique, era, and institution — treat the two F0 figures as corroborating rather than fully independent evidence.
