# 《伪音小白必备秘籍（上）》（Bilibili 专栏）

The most systematic 男伪女 curriculum found in the collected Bilibili articles: a staged course from breath and a transitional voice through to named 声线, with each stage's purpose, drills and failure modes stated. It opens with a recruitment line for a 伪音 QQ group (群号962251904), and the whole article is written as a self-study book with numbered questions.

## What it says

**Questions it answers first (一. 常见问题的解答).**
- On puberty: 不建议在变声期学习伪音。一般需要等到过了变声期才建议学习 ("learning 伪音 during the voice change is not advised; wait until it is over").
- On suitable starting voices: 本音声线偏高，声音比较单薄的男声更容易学女声 ("a higher, thinner base voice learns female voice more easily").
- On load: 错误的练习方式对嗓子的负荷是极大的，甚至对嗓子产生不可逆的伤害 ("wrong practice loads the throat enormously and can cause irreversible damage"), with the operational rule 一天中我们练习伪音的时间应不超过一小时，最好控制半小时左右…量少次多 ("no more than an hour a day, preferably about half an hour, in small repeated sessions").
- On whether pitch is the goal: 大多数人认为高音就是女声，因此都是歇斯底里的往高处爬音阶…高音不等于女声 ("most people think high pitch is the female voice and climb scales hysterically; high pitch is not the female voice").
- On 假音: 伪音有分为假音伪音和真音伪音，说话用的伪音一般是真音 ("伪音 splits into falsetto-伪音 and modal-伪音; the kind used for speaking is normally modal").
- On 太监音: it splits into 男声太监 (too much male voice remaining) and 假音太监 (too much falsetto), each with a different fix.
- On time to learn: 通常学习时间是在3-12个月 ("usually 3–12 months") and 我认为是没有速成的 ("I do not think there is a quick course").

**Named terms (二. 伪音中的名词).** 男虚音 is defined as 女音的雏形，过渡音…并不是放虚了的男音，而是减少了喉音，加强了咽腔共鸣的声音 ("the prototype of the female voice, a transitional sound — not a softened male voice but one with less laryngeal and more pharyngeal resonance"), also called 轻咽音. 女声气息 is 细、轻、高 (thin, light, high). 声带闭合 is credited with 帮我们减去过多的吐气量，保证气息不流失 (cutting excess airflow and holding the onset). 发声点 is 气息带动声带震动的区域, with 靠后、发声点较小 (back, small) as the female target, against the male one. 共鸣 is 自带的混响 ("built-in reverb"), and 咽腔共鸣 and 鼻腔共鸣 are marked 男伪女音中较为重要的 (the important ones for male-to-female), 胸腔共鸣 marked 男伪女音较少用到.

**The practice sequence (三. 练习方法).** Every drill is to be learned in three stages: 学习练习 (find the feeling), 稳定练习 (stabilise it before adding anything), 提升练习 (refine toward the female target), with the warning that skipping stabilisation 非常容易导致声线被带偏、声线混乱.

**Stage content.** The course's own map is: 过渡阶段 (女声气息 + 男虚音 → 中性音/女虚音), 初级阶段 (发声点 → 女实音, 声带闭合, 咬字, 说话), 进阶 (口腔/咽腔/鼻腔共鸣, 气息进阶, 共鸣的糅合), 修饰 (语气, 拓展声线).
- 女声气息: a 胸腹联合 breathing pattern, not deep abdominal and not shallow chest; the action is found through the cat-call (猫叫) movement without its falsetto sound; the air path runs up along the 咽壁, and a small 气息凝聚点 forms on the soft palate — the article warns that a too-large contact area makes the palate dry and sore.
- 男虚音: open the mouth, light 哈 on a comfortable pitch, then 减弱喉音 by opening the oropharynx (yawn feeling, tongue forward to the lower lip) and raising pitch only 2-4 半音, since 一般男声到女声只需要高2-4半音就足够了.
- 女虚音 and 女实音: combining breath and voice, then establishing a stable 发声点 (a short one-second vocal fry brought forward and back, then 以点带声) — the breathy 女虚音 is explicitly unusable for speech until the onset is set.
- 咬字: each syllable split into 字头–字腹–字尾 (initial–nucleus–coda), with the sequence 虚-实-虚 and pitch 低-高-低, practised as single units before words.
- 共鸣: 口腔共鸣 (open the oral space, smile-lift the corners), 咽腔共鸣 (yawn to raise the soft palate, lips spread laterally, sound along the 咽壁), 鼻腔共鸣 (distinguished from 鼻音, which is a blockage effects); all three are to be learned first with the base voice, then transferred.
- 声线拓展 gives explicit 共鸣 ratios and semitone targets: 御姐音 as 咽腔 > 口腔 > 鼻腔 > 胸腔, 高出本音1-4个半音; 少女音 口腔 + 咽腔 + a little 鼻腔, 高出3-5个半音; 萝莉音 增加鼻腔共鸣比例 with 6-8 半音, and each with its own articulation and 语气 notes.

**On manner (十三. 语气的学习).** Copying an imagined female voice produces 过度女音 ("an over-done female voice"); the instruction is to 找真正的女声进行模仿学习, working from 起音, 尾音, 停顿起伏 and 咬字的特色 of a real speaker.

## Bearing on voice training
- The course is a complete Chinese-language statement of what the vault's concept notes describe piecewise: breath support ([[breathing-and-support]]), the modal–falsetto distinction and its "male-void" transitional register ([[vocal-registers]], [[phonation]]), the onset and vocal-fold closure ([[vocal-folds]]), resonance and its relation to tract shape ([[formants]]), and articulation and prosody as the final layer ([[articulation]], [[intonation]]).
- Its 半音 targets and 共鸣 ratios give the community's folk version of cue weighting: pitch moves of 2–8 semitones depending on the target, with 咽腔 carrying the perceptual load. That is close to the 2021 handbook's ranking (F0 a prerequisite, F2 important, F1 secondary) though arrived at from drill practice rather than from acoustics [[aogi2021]], and consistent with pitch dominating but not determining perceived gender [[leung2018]].
- Its safety content — one hour a day, small sessions, stop when uncomfortable, no puberty practice, no speeding up — is the fullest statement of the community's own harm-reduction rules, and it agrees with the practice caps and the 毁声 warning in 萌娘百科 [[moegirl-weisheng]] and with the caution the 2024 essay course gives about 紧嗓子 [[bili-cv460384]].
- The 太监音 diagnosis (a stage produced by incomplete removal of male voice or by falsetto) is the same failure [[bili-tonghuaqiuye]] describes from experience and [[bili-cv10585868]] teaches a fix for.
