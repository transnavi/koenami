# Pedagogy (Voice Resource Project wiki)

## What it says
- Scope: "Pedagogy is how we describe different methods, beliefs and attitudes towards teaching and learning. It could be what terms we use or which exercises we recommend or even the order in which we recommend them. It could be as fundamental as how vocal mechanisms physically work. This site aims to be fairly neutral."
- The vetting rule, quoted in the page's own question-and-answer form: "does the method work and do what we claim it does most of the time and have a fairly long track record?" and, if so, "does it have risks of learning bad vocal health or behaviour?" — "if yes, are there reliable methods of avoiding these risks that are simple and able to be done by complete beginners, and is it unreasonable that someone would forgo these things or not be told about them? if yes, we preface the method with how to avoid the risks, else we scrap it".
- The summary given: "we want information and methods that work, and as long as there aren't any risks associated with it, then that's useful information."
- The page also points readers to a problems list for anything that goes wrong with a method, "whether listed on the site or not", and invites new information.

## Bearing on voice training
- States a inclusion criterion for a community resource: long track record, then a risk gate with beginner-usable mitigations — which is how the wiki's exercise pages end up carrying warnings rather than being removed [[community-training]] [[voice-health]].
- The criterion is explicitly not evidence-grading as a clinical guide would do it: "track record" and "works most of the time" replace study design, so community pages and systematic reviews are answering different questions [[voice-therapy-techniques]] [[brasil2026]].
