# Voice and communication change for gender nonconforming individuals: giving voice to the person inside

## What it says
- Written to support clinicians under WPATH SOC7: reviews the evidence-based literature on transgender voice and communication, then covers assessment, voice-feminization protocols and feminizing surgeries, with a section on speech and voice masculinization (an area with little prior attention), ending with recommendations for good clinical care.

## Bearing on voice training
- The most complete clinical map of what training addresses (pitch, resonance, intonation, voice quality, articulation, nonverbal communication) and of when surgery enters.

## Notes
- Abstract-level claims; the protocol details cited in the tutorial come from the body and should be page-cited when the full text is read.
