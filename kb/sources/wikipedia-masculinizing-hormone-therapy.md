# Masculinizing hormone therapy (English Wikipedia)

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17: "Masculinizing hormone therapy is a form of transgender hormone therapy which develops male secondary sex characteristics and suppresses or minimizes female ones. It is used by trans men and transmasculine individuals as part of gender transition, to align their body with their gender identity."
- The summary adds the purpose statement: this "can alleviate gender dysphoria, and help individuals be correctly perceived as their respective gender ('passing')" (summary extract).
