# Voice gender diversity: expression, perception and acoustics

## What it says
- Synthesises 45 studies (January 2020–January 2025) on how gender is expressed and perceived in the voices of gender-diverse people.
- Gender-diverse voices are frequently perceived, or acoustically represented, as falling between binary categories or as misaligned with affirmed identity.
- As with binary voice gender, fundamental frequency and formants are key components of expressed and perceived gender.
- Findings may be biased by binary methods, a focus on transfeminine voices and few studies of listener identity; some studies report gender non-conforming listeners understand voice gender differently from cisgender listeners.

## Bearing on voice training
- Grounds rating femininity and masculinity as separate scales, and the "androgynous = in between" framing.
