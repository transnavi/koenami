# List of Microbehaviours (Voice Resource Project wiki)

## What it says
- The page's own account of how little is known: 「These are mostly very vague for now, since not much is known about what is actually happening. There are no terms available that can describe most of these so it won't be like "vocal weight" or "resonance", it will be more like "that thing that happens that makes voices that are low pitch sound bright and higher in pitch than they actually are". This will be added to with more information as time goes on.」
- At the time of the fetch it holds exactly two entries.
- **Low pitch brightness** — 「This might not be a microbehaviour and instead just be something as simple as adding twang.」 The examples listed are imawonder, 39daph, The Narrator (Baldur's Gate) 「(only sometimes)」 and Drawing With Waffles 「(occasionally)」.
- **High resonance deepness** — 「Deepness is usually associated with either breathiness or low resonance, but in this case it's high resonance, but without being bright.」 The examples are Nurse and The Narrator (Baldur's Gate) 「(only sometimes)」.
- The examples are named voices from the same list the wiki keeps separately ([[sumi-voice-examples]]); the page itself plays no audio.

## Bearing on voice training
- This is the concrete payoff of the mimicry argument: the microbehaviour page says the level below onsets is nearly impossible to train consciously ([[sumi-microbehaviours]]), the mimicry page says the way in is imitation ([[sumi-mimicry]]), and this page is the small list of what to look for when imitating — a bright low-pitched voice, and a deep voice that is high in resonance without being bright ([[voice-impressions]]).
- Both entries are hedged by the wiki itself, and the first concedes it may be ordinary twang rather than anything new — which is the honest state of this corner of the vocabulary ([[sumi-questionable-terms]], [[vocal-weight]]).

## Limits
- Wiki page "under construction"; two entries, no acoustic definitions, no source cited for any example voice, and the example names are the wiki's own judgement of public voices. Audio and video embeds on the page were not played.
