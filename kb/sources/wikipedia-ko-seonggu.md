# 성구（한국어 위키백과）

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17: 사람의 목소리의 성구(聲區, vocal register)는 같은 곳에서 발성이 일어나므로 음색이 같은 구역을 말한다 — a register is a region of the voice where phonation happens in the same place, so the timbre is the same.
- Interlanguage titles: en Vocal register · ja 声区. (No zh/th/es langlinks.)

## Bearing on voice training
- Terminology anchor for [[vocal-registers]] in Korean: 성구 is the phonetics headword over the singing-community vocabulary 가성/진성/흉성/두성/믹스보이스, whose singing usage is the Korean counterpart of the Japanese 声区 and chest/head-voice traditions [[herbst2020]] [[henrich2006]].
