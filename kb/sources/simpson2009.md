# Phonetic differences between male and female speech

## What it says
- Describes the main phonetic differences between male and female speech and critically discusses the explanations offered for them (abstract).

## Bearing on voice training
- Reminder that not all sex differences are anatomical; some are learned and language-specific, which matters for Japanese-specific training advice.
