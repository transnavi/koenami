# ニコニコ動画「両声類」タグの動画群

The corpus behind the word. [[nicodic-ryoseirui]] describes the tag as of 2015 and asks whether it still holds; the snapshot API measures it directly.

## What it says
- Tag search on 2026-09-17 returns 7,349 videos whose tag field matches 両声類, from 2007-05-27 to 2026-09-14. It is not a niche corner: for comparison the same day's snapshot gives 女声 5,318, 声真似 11,348, 男声 2,347 and 多声類 1,396.
- The oldest item the snapshot holds is sm357015, 「涼宮ハルヒの憂鬱 OP「冒険でしょでしょ？」を歌ってみた【アッ子Ｐ】」 (2007-05-27, 195,547 views, uploader アッ子Ｐ), tagged 両声類 alongside 「発毛を実感する声」 and 「彼女は男性です」 — the last tag being the joke the ニコニコ大百科 article says the scene uses for a man singing a woman's part.
- The most-viewed item is sm7862473, 【赤飯×ピコ】magnet歌いました【赤ピコ飯まー☆】, 3,263,956 views at the 2026-09-17 check, uploaded 2009-08-07 by 赤飯, tagged 両声類 (see [[nicovideo-akapiko2009]]).
- The tag is still being applied: the newest matches on 2026-09-17 include uploads from 2026-09-03, 2026-09-09 and 2026-09-14 — so [[nicodic-ryoseirui]]'s 2015 closing observation that the corpus keeps growing is still true eleven years later.
- The snapshot index is not the live tag page; it counts uploaded videos the crawler has recorded, so it is a floor rather than an exact total.

## Bearing on voice training
- Establishes scale for the vocabulary the Japanese guides use. 両声類, 女声 and 男声 are long-running tag categories with thousands of examples each, not ad-hoc coinages from trans health circles; a learner searching Japanese practice material meets this corpus before they meet any clinical source.
- The corpus is performance-led (singing, mimicry, two-role scripts), which is why its success criterion is 声質 as heard rather than a measured F0 ([[nicodic-ryoseirui]], [[pixiv-ryoseirui]]).

## Limits
- Machine-indexed metadata only: titles, uploader handles, dates, view counts and tags. No audio was analysed and no claim here is about the acoustic content of any video.
- View counts are the time of the 2026-09-17 query.
