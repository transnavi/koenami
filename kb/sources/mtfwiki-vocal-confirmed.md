# 嗓音训练已知资源（MtF.wiki）

## What it says
- A crowdsourced, price-listed directory of 跨性别 voice-training providers, with the standing warning 本页面所列内容与本网站均无经济利益往来…请自行甄别 ("no financial ties to listings; judge for yourself").
- Mainland China entries it lists (with fees as of the page's 2026-07-09 update): 北京友谊医院 (multiple doctors, 267 CNY/次); 上海市长宁区精神卫生中心 陈臻 800 CNY/次; 上海市第九人民医院 金河庚 3,000 CNY/次 from 5 sessions; 广州 南方医科大学南方医院 李衍菲 约300 CNY/次; 广东省妇幼保健院 蒙朦 约500 CNY/次; 广东省人民医院 葛平江 挂号30 CNY, 约170 CNY/次, 4次约660 CNY; 长沙 中南大学湘雅二医院 胡文倩 约200 CNY/次; 天津 天津医科大学第二医院 黄永望 200 CNY/次. Also US and Canada SLP entries (Jennifer Kim at UCI Health; Northwestern Medicine's team; The Speech People, Julia Cohen, $145/hr in 2024).
- Separately, under 其他资源, it lists providers it explicitly states are NOT licensed medical institutions (未按《医疗机构管理条例》领取"医疗机构执业许可证"): 凯瑟琳奈 基于医学研究的嗓音女性化练习, and 宁韵声音 上海跨音医疗咨询有限公司 — with the caution that the site has not verified their claims.
- Related materials it links: 《成为女孩子的声音！》by 佐倉奈緒 (vocal.mtf.wiki), the TransVoice Wiki (transvoice-wiki.mtf.wiki) and RLE.wiki — showing which foreign/community texts the Chinese community treats as canonical.
- Default pathway: 若无相关条目，大致流程可以参照陈臻 ("if your region has no entry, the general flow follows the Chen Zhen page", see [[mtfwiki-chenzhen]]).

## Bearing on voice training
- The single best map of who actually delivers 嗓音女性化训练 in mainland China and at what price — psychiatric (陈臻 at a mental-health centre), ENT (友谊医院, 广东省人民医院) and rehabilitation settings side by side; a portrait of scarce, uneven provision consistent with the Taiwan SLP survey's findings about unfamiliarity and low skill confidence ([[litosseliti2019]]).
- The licensed/unlicensed split is the community's own regulatory reading of the grey market between hospital therapy and private coaching.

## Limits
- Crowdsourced, self-disclaimed directory; fees and availability drift; named individuals are recorded as the page lists them, not vetted.
- Prices are CNY as printed on the page; no independent verification.
