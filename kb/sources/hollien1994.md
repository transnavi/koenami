# Longitudinal research on adolescent voice change in males

## What it says
- Forty-eight males tracked over five years, with speaking fundamental frequency (SFF) and phonational frequency range (PFR) measured alongside height, weight and six body dimensions (abstract).
- Findings, as listed in the abstract: "(1) stable adolescent voice change (AVC) measurements could be obtained, (2) maturation patterns appeared predictable on the basis of AVC changes, and (3) both the onset and duration of voice change extended over a longer period of time than had been previously thought."

## Bearing on voice training
- Sets the outer bounds of the male voice change: it starts earlier and runs longer than the textbook picture, so a teenager's voice is a moving target for months to years. Read next to [[harries1997]], which locates the *abrupt* part of the drop late in puberty — long onset, short cliff.

## Notes
- 48 boys, United States, measurements from the 1980s–90s; no female comparison group.
