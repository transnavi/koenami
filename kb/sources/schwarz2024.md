## What it says
- Adapts the Portuguese-validated Trans Woman Voice Questionnaire into a Transgender Man Voice Questionnaire and applies it to 31 Brazilian trans men aged 18–50 on hormone therapy, at the Gender Identity Program of Hospital de Clínicas de Porto Alegre (the same programme as [[schwarz2017]]).
- Gender-specific wording was changed in each of the 30 Likert items; a question 31 was added ("After GAHT, my voice became completely male", yes/no) plus two overall self-rating questions. Total score 0–120, higher = more dissatisfaction.
- Mean age 30.13 ± 7.6; 19.4% smokers; mean hormone-therapy duration 29.7 ± 24.9 months; 95% on intramuscular testosterone cypionate with serum testosterone in the male reference range.
- Mean total score 51 ± 17.72. A significant negative correlation was found between total score and duration of hormone therapy (r = −0.484, p = 0.006) — longer testosterone exposure, less voice dissatisfaction.
- Internal consistency was high (Cronbach's α = 0.95; split-half Spearman–Brown 0.96). For a screening short form the authors propose deleting questions 8, 10, 12, 13, 14, 17, 19, 23, 27 and 29 and modifying question 1.
- Concludes that longer hormone therapy favours voice deepening and satisfaction, and that the instrument is reliable as a screening tool.

## Bearing on voice training
- The Portuguese-language counterpart to [[nygren2016]] on the testosterone time course, but measured as reported satisfaction rather than F0; both point to months, not weeks.
- A reminder that the TVQ family is female-directed by construction: every Portuguese, Spanish and Turkish "trans male" version so far is an adaptation of a trans-woman instrument ([[sirin2021]]).

## Notes
- n = 31, single Brazilian programme, cross-sectional; the negative correlation with hormone duration cannot separate hormonal effect from time since transition.
- The proposed short form is derived from this sample and has not been validated independently.
