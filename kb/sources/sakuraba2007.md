# 男性から女性へ性別の移行を希望する性同一性障害者(MtF)の発話音声の分類に関する試案

**Record-only note.** Verified in CiNii in two versions (an IEICE technical report and the same work in the Auditory Research Meeting's proceedings); no abstract is deposited and the text was not reachable in this session. Nothing about its findings is asserted.

## What it says
- What the records establish: a 2007 proposal by 櫻庭京子, 丸山和孝 and 峯松信明 on classifying MtF speakers' connected speech, published as 電子情報通信学会技術研究報告 106(613) 1–5 (2007-03-26) and as 聴覚研究会資料 37(2) 75–79 (2007-03); NAIDs 110006250624 and 40015442025.
- The word 試案 in the title marks it as a provisional scheme rather than a validated instrument, and the venue is an engineering group rather than a clinical society.

## Bearing on voice training
- Read with [[sakuraba2005]] and [[sakuraba2009]], it is the middle step of the same group's programme: a proposal for sorting MtF voices into categories, which the 2009 listening study then tests against listener judgements ([[machine-gender-scoring]], [[gender-perception]]). The vault's automatic-scoring tools approach the same question from the software side ([[acoustic-gender-space]], [[praat]]).
- The co-author 丸山和孝 wrote the 2007 University of Tokyo master's thesis on automatically estimating perceived femininity, already in this vault as [[maruyama2007]]; the collaboration places this classification proposal squarely in speech engineering rather than clinical practice ([[machine-gender-scoring]], [[voice-across-languages]]).
