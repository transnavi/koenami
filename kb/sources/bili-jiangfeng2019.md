# 【男伪女音（人体自带变声器-咽腔教学）】伪音教学（姜峰）

## What it says
- A 12¾-minute 男伪女 (male-fakes-female) tutorial posted 2019-08-31 with 6.31M plays; its framing is in the title — the pharyngeal cavity (咽腔) as 人体自带变声器, "the voice changer built into the human body", with a follow-up 发声位置 (placement) video promised in the description.
- 咽腔教学 is the single most-cited placement concept in the 伪声 lineage: the 萌娘百科 entry's production theory makes 男伪女 mean 主要改变咽腔共鸣，减少喉腔共鸣，去除胸腔共鸣 ([[moegirl-weisheng]]), and the 2019 科学伪声 series teaches 咽腔共鸣 for 戏腔 the same way.

## Bearing on voice training
- 咽腔共鸣 is the folk version of what [[aogi2021]] re-teaches as F2/喉位 (larynx height and second formant): raising and narrowing the pharyngeal space shortens the effective vocal tract and brightens the voice — see [[formants]] and [[vocal-tract-anatomy]] for the sourced acoustics.
- The 变声器 (voice-changer) metaphor is also the community's honesty marker: the same ecosystem sells software changers and post-processing as "cheating" routes ([[moegirl-weisheng]]).
