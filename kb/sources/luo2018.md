# Progress of the laryngeal framework surgery（Luo R et al. 2018）

## What it says
- History as the abstract gives it: "Isshiki systematically developed thyroplasty in the 1970s"; laryngeal framework surgery was then "classified by the Phonosurgery Committee of the European Laryngological Society in 2001" on the basis of Isshiki's classification, after which it "became more systematic and standardized" (abstract).
- The review's scope is progress "over the last five years" in clinical application and in basic theoretical research; the Chinese abstract adds that advances in imaging and computing pushed the field forward (发展推动着喉框架手术不断进步).
- Affiliation spread: Department of Otorhinolaryngology, Union Hospital, Tongji Medical College, Huazhong University of Science and Technology (Wuhan); Beijing Friendship Hospital, Capital Medical University; and the EYE & ENT Hospital of Fudan University (Shanghai) — three of the Chinese centres that do this work.

## Bearing on voice training
- Supplies the Chinese-language account of the framework both directions of gender-affirming phonosurgery sit inside: feminizing procedures such as Wendler glottoplasty ([[zhang2023cn]], [[sun2025]]) and masculinizing type III thyroplasty ([[wang2009]]) are all described internationally as modifications of the Isshiki/ELS laryngeal-framework taxonomy.
- Read alongside the mainland surgical series, it shows the technique vocabulary a Chinese reader meets is the same one as in the English literature, even though the transgender indication is not what these articles are written for.
- A co-author from the Beijing Friendship Hospital ENT department (Zhang C) appears in both this review's byline and the later Wendler glottoplasty series [[zhang2023cn]] from the same department.

## Notes
- Narrative review of a technique family, from a peer-reviewed Chinese specialty journal; graded medium. It is not about transgender voice.
- Only the English abstract published with the article (plus the Chinese abstract in PubMed's "Publisher" field) was read; the full text was not accessed. Neither PubMed, Europe PMC nor OpenAlex carries the original Chinese title, so the translated title is used here.
