## What it says
- Reports the development of the Transsexual Voice Questionnaire for Male-to-Female Transsexuals (TVQ(MtF)), evolved from review of the existing Transgender Self-Evaluation Questionnaire (TSEQ).
- Phase I: two speech pathologists and two MtF individuals reviewed the TSEQ and compared it against thematic analysis of 137 voice-related extracts from in-depth interviews with 14 MtF individuals, leading to substantial revision into the new TVQ(MtF).
- Phase II: 29 Australian and 6 Canadian MtF transsexuals completed the TVQ(MtF) twice, 4–6 weeks apart, to assess internal consistency and test–retest reliability.
- Internal consistency was high (Cronbach's α = .964 at time 1, .974 at time 2); test–retest reliability was excellent (ICC = 0.979, 95% CI 0.968–0.988).
- Voice pitch was reported as the most problematic aspect of vocal functioning; psychosocial issues were the most frequently reported voice-related impacts.

## Bearing on voice training
- This is the original psychometric validation paper for the instrument that later becomes "TVQ(MtF)" / commonly cited as TWVQ in subsequent literature (see schwarz2017, salm2020, robotti2022 for translations) — the anchor citation for any use of this PROM.
- Establishes that pitch and psychosocial impact, not just acoustic accuracy, are central to what trans women report as voice-related difficulty, supporting outcome measures beyond F0 alone.

## Notes
- Small development sample (n=35 total across two countries); preliminary psychometric evaluation only (reliability, not full validity/responsiveness testing).
- Population: MtF transsexual adults in Australia and Canada.
