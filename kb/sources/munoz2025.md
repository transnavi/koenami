## What it says
- Colombian review (Corporación Universitaria Iberoamericana's journal Areté) of voice management in trans women "desde una perspectiva fonoaudiológica y psicológica", self-described as a PRISMA-guided systematic review; 10 articles were retained after screening.
- Frames voice as a "componente esencial en la búsqueda de afirmación de género" involving biopsychosocial, personal and self-perception aspects, and lists the routes as hormonal, surgical and non-surgical ("rehabilitación vocal").
- Discussion holds that vocal rehabilitation by the fonoaudiólogo is a therapeutic option "sea apoyada en la intervención quirúrgica y no quirúrgica", and stresses interprofessional work with this population.
- Concludes that laryngeal surgery by an otolaryngologist/laryngologist modifies the laryngeal structure and voice qualities, and that results "han sido tan positivos que son considerados actualmente como la opción clínica más efectiva para la feminización de la voz".

## Bearing on voice training
- Shows how Spanish-language Latin American reviews frame the division of labour between fonoaudiología and laringología, and the vocabulary they use ("rehabilitación vocal", "feminización de la voz").
- Its conclusion that surgery is "la opción clínica más efectiva" is stronger than the surgical evidence in [[chowdhury2025]] and [[aires2023]] supports, and stronger than its own sibling Spanish review [[malebran2021]]; read the primary sources before repeating it.

## Notes
- Labelled a systematic review but built on 10 articles retrieved partly through Google Académico and Dialnet, with no risk-of-bias appraisal and no quantitative synthesis reported in the abstract; graded low on that basis.
- Regional journal, not indexed in MEDLINE; the review covers trans women only.
