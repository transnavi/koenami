# Mapping Procedures Used by Brazilian Speech-Language Pathologists in Vocal Assessment and Gender-Affirming Voice Therapy of Transgender Women

## What it says
- Survey of 52 Brazilian SLPs (mean age 39.5, mean 15.67 years of experience, mostly cisgender women specialised in voice) who had treated transgender women in the previous 12 months, recruited through social media, August 2022–March 2023 (abstract).
- Multidimensional vocal assessment is the common practice but "does not necessarily include aerodynamic assessments"; gender-affirming voice therapy sessions are primarily individualized (abstract).
- Most-used therapeutic strategies listed in the abstract: diaphragmatic-abdominal breathing, fricative sounds, tongue or lip trill, humming, resonance tube phonation in water (low resistance), chewing technique, jaw opening and tongue mobility exercises, yawn-sigh technique, auditory repetition, in-ear monitors, pitch and loudness modulation, overarticulation, chant speech, vowel-only reading, plus guidance, awareness/proprioception, a supportive approach and vocal hygiene.
- SLPs reported good satisfaction with these strategies "except for respiratory level, where satisfaction was average" (abstract).

## Bearing on voice training
- A practice census of what a course of transfeminine voice therapy actually consists of in one large country: the strategy list is a map of [[voice-feminization-therapy]] as delivered — pitch and loudness modulation for [[fundamental-frequency]], resonance tubes and humming for [[formants]] — and the individualized-session norm matches the NHS lane in [[nhse1719]].
- The semi-occluded and resonance-tube items connect clinical practice to the SOVT literature ([[titze2006]], [[asadollahpour2025]]).

## Notes
- Convenience sample via social media; Brazilian practice, Portuguese-language service context. Companion to [[dornelas2021]], the protocol paper from the same group.
