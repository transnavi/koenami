# Feminización de la Voz. Ejercicios, cirugía y más.

Filed as an example of what a Spanish speaker searching for voice feminization actually lands on: a long, confident, keyword-organised page from a Madrid speech-therapy clinic, whose surgical claims are wrong.

## What it says
- The definition offered: 「La feminización de la voz se refiere a un proceso mediante el cual una persona con una voz de tono masculino puede adquirir un tono de voz más afeminado」, involving 「cambios en la entonación, el ritmo y el timbre」, and described as something 「algunas personas transgénero o no binarias」 may want.
- The exercise list is the ordinary one: diaphragmatic breathing, relaxation of neck, jaw and throat muscles, speaking at a higher pitch, speaking more softly, and 「hablar en un tono de voz más agudo」 by «modificar ligeramente la posición de la lengua y la mandíbula».
- On surgery the page states that voice feminization 「también puede lograrse mediante una cirugía llamada cordectomía parcial. Esta cirugía implica la eliminación parcial de las cuerdas vocales masculinas」, and that masculinization can be achieved by 「una cirugía llamada cordectomía total, en la que se eliminan completamente las cuerdas vocales afeminadas y se implanta un implante de cuerdas vocales masculinas」.
- It notes that surgery is 「irreversible y puede tener ciertos riesgos y efectos secundarios」, listing allergic reactions, infection, bleeding and 「cambios en la voz, como una voz más grave o ronca después de la cirugía」.
- Online work is described as possible, by videocall or by sending recordings for review. A bibliography is appended (Nolan 2019; Yilmaz 2021; Bultynck 2021; Umeno 2019; Salas-Humara, among others).

## Bearing on voice training
- The last two surgical statements are not accurate descriptions of any current practice: partial cordectomy to raise pitch and total cordectomy with a "vocal cord implant" to lower it are not the feminisation and masculinisation operations, which are thyroplasty and glottoplasty ([[voice-surgery]], [[kyoto-ent-clinic]]). The page is useful to this vault precisely as evidence of the Spanish-language information a patient meets before reaching a specialist.
- Its exercise list, by contrast, is unremarkable and overlaps the community guides ([[community-training]]) and the clinic protocols ([[ciosalud-feminizacion-voz]]): breathing, relaxation, pitch, resonance, articulation.

## Notes
- Graded community: an SEO-shaped clinic blog with no author byline, no date beyond the post stamp (19 December 2022), no outcomes and no prices. The bibliography at the end cites real papers that the body does not follow — one of its entries is already in this vault as [[yilmaz2021]], which describes laser reduction glottoplasty, not the cordectomy the article describes; its "Umeno 2019" is the Japanese clinical practice guideline for voice disorders, a different document from the vault's [[jspn-guideline2018]].
- The surgical descriptions are recorded here as the page's claims, with the correction beside them; this note does not repeat them as fact.
