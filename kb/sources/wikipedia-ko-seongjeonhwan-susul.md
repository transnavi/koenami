# 성전환 수술（한국어 위키백과）

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17: 성전환 수술(性轉換 手術, 영어: sex reassignment surgery, gender-affirming surgery)은 용모나 성징을 다른 성으로 변경하는 외과 수술이다 — surgery changing appearance and sex characteristics to another sex; the headword carries both the older and current English names.
- Interlanguage titles: en Gender-affirming surgery · ja 性別適合手術 · zh 性別肯定手術 · es Cirugía de afirmación de género. (No Thai langlink.)
- A search for 성별 확정 수술 (the literal Korean rendering of "gender-confirming surgery") resolves to this article — the domestic default term is 성전환 수술, with 성별 확정 수술 as a searched variant.

## Bearing on voice training
- Terminology anchor: Korean discussion of transition-related surgery defaults to 성전환 수술, so 음성여성화수술 (voice feminization surgery) is understood as one 성전환 수술 among others — the frame in which the 56.6% voice-feminization satisfaction figure sits [[lee2025]].
