# 跨性別（中文维基百科）

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17 (extract in traditional Chinese): 跨性別是指性別認同與出生時指定性別不同的人士。 — people whose gender identity differs from the sex assigned at birth.
- Interlanguage titles: en Transgender · ja トランスジェンダー · ko 트랜스젠더.
- The article title spans both scripts: 跨性別 (traditional) and 跨性别 (simplified) are the same term; mainland clinical writing historically used 易性症 (the diagnosis used on mtf.wiki's provider pages, e.g. [[mtfwiki-chenzhen]] 不需要提供易性症证明), and the community shorthand is 跨儿 or MtF/FtM ([[mtfwiki-slang]]).

## Bearing on voice training
- Terminology anchor for who the Chinese voice-training material is for: 跨性别女性 (MtF) is the audience of [[aogi2021]], [[mtfwiki-vocal-therapy]] and the Taiwan services ([[voicedoctor-tw]], [[tapcpr-friendly-2024]]).
