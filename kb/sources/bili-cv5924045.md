# 【伪音教程】各个声音的位置与共鸣和气息的关系（Bilibili 专栏）

The dataset's clearest statement of the community's voice-type taxonomy as a two-dimensional grid: where the voice sits (靠前/靠后, 靠上/靠下) and what kind of breath carries it.

## What it says
- The article opens by correcting a common misconception: 我们平时说话都会带有共鸣，只不过不会使用共鸣来美化声音…于是我们经常会误以为我们平时说话是不带共鸣的，但这个理解往往是错误的 ("we always speak with resonance, we just do not use it to beautify the voice, so we wrongly think ordinary speech has none").
- Its cavity assignment (一·前言 and 二·关于声音位置与共鸣): 男声共鸣 is 喉腔共鸣 and 胸腔共鸣; 女声共鸣 is 鼻腔共鸣 and 咽腔共鸣; 胸腔共鸣 is listed as 不可调节共鸣 ("not adjustable"). The four positions are then given as directions — 靠前 (oral resonance moved forward, using the nasal-initial 前鼻音), 靠上 (nasal + pharyngeal), 靠后 (oral resonance moved back, oral joined to pharyngeal and pharyngeal to laryngeal), 靠下 (laryngeal resonance increased).
- The voice-line table (二·关于各个声音的位置与气息), each entry a position plus a breath character:
  - 萝莉音: 靠前靠上, 气息细弱圆润;
  - 少女音: 靠上靠前 (less extreme than 萝莉音), 气息细柔 but stronger;
  - 少御音: 靠上靠后, 气息细，较为凝实;
  - 正太音: 靠后靠上, 气息细弱圆润;
  - 御姐音: 靠后靠下, 气息凝实，增强;
  - 少年音: 靠下靠后, 气息凝实，增强;
  - 老年音: 靠下靠后, 气息弱，柔;
  - 巫婆音: 靠下靠前, 气息弱，柔 but 相对老年音会更加凝实.
- The taxonomy is credited to 娭悦 and carries a no-repost notice (⚠️【若非作者允许请勿转载】).

## Bearing on voice training
- The grid is the folk equivalent of a formant-and-weight space: 靠前/靠后 and 靠上/靠下 are directions in the acoustic space the vault's [[formants]] note describes as F2 (front–back, tracking larynx height and tongue position) and F1 (high–low, tracking jaw opening), while 气息细弱/凝实 is what the voice-quality literature calls breathiness against vocal weight ([[voice-quality]], [[vocal-weight]]).
- Its consistency with the other Chinese sources is close where the vocabulary is shared: 御姐音 as 靠后靠下 with a strong breath is the same recipe as the handbook course's 咽腔-first, 胸腔-least, small pitch raise [[bili-cv9417414]], and the 女声共鸣 = 鼻腔 + 咽腔 split is the cavity scheme 萌娘百科 gives as 男伪女，主要改变咽腔共鸣，减少喉腔共鸣，去除胸腔共鸣 [[moegirl-weisheng]].
- 胸腔共鸣 marked 不可调节 is a claim about the chest as a resonator rather than about the folds, and it is the point at which the folk model is least defensible: chest vibration is a sensed resonance of the tracheobronchial tree, not an independently tunable filter.
