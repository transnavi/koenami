# Ses Estetiği

## What it says
- Definition as given in the Turkish abstract: «Ses estetiği ameliyatları, sesin perdesini hastanın yaşı, cinsiyeti ve fiziksel görünümüyle uyumlu hale getirmek amacıyla uygulanan larengeal çatı cerrahileridir» — voice-aesthetic surgery aims to bring vocal pitch into line with the patient's age, gender and physical appearance, and consists of laryngeal framework procedures.
- The operations divide into two directions, voice feminization and masculinization, «vokal foldların gerginliği, kalınlığı ve boyu gibi fiziksel parametrelerin değiştirilmesi esasına dayanır» — they work by changing the physical parameters of tension, thickness and length of the vocal folds.
- Feminization techniques named: «Wendler glottoplasti, VFSRAC, LAVA ve krikotiroid yaklaştırma»; the chapter states these "özellikle erkekten kadına dönüşüm yapan transseksüel bireylerde ve androfoni gibi hormonal durumlarda tercih edilir" — preferred especially for male-to-female transgender individuals and in hormonal conditions such as androphonia.
- Masculinization as described: «Ses kalınlaştırma ise mutasyonel falsetto hastaları, kadından erkeğe geçen transseksüeller veya mesleki/estetik nedenlerle daha tok bir ses isteyen kişilere uygulanır» — applied to patients with mutational falsetto, female-to-male transgender people, or people wanting a fuller voice for professional or aesthetic reasons; the techniques named are Isshiki's lateral approach, the European Laryngological Society's medial modification, and Koçak's window-relaxation ("baklava") thyroplasty.
- A common requirement across the techniques: «Tüm bu yöntemlerde ortak olarak ameliyat sonrasında sesin iyileşmesini çabuklaştırmak adına belirli bir süre ses istirahati zorunludur» — a period of voice rest after surgery is obligatory.

## Bearing on voice training
- The Turkish-language counterpart of the surgical catalogue in this vault, and the source of the Turkish terminology a Turkish-speaking client will meet: ses estetiği, ses inceltme, ses kalınlaştırma, ses istirahati [[voice-surgery]] [[voice-masculinization]].
- Its list of masculinization candidates puts trans men, mutational falsetto and professional voice users in one indication, which matches the vault's finding that the same pitch-lowering operation serves several populations [[voice-masculinization]].
- The chapter is by the same author whose YouTube channel explains why raising frequency alone does not make a voice sound feminine [[yt-kursatyelken]], so the written and video Turkish material can be read as one line.

## Notes
- Textbook chapter, not an outcome study: it describes techniques and indications without reporting the author's own case series in the abstract read.
- Terminology is the chapter's own and uses transseksüel, the older Turkish term.
- Only the Turkish and English abstracts and the reference list were read; the chapter body was not.
