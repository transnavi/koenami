# 未成年练伪音会怎么样（Bilibili 专栏）

## What it says
- The article argues against any 伪音 practice during 变声期 and gives a timeline for the period: 其实变声期其实是个长周期，至少到16岁嘛，一般18岁左右才能作为依据算完全变过去 ("the voice change is a long period, lasting at least to 16, and it is only around 18 that one can say it is fully over").
- It supplies an anatomical figure as the reason: 一般变声期结束的情况下都会有不同，毕竟成年男子声带要有18到24毫米，女性15到18毫米，这样的不同会产生本质差距 ("adult male vocal folds are 18–24 mm and female 15–18 mm; the difference is fundamental").
- The failure modes it reports from observation are: 有的是靠变声前的本钱以为学会了结果变完声就不会的了 ("some relied on their pre-change ability, thought they had learned it, and lost it once the change finished"); 有的就是声音变成异性声音回不来了 ("others ended up with a voice of the other sex that will not come back"); 声音嘶哑; 找不到以前的发音点，导致自己的本音找不回来; 嗓子不舒服，好像有什么东西卡在喉咙里的感觉; and 唱歌还会走音.
- Its only allowance is a cap: 如果你真的忍不住想试着玩玩的话，建议不要用伪音说话超过半小时，否则会影响到自己，有可能会导致声带充血，咽喉炎之类的 ("if you really must try it, do not speak in 伪音 for more than half an hour, or you may end up with vocal-fold congestion, pharyngitis and the like").
- The closing advice is ordinary vocal care (多喝热水, no shouting) followed by a recruitment pitch: 练声不要瞎练，逮到什么练声方法就直接练了 ("do not practise blindly, grabbing whatever method you find") and an invitation to contact the author for free beginner materials and a trial class.

## Bearing on voice training
- The length and the age boundary given here (to 16, settled around 18) match the sourced account of the voice change in the vault's [[puberty-voice-change]] note, and the vocal-fold figures it cites (18–24 mm against 15–18 mm) are in the range the vault's [[vocal-folds]] note gives for adult male and female folds, so the article's premise is sound even though it cites nothing.
- The list of outcomes is the community's collected folklore on the topic and should be read as such: 声带充血 and 咽喉炎 after half an hour of 伪声 are asserted without a source, and 找不到以前的发音点 (losing the original placement) is a habit description rather than a lesion. The accounts that report the same result in the first person are [[bili-lele2021]] and [[bili-mena]].
- The article's practical standard — no practice during the change, and a hard ceiling if one insists — matches the rule in 萌娘百科 (变声时不能练了，且每天不宜超过20分钟) [[moegirl-weisheng]] and the 2024 course's 不建议在变声期学习伪音 [[bili-cv9417414]], making it the third independent statement of the same community consensus.
