# Voice Passing: a non-binary voice gender prediction system for evaluating transgender voice transition

## What it says
- Describes voices with a continuous Voice Femininity Percentage (VFP) for transgender speakers and their therapists.
- 41 French cis and trans speakers were recorded; 57 listeners estimated VFP per voice.
- Binary gender classifiers trained on external data, applied on overlapping windows and calibrated, predicted VFP better than F0- or vocal-tract-length-based models; training style and DNN architecture affected estimates, and accuracy depended on speaker age.

## Bearing on voice training
- Closest published analogue to a femininity score; calibrated on French listeners, so not evidence for Japanese perception.
