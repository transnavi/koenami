# A construção social da Voz na performatividade do gênero

## What it says
- Sample: excerpts from videos by six well-known Brazilian YouTubers — 「2 homens cis (Christian Figueiredo e PC Siqueira), 2 mulheres cis (Kéfera Buchmann e Niina Secrets) e 2 mulheres trans (Mandy Candy e Thiessa)」 — with one specific video tag chosen so that the textual genre, and therefore the prosodic setting, would be comparable (abstract).
- Acoustic analysis found the trans women's voices 「possuem características mais femininas do que masculinas」. Women in both groups had higher f0 than the men, and 「tessitura de até 40 Hz a mais」 — a pitch range up to 40 Hz wider.
- A remote perception test with 79 listeners: 「as mulheres trans possuem passabilidade no que se refere a suas vozes, tendo tido poucas respostas que mostram 'confusão' do participante」.
- A counter-example the authors draw out themselves: Christian (a cis man) had higher f0 values than PC Siqueira (a cis man), so 「as categorias de traços masculinos ou femininos não é linear」 — the masculine/feminine trait categories are not linear, however real the physiological differences in vocal tracts are.

## Bearing on voice training
- The intonation result is the substance: women, cis and trans, showed a wider pitch range than men — the melodic-variation finding that the Ghent intonation trials and the Brazilian marker study approach from the outcome side ([[intonation]], [[irineu2025]]).
- The within-category overlap (one cis man above another) is the descriptive-linguistics version of the point [[leung2018]] makes statistically: these categories are distributions, not bins ([[voice-across-languages]], [[gender-perception]]).
- The claim that these particular trans women have 「passabilidade」 by voice is measured against 79 remote listeners on YouTube-sourced audio, with the recording conditions uncontrolled.

## Notes
- Six speakers, excerpts rather than controlled recordings, a convenience listener sample, and a journal with light peer review — graded low. The trans participants are two public figures whose transition status and treatment history are not stated.
- Claims taken from the Portuguese abstract; the acoustic values per speaker were not read.
