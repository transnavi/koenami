# Gender Identity Services for Adults (Non-Surgical Interventions): Service Specification No. 1719

## What it says
- NHS England's commissioned model for non-surgical gender identity services for adults: specialist assessment, non-surgical care packages and immediate associated aftercare provided by specialist Gender Dysphoria Clinics (GDCs), read with the companion surgical specification (section 1.1–1.2 of the specification).
- The multidisciplinary team must include, among its competencies, "Expertise in gender-specific voice and communication development" (section 7, paragraph 24, staffing and governance).
- The non-surgical treatment pathway diagram carries a dedicated "Voice & Communication" lane: assessment by a Speech & Language Therapist, a decision on whether intervention is required, then GIC-specialised 1:1 intervention and/or GIC-specialised group intervention, or referral on to a local Speech & Language Therapy service, with referral back to the lead clinician on completion (Appendix A, Stage 2 diagram).
- Care is delivered by multidisciplinary and interdisciplinary teams and networks; treatment can be accessed "concurrently or sequentially in the most appropriate order for the patient"; access to surgical intervention is only by referral from a commissioned specialist GDC (sections 8, paragraph 35, and the Stage 2 diagram).
- Self-referral is allowed alongside GP and professional referral, and providers must not disadvantage people who self-refer (paragraph 37 and Appendix B).
- Transfer from the children and young people's Gender Identity Development Service is planned before the young person's 17th birthday, with a lead-in period of joint working (paragraph 39, Appendix D).

## Bearing on voice training
- The NHS answer to "who provides voice work": voice and communication assessment and therapy sit inside the specialised gender identity service, delivered by speech and language therapists either in the GIC or in local SLT services — the institutional shape behind [[speech-language-pathology]] and [[transgender-care-context]].
- Voice intervention is one lane among talking therapies, hormone therapy and lived-experience support, ordered by the patient's treatment plan, not a fixed sequence.
