# Thyroid cartilage (English Wikipedia)

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17: "The thyroid cartilage is the largest of the nine cartilages that make up the laryngeal skeleton, the cartilage structure in and around the trachea that contains the larynx. It does not completely encircle the larynx."
- Short description: "Cartilage that makes up the laryngeal skeleton."

## Bearing on voice training
- The shield-shaped cartilage whose front angle is the laryngeal prominence ([[wikipedia-adams-apple]]), and the one the cricothyroid tilts in order to raise pitch ([[wikipedia-cricothyroid-muscle]]).
