# Eindelijk Een Vrouwelijke Stem! | Transgender Update #11 | JessieMaya

## What it says
- A Dutch-language vlog uploaded 2017-09-27. The description opens: "IN deze video neem ik jullie mee op mijn eerste afspraak om mijn stem hoger te maken. In deze afspraak testen ze de capaciteit van mijn stem en filmen ze me stembanden." — the first appointment for raising the voice, including a capacity test and filming of the vocal folds.
- The creator introduces herself in the description: "Haai, Ik ben Jessie Bosch, ben 20 jaar oud en woon in Apeldoorn", a make-up channel that also carries trans content "om andere mensen die dit ook zijn misschien te helpen en te inspireren".

## Bearing on voice training
- A Dutch-language record of what the first appointment consists of — capacity testing and laryngoscopy — from the client's side, the same assessment step the Ghent programme describes clinically [[leyns2023]] and the German private clinic lists in full [[medical-voice-center]].
- With [[maart2020]] and [[transvisie]] it gives the Dutch-language community side of the same care: people describing their own route into logopedie, where the Dutch-language outcome studies are written by clinicians [[leyns2023]].

## Limits
- One creator's vlog from 2017; no measures, no follow-up, and the video was not transcribed for this note, so the appointment's content is recorded only as the description frames it. Graded community.
- The creator carries no clinical credentials; the clip documents the client's experience, not the protocol.
