# Phoniatrische Begutachtung vor der Stimmangleichung bei Mann-zu-Frau-Transsexualismus（Rosanowski & Eysholdt 1999）

## What it says
- The clinical picture it starts from: "Sex change surgery for patients with gender identity disorders has been performed successfully during the last decade, especially for males becoming females. A troublesome problem for some of these individuals is the retention of a male voice." (PubMed's English rendering of the German abstract.)
- On hormones, stated as a limit: "Castration and estrogen therapy may produce only minor changes in pitch, with patients requiring further surgical treatment."
- The techniques it names for that: "glottoplasty, cricothyroid approximation and/or anterior commissure advancement" — the surgical repertoire as of 1999.
- The governance gap it identifies: "Although the voice is highly gender-specific, the standards of care for transsexuals do not cover guidelines for a diagnostic approach and surgical therapy."
- Scope of the paper: it answers "questions concerning specific aspects of German social law... in a representative case of a male-to-female transsexual", and reviews the literature to show "there is no common thought about whether and when a specific surgical procedure should be performed in a gender-transforming process and which method should be used for long-term results and patient satisfaction."
- Its call: although "worldwide experience with these operations and overall good results are assumed, further reports by phonosurgeons performing these operations are required concerning actual results", and "medical scientific societies should offer phonosurgery as an optional treatment for male-to-female transsexuals in their guidelines and standards of care."
- Setting: Abteilung für Phoniatrie und Pädaudiologie, Klinikum der Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg.

## Bearing on voice training
- The 1999 baseline for everything the later German sources in this vault add: at that date the standards of care said nothing about voice, the evidence for phonosurgery was described as assumed rather than reported, and the assessment question being asked was a *phoniatric expert opinion* — the report that decides what, if anything, is operated ([[transgender-care-context]], [[clinical-guidelines]]).
- The three procedures it lists define what "voice surgery" meant before the Wendler glottoplasty became the German default [[meister2017a]]; cricothyroid approximation, named here, is the technique whose outcomes the German literature later reports on [[welzel2002]].
- Its account of the hormonal limit on pitch is the same asymmetry the Swiss unit reports from the other side [[descloux2012]] and [[hormones-and-voice]] documents.
