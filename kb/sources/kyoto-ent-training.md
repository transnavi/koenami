# ボイストレーニング（京都耳鼻咽喉音聲手術医院）

## What it says
- Who it is for and on what footing: 「音声外来の診察にて必要と診断された方を対象にボイストレーナーによるボイストレーニングを行っています（任意）。」 — referral from the clinic's own 音声外来, and optional.
- What it consists of: 「発声や構音訓練・スピーチスキル・音声表現スキルを組み合わせ、患者様が毎日の生活や仕事をする上でストレスなく快適に話していただくための訓練となります。」 The four bullets listed under 「音声外来による専門的なボイストレーニング」 are 「過緊張性発声など発声習慣の改善」, 「発声習慣や発声方法を改善するトレーニング」, 「患者様の発声環境や職業に合わせた話し方トレーニング」, and 「話声の高さなど、術後音声での話し方の調整」.
- Method: 「トレーニングの効果や外科的治療の必要性を医師を含め検討しながら」 — the trainer works under the clinic's doctors, and surgery stays on the table as an alternative.
- Price and billing: 「ボイストレーニングは自費診療です。費用は、30分　6600円（税込）です。」 Self-funded, 6,600 yen per 30 minutes, the same figure given on the clinic's gender-dysphoria page ([[kyoto-ent-clinic]]).
- Distance: 「遠方などの理由で来院が難しい患者様にはオンラインでもボイストレーニングを実施しています。」 Booking is by telephone to the 京都駅前院.
- The page also carries the clinic's 音声外来 contact route: a form from which a recording or video of the voice can be sent in, read from a specified passage, under 100 MB and 30 seconds.

## Bearing on voice training
- This is the price and the pathway the vault's tutorial says is missing for Japan: after a diagnosis at a 音声外来, a ボイストレーナー (not a 言語聴覚士 here) delivers the training, and it is 自費 — the surgical stream is likewise 自費 for pitch operations ([[kyoto-ent-wendler]]), while the speech-language pathologist's rehabilitation is 保険診療 ([[kyoto-ent-rehab]]). The three billing routes sit on three pages of one clinic.
- The content it names — 発声習慣, speaking situation, 職業, and specifically 「術後音声での話し方の調整」 — is the Japanese clinical version of what community training describes as habit change rather than exercise ([[sumi-getting-started]], [[community-training]]).
- Online delivery of paid training is offered, which is how a Japanese reader outside Kansai would reach it.

## Limits
- Clinic marketing; no outcome data, no named trainer, no qualification stated for the ボイストレーナー, and no indication of how many sessions or what happens when insurance-funded rehabilitation is what the patient actually needs. The price is the clinic's own and may change.
