## What it says
- Narrative review from the Department of Otorhinolaryngology Head & Neck Surgery, West China Hospital, Sichuan University, on progress in voice feminization therapy for transgender women.
- States that voice feminization is an essential component of gender transition for transgender women and requires combined treatment integrating surgery with speech and communication therapy.
- Notes that systematic reviews and meta-analyses on the efficacy of voice feminization treatment have appeared in recent years, but that clinical cases of transgender voice therapy in mainland China remain few.
- States the paper's aim is to review research progress on voice feminization treatment for transgender patients.

## Bearing on voice training
- Establishes, from a mainland Chinese ENT department, that voice therapy access for transgender patients within China is still limited — useful context for why community-run guides (see [[digital-transchinese-mtf-voice]]) fill a gap that clinics do not yet cover at scale.
- Frames voice feminization as inherently combined surgical + behavioral therapy, consistent with the protocol in [[sun2025]].
