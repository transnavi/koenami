# 漂亮高音的根本，唱歌的核心肌肉运用（Bilibili 专栏）

A long singing-technique article built on a two-force model of larynx-height stability. Like [[bili-cv8373178]] it has no gender content; it is recorded because the control it describes is the one the gendered-voice tutorials manipulate.

## What it says
- The thesis: 没有稳定喉位，就没有高质量的高音。没有理解核心肌肉的对抗原理，声音就不容易控制和延长 ("without a stable larynx height there is no good high note; without understanding core-muscle opposition the voice is hard to control or sustain").
- The model is an equilibrium: 发声时主力是不断的向下渐变用力 ("the main force during phonation is a gradually increasing downward effort"), which 被动得到高位置共鸣 — the high resonance is obtained passively rather than pushed for.
- The mechanical chain it prescribes: 先找胸口发声位置 ("find the chest as the onset position"), then let that 向下找 the 丹田 support, so that 发声点与气息搭上; the inhale 目的下降横膈膜以及下放喉位，但胸部不动不憋. What must be avoided: 胸口的力点 不可以在嗓子声带上 [[bili-cv8373178]]'s point, restated here as 胸口支点; and the opposite error, 腰腹主动用力挤压气息，横膈膜上移，气息力量过强或者气息过多直接怼到声带，喉位高、主动上挤.
- The analogy given for the balance is explicit: 人往下的速度等于上行电梯的速度时，别人看你位置没有动…还有悬浮的直升机（重力与升力相等） — a downward effort matched against the automatic upward force leaves the larynx in place.
- The summary instruction: 运行气息就是胸口支点力量渐变不断的向下延伸即可，腰腹会自动搭配合适的力 ("breath management is just the chest support extending gradually downward; the abdomen supplies the matching force by itself"), and the diagnostic is auditory: 声音是通透、集中、有芯的用声音效果判断，发声方法是否正确.
- The article insists on indirect control of the diaphragm: 横膈膜肌肉不能主动被控制，就像你的心肌 ("the diaphragm cannot be controlled voluntarily, like the heart muscle").

## Bearing on voice training
- The 两个力 model — a downward effort against the automatic upward pressure of exhaled air — is a coherent description of what keeps a larynx from rising during sustained phonation, and it is the singing-side statement of the control that the gendered-voice tutorials reach for with drills (raised larynx for the female direction, pressed-down for the male) [[vocal-tract-anatomy]], [[larynx-anatomy]].
- The insistence on passively obtained resonance and on not pushing air at the folds is the opposite policy to the pressed phonation the community's harm accounts describe, and it aligns with the vault's [[phonation]] and [[breathing-and-support]] notes; the claim that the diaphragm is not voluntarily controllable is a simplification — it is not under direct voluntary control in the way limb muscles are, but it is influenced by posture and by the abdominal wall, which the same article concedes through its 胸腹式 description.
- Its 42 likes against 900 views is the highest like-to-view ratio in the collected articles, which suggests a small, committed readership for singing technique of this kind.
