# Speech after gender: a trans-feminine perspective on next steps for speech science and technology

## What it says
- Presents the Versatile Voice Dataset (VVD): three gender-affirming voice teachers modifying their voices along gendered axes.
- Gender classification systems are highly sensitive to voice modification, and speaker-verification systems fail to identify modified voices as the same speaker as modification becomes drastic.
- Proposes modelling individual qualities of vocal texture — pitch, resonance and weight — instead of categorical gender.

## Bearing on voice training
- Source of Koenami's teacher demonstration clips and of the pitch/resonance/weight vocabulary.
