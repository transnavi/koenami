## Bearing on voice training
- The 1.5 million-play 小嗓 tutorial in the dataset, [[bili-liujiu2023]], treats 小嗓 as the 伪声 target register without defining it; the theatre-trained accounts do define it, as a high, forward, projected register reached by calling rather than by speech [[bili-jingjupan]], [[bili-liujiapeng2020]]. This video's title-note settles the terminology: in the opera frame the word names falsetto production, while in the 伪声 frame it is used loosely for any high placed voice.
- The 一个数学公式 promise is a folk-model artefact of the same kind as the cavity schemes: a compact rule standing in for the acoustics of the register ([[vocal-registers]], [[singing-and-voice]]).
