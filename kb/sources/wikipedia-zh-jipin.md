# 基本頻率（中文维基百科）

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17 (extract in simplified Chinese): sounds decompose into sine waves; 其中频率最低的正弦波即为基音，而其他频率较高的正弦波则为泛音 — the lowest component is the fundamental (基音), higher ones are overtones (泛音).
- Interlanguage titles: en Fundamental frequency · ja 基本周波数 · ko 기본 주파수.
- 基频 is the clinical term (医院 pages write 基頻 targets in Hz, [[voicedoctor-tw]]); community teaching mostly says 音高 (pitch) — [[aogi2021]] explicitly equates the two (基频F0…和声乐理论中的音高Pitch是等价的).

## Bearing on voice training
- Terminology bridge: 基頻/基音/音高 all point at [[fundamental-frequency]]; the 基音–泛音 pair is the same source–harmonics structure as [[harmonics-and-spectrum]].
