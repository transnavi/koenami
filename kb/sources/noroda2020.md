# 【両声類】私流！男声の出し方講座【彼は女性です】

The mirror of the 女声 material: a woman teaching a 男声, under the 両声類 frame rather than a trans frame. In the vault's Japanese material this is the only behavioural masculinization source found in this pass.

## What it says
- Platform metadata, 2026-09-17: title 「【両声類】私流！男声の出し方講座【彼は女性です】」, channel 牛乳大魔王のろだ, published 2020-06-08, 51,113 views.
- The uploader's own description: 「はじめまして。のろだと申します。両声類に憧れて動画をアップロードさせていただきます。」 — the motive given is aspiration to the 両声類 category, not gender transition. The rest of the description is channel housekeeping, and it names a Twitter account for daily activity.
- The parenthetical 「彼は女性です」 in the title is the disclosure convention the ニコニコ大百科 article describes for this scene ([[nicodic-ryoseirui]]); it is there so the 男声 is read as a performance rather than as the speaker's own voice.

## Bearing on voice training
- Behavioural masculinization in Japanese is presented here through the 両声類 vocabulary (男声/女声 as producible objects), not through the clinical route. The vault's masculinization evidence otherwise runs through testosterone ([[irwig2017a]], [[nygren2016]], [[ziegler2018]]) and intonation training ([[papeleu2026]]); the Japanese surgical option is 甲状軟骨形成術Ⅲ型 ([[nakamura2021]], [[met-ent-tokyo]]).
- The 「私流」 in the title — *my own way* — is the norm in this corpus: methods circulate as personal accounts rather than protocols, which is why the vault's Japanese community material is graded as community rather than low.
