# 声带手术综述（MtF.wiki）

## What it says
- Lead warning: 声带手术的效果因人而异，且可能有严重的副作用。副作用包括音量降低、声音严重嘶哑、手术后剧烈咳嗽导致缝线裂开。效果…无法预测 — outcomes range from satisfying to negligible, with volume loss, severe hoarseness and suture rupture from coughing listed; and 全麻喉部插管可能会刺激声带，一般不推荐在接受声带手术后一年内接受全麻手术 (no general anesthesia within a year, if avoidable).
- Prerequisites it lists: 传染病四项, 嗓音检查 (高/低音, 音域, 发声方式, 最长音时间, 基频), 喉镜检查; and the principle 原则上建议先进行嗓音女性化训练。嗓音女性化手术只能改变发声频率，且响度会有不同程度的损失 ("in principle train first; surgery changes only frequency, with some loudness loss").
- Mainland technique landscape: 国内医院目前做内镜下的声带截短术为主，环甲接近术主要用于不适用声带截短术的病例或同时切除喉结的病例 — endoscopic 声带截短术 (vocal-fold shortening, i.e. Wendler-type glottoplasty) is the default; 环甲接近术 (cricothyroid approximation) mainly for cases unsuited to shortening or combined with Adam's-apple removal.
- Its technique table: 声带截短术 (喉镜下将声带缝短; 稳定微创无痕迹; irreversible, 响度变小, 效果抽奖 "outcome is a lottery"), 环甲软骨接近术/环状软骨固定术 (可同时处理喉结; reversible but may need re-operation), 甲状软骨声带缩小术, 激光嗓音 (laser vaporization of the posterior fold), 声带横膈膜整形术.
- Voice factors it names for overall outcome: 话语基频、共振、变调、嗓音质量、清晰度、说话强度、言语率和韵律 — F0, resonance, intonation, quality, clarity, intensity, rate, prosody.
- Contraindication-like cases reported from clinic visits: 先天性声带发育不良, 声带闭合不全, 声门闭合不全, 声带过度松弛, 发声模式过于单一.
- Lifestyle factors it lists as harming voice long-term: smoking (incl. e-cigarettes, second-hand), alcohol, coffee, late sleep, eating 2–3 h before bed (reflux), spicy/acidic food and ice water; post-op 三个月不能吃刺激性食物, and coughing risks 开线.
- References it cites include a CNKI article URL, the Japanese yesonvc clinic pages, a Taylor & Francis article, and archived community essays (嗓音手术与嗓音训练, 寻找真实的声音- MTF嗓音拯救指南, 北京友谊医院声带手术指南).

## Bearing on voice training
- The community's surgery-side counterpart to [[mtfwiki-vocal-therapy]]: same ordering (training first), same caveat set (volume loss, irreversibility) as the clinical reviews behind [[voice-surgery]] ([[song2017]], [[chowdhury2025]]), and the mainland technique names that match the Beijing and Nanjing series in [[zhang2023cn]] and [[sun2025]].
- 声带截短术 is the exact term the Nanjing paper uses (声带截短), so community vocabulary and clinical vocabulary coincide here.

## Limits
- Community wiki page with mixed sourcing (clinic marketing, archived forum essays, one CNKI item); complications and contraindications are reported as community-collected advice, not as a reviewed complication series.
- The technique table's "N/A" cells (from the original HTML table) mean the page itself lists no pros/cons for those entries.
