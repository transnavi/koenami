# 如何用“弹性喉位”解决你的高音音质硬伤（Bilibili 专栏）

A singing-pedagogy article about larynx height. It has no gender dimension, and is recorded here because larynx height is the single mechanism the gendered-voice tutorials in this dataset manipulate most, and this is a description of it written from the vocal-technique side.

## What it says
- The premises: 甲状软骨，俗称喉结…它在我们共鸣腔的变化和打开中，起了非常关键的作用，就好像声道的一个阀门 ("the thyroid cartilage, commonly called the Adam's apple, is crucial to changing and opening the resonating cavities; it is like a valve on the vocal tract"), and the requirement is a larynx that is both free and stable — 又自由又稳定的“弹性喉位”.
- The mechanical condition: 凡是在一个有限空间内，需要自由而可控的物理运动，一定是要在一个划定的范围或者轨道里的 ("free, controllable movement inside a limited space must stay within a defined range or track").
- The failure it names: the larynx must not be pushed by the jaw or the hyoid — 我们的喉位首先不可以用下颚还有舌骨去压它，那下巴、舌骨在咬字和发声时就不可以发力参与，比如后撤，下压动作 — and the reason is a chain reaction: 需要一个正确的压缩力点，这个力点一定不可以在嗓子声带上，否则会引起连锁反应，舌根下巴肌肉都参与发力，这是自学的声乐爱好者，或者受过错误训练的人最容易进入的误区 ("the compression point must not be on the throat or folds, or the tongue root and jaw join in; this is the commonest error of self-taught singers and of people trained wrongly").
- The remedy: 把力点从你的喉结前方移到你的喉结后方 ("move the effort point from in front of the larynx to behind it").
- The training order it gives (按主次): 1. 胸声力点转移 2. 声带适度闭合 3. 放松的下颚和舌骨 4. 打开咽腔, with the follow-up promised for 打开咽腔 and the invitation to consult the account's teachers.

## Bearing on voice training
- The article supplies what the 伪声 material assumes: a raised or lowered larynx must be held in a controlled range while free of jaw and tongue-root force, or the surrounding muscles take over. That is the mechanism behind the community's recurring distinction between a raised larynx and a squeezed throat — 紧嗓子是伪声的大忌 in the essay course [[bili-cv460384]], and the handbook's argument that 损伤是因为高喉位通常自然伴随着 narrow throat 或者说 throat tightness, not high larynx height as such [[aogi2021]].
- Its 力点 shifting is the same instruction as the singing-side 甲倾/边缘化 vocabulary in a 2026 男伪女 tutorial [[bili-qingyunlin2026]]: the community's advanced technique and the singing teacher's basic one are the same control. The vault's [[larynx-anatomy]] and [[phonation]] notes carry the sourced anatomy of the muscles involved.
