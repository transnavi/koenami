# Voice, articulation, and prosody contribute to listener perceptions of speaker gender: a systematic review and meta-analysis

## What it says
- 38 articles met eligibility; meta-analysis showed speaking fundamental frequency contributing 41.6% of the variance in gender perception.
- Auditory-perceptual and acoustic measures of pitch, resonance, loudness, articulation and intonation were associated with listeners' perceptions of speaker gender; tempo and stress were not.
- Mixed findings on whether a breathy voice quality contributes.
- The authors note significant risk of bias in this body of research.

## Bearing on voice training
- Pitch matters most but leaves ~58% unexplained; resonance, loudness, articulation and intonation are legitimate training goals; breathiness is not a reliable lever.
