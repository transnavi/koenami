# Pitch (music) (English Wikipedia)

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17: "Pitch is the quality that makes it possible to judge sounds as 'higher' and 'lower' in the sense associated with musical melodies. Pitch is a perceptual property that allows sounds to be ordered on a frequency-related scale. Pitch is a major auditory attribute of musical tones, along with duration, loudness, and timbre."
- Short description: "Perceptual property in music ordering sounds from low to high."
- The wording that matters: pitch is *perceptual* and only "frequency-related". It is not a synonym for fundamental frequency, though the two are used interchangeably in everyday voice talk.

## Bearing on voice training
- Keeps the vocabulary honest in [[pitch-and-scales]]: a voice app measures F0 in hertz, a listener hears pitch, and a singer counts semitones. Each is a different quantity and the mapping between them is neither linear nor exact.

## Limits
- Community-edited, and written from music theory rather than speech; the pitch of a speaking voice is an average over a constantly moving F0 contour, which the article does not address.
