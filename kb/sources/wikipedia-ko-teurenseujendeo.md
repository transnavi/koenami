# 트랜스젠더（한국어 위키백과）

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17: 트랜스젠더(영어: Transgender)는 사회적 성(gender)과 생물학적 성별(sex)이 일치하지 않는 사람 — a transgender person is one whose gender and biological sex do not match; the article notes some transgender people seeking medical transition are recognised as 성전환자 (transsexual), and that the term's scope extends to crossdressers under some definitions.
