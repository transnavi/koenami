# 甲状軟骨形成術4型の長期経過後に牽引糸を切断した1例

## What it says
- The situation: a patient diagnosed with 男性から女性型の性同一性障害（MTF/GID） who had had type 4 thyroplasty at another institution and 「4年後にやはり自分は男性であるとの結論に達し，TP4を元に戻してほしいという主訴で当院へ紹介され受診した」.
- The consent the surgeons took: 「牽引糸の抜糸手術を行っても声が元に戻らない可能性があることを説明の上，手術を施行した。」
- What was found and done: 「手術ではゴアテックスボルスターと牽引糸を取り除いたが，術前の予想通り甲状軟骨と輪状軟骨が近接したまま一体化しており」.
- The pitch outcome: 「話声位は術前206 Hz，術中201 Hz，術後202 Hzと，低下を認めなかった」.
- The conclusion: 「術後ある程度の時間が経過するとTP4は不可逆的となることが推測された。」
- Keywords listed with the article: gender identity disorder, 性同一性障害, thyroplasty, 甲状軟骨形成術, speaking fundamental frequency, 話声位.

## Bearing on voice training
- This is the Japanese clinical statement that type 4 thyroplasty becomes irreversible: after enough time the cricoid and thyroid have fused, so removing the hardware does not return the voice. It is the counter-case to the assumption that a pitch-raising operation can be undone ([[voice-surgery]]).
- The patient's own route — a 47-year-old who concluded four years later that he was male again — is the detransition case the community and the surgical literature rarely record; the note is about the acoustics, but the referral reason is part of the report.
- For training, the irreversible case and the retained-hardware infection case ([[mizawa2022]]) both point the same way: post-surgical pitch is a fixed constraint that the practice plan has to accept, and the training target becomes everything above and around it — resonance, weight, intonation ([[voice-therapy-techniques]]).

## Notes
- Single case, abstract only; no listener ratings, no voice-quality measures beyond sF0 at three time points. The clinic that performed the primary operation is not named.
