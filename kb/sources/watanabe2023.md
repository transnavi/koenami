# Coco-Nut: corpus of Japanese utterance and voice characteristics description for prompt-based control

## What it says
- A corpus of diverse Japanese utterances with transcriptions and free-form descriptions of voice characteristics, built by automatic collection, quality assurance and crowdsourced annotation.

## Bearing on voice training
- Source of everyday Japanese descriptors such as 甘えた声 used as a tentative scale in Koenami.
