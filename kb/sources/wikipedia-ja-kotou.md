# 喉頭

The Japanese article for *larynx*; interlanguage links include **Larynx** (en), 후두 (ko), 喉 (zh), กล่องเสียง (th) (langlinks API 2026-09-17).

## What it says
- Intro, in full: 「喉頭（こうとう、（英: larynx）は、咽頭と気管の狭間で、舌骨より下にあり気管より上にある、頸部中央に一つ存在する器官である。」 — position by neighbouring structure, which is what the Japanese clinical pages mean when they talk about approaching the larynx.

## Bearing on voice training
- The everyday word in the training guides is not 喉頭 but 喉仏 (Adam's apple), visible and palpable; the melody-method warning not to press it ([[wikipedia-melanie-ho]]) and the brushvoice observation that it rises and hides during 女声 ([[brushvoice-joseigoe]]) both track the thyroid cartilage, a part of this organ.
- The surgical pages that offer 甲状軟骨形成術Ⅳ型 and 声帯短縮術 ([[kyoto-ent-clinic]], [[met-ent-tokyo]]) operate on this structure.
