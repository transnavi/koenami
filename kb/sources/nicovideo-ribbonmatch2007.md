# アラジンのテーマソングの男女のパートを1人で歌いこなす男性

A 2007 upload showing the pattern the 両声類 tag was invented for: one performer covering both parts of a duet.

## What it says
- Metadata, 2026-09-17: uploaded 2007-12-11 by リボンマッチ; 1,627,025 views; tags include Nick_Pitera, A_Whole_New_World, 両声類, アラジン, アゴジン, ホール・ニュー・ワールド, ディズニー.
- The uploader's description: 「「A Whole New World」を、声色を巧みに変えて一人で歌いきる男性。」 followed by links to the same performer's covers of リトルマーメイド and 「Oh Holy Night」. The tag Nick_Pitera identifies the singer as the American performer Nick Pitera, whose one-person duet videos circulated widely; this is a Japanese re-upload with a Japanese title.
- 「声色を巧みに変えて」 — the uploader frames the skill as 声色 (voice colour) switching rather than as singing ability, which is the same distinction ニコニコ大百科 records for the tag ([[nicodic-ryoseirui]]).

## Bearing on voice training
- An early, non-Japanese-performer example of the practice, tagged into the Japanese corpus: shows the 両声類 tag is applied to whoever convinces listeners, including overseas performers, and that the scene's interest is in the switch itself.
- The 2007 date places it before ニコニコ大百科's account of the tag's formation; the article puts tag formation before 2007-11-05, and this upload is 2007-12-11 ([[nicodic-ryoseirui]]).
