# Ley 4/2023 (España) — igualdad real y efectiva de las personas trans

## What it says
- Spain's state-level trans rights law. Article 43 establishes registry self-determination: "Toda persona de nacionalidad española mayor de dieciséis años podrá solicitar por sí misma ante el Registro Civil la rectificación de la mención registral relativa al sexo", with a assisted procedure at 14–16 and judicial defence on disagreement.
- Article 16 obliges public administrations to incorporate LGBTI people's particular needs into health policy, promote research on their specific health needs, and orient training of health professionals "al conocimiento y el respeto de la orientación sexual, identidad sexual, expresión de género y características sexuales".
- Article 17 bans conversion therapies. The law's health articles are general: the words voz (voice) and logopedia (speech therapy) do not appear anywhere in the text (checked on the BOE page fetched 2026-09-17).

## Bearing on voice training
- Spain's legal floor for trans health care is anti-discrimination and depathologization, with no named entitlement to voice therapy at state level — voice services are the regions' business, which is why the Spanish voice literature clusters around regional units like Madrid's Unidad de Identidad de Género at Ramón y Cajal [[mora2018a]] [[casadomorente2021]] and Barcelona's private FemVeu unit [[tornero2019]].
- The contrast the vault already holds with Argentina, whose Ley 26.743 similarly frames rights rather than services [[ley26743]].

## Notes
- Graded high as the authoritative legal text; it establishes rights, not clinical provision, so it supports no outcome claims.
