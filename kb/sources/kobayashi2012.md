# ホルモン剤による女子の音声障害

A 30-year follow-up of one woman given a virilising drug at 17, with her identical twin as the comparison — the longest natural history of androgen voice damage in this vault.

## What it says
- The case: 「17歳のとき月経痛がひどく，男性化作用のあるダイホルモンデポ®を投与され，音声障害を引き起こした女性を30年間にわたり追跡した」 — a woman given ダイホルモンデポ® (a virilising depot preparation) at 17 for severe dysmenorrhoea, followed for 30 years.
- The course in figures: 「声は翻転し，話声位は低下した。18歳の初診時の話声位はB3で，30歳までこの状態が続き，48歳の現在話声位はF3となっている」 — the voice broke over, speaking pitch at first presentation aged 18 was B3, the same at 30, and down to F3 at 48. (The paper uses letter pitch notation rather than Hz; which national convention it follows, and therefore the exact frequencies, was not determined here.)
- The onset: 「ホルモン投与の当初，話声位は3半音低下を見た」 — a 3-semitone drop at the start of the medication.
- The comparison: 「この状態を声も相同と考えられる一卵性双生児の妹と比較した」 — the state was compared with her monozygotic twin sister, taken as vocally comparable.
- The outcome that matters: 「自家で行った音声訓練にかかわらず，声は回復しなかった」 — despite voice training she did herself, the voice did not recover.

## Bearing on voice training
- The duration is the finding: a 3-semitone drop at 17 was still present, and still worsening, three decades later, and self-directed training did not reverse it ([[hormones-and-voice]], [[voice-health]]). Contrast [[miyata2013]] and [[kusuyama2005]], where therapy in hormonal dysphonia did help — the difference those two papers have and this one does not is that the medication was withdrawn.
- The twin comparison is a crude but useful control: the pitch difference is not explained by the family's voices ([[female-voice]], [[sex-differences]]).
- The break in the voice at onset (翻転) is the same register event the vault records as a puberty-change mechanism ([[puberty-voice-change]], [[vocal-registers]]).

## Notes
- n = 1 with a sibling comparison, no acoustic instrumentation reported in the abstract; graded low.
- Uses letter pitch notation (B3, F3) rather than Hz, so the size of the change has to be read from the paper's own scale.
