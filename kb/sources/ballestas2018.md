# Cirugía de feminización de voz con técnica glotoplastia de Wendler en Barranquilla, Colombia

The largest Latin American surgical series located in this pass, and the only one that names the private service delivering it.

## What it says
- 36 trans women in the male-to-female transition underwent surgery and post-operative voice-therapy rehabilitation with the VOICEFEM – Voice Feminization Colombia team; pseudo-experimental before-after design (bilingual abstract).
- The technique as described: 「la creación de una sinequia de las cuerdas vocales (CCVV) previa desepitelización de la cara interna del tercio anterior de las mismas, 2 puntos de sutura con Vicryl 4/0 y vaporización con electrocauterio en la región lateral de la cara superior de las CCVV y utilización de goma biológica en la sinequia creada」 — a vocal-fold synechia after de-epithelialising the inner face of the anterior third, two Vicryl 4/0 sutures, electrocautery vaporisation laterally, and biological glue.
- Measures: fundamental frequency, maximum phonation time and the TSEQ questionnaire, before and after.
- Result: 「un aumento de la Frecuencia fundamental promedio de 112Hz (P<0.05) a los 6 meses posteriores al procedimiento quirúrgico y una disminución de cerca de 30 puntos en los resultados del cuestionario TSEQ」 — a mean F0 rise of 112 Hz at six months and about a 30-point drop on the TSEQ.
- Conclusion: the technique 「ofrece resultados favorables con aumentos significativos de la frecuencia fundamental a mediano plazo」 and must be combined with post-surgical voice therapy 「para obtener el desenlace óptimo esperado」.

## Bearing on voice training
- The 112 Hz mean gain is close to the Marbella figure of 106 Hz in [[casado2016]] and far above the 47.9 Hz of [[aires2023a]] — three Wendler series, three different magnitudes, which is a caution about reading any single F0-increase number as the technique's effect ([[voice-surgery]]).
- The TSEQ's ~30-point drop is the same self-report-dominant pattern seen in [[aires2023a]] and [[martinho2025]]: the questionnaire moves more than the acoustics explain ([[patient-reported-outcomes]]). A private service operating at 36 cases is also the Latin American counterpart to the East Asian surgical centres in the vault ([[yeson-voice-center]], [[kamol-hospital]]).

## Notes
- No control group, no randomisation, no blinding reported; follow-up is stated at six months only, and the before-after design cannot separate surgery from the concurrent voice therapy. Graded low despite the sample size, because of the design.
- No ethical approval statement or participant characteristics appear in the abstract read here.
- Claims taken from the bilingual abstract as deposited; the Colombian full text was not read.
