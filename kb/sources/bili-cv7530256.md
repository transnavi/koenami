# 伪声男伪女被忽略的重要技巧（一）（Bilibili 专栏）

A short argument for one cavity over the others, written as a correction to the tutorial market.

## What it says
- The opening claim: 目前市面上的男伪女教程一大把都是玄学教程，即使一些人气伪声UP也没有提到一个重要技巧，就是『口腔共鸣』 ("most male-to-female tutorials on the market are mysticism; even popular 伪声 uploaders never mention one important technique — oral resonance").
- The evidence offered is a contrast: 男生说话的时候口腔共鸣是很弱的，而一些字口腔共鸣甚至完全没有，相反女生口腔共鸣是很强的 ("men's speech has very weak oral resonance, some syllables none at all; women's is strong").
- The conclusion drawn: 口腔咽腔鼻腔都是能增加你的女性感的，单靠一个咽腔显然无法获得足够的女性感 ("oral, pharyngeal and nasal all add femininity; pharynx alone is plainly not enough").
- The practical route is the consonant inventory: p, f, t, k, h, q, x, zh, ch, sh, z, c, s 打头的字都可以感受口腔共鸣 ("words beginning with these can be used to feel oral resonance"), attributed to 公仪清晖 who 也提到过口腔共鸣的练习方法. Two further claims: 口腔共鸣有削弱胸喉共鸣和附加鼻音的效果 ("oral resonance weakens chest and laryngeal resonance and adds a nasal component"), and 口腔共鸣的运作比较玄，与气息也有莫大的关联 ("how oral resonance works is rather mysterious and closely bound up with breath").

## Bearing on voice training
- The article's premise is a real acoustic asymmetry: oral constriction shapes F1 and F2 directly, so the vowel space is where the front/back and open/closed adjustments that shift formants are made ([[formants]], [[articulation]]). Which of the cavities dominates is, however, an empirical question and the article answers it by assertion, contrasting explicitly with [[bili-laxiaojiao2020]] and [[bili-jiangfeng2019]], which make 咽腔共鸣 the decisive one.
- The consonant list is the community's most concrete practice aid in the collected articles — a set of syllables chosen because they place the articulation forward — and it is the same 靠前 placement the taxonomy assigns to 萝莉音 and 少女音 [[bili-cv5924045]].
- Its own admission that the mechanism is 玄 and unexplained is worth recording: it is the clearest instance of the layer acknowledging that its cavity model is descriptive rather than causal, which is the gap the acoustics-based handbook set out to fill [[aogi2021]].
