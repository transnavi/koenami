# 機能性発声障害を伴うホルモン音声障害に対し，音声治療と理学療法と甲状軟骨形成術4型を併用した症例

A Japanese case report on hormone-induced voice disorder (ホルモン音声障害). The patient is a cis woman treated for menopausal symptoms, not a trans person — it is in this vault because the mechanism, the vocabulary and the surgical step belong to the same Japanese literature as the trans surgical notes.

## What it says
- Cause, from the abstract: 「女性更年期障害の治療として，男性・卵胞ホルモン合剤注射治療を受け，副作用により話声位の低音化，声域の狭小化をきたした。さらに機能性発声障害を併発し自由会話が困難となった。」 A combined male-and-follicle hormone injection lowered speaking F0, narrowed range, and a functional dysphonia on top made free conversation difficult.
- Sequenced treatment: 「機能性発声障害に対する音声治療（VT）により自由会話が可能となり，喉頭ストロボスコピー検査で声帯粘膜波動が確認できるようになった。」 Voice therapy restored conversation and mucosal wave on stroboscopy.
- Then, since the pitch problem remained: 「しかし，話声位の低音化，声域の狭小化は改善せず，甲状軟骨形成術4型（TP4）を実施した。」
- Physical therapy alongside it, given before surgery: 「理学療法士による，呼吸筋群の活性化，腹横筋の活性化，頸部マッサージの理学療法（PT）」 — activating respiratory muscles and transversus abdominis, plus cervical massage.
- Result as claimed: 「器質的変化をきたしていた声帯がTP4により伸長され，声帯筋緊張が保たれた状態でVTを継続し，声域の拡大，ピッチマッチが可能になり，抑揚の少ない童謡歌唱が可能となった。」 Range and pitch matching improved, enough for a simple children's song; and the authors' reading of the case: 「不可逆的器質変化が発声筋群のみならず，呼吸筋群にも起きた可能性が考えられた本症例には，VT，PT，TP4の併用は有効であった。」

## Bearing on voice training
- The clearest Japanese-language account in the vault of *acquired* hormone-related voice change in the lowering direction — the mechanism the trans-masculine literature reports as intended ([[irwig2017a]], [[nygren2016]], [[nimura2015]]). It is a side effect here, which is why it appears under ホルモン音声障害.
- The combination — behavioural therapy, respiratory physical therapy, then surgery for what therapy did not reach — is the same ordering the trans literature uses ([[kobayashi2022]], [[yomedanchii-voice]]). The respiratory-muscle and transversus abdominis work is the clinical version of the 腹式 support talked about in the community ([[brushvoice-joseigoe]]) and in [[breathing-and-support]].

## Limits
- Low-grade: a single case report, one patient, no control, published in a Japanese clinical-society journal. The abstract is all that was read.
- The patient is not trans and the hormone exposure is a menopausal treatment regimen; nothing here transfers to gender-affirming hormone therapy without saying so.
