# A vocal treatment plan for voice disorders after phonosurgery — a preliminary study（Wang NM, Huang TS 1994）

## What it says
- Twenty-five subjects at Chang Gung Memorial Hospital, Taipei, "received phonosurgery of benign vocal pathology but persistent voice disorders postoperatively, characterized by a hoarse, low-pitched, soft voice or efforted phonation" (abstract).
- Each subject "filled out a self-evaluation questionnaire of vocal behaviors before surgery"; the vocal folds were examined by an ENT doctor with indirect laryngoscope or direct fiberscope; voice was evaluated on perceptual and acoustic parameters pre- and postoperatively.
- The 25 were sorted into three postoperative vocal rehabilitation programmes — "voice rest group, vocal hygiene group, and direct voice therapy group". The grouping criteria were "the subjects' amount of vocal use, environmental pressures, vocal abuse or misuse behaviors and time flexibility" (abstract) — the plan was matched to the patient's vocal load, not to the lesion.
- Therapy strategies were "reducing amount of vocal use, changing life style, analyzing environmental pressures and treating voice problems with established facilitating techniques"; the programme ran three to six months, with re-evaluation six months after surgery.
- The stated implication is "to provide a holistic voice treatment plan and obtain better surgical intervention results" (abstract).

## Bearing on voice training
- The earliest Chinese-language (Taiwan) statement in this vault that phonosurgery is only half a treatment: the voice outcome of an operation on benign pathology was framed in 1994 as a rehabilitation-planning problem decided by vocal load, environment and misuse behaviour.
- The same pairing appears 30 years later in the mainland trans surgical series, where voice rest is followed by mandatory speech rehabilitation [[sun2025]], and in the Taiwanese trans telepractice programme [[lin2026]].
- Note the intake instrument: a pre-operative self-evaluation questionnaire of vocal behaviours, which is the [[patient-reported-outcomes]] logic avant la lettre.

## Notes
- Retrospective single-centre case series of 25 patients with benign vocal pathology; not a trans population and not about pitch. Included for the Chinese-language clinical framing of post-phonosurgical rehabilitation.
- Only PubMed's English rendering of the Chinese article was read; the original Chinese title and full text were not retrievable from the databases consulted (PubMed, Europe PMC and OpenAlex all index this record with a translated English title only).
