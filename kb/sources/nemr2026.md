# Voz e identidade: reflexões sobre congruência vocal em pessoas transgênero e laringectomizadas

A two-author letter in CoDAS — no abstract, no data. It is filed here because it names things that Brazilian clinical writing usually leaves implicit: what trans and laryngectomised people have in common about voice, and what they do to their voices when services are not available.

## What it says
- The letter's question: 「o que pessoas transgênero e pessoas laringectomizadas esperam de suas vozes?」 It proposes that communication and life run in the same direction for both groups but inversely proportioned — 「Para pessoas trans, a presença da primeira pode comprometer a segunda; para pessoas laringectomizadas, a ausência da primeira é o preço para preservar a segunda.」
- Being identified as trans by voice (or by the laryngeal prominence moving during swallowing) can, 「em casos extremos」, put a person's life at risk (the authors are writing from Brazil, where they say violence against trans people 「ultrapassa os limites da civilidade」).
- On what people do without medical supervision, attributed to the authors' own clinical observation: 「algumas mulheres trans se submetem a procedimentos arriscados, como a injeção de silicone industrial no pescoço, sem supervisão médica」 to disguise the laryngeal prominence; and some trans people smoke deliberately to alter the voice — 「homens trans … para alcançar frequências mais graves, e mulheres trans … para introduzir vocal fry ou outros ruídos」.
- On avoidance: both groups avoid the telephone and, especially now, voice messages — laryngectomised people from fear of not being intelligible, 「já pessoas trans evitam enviar áudios que possam revelar, pela voz, que se trata de uma pessoa trans」.
- On intensity: laryngectomised speech is weaker, while trans women 「controlam sua intensidade vocal para não chamar atenção」; trans men may lose decibels through hormone treatment's structural effect on the vocal folds.

## Bearing on voice training
- An explicit statement, in a Brazilian speech-language-pathology journal, that passing and safety are the same subject ([[transgender-care-context]], [[voice-health]]). It belongs beside [[dornelas2021]], whose protocol names 「passabilidade social por meio da voz」 as the goal.
- The self-administered strategies it names — deliberate smoking for vocal fry, unregulated industrial silicone — are the end of the road when voice services are unavailable, and they are the reason the access argument in [[cielo2021]] and [[sosthenes2025]] is clinical rather than political decoration.

## Notes
- A letter, not a study: no participants, no measures, no systematic review. The claims about smoking and silicone are the authors' clinical observations, stated as such, and are reproduced here with that attribution.
- The Portuguese and English versions were both read from the PMC page; they are translations of one text.
