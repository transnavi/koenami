# 跨性别男性从小到大的心酸经历（小柒）

A first-person account by a 22-year-old trans man, written as a life narrative rather than as advice. Its relevance here is narrow and specific: it is the dataset's fullest Chinese-language description of what testosterone did to one person's voice, in among the social history.

## What it says
- The narrative covers school years, bathrooms, housing, and a period of suicidal ideation with a letter to his parents at 15 or 16, then finding the community in 2019 through a 兄弟会 group of several hundred trans men.
- On hormones, in his own words: 我看到兄弟们打激素，能变声音能慢慢长胡子腿毛喉结等，我自己也心动了，我也了解了激素的坏处，我纠结了有一段时间…最终我还是觉得勇敢的去做自己想做的吧; 自己给自己打针这听起来都让人害怕; 第一针心里非常的忐忑就那样打下去了，之后打的第二针就没有那么慌张了.
- The voice and body changes he reports: 我在打激素之前涂了兄弟推荐长胡子的东西，然后腿毛胡子有慢慢在长，打了激素我怕长太多就没涂了，现在一样持续在长，打了激素之后喉结也慢慢变大一些，胡子腿毛也有了些慢慢自己自信了很多.
- He distinguishes himself from a classmate he met as a 束胸-wearing 女同性恋: 她承认她自己是女生只是喜欢以男性方式生活…而我不同，我不存在承不承认自己是个女生，而是我从出生下来，我就是个男生.
- The plans stated: 我打算明年就去做平胸手术，之后有钱慢慢去做下体手术改证明结婚什么的.
- The closing appeal is to acceptance: 跨性别的孩子根本没有错，父母也没有错，错的是上帝，错的是命运.

## Bearing on voice training
- The voice content is the hormone trajectory, reported without measurement: testosterone produced a gradual deepening and a larger Adam's apple (喉结也慢慢变大), which is what the vault's [[hormones-and-voice]] note describes in sourced terms — a permanent F0 drop from vocal-fold thickening and lengthening, largely complete within roughly a year. Nothing here is timed or acoustically described, so the account is corroborative rather than evidential.
- The narrative also documents the route by which trans men in China found both hormones and surgery in 2019–2020: through a peer 兄弟会 group rather than through a clinic, with self-injection (自己给自己打针) and a beard-growth product bought on a peer's recommendation. That is the care context the vault describes in [[transgender-care-context]] and [[chinese-community]], where the wiki's provider directories exist precisely because this route is otherwise unguided.
- Voice training does not appear in the account at all: for this speaker the voice change came from hormones, which is the ordinary path for trans men and the reason Chinese-language 女伪男 material is thin relative to the MtF side ([[voice-masculinization]], [[bili-fledence2025]]).
