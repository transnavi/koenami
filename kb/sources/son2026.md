# Gender Affirming Voice Surgery

## What it says
- Review in the journal of the Korean Society of Laryngology, Phoniatrics and Logopedics (대한후두음성언어의학회지). Premise: "hormone therapy alone often fails to achieve satisfactory voice changes, necessitating phonosurgical intervention" in transfeminine individuals (abstract).
- Cricothyroid approximation "can elevate fundamental frequency (F0) by 40–60 Hz without mucosal injury, though its effect is limited"; endoscopic approaches including Wendler glottoplasty "demonstrate greater F0 elevation of 60–100 Hz with success rates of 75%–85%" (abstract).
- Despite mucosal-injury risk, "a range of modified surgical approaches is now actively employed in clinical practice"; good outcomes need "appropriate patient selection, preoperative voice therapy, skilled surgeons, and intensive rehabilitation" — a multidisciplinary approach (abstract).

## Bearing on voice training
- A Korean-society review repeating the F0-increment ladder (CTA 40–60 Hz, glottoplasty 60–100 Hz) that international reviews also report ([[song2017]], [[aires2023]], [[chowdhury2025]]), and putting preoperative voice therapy inside the surgical pathway ([[voice-feminization-therapy]]).
- Evidence that gender-affirming voice surgery is now a review topic in the domestic Korean otolaryngology literature, not only in the international journals where the Yeson series appeared ([[kim2017]], [[kim2024]]).
