# Voice gender scoring and independent acoustic characterization of perceived masculinity and femininity

## What it says
- Proposes an Extreme Random Forest scoring model of perceived masculinity/femininity and clusters acoustic factors that contribute to perceivers' judgements.
- Machine ratings correlated with human ratings up to r = .63 for females and .77 for males at an optimal speech duration of 7 seconds.

## Bearing on voice training
- Shows automatic femininity/masculinity scores can track listeners moderately well, on English speech.
