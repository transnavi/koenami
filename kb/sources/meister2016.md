# Zufriedenheit nach Stimmerhöhung bei Transsexualität – Entwicklung eines Therapiealgorithmus（Meister et al. 2016）

## What it says
- The opening premise, in the abstract: "Voice feminization is an important step in the therapy of male-to-female transsexualism. Approaches are conservative voice therapy and surgical interventions." (PubMed's English rendering of the German abstract.)
- Its ranking of the perceptual cues: "The most powerful parameter of gender perception is the fundamental frequency. Besides the vocal pitch, there are other parameters influencing gender perception of a voice, e. g. intonation, prosody or formant frequencies."
- Sample and instruments: 21 male-to-female transgender persons after surgical elevation of the vocal pitch, assessed with the Voice Handicap Index (VHI), the Life Satisfaction Questionnaire (FLZ) and "a new addendum"; from these "a new algorithm for voice feminization in male-to-female transsexualism was deduced" (Material und Methoden).
- The result that drives the algorithm: "After elevation of the vocal pitch, the self-confidence of the male-to-female transgender persons has increased. Despite of an elevated pitch some persons were not satisfied with their voice."
- Its conclusion is a division of labour between the two interventions: "Surgical intervention changes only the pitch of a voice. To change other parameters, conservative voice therapy is necessary. If the transgender persons are able to reach a satisfying female voice with conservative voice therapy alone, surgical intervention is not indicated."
- Setting: Klinik und Poliklinik für Hals-, Nasen- und Ohrenkrankheiten, plastische und ästhetische Operationen, Universitätsklinik Würzburg.

## Bearing on voice training
- The German-language statement of the rule that governs the whole training pathway: surgery moves pitch and nothing else, so a satisfying outcome requires therapy for the rest, and therapy that already succeeds makes surgery unnecessary ([[voice-feminization-therapy]], [[voice-surgery]]).
- The satisfaction finding is the reason the pathway is arranged this way rather than by technique ranking: a raised F0 did not by itself produce satisfied patients, and the authors responded with an assessment-driven algorithm built on VHI and life-satisfaction scores — a patient-reported outcome used to route treatment, not only to measure it ([[patient-reported-outcomes]]).
- It is the German-language companion to the same Würzburg cohort reported in English in [[meister2017a]] (21 patients, same instruments, same period): read together, the two give the group's own German framing of the algorithm and the English outcome data.
- Its cue list (F0 first, then intonation, prosody, formants) is the ordering the meta-analytic evidence later quantifies ([[leung2018]]); the "formants" item is the one the telephone-test follow-up measured and found lagging behind pitch [[meister2017]].
