## Bearing on voice training
- 小嗓 is Chinese opera vocabulary: the traditional-theatre term for the high, falsetto-derived register used by young-male (小生) and female role types — bilibili's singing ecosystem also uses it for mixed voice (强混声). The 伪声 subculture borrows it to name its target register, which links directly to [[vocal-registers]].
- "全程女声" (taught while speaking in the female voice the whole time) is the genre's demonstration ethic — the teacher audibly performs the endpoint.
