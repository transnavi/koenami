# Human voice (English Wikipedia)

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17: "The human voice consists of sound made by a human being using the vocal tract, including talking, singing, laughing, crying, screaming, shouting, humming or yelling. The human voice is specifically a part of human sound production in which the vocal folds are the primary sound source. Other sound production mechanisms produced from the same general area of the body involve the production of unvoiced consonants, clicks, whistling and whispering."
- The definition draws a line this vault relies on: "voice" proper means the vocal folds are the sound source. Whispering, whistling and voiceless consonants come out of the same anatomy but are not voice in that narrow sense.
