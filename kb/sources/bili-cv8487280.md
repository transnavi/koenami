# 初学伪音你需要避免踩的坑（女生篇）（幻音学院）

Published by the teaching organisation whose channel is at [[bili-huanjinxueyuan]], this is the dataset's most direct harm list from inside the 伪声 market.

## What it says
- The article names three 坑 (pits), each headed with the same typo'd 一、:
  - 压音坑 — 压着嗓子讲话 ("speaking with the voice pressed down"). The stated consequences are progressive: short term 嗓子会疼; long term 我们的声音就开始变的低沉，变得沙哑，甚至讲话讲的略久就会开始各种不舒服，拼命的清嗓子，总是觉得嗓子里有异物感，而这个异物感，就是我们嗓子不舒服的一个标志 ("the voice becomes low and hoarse, speaking for a while brings discomfort and constant throat-clearing, and there is always a foreign-body sensation in the throat — and that sensation is the sign of an unhappy throat"), and 久而久之我们就习惯了压嗓子说话，这个异物感就会越来越严重.
  - 端腔坑 — 端腔 is described as 用一个不正规的口腔方法讲话 ("speaking with an abnormal oral method"), which is what makes the popular 端腔御姐音 sound wrong in ordinary conversation; 时间长了就会习惯性端腔，不会自然的讲话, and the advice is 建议小可爱们不要学习哦.
  - 盲目女伪男 — blind female-to-male work at the higher male lines is presented as damaging: 女伪男尤其是那些少年之后的声音是会毁嗓子的 ("female-to-male, especially the voices past 少年, ruins the throat"), with the consequences listed as 说话发不大声，声哑，咽喉痛，吞烟困难 ("cannot speak loudly, hoarseness, sore throat, difficulty swallowing") and, in serious cases, surgery. The article points the reader to 网上 for examples rather than to any study.
- The advice section is ordinary vocal hygiene — 用科学发声方法来说话, a light diet without chilli, 清热解毒 foods (绿豆, 百合, 莲子, 雪梨), 冰糖雪梨 for the throat, 胖大海 and 甘草 in water, warm water, and 金嗓子 when nothing else is available.
- The article closes with the organisation's recruitment block: a WeChat public account, a Zhihu account, a Weibo account, free public classes twice weekly in a QQ group that also streams to a Bilibili room, and eleven topic-specific QQ groups (伪音, 音色, 声控, 配音, 戏腔, 声乐, Beatbox).

## Bearing on voice training
- The 压音 description is the community's own account of the injury risk [[bili-weiyinjiaolianershu]] states in a video title, and its marker — a persistent 异物感 with hoarseness and throat-clearing after sustained use — is the cluster the vault's [[vocal-fatigue]] and [[voice-health]] notes describe as the consequence of sustained excessive laryngeal effort. It is community observation, not a clinical finding, and no examination or follow-up is reported.
- 端腔 as a named trap is otherwise unattested in this dataset; it describes a stylised oral posture that reads as affected in ordinary speech, which is the everyday version of the generalisation problem [[bili-xiaoya]] addresses in the opposite direction (a voice that holds through conversation rather than only in a demo).
- The 女伪男 list — 少年音 and lower, hoarseness, pain, difficulty swallowing, surgery — is the strongest harm claim in the corpus and it comes from a body that also sells 伪音 teaching. It agrees with the general shape of the pitch-lowering literature for trans men (sustained low-pitched phonation is effortful and the durable change comes from testosterone rather than from pressing, [[voice-masculinization]]) but it states no mechanism.
