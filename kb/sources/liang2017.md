# Voice therapy for mutational falsetto — vocal aerodynamics (Liang FY et al. 2017)

## What it says
- Retrospective study of 26 mutational-falsetto (男声女调-type) patients at Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hospital, Guangzhou, October 2010–May 2014 (abstract, PubMed, 2026-09-17); control group comparison; assessments were VHI-10, F0 and aerodynamics (subglottic pressure, aerodynamic power, mean expiratory airflow, maximum phonation time).
- Before therapy the patients' mean F0, aerodynamic power and subglottic pressure were significantly higher than controls and maximum phonation time significantly shorter; after voice therapy the measures moved toward normal (the abstract reports significant improvement; exact post values not quoted here).
- Authors span Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hospital (Guangzhou), the Chinese University of Hong Kong, and hospitals in Shandong, Guangdong and Henan — a mainland–HK clinical network working on the high-pitched male voice.

## Bearing on voice training
- Chinese clinical evidence that behavioural therapy alone can lower a persistently high male F0 — the same direction of travel (pitch change under training, with aerodynamic normalization) that trans-feminine therapy assumes, and the therapy-side counterpart to the same group's relaxation-laryngoplasty literature.
- The aerodynamic pattern (high subglottal pressure, short MPT in the falsetto voice) gives [[breathing-and-support]] a Chinese-population data point: the strained high voice is an inefficient aerodynamic configuration.

## Limits
- Retrospective, uncontrolled before/after in 26 patients with mutational falsetto — cisgender men, not trans women; generalization to feminization training is by analogy only (the vault's trans-specific outcomes remain [[leyns2021]], [[leyns2023]], [[sodersten2026]]).
- Abstract-level reading; the specific therapy techniques used are not named in the abstract.
