# Morphological Structure of the Vocal Cord as a Vibrator and its Variations

## What it says
- The record itself: a six-page article in Folia Phoniatrica 26(2), received 1973-02-07, accepted 1973-08-08, by Minoru Hirano of the Department of Otolaryngology, Kurume University School of Medicine (affiliation printed on the Karger article page fetched 2026-09-17).
- This is the paper in which Hirano set out the layered description of the vocal fold treated as a vibrator; the body–cover reading of that layering is described in the fetched literature as "Hirano's classic body-cover model" [[wierzbicka2015]], and the oscillation theory built on it calls itself a theory "developed on the basis of the body-cover hypothesis" [[titze1988]].
- The Karger page reports 402 Web of Science and 461 Crossref citations as of the 2026-09-17 fetch — a measure of how much of the later vocal-fold literature is downstream of it.

## Bearing on voice training
- The reason "pitch" and "weight" are separable training targets: the cover can be stretched and thinned while the body stays comparatively passive, so the same larynx can produce very different vibratory patterns.
