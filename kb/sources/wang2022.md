# 跨性别女性嗓音问卷简体中文版的引进与研制

## What it says
- Authorised simplified-Chinese translation of the Trans Woman Voice Questionnaire (TWVQ-SC), tested on 279 trans women (score 86, IQR 61–100), 128 cis women (34, 31–39) and 89 retest trans women (83, 65–97).
- Cronbach's α 0.97; item–total correlations 0.40–0.86; test–retest ICC 0.84; four factors explaining 65.12%; correlation with VHI-10 r = 0.85; trans women scored significantly higher than cis women.
- Concludes the TWVQ-SC is reliable and valid for clinical self-assessment.

## Bearing on voice training
- A validated self-report instrument for Chinese-speaking users, and a model for a Japanese one.
