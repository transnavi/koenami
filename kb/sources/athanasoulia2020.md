# Transsexualität und Transgender-Medizin – Was jeder Internist wissen sollte（Athanasoulia-Kaspar et al. 2020）

## What it says
- The diagnostic frame it uses is the ICD-10 one:  Transsexualismus (synonym: „Transsexualität", „Transidentität" bzw. „transsexuelle Geschlechtsdysphorie", TGD) liegt nach ICD-10 F64.0 vor, wenn bei einer Person der Wunsch besteht, als Angehöriger des anderen Geschlechts anerkannt zu werden (German abstract, DEFINITION). It glosses the concept in the English abstract as "the desire to live and be accepted as a member of the opposite sex, usually accompanied by a sense of discomfort with, or inappropriateness of, one's anatomic sex".
- The prevalence figure it prints: „Die Prävalenz der TGD wird auf 0,6 % geschätzt" (DEFINITION/PRÄVALENZ), with the pathogenetic mechanisms described as not adequately understood.
- The treatment list is where voice appears: „Die Therapie ist eine interdisziplinäre Herausforderung von 6 Fachdisziplinen" — psychotherapeutic care, endocrinological hormone treatment, surgical gender-affirming operations and chest construction, «die logopädische Behandlung inkl. Stimmtherapie», «die HNO-Mitbetreuung mit Phonochirurgie» and dermatological treatment of alopecia/acne or hirsutism (THERAPIE).
- The diagnostic gate it describes: the diagnosis is made by a psychologist or psychiatrist familiar with the field, "after exclusion of other differential diagnoses such as transvestism, borderline personality disorder, multiple personality disorder or a psychotic disorder."
- On hormones it adds only that hormonal therapy "is a very important element of the treatment process" and, "due to the complexity of transsexualism... should be undertaken by endocrinologists with experience and expertise in this field."
- Affiliations: Medicover Neuroendokrinologie München, Medicover Berlin-Mitte, and Medizinische Klinik und Poliklinik IV, LMU München.

## Bearing on voice training
- The German internists' map of the pathway, and the reason the vault's German clinic and association sources find an audience: it puts «logopädische Behandlung inkl. Stimmtherapie» and «HNO-Mitbetreuung mit Phonochirurgie» in the same six-discipline list, one item for the training and a separate item for the surgery ([[transgender-care-context]], [[voice-feminization-therapy]]).
- Its prevalence estimate (0.6%) and its diagnostic gate are the framing a patient meets before arriving at a voice clinic; both differ from what the community sources assume about how people come to voice work ([[community-training]]).
- Records that this German clinical literature was still writing under ICD-10 F64.0 in 2020, four years before ICD-11's gender incongruence chapter was in force.
