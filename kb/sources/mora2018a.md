## What it says
- Translation, back-translation, pretesting and preliminary psychometric study of the authorised Spanish version of Dacakis and Davies' Transsexual Voice Questionnaire (TVQ MtF), carried out by the Unidad de Identidad de Género and the Voice Unit of Hospital Universitario Ramón y Cajal, Madrid — a tertiary referral centre for surgical voice feminisation (abstract, "Setting").
- Thirty trans women completed the questionnaire; 18 of the 30 repeated it about two weeks later.
- Feasibility 100%; Cronbach's α = 0.976; intraclass correlation coefficient = 0.885 (abstract, "Results").
- Correlation with the SF-12 v2 was weak and non-significant — 0.102 with the physical component (P = 0.592), −0.263 with the mental component (P = 0.161). No ceiling effect; a floor effect in 2 of 30 questionnaires (6.7%).
- Concludes that "feasibility, internal consistency, and reliability outcomes in our study support the validity of the authorized Spanish version of the TVQ MtF".

## Bearing on voice training
- This is the Spanish-language counterpart of [[dacakis2013]] and of the German [[salm2020]], Italian [[robotti2022]], Brazilian Portuguese [[schwarz2017]] and Turkish [[taskin2022]] versions; scores from Spanish clinics are only comparable through it.
- The weak correlation with a generic health questionnaire (SF-12) is the same pattern reported in other translations: voice-related distress is not captured by general quality-of-life instruments.

## Notes
- n = 30 for the full sample, 18 for test–retest; recruited in one Madrid hospital, so mainly peninsular Spanish, and skewed toward people already seeking feminisation surgery.
- Construct validity rests on correlation with SF-12 only; no acoustic or listener measures in this study.
- Key collides with [[mora2018]] (the Laryngoscope CTA-versus-glottoplasty study by the same Madrid group), hence the `a` suffix.
