# The Voice Book for Trans and Non-Binary People: A Practical Guide to Creating and Sustaining Authentic Voice and Communication

## What it says
- A clinician-written guide addressed to clients rather than to clinicians: Matthew Mills and Gillie Stoneham, both UK speech and language therapists, on creating and sustaining a voice that matches gender identity (repository record: title, authors, publisher Jessica Kingsley Publishers, 2017).
- Covers the whole gender-diverse readership in its title and scope — trans women, trans men and non-binary people — at a time when the clinical literature was still almost entirely transfeminine ([[nonbinary-voice]]).

## Bearing on voice training
- The client-side companion to [[adler2019]]: where the textbook briefs the clinician, this book puts exercises and explanations in the reader's own hands, which is the shape much community practice later took ([[community-training]]).
