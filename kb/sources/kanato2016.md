# 歌声の印象評価尺度の構築に基づく多様な印象の自動推定手法

## What it says
- From 47 impression words for singing voices, listening experiments and factor analysis yielded three factors — 迫力性 (power), 丁寧さ (politeness/refinement) and 明るさ (brightness) — as summarised on Koenami's method page.

## Bearing on voice training
- Source of the 明るさ and 上品さ scales in Koenami's listening review; singing-voice data, so transfer to speech is an assumption.
