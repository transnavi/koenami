## What it says
- Cross-sectional online survey validating the German translation of the Transsexual Voice Questionnaire for Male-to-Female Transsexuals (TVQMtF) against a generic voice-related QoL measure (Voice Handicap Index) and transition-related items; 127 trans women analyzed.
- Excellent internal consistency (Cronbach's α = 0.97) and split-half reliability (r_SB = 0.95).
- Good convergent validity: significant association between German TVQMtF total scores and Voice Handicap Index scores (r = 0.88, P < 0.001) and with vocal self-perception (r = −0.57, P < 0.001).
- Confirmatory factor analysis and model modification found an acceptable two-factor structure retaining 22 of the original 30 items.
- Concludes the German TVQMtF is the first reliable and valid German-language measure of voice-related quality of life for trans women, recommended for clinical and research use in voice therapy and surgery.

## Bearing on voice training
- Supports cross-linguistic use of the TVQ(MtF) family of instruments in German-speaking clinical/research settings; the strong TVQMtF–VHI correlation (r=0.88) cross-validates the two most commonly used PROMs in this literature against each other.
- The two-factor, 22-item modified structure is directly relevant if the KB later documents instrument scoring/structure differences across language versions.

## Notes
- n=127, online self-selected survey (no clinician-administered verification), single country (Germany); cross-sectional, no test-retest reliability reported for this translation.
