## What it says
- Published in Spanish and English in the journal of the Sociedad Española de Otorrinolaringología y Cirugía de Cabeza y Cuello; work done across Hospital Quirónsalud Marbella, Hospital Universitario Ramón y Cajal (Madrid), the Unidad de Identidad de Género de la Comunidad de Madrid, Hospital Central de Asturias and the Universidad de Oviedo.
- 51 trans women who had voice feminisation surgery between January 2017 and December 2018 completed the long Spanish TVQ MtF (SvTVQMtF, see [[mora2018a]]) before and after surgery; the 10 items with the largest pre–post change were selected by expert-panel consensus to form the short SvTVQMtF-10.
- Correlation between the two questionnaires exceeded a Pearson coefficient of .90; each SvTVQMtF-10 item correlated significantly with its total score; Cronbach's α = .79 (abstract, "Results").
- A significant negative correlation was found between SvTVQMtF score and fundamental frequency after surgery — the higher the postoperative F0, the lower (better) the reported handicap.
- Concludes the SvTVQMtF-10 "is a valid short version… and can be used to quantify voice-related quality of life in MtF transsexuals".

## Bearing on voice training
- A 10-item Spanish instrument is short enough for repeated use during a training programme, where the 30-item form is usually administered only at entry and discharge.
- Supplies the direct link between a Spanish patient-reported score and [[fundamental-frequency]], in a surgical rather than behavioural cohort.

## Notes
- The short form was derived from the same 51 patients used to validate it, so its item selection and its psychometrics are not independent; no external validation sample.
- Surgical population only; α = .79 is acceptable but markedly lower than the .976 of the full Spanish form.
- The terminology of the paper ("transexuales", "transexualismo") is that of 2018–2020 Spanish clinical writing.
