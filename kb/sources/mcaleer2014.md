# How do you say 'hello'? Personality impressions from brief novel voices

## What it says
- 320 participants rated 64 sub-second utterances of "hello" on ten personality traits.
- Judgements were consistent across listeners; a two-dimensional space of Valence (trust, likeability) and Dominance summarises ratings for male and female voices.
- Perceived female vocal attractiveness is largely controlled by valence; male attractiveness by valence and dominance together.

## Bearing on voice training
- Basis of the 温かさ (warmth) and 張り (dominance-like) scales in Koenami's listening review.
