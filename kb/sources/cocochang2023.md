## Bearing on voice training
- The Korean-language version of the community course structure the vault holds in English ([[community-training]], [[sumi-getting-started]]): a curriculum announced up front, a stated difference from other teaching, and a goal-setting step before any exercise.
- It is 여목 material — the Korean analogue of 両声類 and Japanese 女声 practice, made for anyone who wants a female voice rather than specifically for trans women ([[korean-voice]], [[kitahara2017]], [[wikipedia-ja-ryouseirui]]) — and it is the genre a Korean reader meets before any clinical service.
- It is also evidence of the order the honest version of the vault's tutorial notes: information circulates first through YouTube in Korean, and the clinical route is comparatively hard to find ([[yun2024]], [[transgender-care-context]]).
