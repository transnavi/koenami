## Bearing on voice training
- 小嗓 in Peking opera belongs to 小生 (young men) and 旦 (female roles) alike, which is why it can be borrowed to name a cross-register target without implying a gender change: the register is already used by male singers for male characters. That is the fact the 伪声 borrowing hides, and this video is the 小生 side of it, against the 旦 side at [[bili-hanwen2022]] and the classroom version at [[bili-jingjupan]].
- The vault's [[vocal-registers]] note describes the register distinctions (modal, falsetto, mixed) that the opera term 假声 loosely names; the 小嗓 label is theatre vocabulary applied to those registers, not a separate mechanism.
