# Transgender health care (English Wikipedia)

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17: "Transgender health care includes the prevention, diagnosis and treatment of physical and mental health conditions which affect transgender individuals. A major component of transgender health care is gender-affirming care, the medical aspect of gender transition."
- The summary lists the questions the field implicates: "gender variance, sex reassignment therapy, health risks, and access to healthcare for trans people in different countries around the world", and notes that gender-affirming care "can include psychological, medical, physical, and social behavioral care" (summary extract).
