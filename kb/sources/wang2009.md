# Relaxation laryngoplasty in the management of mutational falsetto（王LP等 2009）

## What it says
- Chinese-language series from 盛京医院 (Shengjing Hospital, China Medical University, Shenyang): 30 adult mutational-falsetto patients who had failed voice-modification training, treated with thyroplasty type 3 (松弛性喉成形术/relaxation laryngoplasty) in its classic form plus two modifications (group 1: thyroid-cartilage strip cut and overlapped without suture; group 2: strip not cut, folded inward without suture) (abstract, PubMed, 2026-09-17).
- Results: mean F0 fell from 276.5±46.7 Hz preoperatively to 127.5±32.1 Hz at one month (P < 0.001); normalized noise energy worsened slightly but significantly (−10.9±4.9 dB to −7.7±4.1 dB, i.e. noisier voice); improved group 1 dropped F0 most; improved group 2 had the smallest NNE increase.
- Reading-essay comparison was also reported as improved (abstract wording).

## Bearing on voice training
- The mainland's own type-III thyroplasty lineage — pitch lowering by shortening/tension-releasing the fold-bearing cartilage — in Chinese, a decade before the same journal family published the pitch-raising series for trans women ([[zhang2023cn]], [[sun2025]]); it anchors [[voice-surgery]]'s bilateral (raise/lower) technique set in the Chinese literature.
- The F0 trajectory (276→128 Hz) and the noise-energy trade-off illustrate the surgery rule both communities state: pitch moves, quality pays ([[mtfwiki-vocal-surgery]]: 响度会有不同程度的损失).
- Its patients failed training first — an early Chinese instance of the train-before-surgery ordering.

## Limits
- Retrospective, 30 patients, cisgender men with mutational falsetto; follow-up reported to one month in the abstract; graded medium as a peer-reviewed case series. Abstract read via PubMed's English rendering of the Chinese article; the full Chinese text was not fetched.
