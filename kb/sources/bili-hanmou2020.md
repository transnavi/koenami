- Character-copying is the genre's standard way of specifying a 声线: 瑶's 少女音 here, 嫦娥's voice [[bili-yizhiyaoyan]], 长离's 御姐音 [[bili-tonghuaqiuye]], 熊二 [[bili-jiangfeng2017]].
