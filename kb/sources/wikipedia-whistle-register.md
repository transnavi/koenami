# Whistle register (English Wikipedia)

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17: "The whistle register is the highest register of the human voice, lying above the modal register and falsetto register. This register has a specific physiological production that is different from the other registers and is so called because the timbre of the notes that are produced from this register is similar to that of a whistle."
- Placed above falsetto, with a production the article calls "specific" and "different".

## Bearing on voice training
- M3 in the laryngeal-mechanism model ([[roubeau2009]]). [[herbst2020]] reports Echternach high-speed endoscopy of C6–G6 showing full glottal closure, and argues whistle may not be "fundamentally different from the falsetto register as far as fundamental laryngeal mechanics are concerned" — a disagreement with this article's "specific physiological production that is different".
