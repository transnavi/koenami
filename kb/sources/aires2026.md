# Glottoplasty for vocal feminization: position paper of the Brazilian Academy of Laryngology and Voice (ABLV)

## What it says
- Ten otolaryngologists from distinct Brazilian academic institutions, all experienced in transgender voice surgery, produced a position paper using a modified Delphi method supported by a narrative literature review, with recommendations classified by Oxford Centre for Evidence-Based Medicine levels (abstract, "Methods").
- Indication statement: "Glottoplasty is indicated for transgender women experiencing vocal gender incongruence, regardless of their baseline fundamental frequency." Absolute and relative contraindications are defined.
- Recommended preoperative assessment: "videolaryngoscopy, acoustic voice analysis, and validated quality-of-life questionnaires".
- Perioperative management: "voice rest, individualized medication protocols, and post-operative voice therapy".
- Honest evidence note in the paper itself: "Most recommendations are based on level IV and V evidence, reflecting the current paucity of prospective studies."

## Bearing on voice training
- A national body's clinical position that surgery is not gated on baseline pitch and that postoperative voice therapy is part of the protocol — the clinical counterweight to the framing where surgery is the last resort after training [[clinical-guidelines]] [[voice-surgery]].
- Cite it for what it is: expert consensus at the lowest evidence levels [[transgender-care-context]] [[aires2023]].

## Notes
- This is a consensus statement, not outcome data; the recommendations are the opinion of ten surgeons. Portuguese-language professional society, published in English in the Brazilian Journal of Otorhinolaryngology. The abstract does not list the absolute and relative contraindications themselves.
- Claims taken from the PubMed abstract.
