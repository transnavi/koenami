# A voz da diversidade: a Fonoaudiologia no mês do orgulho LGBTQIAPN+

An editorial in a Brazilian speech-language-pathology journal, published in Portuguese and English. No abstract is deposited with the record, so this note reports only what the citation itself establishes.

## What it says
- The journal Revista CEFAC published this for LGBTQIAPN+ pride month 2024 in two language versions, this one and 「The voice of diversity: Speech-Language Pathology in LGBTQIAPN+ pride month」 (10.1590/1982-0216/20242665324); three speech-language pathologists at UNESP and UFES are the authors (Crossref records, 2026-09-17).
- Its subject is the profession's relation to LGBTQIAPN+ populations, set out as an editorial rather than a study; neither language version's record carries an abstract.

## Bearing on voice training
- Its value here is as a marker of the field's own framing: Brazil's largest speech-language-pathology journal names LGBTQIAPN+ voice as a professional concern in 2024, in the same years the clinical literature in [[cielo2021]], [[sosthenes2025]] and [[rosa2026]] is arguing that access is the bottleneck ([[speech-language-pathology]], [[transgender-care-context]]).
