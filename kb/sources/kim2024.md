# Update of Vocal Fold Shortening With Retrodisplacement of Anterior Commissure for Voice Feminization

## What it says
- Yeson Voice Center follow-up to [[kim2017]]: retrospective analysis of 506 patients drawn from a cohort of 1,025 MtF trans women who underwent VFSRAC between 2003 and 2021; the Type II update (a suture-technique modification) covers cases from 2015–2021, compared against the 2003–2014 Type I method (abstract).
- Type II preoperative mean speech F0 was 134.5 Hz; postoperatively 196.7 Hz at 3 months, 212.3 Hz at 6 months, and 207.5 Hz beyond 1 year — "exceeding outcomes observed with the Type I method" (abstract).
- Postoperative subjective and objective assessments showed augmented voice femininity; acoustic/aerodynamic indices within the normal range; mucosal-wave regularity preserved (abstract).
- Conclusion: the updated Type II VFSRAC "has demonstrated feasibility and consistently yielded favorable results for individuals desiring a naturally feminine voice"; level of evidence 3 (abstract).

## Bearing on voice training
- The largest updated Korean series; together with [[kim2017]] it documents the durability claim (beyond 1 year) that the clinic's public pages repeat ([[yeson-voice-center]]).
- The 6-months-versus-1-year dip pattern (212.3 → 207.5 Hz) is one of the few places where a VFSRAC series shows its own long-term numbers rather than a 6-month peak.

## Notes
- Retrospective, single center, surgeon-reported; "drawn from a cohort of 1025" — the selection rule for the 506 analysed is not visible in the abstract.
