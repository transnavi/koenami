## What it says
- Notes that in Japan, type IV thyroplasty has been the predominant voice-feminization surgery for transgender women, while anterior glottoplasty (声帯短縮術, vocal-fold shortening) has not been widely practiced.
- Reports pre/post voice changes for anterior glottoplasty at the authors' institution and compares them against type IV thyroplasty performed at the same institution.
- Both procedures achieved a postoperative voice frequency >200 Hz, described as within the feminine range.
- Concludes each technique carries its own advantages/disadvantages and procedure choice should be individualized.

## Bearing on voice training
- Corroborates that surgical pitch-raising (whichever technique) can reliably push speaking F0 above ~200 Hz, useful as a benchmark when comparing surgical vs. behavioral (voice-therapy-only) pitch targets.
- Reinforces that no single feminization procedure is uniformly superior — patient-specific tradeoffs matter, which is relevant when a KB user is weighing behavioral training against surgery.

## Notes
- Single-institution Japanese comparison; abstract does not give patient counts or exact F0 means/SDs — only that both exceeded 200 Hz.
- Population: MTF/GID surgical patients, Japan.
