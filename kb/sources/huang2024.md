# Acoustic analysis of Mandarin-speaking transgender women

## What it says
- 15 trans women, 20 cis men and 20 cis women in Shanghai; formants F1–F4, CPP, jitter, shimmer, HNR, NHR, f0 and intensity across vowels, passages and free talking, plus VHI-10 and the Voice Fatigue Index.
- Trans women's F1 was higher than cis women's for /i/ and /u/ and higher than cis men's for /a/, /i/, /u/; F2 and F3/F4 patterns differed by vowel; f0 of trans women was lower than cis women's in vowels, passage and free talk.
- Trans women differed from both cis groups without surgery or training; the authors suggest a risk of vocal fatigue and voice disorders as they modify their voices.

## Bearing on voice training
- Mandarin data on which formants trans women shift on their own, and a warning about strain.
