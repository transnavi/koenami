# Effects on voice fundamental frequency and satisfaction with voice in trans men during testosterone treatment — a longitudinal study

## What it says
- 50 trans men (18–64); voice data before treatment and at 3, 6 or 12 months for 49, and at 18/24 months for 28.
- Mean and mode F0 decreased significantly by 3, 6 and up to 12 months, when group data matched male reference data; mean F0 was 125 Hz at 12 months with large inter-individual variation.
- Sound pressure level did not change. Voice satisfaction correlated with lower F0. 24% reported voice symptoms (instability, fatigue) and had received voice therapy. F0 did not correlate with androgen levels.
- Recommends systematic voice assessment during testosterone treatment.

## Bearing on voice training
- Timeline for pitch change on testosterone, and the size of the group that needs voice therapy.
