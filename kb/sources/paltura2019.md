# An Examination of Vocal Tract Acoustics following Wendler's Glottoplasty

## What it says
- Turkish study (Gaziosmanpaşa Taksim Education and Research Hospital and Üsküdar University, Istanbul): F0 and formants F1–F4 of 20 Turkish-speaking trans women six months after Wendler glottoplasty, against 20 male and 20 female age-matched controls (abstract, "METHODS").
- Median F0 after surgery was statistically similar to the female controls (abstract, "RESULTS").
- F1 of the operated speakers differed from females but resembled males; F2 resembled females; F3 and F4 "were significantly different from both male and female controls".
- The authors' reading: the technique raises F0 effectively, "however, these individuals report their voice does not sufficiently project femininity. The results obtained with regard to FF levels may be the reason for this problem", and voice therapy is recommended alongside surgery.

## Bearing on voice training
- Direct Turkish-language evidence for the vault's standing point that surgery addresses the source while the filter — formant spacing — stays masculine unless trained [[formants]] [[voice-surgery]]; the same pattern the Beijing series reports [[zhang2023cn]].
- Supports the combined surgery-plus-therapy pathway argued in [[casado2017]] and [[heydrich2023]].

## Notes
- Turkish speech material; formant values are language- and vowel-dependent, so the male/female similarity pattern may not transfer to other languages [[voice-across-languages]].
- No voice-therapy arm; the six-month timepoint may precede full stabilisation.
