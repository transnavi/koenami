# 伪音学习(四) 改变气息与说话（Bilibili 专栏）

Part four of a numbered series, written as a learner's log rather than a teacher's course, and unusually candid about the stage it is describing.

## What it says
- The series' own map: 上上篇是讲解改变声带发声频率，用专业术语就是声带闭合；上一篇是讲解改变声音传播共鸣，用专业术语就是咽腔共鸣；这一篇是讲解改变气息以及说话 ("the first part was changing the vocal-fold frequency — in technical terms, closure; the previous part was changing the resonance path — pharyngeal resonance; this part is breath and speech").
- The breath method: 感受气息通过嘴内大约最高的位置的感觉（用专业术语就是硬颚和软腭交接的地方） ("feel the air pass the highest point inside the mouth — the junction of hard and soft palate"). The instruction is deliberately loose: 注意只要找到这种感觉就好，不一定要和教程做的一模一样 ("just find the sensation; you need not copy the tutorial exactly"), and the earlier steps are demoted to aids: 其实胸式呼吸、抬软腭和抬舌根只是为了方便找到这种感觉，如果能熟练找到这种感觉就可以不用胸式呼吸，也不用抬软腭和抬舌根.
- The decomposition of speech: 男生说话=男生气息+男生发音 而女生说话=女生气息+女生发音, and the progression follows from it — 先用女声气息+男生发音发中性音，再把男生发音转换成女生发音 ("first use feminine breath with male articulation for a neutral voice, then convert the articulation"). The author states this is the hardest stage and admits being stuck in it: 个人认为这是伪音里最难的部分…我已经卡在这个阶段挺长一段时间了.
- Why single sounds are not speech: 说话不仅仅是单音节的组合，说话中那种连贯的感觉，不是单音节单音节的发音能发出来的 — the transition between syllables is the skill, not the syllables.
- The article's Q&A is the most useful part of the record. On 三分钟速成 videos: 这些教程的方法本身没有什么问题，但是这些视频的up主其实已经能熟练掌握伪音了，然后才能提出这些方法…除非感知能力很好，不然初学者很难直接用这些方法发出伪音 ("the methods are not wrong, but the uploaders could already do 伪音 before proposing them; beginners cannot use them directly"). On which Bilibili tutorials are good: 感觉姜峰老师，素哒老师，慕容云磊老师的教程比较适合学习，不过并不是他们的所有教程都适合练习，也没有必要把所有方法都练习一遍 ("Jiang Feng's, Su Da's and Murong Yunlei's tutorials suit learning, though not all their videos suit practice, and there is no need to practise every method"). On difficulty: 想要做到自然说话，长期练习是必须的.
- The article explicitly declines authority: 这些内容是我的一些理解…我认为伪音没有绝对的对错，只有适合与不适合，所以如果有些看法与我不一样，按照你自己的理解练习就好.

## Bearing on voice training
- The 气息 must pass the 硬颚和软腭交接处 target is the community's ordinary way of locating a forward, concentrated airstream; it is a different description of the same sensory target the handbook calls the 气息凝聚点 on the soft palate and warns must be small, or the palate dries [[aogi2021]]. The vault's [[breathing-and-support]] note describes supported speech breathing in terms of the pressure and flow it produces, which the community version approximates by sensation.
- The 男生气息+男生发音 → 女生气息+女生发音 decomposition is the layer's clearest framing of the generalisation problem: register, pitch and resonance can be changed in isolation on a sustained vowel, but adapting them to connected speech is a separate and harder step, which is why single-syllable demos are misleading. That is the issue [[bili-xiaoya]] makes the subject of a video and [[bili-cv9417414]] addresses with 延长音 and 一字一顿 practice.
- The demotion of 胸式呼吸/抬软腭/抬舌根 from method to scaffolding, and the explicit statement that one may drop them, is the most sensible position on those drills found in the corpus, and it contradicts the drill-first framing of [[bili-cv9623302]] while agreeing with [[bili-cv9417414]]'s insistence that the sensation matters more than the posture.
- The recommendation of 姜峰, 素哒 and 慕容云磊 is the community's own short list of what to watch, and it names two channels in this vault ([[bili-jiangfeng]], [[bili-muronyunlei]]) as suitable sources — a useful record of which creators the layer itself trusted in 2020.
