# Octave (English Wikipedia)

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17: "In music, an octave (from Latin octavus 'eighth') or perfect octave (sometimes called the diapason) is an interval between two tones, one having twice the frequency of vibration of the other. For instance, the interval between C4 and C5 (in scientific pitch notation) is an octave."
- Short description: "Interval between one musical pitch and another with double its frequency."

## Bearing on voice training
- The physical fact behind the log scale: doubling hertz is one octave everywhere (100→200 Hz and 200→400 Hz are both one octave), so a "one-octave drop" at puberty is a *ratio*, not a fixed number of hertz. Wikipedia's [[wikipedia-voice-change]] uses that wording of the male change; this page is what the wording means.
