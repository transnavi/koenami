# MTF声音女性化练习手册（あおぎ葵, 2021）

A long-form Chinese practice handbook for MTF (trans-feminine) voice feminization, posted to Bilibili as an opus on 2021-07-12 by the uploader あおぎ葵, with a PDF mirror distributed via a Baidu Pan link (提取码 j7uq) printed at the top of the post. It is the handbook cited on Koenami's method page and referenced in [[community-training]]. A companion video, MTF声音女性化练习 (BV14zGH6uEdV, 2026-05-24), carries the note 原手册详见21年发布文章，新手册待上线 ("see the 2021 article for the original handbook; new handbook pending").

## What it says
- 写在前面 (foreword): the author frames voice feminization as learnable science, not talent — 声音女性化是完全可以用科学、准确的语言进行解释的 — built on two pillars, 语音声学 (voice acoustics) and 声乐技术 (vocal technique), and warns against skipping the knowledge framework to jump straight into drills.
- The foreword explicitly critiques the 伪声 entertainment-tutorial route that many MTF learners default to: 很多MTF选择参考"伪声"等侧重娱乐性的教学视频…而往往因为这类视频中模糊的表述和单一的训练方式，始终不得其门而入 ("many MTF turn to entertainment-oriented 伪声 videos, and because of their vague wording and single training method never get through the door"). This is the handbook's reason for existing.
- 背景知识 teaches vocal-tract anatomy in Chinese with English glosses — 声带 (vocal folds), 咽部 (pharynx), 会厌 (epiglottis), 喉部 (larynx), 软腭 (velum), 舌头 (tongue), 舌骨 (hyoid bone), 甲状软骨/喉结 (thyroid cartilage), 环状软骨 (cricoid) — and the laryngeal muscles: 喉外肌群 split into 舌骨上肌群 (suprahyoid, raise the larynx) and 舌骨下肌群 (infrahyoid, lower it), plus 喉内肌 甲杓肌 (thyroarytenoid), 环杓侧肌 (lateral cricoarytenoid), 环杓后肌 (posterior cricoarytenoid), 杓间横肌 (transverse arytenoid).
- It teaches 源-滤波器理论 (source–filter theory) and 共振/共振峰 (resonance/formant), citing Jeans (1938), Fant (1960), Benade (1976) and the American Acoustical Society (1994) definitions, and works the 17 cm半开管 model (adult-male vocal tract) giving resonances at 500/1500/2500 Hz. It explains spectrograms (时变频谱图) and equates 基频 F0 with 音高 (pitch).
- 声音的性别感知: vowel F1 通常在200–800 Hz, F2 约为800–2000 Hz; F1 tracks jaw opening, F2 tracks 喉位 (larynx height) most. It states male F0 85–155 Hz vs female 165–255 Hz and calls high F0 声音被感知为女性的先决条件 ("the prerequisite for a voice being perceived as female").
- Ranking of cues: F0 is 先决条件/基石 (prerequisite/cornerstone); F2 is 声音女性化的重要特征 (the important feature), because a high larynx shortens the male tract (平均16.9 cm) toward the female tract (平均14.1 cm); F1 is 次要特征 (a secondary feature). The author records a dissent from the popular foreign "gender knob" framing: 笔者对此持保留意见, arguing those tutorials 混淆了声学意义上的共振和解剖学意义上声道腔体的区别 and that R1/F1 is driven by jaw opening and tongue position, not larynx height (which drives R2/F2).
- Non-resonance cues it lists: more 声音调制 (F0-SD, intonation range) as typically female, plus higher 谐波噪声比 (HNR, breathiness) and lower 抖动 (jitter), citing Suire et al. 2020 and Van Borsel et al. 2009.
- 使用praat辅助声音练习: it recommends Praat (https://www.fon.hum.uva.nl/praat/) for viewing spectrogram, pitch, intensity and formant tracks, and cautions that the software's computed tracks are approximations (可能存在失准情况). See [[praat]].
- Drills, organized by target: raise F0 (爬音阶 → find the breaking point/破音点 → settle slightly below as the female F0, 具有女性特征的音高通常在180Hz以上); raise F2 via 高喉位 (吞咽训练, 前吞咽训练, "wow"训练, EEE/Errr训练, 大狗小狗训练, 伸舌训练), then vowel drills, then 喉腔开放和放松 (哈欠练习, 放松下巴, 放松声带); raise F1 via 张口训练, 咧嘴训练, 舌位训练; and 修饰性因素 — 改善气流 (breath/HNR), 增加声调起伏 (intonation), plus reducing jitter and vocal-fold thickness as advanced, and Twang技巧 (国内一般译为咽音) for laryngeal health.
- On the community worry 伪声毁嗓 ("fake voice ruins the throat"), the handbook takes a specific position: high larynx itself is not necessarily damaging — 损伤是因为高喉位通常自然伴随着narrow throat或者说throat tightness的现象 — and correct practice that keeps 喉腔开放 (open throat) at a high larynx avoids injury; it nonetheless warns against 闭气吞咽 (breath-holding swallow) training and stresses pairing high-larynx work with open-throat/relaxation work.

## Bearing on voice training
- A Chinese-language bridge between the 伪声 subculture and clinical voice science: it re-explains resonance, F0, F2 and breathiness in the same acoustic terms the vault's concept notes use ([[fundamental-frequency]], [[formants]], [[voice-quality]], [[intonation]]), and it directly names where it parts company with entertainment 伪声 teaching.
- Its cue ranking (F0 prerequisite, F2 important, F1 secondary, plus intonation/HNR) and its tract-length figures (16.9→14.1 cm) parallel the cross-language findings in [[huang2024]] and the meta-analytic weighting in [[leung2018]]; its dissent from the "gender knob = F1" framing is a useful recorded disagreement.
- The 伪声毁嗓 discussion is the community's own harm-reduction argument and a documented instance of the vocal-health debate ([[voice-health]], [[vocal-fatigue]]).
