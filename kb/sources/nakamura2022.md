# 男性から女性型性同一性障害症例の話声位とQOLを上昇させる甲状軟骨形成術4型

The same author's summary of type-4 thyroplasty for the panel on voice in GI care, and the one Japanese surgical paper in this group whose stated outcome is quality of life rather than pitch change alone.

## What it says
- Framing: 「Male to female gender identity disorder (MTF/GID) is a rare condition. Such patients tend to complain that their speaking fundamental frequency (SFF) does not fit because it is too low.」 The complaint named is fit, not absolute pitch — the same distinction the Japanese sources use elsewhere ([[sakuraba2009]], [[yomedanchii-voice]]).
- The operation: 「Type 4 thyroplasty (TP4) conversely increases the pitch of the SFF as the tension of the vocal fold is tightened causing the SFF to become high-pitched.」
- Its stated justification, which is the paper's title claim: 「While vocal pitch disorder is not life-threatening for patients with MTF/GID, it can cause substantial worry and stress. TP4 can therefore improve the quality of life of patients with MTF/GID.」
- Keywords: 甲状軟骨形成術, 性同一性障害, 性別違和, 話声位, 音声外科手術. Published 2022-12-01 in the same issue as [[matsumoto2022]], [[nimura2022]] and [[kobayashi2022]].

## Bearing on voice training
- The Japanese claim that the operation's value is QOL, not centihertz, is the same move as the rehabilitation paper beside it — surgery as one part of care rather than the endpoint ([[kobayashi2022]]). The vault's surgery-and-therapy comparison literature is non-Japanese ([[casado2017]], [[park2024]], [[aires2023]]).
- 話声位 is the quantity named in the title; for what Japanese listeners accept as female at that quantity, see [[sakuraba2009]].

## Limits
- Low-grade: conference paper, English abstract only; the Japanese full text is behind J-STAGE authentication and was not read. No case numbers, follow-up interval or QOL instrument appears in the abstract, so the title's QOL claim cannot be quantified from it here.
