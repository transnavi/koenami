# Wendler's Glottoplasty in Italian Transgender Women: A Retrospective Cohort Study on Efficacy and Safety（Scarpa et al. 2025）

## What it says
- Cohort: 10 Italian transgender women aged 25–40 who underwent Wendler's glottoplasty at the University of Salerno between January 2020 and December 2022; retrospective observational design (abstract, Methods).
- Assessment battery: "acoustic analysis, laryngostroboscopy, and validated questionnaires such as the Italian versions of the Transsexual Voice Questionnaire for Male-to-Female Transsexuals (I-TVQMtF) and the Voice Handicap Index (I-VHI)", analysed with paired t-tests and effect sizes. Note that both instruments are Italian translations — the trans-specific one is the questionnaire first validated in Italian by Robotti and colleagues.
- Pitch: mean F0 rose "from 151.4 Hz (±10.8 Hz) preoperatively to 231.6 Hz (±13.5 Hz) postoperatively (P < 0.001), achieving a range typical of cisgender women" (Results).
- Questionnaires: I-TVQMtF fell from 71.6 (±9.2) to 24.9 (±5.1) and I-VHI from 59.2 (±8.9) to 19.8 (±5.4) (P < 0.001); perceptual femininity ratings on a visual analogue scale improved from 4.4 (±0.3) to 1.9 (±0.4) (P < 0.001).
- Laryngostroboscopy and safety: "confirmed 90% glottic closure and 80% symmetrical vocal fold vibration", with "minor complications, such as transient dysphonia (10%), resolved with conservative management."
- Conclusion: Wendler's glottoplasty is "a safe and effective procedure for vocal feminization in Italian transgender women", and the authors read the results as evidence for "integrating surgical and multidisciplinary care".

## Bearing on voice training
- The Italian-language clinical counterpart to the Chinese and German glottoplasty series in this vault: a small single-centre cohort, a large F0 gain, and improvement on both a trans-specific and a generic voice questionnaire — the pattern [[zhang2023cn]] shows in Mandarin and [[meister2016]] in German.
- The two questionnaires matter for how Italian services measure: the trans-specific instrument is the I-TVQMtF validated by [[robotti2022]], and the generic one the Italian VHI. Where the Taiwanese [[wang2011tw]] and mainland [[wang2022]] work supplied the Mandarin pair and [[morsomme2019]] the French TWVQ, this is the Italian pair in one study.
- The PA-VAS femininity rating and the laryngostroboscopy numbers add the perceptual and the structural view alongside the acoustic one, which is the three-part assessment the Belgian review said was still missing [[lechien2014]].

## Notes
- Retrospective single-centre cohort of 10 patients; graded medium. No control group, no comparison against voice therapy alone, and the follow-up interval is not stated in the abstract.
- Only the PubMed abstract was read; the full text is paywalled and was not accessed.
- The study is in English and published in a US journal; it is included under the Italian heading for the Italian cohort and the Italian instruments.
