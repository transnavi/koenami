# Fonoterapia para mulheres transgênero

A narrative review of the therapeutic principles, written for Brazilian speech-language pathologists rather than as a systematic appraisal — but it states a division of labour clearly that the rest of this literature leaves fuzzy.

## What it says
- Search: PubMed and Science Direct, articles on gender dysphoria and voice from the previous 20 years, run January 2018 to September 2021, keywords "(Pessoas trans) OR (Transexualismo) OR (Transexual) OR (Identidade de Gênero)) AND ((treinamento vocal) OR (voz) OR (qualidade de voz))". 31 articles were included (abstract, "Métodos").
- The central claim: 「há necessidade de acompanhamento multiprofissional e ratifica-se a importância da fonoterapia, uma vez que o tratamento hormonal e as cirurgias laríngeas não contemplam todas as alterações desejadas na voz e na fala em mulheres transgênero」 — hormones and laryngeal surgery do not cover everything the speaker wants changed.
- What the therapist is told to work on: beyond raising f0, the speech-language pathologist 「deve estimular os maneirismos femininos, minúcias da voz e delicadezas da fala」 — feminine mannerisms, fine detail of voice, delicacy of speech.
- And a combination claim: 「Verificou-se a importância da realização de cirurgia laríngea associada à fonoterapia para modificação vocal de mulheres transgênero」 — surgery with therapy, not surgery alone.

## Bearing on voice training
- The Brazilian statement of the surgery-plus-therapy arrangement the vault carries from Spain and Belgium ([[casado2016]], [[casado2017]], [[papeleu2026]]). The division is F0 from the operation, everything else from therapy — which is precisely the source/filter split [[dornelas2021]] builds a service protocol on.
- The "maneirismos femininos" instruction is the point where clinical writing and the community guides diverge: the vault's community material treats gendered speech style as a choice the speaker makes about themselves ([[transvoice-wiki]], [[community-training]]), while this review presents it as a treatment target. Both framings are recorded here; the note takes neither.

## Notes
- Narrative review with a stated search but no reported appraisal, screening counts or quality assessment; its own authors do not call it systematic. Graded low.
- Claims taken from the Portuguese abstract; the 31 included articles were not listed in the abstract and were not read here.
