# 女声ボイトレ「進捗レポート：2026年8月29日」

The sequel to the July report, with F0 and formant numbers for each configuration and two problems the writer works on: a stiff hyoid, and not understanding "forward" and "back" voice placement.

## What it says
- After a month off — a cough reflex that made speaking trigger coughing and needed medication — the writer restarted from 「地声系ＴＡ筋の脱力」. Their tendency is 「声が重くＴＡ系に力入る」, so their teacher gave 「ふくろう」「あくび」 as relaxation cues; the writer adapted them into an exercise: 「少しアレンジして音域を下げながら『↑ホーッ↓』と繰り返し練習して、感覚をつかんだら途中で声のフォームを固定してロングトーンに持ち込み、さらにそのフォームから台詞読みにつなげる」.
- Published measurements for each configuration (F0, F1–F4 with the app or Audacity): 普通の地声 F0 = 180 Hz (F#3), F1 = 346 Hz, F2 = 825 Hz, F3 = 1425 Hz, F4 = 2236 Hz; 高めの地声 F0 = 223 Hz (A3), F1 = 348 Hz, F2 = 679 Hz, F3 = 1454 Hz, F4 = 2502 Hz; 脱力裏声 F0 = 337 Hz (E4), F1 = 869 Hz, F2 = 1434 Hz, F3 = 2240 Hz; エッジ裏声 F0 = 351 Hz (F4), F1 = 679 Hz, F2 = 1431 Hz, F3 = 3181 Hz.
- On the pressed M2 (エッジ裏声): 「この発声法では裏声系CT筋だけでなく、地声系TA筋も使ってる感じで持久力が低いです。２～３分使うと声がおかしくなります。しかし女声で声帯閉鎖（エッジを入れる）は本来、声門閉じる系のＬＣＡ筋を使うのが正しく、私はこの時点では間違った方法でエッジを入れていたようです。」 — the writer's own correction: closure should come from the lateral cricoarytenoid, not from TA.
- A self-diagnosed structural issue: 「私は普通の人と違って、舌骨と甲状軟骨が密着してる感じです、これは筋肉の過緊張の可能性があるらしいです」, and after laryngeal massage of the hyoid-thyroid area: 「喉頭内部がニョーンと伸びる感覚があって、厚く重い声帯が少しは軽くなったような気がしました、あくまでそういう感じだなので、効果のほどはこれからわかるかと思います」.
- On placement language: 「私はボイトレの指導でよく使われる『声を前に』とか『声を後ろに』とか言う、声質の定位表現（前後上下）が解かんないのです。」 The writer explains why the vocabulary fails for them — years spent alone in a room rather than using the voice socially — and concludes that training is 「一種のリハビリ」.
- Reference observation: real 女声 samples show 「F0が200～400Hzくらい振れる抑揚がつく」, i.e. the F0 range across an utterance rather than a target pitch.

## Bearing on voice training
- The published F0/F1 pairs, as measured: 普通の地声 180/346 Hz, 高めの地声 223/348 Hz, 脱力裏声 337/869 Hz, エッジ裏声 351/679 Hz; F1 does not follow F0 across them, and the configuration the writer calls brighter (エッジ裏声) has the lower F1 of the two 裏声 settings [[formants]] [[fundamental-frequency]].
- The self-correction about which muscle produces "edge" is exactly the confusion the English-language wiki warns about under glottal onsets and false-fold closure [[sumi-onsets]] [[vocal-folds]].
- The hyoid-thyroid massage and the report of a "longer, lighter" sensation afterwards is a community-level intervention with no measurement behind it — recorded as the writer's impression, which they also flag as an impression [[larynx-anatomy]] [[voice-health]].
- The complaint about "forward/back" placement language is the same complaint the wiki's terminology page makes about chasing sensations instead of sounds [[sumi-questionable-terms]] [[community-training]].
