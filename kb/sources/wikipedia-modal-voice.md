# Modal voice (English Wikipedia)

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17: "Modal voice is the vocal register used most frequently in speech and singing in most languages. It is also the linguistic term for the most common phonation of vowels. The term \"modal\" refers to the resonant mode of vocal folds. It is characterized by balanced airflow and glottal tension to produce maximum vibration."
- So "modal" names the everyday speaking register (Hollien's modal, Roubeau's M1, pedagogy's chest), not a singing style.

## Bearing on voice training
- The default speaking voice of both sexes is modal, not falsetto. A feminine speaking voice is a modal voice with a different pitch, tract and quality, not a switch into M2.
