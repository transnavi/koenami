# Soft palate (English Wikipedia)

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17: "The soft palate is, in mammals, the soft tissue constituting the back of the roof of the mouth. The soft palate is part of the palate of the mouth; the other part is the hard palate. The soft palate is distinguished from the hard palate at the front of the mouth in that it does not contain bone."
- Short description: "Flexible part of maxilla."

## Bearing on voice training
- The velum: raised, the nasal cavity is shut and vowels are oral; lowered, air enters the nose and the tract gains a side branch. Nasality in Japanese everyday voice-quality words is one listening axis ([[kido2001]]).
