# O impacto da terapia vocal na readequação da identidade de pessoas trans

## What it says
- Integrative review, PubMed and SciELO, original articles in Portuguese and English, on the impact of vocal therapy on trans people's identity readjustment (abstract, "Métodos").
- Screening outcome: 「Entre os 51 artigos sobre voz e o processo transexualizador, sete abrangeram o tema de interesse」 — of 51 articles on voice and the transexualisation process, seven addressed the question. Six discussed the speech-language pathologist's role; one did not present voice therapy as relevant, centring instead on trans women's self-perception of voice quality.
- Scope of the profession as stated: assessment, guidance, therapy (habilitation and rehabilitation) and refinement of 「a voz, a fluência e a articulação da fala」, which 「colaboram de forma significativa para a harmonização vocal no processo de transexualização」.
- Conclusion: publications on trans communication are on the rise and speech-language-pathology input is clearly relevant, 「entretanto, ainda são escassos os estudos que abordam diretamente o papel e atuação deste profissional」 — studies that address the professional's role directly are still scarce.

## Bearing on voice training
- The rarity count is the useful content: 7 of 51 Brazilian and international articles on voice in the transexualisation process actually ask what voice therapy does for identity ([[voice-therapy-techniques]], [[speech-language-pathology]]). That scarcity is the reason this vault's Brazilian block is mostly cross-sectional description rather than outcome evidence.
- "Harmonização vocal" is used here as the umbrella term for the whole professional remit, the same sense it carries in [[godoy2026]] and [[brasil2026]].

## Notes
- Integrative review in a broad-scope journal; no quality appraisal of the seven included articles is reported in the abstract, and no effect is estimated. Graded low.
- Claims taken from the Portuguese abstract; the seven included studies were not listed in the abstract and were not read here.
