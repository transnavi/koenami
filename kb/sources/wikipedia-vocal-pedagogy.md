# Vocal pedagogy (English Wikipedia)

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17: "Vocal pedagogy is the study of the art and science of voice instruction. It is used in the teaching of singing and assists in defining what singing is, how singing works, and how singing technique is accomplished."
- [[wikipedia-voice-therapy]] treats vocal pedagogy as the related field that alters the voice for singing, while voice therapy is the speech-language pathologist's field.
