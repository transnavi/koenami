# 嗓音疾病诊治的规范化之路（徐文2023）

## What it says
- The Chinese abstract's framing: 本文综述了嗓音疾病诊治的发展历程和现状，强调了嗓音医学作为一门专业学科的重要性 — it reviews the development and present state of voice-disorder care and argues for voice medicine as a specialty in its own right (abstract, Chinese text).
- The timeline it gives: 自20世纪70年代以来，国际上嗓音医学迅速发展，中国自20世纪80年代起也取得了显著进步，尤其在嗓音与言语障碍疾病的基础研究、综合评估、外科手术改进等方面 — international voice medicine grew rapidly from the 1970s, and China has made marked progress since the 1980s in basic research, comprehensive assessment and surgical improvement.
- What it says standardisation covers: 主观听感知评估、客观声学检测、嗓音相关生活质量评估 (subjective audio-perceptual assessment, objective acoustic measurement, voice-related quality-of-life assessment), plus 喉部形态及声带振动评价的规范化 (standardised evaluation of laryngeal morphology and vocal-fold vibration), 嗓音显微外科手术技术的创新, 声带注射喉成形术的发展, and the importance of 嗓音及言语康复治疗 (voice and speech rehabilitation).
- The institutional claim: 中华医学会耳鼻咽喉头颈外科学分会嗓音学组和咽喉学组，以及中华耳鼻咽喉头颈外科杂志编辑委员会咽喉组共同制订了一系列专家共识 — the Chinese Medical Association's voice and laryngopharyngology subspecialty groups together with the journal's editorial board have jointly issued a series of expert consensus documents, which it credits with raising diagnostic and treatment standards.
- Affiliation: Department of Otorhinolaryngology Head and Neck Surgery, Beijing Tongren Hospital, Capital Medical University.

## Bearing on voice training
- Documents the professional apparatus behind the Chinese instruments and guidelines this vault holds: the TWVQ-SC [[wang2022]] and VHI work came out of the same specialty groups whose consensus series this editorial announces, and the 2024 assessment consensus [[xu2025]] is one of that series (described two years later in [[xu2025a]]).
- Explains why the Chinese-language trans voice literature is thin in the way [[lin2024]] and [[chen2024cn]] report: Chinese voice medicine standardised around voice disorders and phonosurgery first, and the trans indication arrives into that framework rather than creating its own.
