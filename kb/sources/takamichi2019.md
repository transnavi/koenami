# JVS corpus: free Japanese multi-speaker voice corpus

## What it says
- 100 speakers in three styles (normal, whisper, falsetto); 30 hours including 22 hours of parallel normal voices; designed for speech-synthesis research.

## Bearing on voice training
- Koenami's core Japanese reference set (studio read speech by native speakers); its terms allow only a small web excerpt.
