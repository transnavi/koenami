# 음성장애지수(Voice Handicap Index)의 문항별 분석을 통한 한국 트랜스젠더의 주관적 음성인식 특성

## What it says
- Design, from the English abstract: 「This study analyzed a nationwide cross-sectional survey of 232 Korean transgender people which included 145 MtF and 78 FtM transgender people. As a control group, 83 non-transgender adults without voice disorder completed the VHI-10 questionnaire.」
- The headline result: 「The mean score of VHI-10 was 16.57 among MtF and 10.83 among FtM transgender people. Compared to the control group whose mean score was 3.27, VHI-10 score was statistically significantly higher among MtF (z=8.993, p<.005) as well as FtM transgender people (z=5.914, p<.005).」 Against the cut-off the abstract cites: 「Considering that previous research suggested that the VHI-10 score of 11 or higher could be used to identify people with abnormal perceived voice quality, MtF transgender groups had mean score which are not within the normal range.」
- Item level: 「MtF transgender people had statistically significantly higher score compared to the control group in all of 10 items. FtM transgender people had statistically significantly higher score in 8 items except the three items (e.g., F1, F3). When the score of MtF was compared to that of FtM transgender people, MtF transgender people have statistically significantly higher score in F1, F3, P17, F19, E23, E25 compared to FtM transgender. Especially, MtF group have high score regarding emotional questions.」
- The framing of the problem: 「Although transgender people do not have any voice disorder, their voice affect quality of their lives profoundly.」 And the conclusion: 「Especially, MtF transgender people, who cannot get benefit to change their voice in a process of hormone treatment, reported poor perceived voice quality, leading to experiencing difficulties in their social lives.」

## Bearing on voice training
- This is the Korean measurement the vault otherwise holds only for other languages: a VHI-10 comparison of trans and non-trans speakers, and the first one in the vault with both MtF and FtM arms plus controls ([[patient-reported-outcomes]], [[korean-voice]]).
- The MtF mean of 16.57 sits above the 11-point abnormality cut-off the thesis cites while no participant had a voice disorder — the self-report handicap comes from the mismatch with gender rather than from pathology, which is the distinction the vault's clinical notes keep making ([[speech-language-pathology]], [[clinical-guidelines]]).
- That the MtF group scored highest on the emotional items is the self-report counterpart of what the community describes as the social cost of the voice ([[voice-impressions]], [[transgender-care-context]], [[yun2024]]).

## Limits
- A Korean master's thesis, not peer-reviewed; the survey is cross-sectional and self-report only, with no acoustic measures and no listener ratings, and the thesis itself was not read — the RISS record's English abstract is the source of everything quoted here. The item labels (F1, P17, E23 …) are as given in the abstract.
