# Cách Luyện Giọng Nói Trầm Như Nam | LGBT Việt Nam

## What it says
- A Vietnamese-language video (uploaded 2020-07-22) whose title and framing address voice lowering for trans men: «Cách Luyện Giọng Nói Trầm Như Nam» — how to practise a deep voice like a man's — filed under the channel's LGBT Vietnam material (oEmbed and description, 2026-09-18).
- The description states the channel's purpose and its later history in the author's own words: the goal from the start was «chia sẻ những gì Linh có đến với các bạn LGBT+, đặc biệt là Transguy, để có thể tự tin về chính mình» — to share with LGBT+ people, especially trans guys, so they can be confident in themselves.
- The same description records that the author stopped the trans topic at the end of 2022 — «Cuối năm 2022, Linh quyết định dừng lại những chủ đề này vì Linh nhận ra, những gì mình chia sẻ chưa phải gốc rễ» (what she had shared was not yet the root of the matter) — and that from March 2025 she resumed sharing for a wider audience of 18–30-year-olds through inner-coaching work.
- The videos were left online deliberately: «Linh vẫn để lại những video này vì: Biết đâu đó, những gì Linh đã chia sẻ, có thể giúp ích cho ai đó» — in case what she shared still helps someone.

## Bearing on voice training
- The masculinising counterpart to the Vietnamese feminising series in this vault: the same language sphere has community material aimed at trans men's voices, and it is pedagogical rather than surgical [[voice-masculinization]] [[yt-senem]].
- A dated document of the Vietnamese trans community's online self-teaching: the author's own retrospective says the material did not reach the root of the subject, which is the standing caveat on peer-produced voice instruction [[community-training]] [[voice-across-languages]].
