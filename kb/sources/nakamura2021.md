# 変声障害と性同一性障害に対する喉頭枠組み手術

A lecture paper from the society's 領域講習 by the Japanese surgeon whose type-4 thyroplasty work is the backbone of the vault's Japanese surgical notes ([[nakamura2007]], [[sanuki2007]]). Where those report outcomes, this one states the mechanism and pairs the two directions — the operation that raises pitch and the one that lowers it. Author affiliation: 日本大学医学部耳鼻咽喉・頭頸部外科学分野.

## What it says
- Framing: 「Mutational disorder (MD) and gender identity disorder (GID) are rare disease. Such patients tend to complain that their speaking fundamental frequency (SFF) does not fit, being too high or too low.」 Both conditions are described by the same complaint — SFF that does not fit — which is why they are handled together.
- Downward operation: 「Type 3 thyroplasty (TP3) reduces the pitch of the SFF. In detail, the tension of the vocal fold is loosened by TP3, which then causes the SFF to become low-pitched.」
- Upward operation: 「Type 4 thyroplasty (TP4) conversely increased the pitch of the SFF as the tension of the vocal fold is tightened causing the SFF to become high-pitched.」
- Rationale for operating at all on a non-life-threatening problem: 「While vocal pitch disorders are not life-threatening for patients with MD and GID, they can cause substantial worry and stress. TP3 and TP4 can thus improve the quality of life of the patients with MD and GID.」
- Keywords: 変声障害, 性同一性障害, 話声位, 機能性発声障害, 甲状軟骨形成術.

## Bearing on voice training
- Gives the mechanism in one sentence each: TP3 slackens fold tension to lower pitch, TP4 tightens it to raise pitch. Everything else in the vault's Japanese surgical notes is outcome data on those two moves ([[nakamura2007]], [[hiroshiba2024]], [[maeda2025]]).
- The pair is the mirror image the vault otherwise lacks on the masculinization side: the Japanese literature here treats 声を低くする手術 as the same family as 声を高くする手術, alongside behavioural masculinization ([[voice-masculinization]]) and testosterone ([[nimura2022]]). For the non-Japanese version of the same distinction see [[song2017]] and [[kalra2026]].
