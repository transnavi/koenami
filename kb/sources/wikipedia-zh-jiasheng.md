# 假聲（中文维基百科）

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17 (extract in traditional Chinese): 假聲，又稱假音，是指用以發出比正常音域高的聲音，並且和正常音域重疊大概一個八度。 — falsetto (假聲/假音) produces pitches above the normal range and overlaps it by about an octave.
- Interlanguage titles: en Falsetto · ja ファルセット · ko 가성.
- 假声 is the term the Chinese community debate turns on: the MtF guide [[digital-transchinese-mtf-voice]] says its method deliberately differs from 假声 teaching, and [[aogi2021]] uses 真声/假声 to locate the pitch transition point (破音点/breaking point) during scale-climbing drills.

## Bearing on voice training
- Terminology anchor for the register named M2 in the laryngeal-mechanism model [[roubeau2009]]: a Chinese learner reading 假声教程 and the vault's [[vocal-registers]] is reading about the same register.
