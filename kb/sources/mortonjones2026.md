# Outcomes of Direct Vocal Fold Testosterone Injections in Hormone-Naïve Adults: A Case Series

## What it says
- Two non-binary testosterone-naïve individuals at the Northwestern Memorial Hospital Voice Clinic, treated to test "the effectiveness of direct testosterone injections to the vocal folds for voice masculinization in testosterone-naïve individuals" (abstract).
- Protocol: "4 direct testosterone injections to the bilateral vocal folds 2 weeks apart". Before each injection and 1 and 3 months after the final one, the team recorded voice samples, a voice-related quality of life survey and a mini-mood and anxiety questionnaire; serum hormone levels were measured before and after.
- "Linear regression indicated a significant decrease in mean F0 3 months postfinal injection in both participants." Both reported improved voice quality of life, and their voice qualities "shifted towards prototypical male".
- "No systemic side effects reported, and posttreatment testosterone and estradiol levels were similar to baseline."
- Conclusion: direct injection "represent[s] a promising therapeutic intervention for voice masculinization in testosterone-naïve individuals".

## Bearing on voice training
- A localised route to the androgen effect on the vocal folds that systemic hormone therapy produces globally — aimed at people who want a lower voice without whole-body hormone effects [[voice-masculinization]] [[hormones-and-voice]].
- Raising the serum levels not changing is the safety claim being made, and it rests on two people [[vocal-folds]].

## Notes
- n = 2, case series, no control group, three-month follow-up, single centre; n = 2 is also the entire published experience with this intervention as far as this abstract indicates.
- Claims taken from the PubMed abstract.
