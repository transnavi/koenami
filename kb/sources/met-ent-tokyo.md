# 声（喉）の手術（メトロポリタンENTサージクリニック東京）

A Tokyo ENT surgery clinic whose voice-surgery page covers both directions and states that it treats trans patients. It is the counterpart to [[kyoto-ent-clinic]] in vocabulary and structure, and the two together give the Japanese clinical picture as it is published to patients.

## What it says
- Scope: 「トランスジェンダーの方への声の手術にも対応しております。自分らしい声に近づくことを目指し、状態に応じた治療をご提案しています。」
- Setting for the operation: 「当院の音声改善手術は、局所麻酔で行う手術です。手術中は実際に声を出していただきながら進めるため、声の変化を確認しつつ調整を行うことが可能です（声帯短縮術を除く）。」 Day surgery is offered, and consultations can be online.
- Upward: 「甲状軟骨形成術Ⅳ型 — 声帯の緊張を調節する筋肉の一つである輪状甲状筋は、二つの軟骨をつないでいます（輪状軟骨と甲状軟骨）。この軟骨どうしを強制的に引き寄せ、声帯の緊張を高めます。…声が低い事に悩んでいる方や、声に力がない方に効果的です。」
- Upward, second technique: 「声帯短縮術（ウェンドラー声帯形成術）— 左右の声帯前方の粘膜を切除したのち、声帯どうしを縫合します。これにより声帯が緊張を保ったまま短くなり、地声が高くなります。手術は全身麻酔で行われます。世界的に主流となってきている手術です。」
- Also offered for the face and neck: 「喉頭隆起切除（喉仏の切除）…極端な切除を行うと声帯にダメージを与えてしまうため、注意が必要です。」 and it can be combined with the pitch operations.
- Downward: 「甲状軟骨形成術Ⅲ型 — 甲状軟骨の一部を短冊状に切除し、声帯の緊張をやわらげます。変声障害や一部の外転型けいれん性発声障害に効果的です。また、声を低くする効果もありますので、声が高くて悩んでいる方にも適応があります。」 This is the Japanese route to a lower voice outside hormone therapy.
- For roughness and weakness it lists 甲状軟骨形成術Ⅰ型 (narrowing the glottal gap for 声帯麻痺) and 披裂軟骨内転術, and for tremor/spasm 甲状軟骨形成術Ⅱ型.
- The audience is not only trans patients: the お悩み lists for the upward operations read 「電話で性別を間違えられることがある」 and 「自分らしい声に近づけたい」, which is the same complaint the community reports ([[yomedanchii-voice]], [[note-maru2blog]]).
- The clinic states it publishes case counts by year and operation since opening in 2024 (「2026年7月15日更新」 on the 手術実績 table), but the table itself is rendered as an image on the page and its numbers were not readable in the fetch.
- Prices are labelled 「保険適用外」 for the operation pages linked from this page.

## Bearing on voice training
- Publishes 「世界的に主流となってきている手術です」 about vocal-fold shortening, which matches the direction of the recent literature in the vault ([[kim2024]], [[casadomorente2024]], [[aires2023]]) but is the clinic's own claim and not sourced.
- The intra-operative voice monitoring it advertises for Ⅳ型 is the technique reported in the Japanese literature ([[maeda2025]]); its absence for the shortening operation is stated plainly in the parenthetical 「（声帯短縮術を除く）」.
- Offers the masculinizing operations (TP3 and Ⅰ/Ⅱ/披裂軟骨内転術) to a Japanese readership that the vault's masculinization notes otherwise cover through testosterone and behavioural work ([[voice-masculinization]], [[irwig2017a]]).

## Limits
- Clinic marketing page, no sources, no outcome figures readable, no named surgeon on the page fetched. Nothing here is verified against a peer-reviewed report, and the 保険適用外 labels are the clinic's own statement about billing rather than a general rule.
- The claim that vocal-fold shortening is becoming the world standard is not supported on the page.
