## Bearing on voice training
- 咬字 is the segmental and prosodic layer that pitch and resonance alone do not supply — the same step the 2021 article course splits into 字头/字腹/字尾 [[bili-cv9417414]] and that the 幻音学院 instructor demonstrates through 尾音 lengthening [[bili-cv10585868]]. It maps onto [[articulation]] and, through the final-syllable lengthening, onto [[intonation]].
- 御姐音 is described elsewhere in the dataset as a 共鸣-ratio recipe — 咽腔 first, 胸腔 least, pitched one to four semitones above the speaker's own voice [[bili-cv9417414]] — so this video and the article course are two routes to the same labelled target.
