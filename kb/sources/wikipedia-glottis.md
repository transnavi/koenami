# Glottis (English Wikipedia)

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17: "In humans the glottis is the middle region of the larynx, where the vocal cords are located. Within this region is the opening between the vocal cords. The glottis is crucial in producing sound from the vocal cords."
