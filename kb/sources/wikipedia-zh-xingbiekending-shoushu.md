# 性別肯定手術（中文维基百科）

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17 (extract in simplified Chinese): 性别肯定手术是一种外科手术或一系列外科手术，其可以改变一个人的性別特徵，使其符合个体所认同的性别。该手术也被称为性别重置手术、性别确认手术、变性手术等。 — surgery that alters sex characteristics to match the identified gender; also called 性别重置手术 (gender-resetting), 性别确认手术, 变性手术.
- It frames surgery as part of treatment for 性别不安 (gender dysphoria).
- Interlanguage titles: en Gender-affirming surgery · ja 性別適合手術 · ko 성전환 수술.
- The name list is useful across the Chinese-speaking regions: mainland usage on mtf.wiki is 性别重置手术/SRS ([[mtfwiki-vocal-surgery]]); the voice-specific operations are named separately (声带截短术, 环甲接近术).

## Bearing on voice training
- Orients the reader that 嗓音女性化手术 ([[voice-surgery]]) sits inside this family but is governed by its own consent and prerequisites — mainland hospitals' voice-surgery requirements are looser than SRS requirements ([[mtfwiki-vocal-surgery]]).
