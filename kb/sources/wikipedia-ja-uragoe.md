# 裏声

Japanese Wikipedia has no article for the English *falsetto* under that name at 裏声; the page carries **no interlanguage links** (langlinks API 2026-09-17, the one Japanese voice article in this set that does not). Its opposite is 地声, which also has no article — the pair lives in the singing-pedagogy vocabulary rather than in the encyclopedia.

## What it says
- Summary endpoint 2026-09-17, in full: 「裏声（うらごえ）は、声帯から生成される声のうち、一般的には頭声区と呼ばれる声区で生成される発声を指す概念。地声（表声）に対する語。」
- So the article defines 裏声 by the register it comes from (頭声区) and by opposition to 地声/表声, without describing how either is produced.

## Bearing on voice training
- The word does real work in the Japanese scene as a *failure label*: ニコニコ大百科 records that a listener who is not convinced by a 両声類's 女声 says they hear 「男の裏声にしか聞こえない」 ([[nicodic-ryoseirui]]), and the メラニー法 page warns against aiming at a falsetto-like result ([[wikipedia-melanie-ho]]). A guide that treats 女声 as 「地声のまま高い声を出す」 is defined against this word ([[brushvoice-joseigoe]]).
- In the register list the 声 article gives for Japanese, 裏声 sits between 表声 and ホイッスル ([[wikipedia-ja-koe]]).
