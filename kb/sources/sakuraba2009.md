# 女性と判定される声の特徴 —性同一性障害者の話声位—

The Japanese listener study that the vault's gaps section said did not exist: a listening experiment on MtF speakers and cis women, with F0 measured on the same recordings. It is the Japanese counterpart of [[gelfer2013]] and the closest local analogue to [[leung2018]].

## What it says
- Design, from the abstract: 「男性から女性へ性別の移行を希望する性同一性障害者（MtF: male to female transgender/transsexual）119名と生物学的女性32名の母音発声（/a//i/）と朗読音声に対して，話者の性別を判定させる聴取実験および基本周波数（F0）の分析を行い，比較検討した．」 n = 119 MtF and 32 cis women; vowels /a/ and /i/ plus connected reading.
- The headline result: 「70%以上女性に聴こえる発話の声の基本周波数（F0）は母音で平均270Hz，朗読で217Hzとなり，生物学的女性の平均値243Hz（母音），217Hz（朗読）に近いものとなった．」 So the F0 at which most listeners call the voice female is 270 Hz for sustained vowels and 217 Hz for reading — and the cis women's own means are 243 Hz and 217 Hz.
- The finding that matters more than the threshold: 「しかしながら，生物学的女性と同じF0値の範囲にあっても，女性と判定されない声が7割近くあり，声の高さだけが女性の声に聴こえる要因ではないことが示唆された．」 Within the cis women's F0 range, nearly 70% of the MtF voices were still not judged female.
- The stated purpose is clinical: 「transsexual voice therapyにおいて，訓練ターゲットとする声の高さを検討するために」 — the paper exists to set a training target, and its own data are what undercut a pitch-only target.
- Keywords as printed: 性同一性障害, 基本周波数, 音響分析, 聴取実験, transsexual voice therapy. Received 2008-06-04, accepted 2008-09-04.

## Bearing on voice training
- Two different targets appear here and they disagree in a useful way: vowel F0 targets run ~27 Hz above the cis mean while reading targets sit at it, so a single number for 「女性と判定される声」 cannot be lifted from this paper without saying which task it came from. Compare the task-dependence of the naturalness data in [[hasegawa2017]] and the whole-utterance averaging problem noted in [[formants]].
- The 70%-within-range finding is the Japanese replication of the pitch-ceiling result the vault already carries from other languages ([[gelfer2013]], [[leung2018]], [[skuk2014]]): F0 is necessary and far from sufficient, which is the premise of resonance and 声質 work in [[brushvoice-joseigoe]] and of surgery-plus-therapy combinations in [[japanese-voice]].

## Limits
- Medium-grade: a single-centre Japanese study, abstract read here, not the full text — the listener panel's size and training, the exact 朗読 material and the 70% criterion's definition were not read and are not reported in this note.
- 2009 data: the MtF participants were recorded before the current surgical and hormonal landscape, and no 両声類 or non-binary speakers were studied.
