# Intervención fonoaudiológica para la feminización de la voz en una persona transgénero (MTF): estudio de caso

## What it says
- Single-case study in Chile, designed as an ABA-reversal longitudinal case series: 「La intervención se estructuró, principalmente, en tres apartados: evaluación inicial, intervención y reevaluación final」 (abstract, "Método").
- The problem statement the authors give: trans people 「usualmente realizan cambios vocales sin una técnica adecuada, recurriendo principalmente a tratamientos quirúrgicos u hormonales, desconociendo la terapia fonoaudiológica como una alternativa」 — they usually change their voices without technique, going to surgery or hormones and not knowing that speech therapy is an option.
- Outcomes reported: 「una variación significativa en las cualidades acústico-perceptuales de la voz, la que presentó mayores características de una voz femenina」, with changes 「en el patrón fonorespiratorio y en la postura」 — in the phonatory-respiratory pattern and in posture.
- Conclusion: the speech-language intervention was effective, producing a more feminine voice in the person treated.

## Bearing on voice training
- The two named mechanisms are breathing pattern and posture, not pitch ([[breathing-and-support]]). That places it with the respiratory-first approaches in the vault rather than the resonance-first ones, and it is a different lever from the semi-occluded tract work in [[fuenzalida2021]] and [[sandoval2019]].
- Its framing — that people arrive having already tried to change their voice without technique — is the same observation [[nemr2026]] makes from Brazil ([[voice-therapy-techniques]]).

## Notes
- n = 1, ABA design with no blinding and no independent listener panel reported in the abstract; graded low. The eight-author list for a single case study suggests a training-clinic report.
- Claims taken from the abstract; the acoustic values, the session count and the reversal-phase data were not read.
