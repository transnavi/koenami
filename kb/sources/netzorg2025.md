# On the production and perception of a single speaker's gender

## What it says
- Collects the Disentangled Source-Filter Dataset (DSFD) with a gender-affirming voice teacher: 45 minutes of audio across 25 pitch, resonance and weight configurations, with electroglottograph (EGG) measurements.
- Average F0, ΔF, contact quotient (CQ) and loudness correlate with the teacher's pitch, resonance and weight settings.
- In perceptual studies, ΔF was the strongest predictor of perceived gender; perceived naturalness and realness were unpredictable from these acoustic metrics.

## Bearing on voice training
- Supports ΔF as Koenami's 響き measure and warns that the indicators say nothing about whether a voice sounds natural. One author is affiliated with TransVoiceLessons.
