## Bearing on voice training
- 发声位置 is the 伪声 layer's name for what the acoustic literature calls the articulatory setting: where in the tract the constriction and the resonance-shaping gestures sit. Its two common referents in this dataset are larynx height and pharyngeal narrowing, which is what raises formants and shortens the effective tract ([[formants]], [[vocal-tract-anatomy]]).
- Placing it after the 咽腔 video ([[bili-jiangfeng2019]]) makes the channel's order cavity first, position second — the reverse of the 2021 handbook's F0-first ordering [[aogi2021]] and of the cue ranking in [[leung2018]], where pitch dominates.
