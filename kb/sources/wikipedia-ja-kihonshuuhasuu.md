# 基本周波数

The Japanese article for *fundamental frequency*; interlanguage links include **Fundamental frequency** (en), 기본 주파수 (ko), 基本頻率 (zh), ความถี่มูลฐาน (th) (langlinks API 2026-09-17).

## What it says
- Intro: 「基本周波数（きほんしゅうはすう、英: fundamental frequency、f_o）は信号に含まれる最も低い周波数成分の周波数である。」 The article writes the symbol as f<sub>o</sub>, not f0.
- It is a signal-processing article, not a voice article: 「音楽では基音の周波数が f_o であり楽音の音高（ピッチ）をほぼ規定する重要な役割を担っている。」 The voice-specific numbers live in the 声 article's 声位 section instead ([[wikipedia-ja-koe]]).

## Bearing on voice training
- Japanese clinical and community writing says 話声位 where English says speaking F0; the same quantity, measured on connected speech ([[sakuraba2009]], [[nimura2015]]). Koenami's Japanese pages use 基本周波数 for the frame-level measure and 話声位 for the utterance-level summary.
- The register list and the 声帯 mechanics that set f_o are in [[wikipedia-ja-koe]] and [[wikipedia-ja-seitai]].
