# 『女伪男教程』赶时间可以直接划到第四个点点的位置~（Bilibili 专栏）

The second most-read article in the collected set, and the only one that is a written companion to a video — the timestamps of the video's sections are embedded in the text (02:27, 02:39, 03:20).

## What it says
- The prerequisite is stated first: 【前提】声带未发育完全的小朋友不要练 ("children whose vocal folds are not fully developed should not practise").
- Part 1 lists three 要点:
  - 潜移默化 — 多听多模仿一些专业cv的声音, credited to a period of 吊儿郎当的“模仿训练” (unserious imitation practice) that shortened the uploader's learning time.
  - 因人而异 — 不要一味追求压低音，每个人都有一个最适合自己的声音…合适就好, with 语气自然更重要 ("natural manner matters more"). The uploader describes their own result as 男音不会很低，但说话不死板，亦无太重气泡音就不会很假了 ("not very low, but not stiff, and without heavy vocal fry it does not sound fake").
  - 张弛有度 — 不要为了伪声去练嘶吼、爆破音等，用嗓过度伤了声带就是不可逆的伤 ("do not practise screaming or plosives for 伪声; overusing the voice and injuring the folds is irreversible damage"), with the uploader's own case: 非要唱海豚音和各种低音（如：申公豹）把自己声带搞伤了, and the rule 嗓子难受就不要继续练.
- Part 2 gives the method in three steps: ①扩开嗓子眼（开嗓） ②声音往下走 ③多练成自然.
- The Q&A contains the mechanism claim: asked why pressing low only produces 胖子音 and breath-holding, the answer is 因为你声音只压到了咽以上的位置…把嗓子眼打开，声音往下走，胸腔共鸣了，就会清亮了 ("you only pressed the voice down as far as above the pharynx; open the throat, let the sound go down, and once it resonates in the chest it becomes clear").
- The follow-along section gives four steps: ①张大嘴，哈～ — open the mouth until the uvula is visible from the front; ②绷住刚才的状态发长“额”的音 — hold that posture and produce a long 「额」, hand on the chest to check for vibration; ③继续保持状态，把“额”换成任何一句话 — keep the posture and swap in any sentence, e.g. 生活如此美好, repeatedly; ④当可以维持住一个偏磁性很重的声音的时候，把嘴闭上一些，像正常说话时的大小（即口腔空间变小，嗓子眼依然是扩开状态） — close the mouth toward normal speech size while keeping the throat open, and work on clarity. The endpoint: 当能维持住一个很有磁性的声音是，就可以加入语气、语调，不那么紧绷，就成功了 ("once the resonant voice holds, add manner and intonation, relax, and you have it").

## Bearing on voice training
- The method is a pharynx-and-larynx-height move with the opposite sign to the 男伪女 tutorials: 开嗓 (open throat), a lowered larynx, and chest resonance instead of the raised-larynx, pharyngeal-narrowing, chest-removed recipe the female direction uses ([[formants]], [[vocal-tract-anatomy]]). The uploader's own note that the low voice should not be forced and that naturalness beats lowness is the opposite of the 速成 framing, and it is the advice the harm videos exist to underline.
- The harm claims are first-person and specific — vocal-fold injury from 海豚音 and forced low singing, called 不可逆的伤 — which places this article with the other community harm accounts [[bili-lele2021]] and the injury framing in [[bili-weiyinjiaolianershu]], and against the sourced caution in [[vocal-fatigue]]. The stated irreversibility is not established by the record.
- The 04 step's instruction — reduce the oral space while keeping the throat open — is the practical version of what the literature calls keeping the articulatory setting while changing the resonance, i.e. the generalisation problem again ([[articulation]], [[bili-xiaoya]]).
- Its 1,277 likes against 9,676 views is the highest engagement ratio of any collected article, and the underlying video is the second-most-viewed 女伪男 tutorial in the dataset after [[bili-qiyaomichiyo2018]], which makes this channel the main modern source for the direction.
