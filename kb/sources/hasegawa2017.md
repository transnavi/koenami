# 男性両声類の女声らしさに関わる特徴量の分析

## What it says
- Reports, from one contest's recordings of male 両声類 speakers producing female voices, that speakers with mean F0 around 200–240 Hz were rated natural and that voices above about 300 Hz were rated less natural — as summarised on Koenami's method page.

## Bearing on voice training
- The only Japanese data point on "how high is too high"; weak, but the tutorial cites it with that caveat.

## Notes
- Single contest, undergraduate work; the PDF was linked from the method page and not re-read for this note.
