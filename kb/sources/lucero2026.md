# Gender-Affirming Voice Therapy: Why Pitch Is Only Part of the Story

## What it says
- Byline Jorge C. Lucero; "18 min read"; dated 20 January 2026, "updated September 1, 2026." Opening target: the slogan "Just raise your pitch to 200 Hz and you'll sound feminine."
- Key-takeaway box (page text): "F0 explains only 41.6% of perceived gender (Leung et al., 2018 meta-analysis)"; "Reference F0 bands: female-typical above ~180 Hz, male-typical ~100–140 Hz, with an ambiguous range between—commonly narrowed to ~145–165 Hz"; "F0 and formants must shift together" citing Hillenbrand & Clark 2009 and Gelfer & Mikos 2005; "Testosterone lowers pitch; estrogen does not change the adult voice — and 21% of people on testosterone do not reach male-typical F0 after one year (Ziegler et al., 2018)"; TWVQ as the outcome anchor.
- The page itself flags: "Beware of precise-looking percentage breakdowns for the 'other 58%'—they are" (sentence cut on the fetched extract) and notes that Leung quantified variance only for F0.
- Client goals are "self-determined"; "not every trans or gender-diverse person wants voice modification"; cites WPATH SOC-8 and ASHA Practice Portal for that frame.
