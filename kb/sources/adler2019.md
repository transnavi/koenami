# Voice and Communication Therapy for the Transgender/Gender Diverse Client: A Comprehensive Clinical Guide, Third Edition

## What it says
- The standard clinical textbook of the field, from its first authors' practice: Richard K. Adler (Professor Emeritus, Minnesota State University), Sandy Hirsch and Jack Pickering; third edition, © 2019, Plural Publishing (publisher page byline and product record).
- Publisher's description: the edition "remains a must-have resource for speech-language pathologists, voice clinicians and trainers who assist transgender/gender diverse clients in aligning" voice and communication with gender identity, and "builds on the work of the first two editions, and meets the clinical and training needs of an even larger and better-informed core of speech language pathologists" (publisher page).
- Adler's own career, per the publisher's biography, "has spanned nearly 50 years as an SLP in public schools, hospitals, private practice, university teaching" — the book descends from the same Sydney/US clinical lineage as [[oates1983]] (publisher page).

## Bearing on voice training
- The reference a clinician is pointed to for what a course of gender-affirming voice and communication therapy contains: assessment, pitch, resonance, intonation, articulation and nonverbal communication work, across feminization, masculinization and non-binary goals ([[speech-language-pathology]]).
- Third-edition authorship changed from the first two editions (Adler, Hirsch & Mordaunt) to Adler, Hirsch & Pickering; citations should name the edition used.
