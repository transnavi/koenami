## Bearing on voice training
- 咽腔共鸣 is treated as the single skill worth isolating — the same choice [[bili-jiangfeng2019]] made for its flagship and [[bili-cv7530256]] argued against, on the ground that 口腔共鸣 is the neglected one. That disagreement inside the community is about which resonator carries the perceptual change, which is a question about formants rather than about effort ([[formants]], [[source-filter-model]]).
- A raised, narrowed pharynx shortens the effective tract and raises F2 and above; the article course formalises the same idea as 高喉位 raising F2 [[aogi2021]], and the cue-weighting literature ranks formant change second to F0 [[leung2018]].
