# Estill Voice Training and the Gender Non-binary Singer

## What it says
- Semantic Scholar abstract, 2026-09-17: "While there has been a good deal of recent research about teaching transgender singers, less has been written specifically about gender non-binary singers. When the author was approached by a student who shared that they were gender non-binary, the author examined her pedagogy to make the classroom a more inclusive space."
- Method named: "Using a Performance as Research methodology, the author surveyed and recorded verbal interviews with her students and discovered that Estill Voice Training (EVT), the foundation of the voice training in her classes, is built to support inclusivity."
- The author's claim, as an Estill Master Trainer: "At its core, EVT is free of aesthetic bias and aims to train all voices to sing in all voice qualities, supporting students' identities and natural singing voices. By focusing on anatomy, EVT uses gender bias-free terminology and offers unlimited vocal options."
- The article also "offers other recommendations for fostering an inclusive environment in the classroom."

## Bearing on voice training
- A practitioner argument that isolating anatomy (thick/thin, larynx height, twang) rather than aiming at a gendered timbre is the feature that makes EVT usable for non-binary singers. That is a pedagogical claim about terminology, not an outcome study of non-binary voices.
