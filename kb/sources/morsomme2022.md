# Can ambulatory biofeedback help a transgender woman speak at a higher pitch?（Morsomme & Remacle 2022）

## What it says
- The problem as stated: "Voice feminization for transgender women remains a challenge. The fundamental frequency of the voice (fo) is one of the main parameters contributing to the perception of gender. One of the aims of voice therapy is to help transgender women acquire a new vocal motor behavior to increase their fo" (abstract, Purpose).
- The hypothesis: "We hypothesized that ambulatory biofeedback could help extend the new vocal behavior to daily life."
- Design: "This prospective case study assessed the impact of two weeks of ambulatory vibrotactile biofeedback (VTBF) applied to one transgender woman, following two weeks of voice therapy (three 60-minute sessions)."
- The device setting: "The VTBF was activated when the participant's voice was lower than 150 Hz for more than 500 ms and was accompanied by two 60-minute counseling sessions."
- Results on F0 monitored during daily activities: a tendency to rise after the two weeks of voice therapy (Δ = 8 Hz or 1 semitone), then a significant increase immediately after the two weeks of VTBF (Δ = 13 Hz or 1.5 semitones).
- The evidence for learning rather than momentary compliance: "The increase in mean fo from day to day and the decrease in the activation percentage from the first to the second week of VTBF (Δ = 6%) indicated a gradual integration of the motor behavior, making it possible to achieve the targeted female frequency."
- Conclusion: "ambulatory VTBF helped the participant to generalize the techniques acquired during the voice therapy sessions to real-life communication situations. These results are promising for therapeutic programs that integrate tools that can be used outside the clinical context."
- Affiliations: Voice Unit, Department of Speech and Language Therapy, University of Liège, and the Fund for Scientific Research FNRS, Brussels.

## Bearing on voice training
- The peer-reviewed form of the Liège biofeedback line, and the vault's clearest measurement of the gap between clinic and daily life: the monitored daily F0 moved 8 Hz over the therapy fortnight and 13 Hz more over the device fortnight, with the falling activation rate used as the marker of habit formation rather than of compliance ([[voice-therapy-techniques]], [[fundamental-frequency]]).
- The 150 Hz threshold and the 500 ms activation rule are concrete, published parameters for a wearable target, comparable to the community guidance's pitch floors and to the thresholds other clinical protocols set ([[community-training]], [[fundamental-frequency]]).
- It answers the same question as the master's thesis that preceded it [[jacquet2017]] and the conference report of the same design [[remacle2018]], and it is the outcome that the Belgian needs survey's respondents were describing when they reported positive results from voice and communication training [[papeleu2025b]].
