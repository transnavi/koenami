# 嗓音训练综述（MtF.wiki）

## What it says
- Positions the practice clinically: 在医学上嗓音女性化训练属于言语治疗的范畴 ("medically, voice-feminization training falls under speech therapy"), and points to English Wikipedia's Transgender voice therapy article (see [[wikipedia-zh-kuaxingbie-yuyin-liaofa]]) as the parallel introduction.
- On sequencing with surgery: 在系统的嗓音女性化训练完成之后，如果希望进一步提升音高，此时可咨询…嗓音外科医师 — consult a phonosurgeon about pitch-raising surgery only after systematic training, and 嗓音女性化手术结束后仍然需要嗓音训练作为补充（调整发音、气质等） ("after surgery, training is still needed as a supplement: pronunciation, bearing, and so on").
- Anesthesia warning: after vocal surgery, general anesthesia with intubation is a risk — 全身麻醉手术涉及气管插管请务必术前与麻醉科医师确认风险.
- On software: 软件并不能指导训练而只是帮助您监控相关的指征（音高） ("software cannot guide training; it only helps you monitor indicators such as pitch") — it lists Voice Tools, Vocal Pitch Monitor, Singscope (iOS/Android), Friture and Praat (desktop), matching the vault's [[tools]] notes.

## Bearing on voice training
- The community's consensus framing for Chinese MtF learners: training first, surgery as an add-on, software as monitoring — the same ordering the clinical reviews behind [[voice-feminization-therapy]] support, and the mirror of [[mtfwiki-vocal-surgery]]'s own advice (原则上建议先进行嗓音女性化训练).
- The 言语治疗 classification is the bridge sentence that connects the 伪声 subculture's self-teaching to the profession described in [[wikipedia-zh-yanliao-zhiliao]] and [[speech-language-pathology]].

## Limits
- Community-maintained wiki tied to a specific site (mtf.wiki); the page was last updated 2024-08-16 per its footer and its sister resource page more recently (2026-07-09), so details like app availability drift.
- No outcome data; claims about sequencing reflect community-collected practice and echoed clinician advice, not a cited trial.
