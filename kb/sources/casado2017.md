# Voice feminization in male-to-female transgendered clients after Wendler's glottoplasty with vs. without voice therapy support

## What it says
- Marbella (ENT, Quirón Hospital) with the Universities of Granada and Málaga (logopedia programme): retrospective study of 18 Spanish-speaking trans women who had Wendler glottoplasty between 2010 and 2014; ten also completed post-operative voice therapy (abstract).
- Measures before and 12 months after: F0, maximum phonation time, the TSEQ self-assessment questionnaire, and perceptual ratings (Visual Analog Scale and a simplified GRBAS) by inter-rater agreement.
- Significant gains in F0, self-reported satisfaction and degree of feminization in all participants; no improvement in maximum phonation time.
- The abstract's key sentence: "The use of voice therapy appears decisive for optimal improvement of this class of patients." WG is judged "a very effective and less traumatic procedure" than other feminization techniques.

## Bearing on voice training
- The Spanish-language evidence for surgery-plus-therapy over surgery alone — the same combination argued from the other side (therapy-first) in [[heydrich2023]] and protocolised in [[ferreira2025]].
- This Marbella–Málaga group is the same clinical lineage as the Spanish TVQ work [[mora2018a]] [[casadomorente2021]] and the technique comparison [[casadomorente2024]].

## Notes
- Retrospective, 18 patients, therapy arm not randomised — the "decisive" claim rests on between-group comparison of ten versus eight.
- TSEQ is the earlier transgender self-assessment questionnaire, superseded in Spanish clinics by the TVQ MtF [[mora2018a]].
