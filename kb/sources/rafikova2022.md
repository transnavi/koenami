# Голос и психологические свойства человека: обзор современных исследований

## What it says
- A Russian-language review of the literature linking psychological characteristics to the acoustic properties of the voice, written for psychologists (abstract).
- The acoustic measures it names as standard in this research are «частота основного тона, интенсивность и скорость речи» — fundamental frequency, intensity and speech rate (abstract).
- On emotion: "негативные эмоции по голосу распознаются лучше, чем положительные, наиболее точно распознаются гнев и грусть" — negative emotions are recognised from the voice better than positive ones, with anger and sadness the most accurately recognised (abstract).
- On charisma: perceived charismatic speech rests on a combination of prosodic and lexical properties, and "роль аудиальных характеристик в распознавании харизматичности ниже, чем при распознавании эмоций, где более заметную роль играют именно голосовые характеристики, нежели лексическое содержание" — auditory cues matter less for charisma than for emotion (abstract).
- On personality: among the Big Five traits, extraversion showed the largest number of significant correlations with acoustic-prosodic and auditory features (abstract).
- On automatic recognition: machine learning and neural networks can now predict personal characteristics with high accuracy, but the authors note that such work "редко бывают основаны на той или иной теоретической модели" — rarely rests on a theoretical model — and call for theoretical interpretation of the empirical results (abstract).

## Bearing on voice training
- A listener's judgement of a voice is not only about gender but about emotion, charisma and personality, and the Russian-language review sets out which acoustic measures carry which of those inferences [[voice-impressions]].
- Its critique of accuracy-first automatic voice scoring — high predictive accuracy without a theoretical model — is the methodological caution that applies equally to automatic gender scoring of a trained voice [[machine-gender-scoring]].
