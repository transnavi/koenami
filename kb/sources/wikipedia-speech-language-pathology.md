# Speech–language pathology (English Wikipedia)

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17: "Speech–language pathology, also known as speech and language pathology or logopedics, is a healthcare and academic discipline concerning the evaluation, treatment, and prevention of communication disorders, including expressive and mixed receptive-expressive language disorders, voice disorders, speech sound disorders, speech disfluency, pragmatic language impairments, and social communication difficulties, as well as swallowing disorders across the lifespan."
- Voice disorders sit inside the discipline's remit alongside language, speech-sound and fluency disorders — the scope within which gender-affirming voice work is one specialty area ([[speech-language-pathology]]).
