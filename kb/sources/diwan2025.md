# Scaling rich style-prompted text-to-speech datasets (ParaSpeechCaps)

## What it says
- 59 style tags (speaker-level intrinsic and utterance-level situational), 342 hours human-labelled and 2,427 hours automatically annotated English speech.

## Bearing on voice training
- English tag inventory (nasal, husky, crisp, …) used to check the coverage of Koenami's Japanese scales.
