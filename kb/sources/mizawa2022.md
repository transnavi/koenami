# 甲状軟骨形成術4型の13年後に感染性肉芽腫を認めた1症例

## What it says
- The operation described, in the abstract's own words: 「甲状軟骨形成術4型（TP4）は牽引糸を用いて甲状軟骨と輪状軟骨を近接させ話声位を上昇させる術式である。牽引糸の固定のために人工材料を用いることがある。」
- The case: a patient diagnosed with 性同一性障害 in 200X and operated on the following year (TP4 plus 喉頭隆起切除術), who presented 13 years later with 「頸部皮膚正中に感染を伴う肉芽様病変と，前連合部にGTと思われる異物」. The patient is described as 78歳男性 at presentation.
- Voice at presentation: 「音声所見はG2R2B2, SFF 269Hz, Jitter係数4.43％，Shimmer係数7.87％，NHR 19.6dB, VHI 63/120点」.
- Management and outcome: the granuloma, the GORE-TEX® (GT) bolus and the traction suture were removed under general anaesthesia; 「甲状軟骨に喉頭内腔への穿孔があり前頸筋群で被覆した」.
- The authors' conclusion: 「人工材料であるTP4の牽引糸とGTは感染の原因になりうることが再認識された。牽引糸とGTを除去してもSFFは低下せず，音声所見に変化はなかった。この1例の経験であるが，TP4が長期経過すると不可逆になりうることが示唆された。」
- Keywords listed with the article: type 4 thyroplasty, 甲状軟骨形成術4型, GORE-TEX, ゴアテックス, late phase infection, 遅発性感染, gender identity disorder, 性同一性障害.

## Bearing on voice training
- Names the late failure mode of the artificial materials in Japanese TP4 — infection around a non-absorbable suture and bolus over a decade after the operation — which the clinic pages advertising the operation do not mention ([[kyoto-ent-clinic]], [[met-ent-tokyo]]).
- The observation that removing the suture and bolus did not lower sF0 (269 Hz) supports the irreversibility the removal case reports separately ([[nishiyama2023]]): what holds the pitch up after years is the healed approximation itself, not the hardware.
- For a trainee this is a reason the pitch a Japanese TP4 produces should be treated as settled, and the postoperative plan built around it rather than around a further surgical revision.

## Notes
- Single case, one institution (日本医科大学系), patient described only as 78歳男性 with no other history given; nothing about listener perception or voice quality beyond the acoustic measures quoted. The abstract is the full text read here; the article's discussion and figures were not fetched.
