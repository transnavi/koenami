# 女声と聴取された性同一性障害者(MtF)の音声の音響分析

A 2003 Acoustical Society of Japan conference abstract, and the earliest Japanese item this pass located that puts listeners and acoustic analysis on the same MtF recordings. It is included here because it is the head of the line that produced [[sakuraba2009]].

## What it says
- Only the bibliographic record is available: 日本音響学会研究発表会講演論文集, volume 2003, number 1, pages 449–450, published 2003-03-18, NAID 10018035900. The title states the design — acoustic analysis of the voices of MtF speakers who were *heard as female* — and nothing beyond the title is verified here.
- The author list overlaps the later journal papers: 今泉敏 and 櫻庭京子 appear on both this abstract and [[sakuraba2009]], with 広瀬啓吉 also on the 2005 training case report and the 2007 classification paper indexed by CiNii.

## Bearing on voice training
- Its position in the literature is what can be asserted: the Tokyo group was publishing listener-judged acoustic work on Japanese MtF voices from 2003, so the vault's earlier note that no Japanese-listener study exists is wrong for this group's output ([[sakuraba2009]], [[imaizumi2003]], [[maruyama2007]]).
- The title's structure — selecting the voices that were heard as female and analysing those — is the inverse of the 2009 paper's framing, which measured the F0 at which listeners call a voice female. The vault's Japanese gap on 声質 versus pitch is exactly the question between them ([[japanese-voice]]).
