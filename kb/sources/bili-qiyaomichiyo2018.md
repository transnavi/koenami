- Two claims are made: the drill is 压音 (pressing the speaking pitch down) and the preservation rule is 伪音归伪音，平时说话用本音 ("fake voice is for practising; speak in your own voice normally").

## Bearing on voice training
- 压音 is the 女伪男 core and the community's most consistently warned-against drill: one channel titles a video 女伪男错误的压音是会伤害声带的 [[bili-weiyinjiaolianershu]] and the 幻音学院 article lists 压音 as the first beginner trap, describing a lasting 异物感 and hoarseness [[bili-cv8487280]]. This 2018 video is where the drill's instruction and the preservation rule were stated, and the rule is exactly what the later "my base voice is gone" accounts did not follow [[bili-lele2021]].
- Mechanically, 压音 lowers pitch and shifts the laryngeal posture — the same variables [[voice-masculinization]] covers, where the durable change for trans men comes from testosterone rather than from pressing, which is why the community treats self-taught 压音 as a stopgap.
