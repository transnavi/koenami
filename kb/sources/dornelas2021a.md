# Estudo de série de casos sobre voz e transexualidade: características acústicas de homens e mulheres trans brasileiras

## What it says
- Six participants — two trans men and four trans women, all over 18 (abstract, "Método"). Acoustic extraction with SoundForge 10.0® and the Multi-Dimensional Voice Program (MDVP-Adv), and vocal-filter analysis with the Analysis Synthesis Laboratory (CSL, Kay Pentax®).
- The formant values 「se mostraram menores quando comparados à literatura nacional e internacional」; f0 values were below what would be expected for the feminine gender and above it for the masculine.
- F0max and F0min varied widely, which the authors read as 「instabilidade fonatória」. Jitter and shimmer and the noise parameters (voice turbulence index, soft phonation index) did not match their normative ranges; the noise-to-harmonic ratio was above normal, 「sugerindo presença de ruído ou rouquidão durante a emissão」. Vocal tremor measures (Fatr and Ftri) were abnormally distributed.
- The stated conclusion is about the tools, not the speakers: 「evidenciando fragilidade dos programas de análise vocal acústica que não contemplam a heterogeneidade cultural e as variadas identidades de gênero」.

## Bearing on voice training
- A short series that makes a large point: normative acoustic software ranges are built from cisgender reference populations, so a trans speaker's output reads as "pathological" (high NHR, unstable F0) when it is simply outside the reference set ([[voice-quality]], [[machine-gender-scoring]]). The vault's caution about whole-utterance and cross-population formant comparison ([[formants]]) applies directly.
- The low formant values together with f0 below the target range is the laryngeal-source/filter split in a Brazilian sample: raising f0 alone leaves resonance where it was ([[source-filter-model]], [[voice-therapy-techniques]]).

## Notes
- n = 6, no control group, no listener data, single centre — graded low. The authors' own conclusion is a caveat about measurement, and it should not be read as an outcome.
- Claims taken from the Portuguese abstract; the individual participant values were not read.
