# 甲状軟骨形成術IV型術後に声帯開大制限をきたした性同一性障害症例

The most detailed complication report in this pass: what goes wrong when the pitch-raising operation is over-tightened, and why local anaesthesia exists.

## What it says
- The framing the authors open with: 「喉頭形成術は覚醒下で患者の音声を確認しながら行う手術であるが，最近では全身麻酔下に行っている施設がある」 — laryngeal framework surgery is meant to be done awake with the patient's voice monitored, but some institutions had moved to general anaesthesia.
- The case: a GID patient who had type IV thyroplasty elsewhere under general anaesthesia and came in with 「呼吸困難，嚥下障害，発声障害」 — breathing difficulty, swallowing difficulty and a phonation disorder.
- Findings at first visit: 「両側声帯外転障害による気道の狭小化を認めた。右声帯はほぼ正中位固定しており，左声帯は外転障害を認めた」 — airway narrowing from bilateral abduction impairment, with the right fold fixed near the midline.
- Management: 「呼吸困難の改善のために甲状軟骨形成術IV型時に甲状軟骨と輪状軟骨を近接させたナイロン糸を切断したが，近接を解除できず，気管開窓術を行った」 — the nylon suture approximating thyroid and cricoid was cut, but the approximation could not be released, and a tracheal fenestration was performed.
- The mechanism as the authors analyse it: 「本症例の病態は甲状軟骨と輪状軟骨の過度の近接により声帯の過伸展による過度の声門閉鎖と喉頭挙上障害が生じたことである」 — excessive approximation over-stretched the folds, giving excessive glottic closure and impaired laryngeal elevation.
- Course: 「甲状軟骨IV型術後1年後には声帯の可動性がわずかに改善し，呼吸困難・嚥下障害・音声障害の改善がみられた」 — slight improvement in fold mobility at one year, with improvement in all three symptoms. The authors' explanation for the recovery is that prolonged over-stretching eventually slackened the folds' tension over time.

## Bearing on voice training
- The reason the Japanese lineage insists on operating awake: type IV works by shortening the effective vibrating length, and the tightening has to be tuned against the patient's own hearing in the room ([[voice-surgery]], [[sanuki2007]], [[kyoto-ent-clinic]]).
- Airway and swallowing, not pitch, are the harms here — the operation's failure mode is over-closure, which is a different risk profile from the glottoplasty complications reported in [[aires2023a]] (granuloma, suture dehiscence) ([[voice-health]]).
- That the nylon suture could be cut without reversing the effect is itself a finding; the same irreversibility appears in the change-of-mind case in [[nishiyama2023]], where the traction thread was removed and speaking F0 did not fall.
