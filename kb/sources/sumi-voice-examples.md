# Voice Examples (Voice Resource Project wiki)

## What it says
- The page is a curated list of public voices, split 「♀️ Feminine Voices」 and 「♂️ Masculine Voices」, embedded as YouTube players, each entry carrying a short tag list and a five-step scale for four measures: vocal weight, resonance, pitch and difficulty.
- The feminine list read during this fetch: Drawing Wiff Waffles, imawonder, Nurse, Moriah Elizabeth, Jimena Reno, Karina Singh, Jackie Liu, PearlescentMoon, Shohreh Aghdashloo, 39daph, pikat, Gazes, Rainbow 6 video, The Narrator (Baldur's Gate) and GeminiTay. The masculine list has one entry, Frank Proto.
- The entries are written as uses, not as samples. Examples: Nurse — 「very low pitch / smooth / deep fem / low resonance」, 「Perfect for learning a deep feminine voice at very (120hz) low pitch.」; imawonder — 「Really good for learning brightness despite a very low pitch」; Moriah Elizabeth — 「Good for learning high pitch brightness and getting out of a low volume configuration.」; Jimena Reno — 「Good for low volume brightness and a good entry level mimicry source. Slightly breathy at times.」; pikat — 「Low pitch deep fem voice with ludicrous glottal behaviour」, difficulty 「very hard (glottal behaviour is very smooth)」.
- The pitch figures are given as intonation ranges in Hz, e.g. Drawing Wiff Waffles 「low-medium (150hz - 330hz intonation, 500hz highest)」, The Narrator 「very low(100 - 160hz, base ~140hz)」, GeminiTay 「medium (200 - 350hz)」, Frank Proto 「medium (130 - 250hz, base ~150hz)」. Difficulty carries its reason in brackets, e.g. Karina Singh 「easy-medium」, Jackie Liu 「hard - intonation, low pitch」.
- One entry is marked by language rather than accent — the Rainbow 6 video is tagged 「Japanese」 and recommended 「for voice acting deep feminine voices」 — and one by accent, PearlescentMoon 「🇦🇺 Australian accent」. One entry names an acoustic measure: GeminiTay's resonance line reads 「medium high - 900-1200 F1 (~1200 /æ/)」.

## Bearing on voice training
- This is the page that makes the mimicry instruction runnable: it supplies the clips, tagged with exactly the axes the rest of the wiki trains ([[sumi-mimicry]], [[sumi-vocal-weight]], [[sumi-resonance]]).
- Most of the entries are low-pitched and feminine, which is the counter-example set to the assumption that a female-reading voice needs a high pitch — the point the pitch and low-fem pages both make ([[sumi-pitch]], [[sumi-low-fem-voices]], [[fundamental-frequency]]).
- The tagging is the community's own ear, applied to named public people; the Hz numbers attached to them are not measurements published by anyone and should be read as the wiki's estimates ([[gender-perception]]).
