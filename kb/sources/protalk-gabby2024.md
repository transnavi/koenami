# 跨性別嗓音治療－個案經驗分享（Gabby, 博淘語言治療所）

## What it says
- A first-person FTM account (posted 2024-03-13) invited by clinic director 鄧敦弘; the writer states it is unpaid and self-funded (我並沒收受任何廣告費用，都是自費上課) and was written because FTM voice material findable online 能查到的…資料不多，且非常簡略.
- Course of therapy: 8 sessions at the clinic, chosen after another clinic admitted 對於跨性別治療不是很有把握; training started from correcting a wrong self-taught model — the writer had tried 壓低聲音(壓緊聲帶) ("pressing the pitch down, squeezing the folds") from a vague internet tip that men speak 從胸腔發音.
- What the training worked on, per the writer: using lip-resonance cues to find 胸腔共鳴 (chest resonance) once HRT had thickened the folds; switching from 頭部共鳴 (head-resonance, high-pitched) habitual production to 丹田 (abdominal) support with relaxed folds; un-feminizing habits — 拖長音 (drawn-out syllables) and 起音較高 (higher onset); home practice with a pitch app, 10 minutes of daily reading aloud and weekly recordings.
- Outcome as measured at the clinic: 音頻由180降至120 Hz over three to four months alongside HRT; by session 7–8 (HRT week 18) telephone callers were gendering him as 先生.

## Bearing on voice training
- A rare Chinese-language FTM training narrative: it documents that testosterone did the F0 work (180→120 Hz matches the testosterone trajectory in [[hormones-and-voice]] sources) while therapy targeted habits testosterone does not change — resonance placement, onset, phrase-final lengthening — exactly the behavioural residue [[voice-masculinization]] describes.
- The failed self-taught 壓低聲音 attempt is a one-case illustration of why pressed phonation is the wrong route to a lower voice ([[phonation]]).

## Limits
- A single self-reported case hosted on the clinic's own blog (evidence: community); numbers are the writer's recollection of clinic measurements; the clinic's role as publisher is a conflict of interest acknowledged by the writer's no-fee statement.
- One FTM case cannot support general claims about therapy outcomes.
