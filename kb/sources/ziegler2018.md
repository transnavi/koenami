# Effectiveness of testosterone therapy for masculinizing voice in transgender patients: a meta-analytic review

## What it says
- Nineteen articles (to October 2017) included, PRISMA guidelines.
- After one year of testosterone, a combined estimate of 21% of participants (95% CI 5–43%) had not reached cisgender male normative f0 (≤131 Hz).
- Combined estimates: incomplete voice–gender congruence 21% (10–34%); voice problems 46% (14–79%); incomplete satisfaction with voice 16% (7–28%).
- Not all patients should expect f0 to reach cisgender male norms after a year; the transition may involve voice problems.

## Bearing on voice training
- Sets expectations for trans men: testosterone usually lowers pitch, but a fifth do not reach the male range and nearly half report voice problems — training and assessment still matter.
