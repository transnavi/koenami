# Advances in the assessment and management of voice disorders（Xu W 2025）

## What it says
- The Chinese abstract's own summary of the assessment framework: 评估方面涵盖了主观听感知、客观声学、生活质量量表、频闪喉镜、空气动力学及喉肌电图等的多维体系 — subjective audio-perceptual judgement, objective acoustic measurement, quality-of-life scales, stroboscopy, aerodynamics and laryngeal electromyography, as one multidimensional system (abstract, Chinese text).
- The date the field marks itself by: 2024年我国发布《嗓音功能评估专家共识》 — "in 2024 China published the Expert Consensus for Assessment of Vocal Function", the document now in the vault as [[xu2025]].
- On treatment it lists 嗓音显微外科技术 (phonomicrosurgery), 声带注射喉成形术 (injection laryngoplasty), 喉框架手术 (laryngeal framework surgery) and 神经修复手术 (laryngeal reinnervation) as having 「改善发声及吞咽功能取得良好效果」 (the English abstract: "yielded favorable functional outcomes in phonation and swallowing"), and states that 嗓音康复治疗 (voice rehabilitation) "plays an indispensable role in comprehensive voice care" (abstract).
- The closing forecast: 未来嗓音疾病将实现更精准的评估、更有效的治疗和更全面的康复支持 (more precise assessment, more effective treatment, more comprehensive rehabilitation support).
- Author affiliation: Department of Otorhinolaryngology Head and Neck Surgery, Beijing Tongren Hospital, Capital Medical University — the department that also led the 2024 consensus.

## Bearing on voice training
- The Chinese-language statement that voice rehabilitation is not an optional extra beside surgery but part of comprehensive care — the clinical-professional version of the ordering the community guides argue for ([[community-training]], [[voice-therapy-techniques]]).
- Puts a date on the standardisation effort that [[xu2025]] embodies, and names the assessment dimensions (including quality-of-life scales) a Chinese clinic is expected to cover — the framework any trans voice service in China would be measured against ([[chinese-voice]], [[clinical-guidelines]]).

## Notes
- Three-page editorial review in the second of the two national Chinese ENT journals; graded medium as a peer-reviewed review article without primary data.
