# Voice change (English Wikipedia)

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17: "A voice change or voice mutation, sometimes referred to as a voice break or voice crack, commonly refers to the deepening of the voice of men as they reach puberty. Before puberty both sexes have roughly similar vocal pitches, but during puberty the male voice typically deepens an octave, while the female voice gradually becomes richer."
- Short description: "Deepening of the voice of people as they reach puberty."
- The extracts API (same day) adds a second sense of "voice crack": a person's voice "suddenly and unintentionally enters a higher register (usually falsetto) for a brief period", attributed to singing or talking outside the natural range, stress, fatigue, emotional tension, or the physical changes of puberty. An instance "lasts for only a moment and generally occurs less frequently as the individual grows into maturity."

## Bearing on voice training
- The folk picture this vault has to unpack: one octave down, in boys, at puberty. Measured work puts the drop late and over a longer window than that sentence, and records a milder change in girls as well ([[hollien1994]], [[harries1997]], [[pedersen1990]], [[hacki1999]]).

## Limits
- Community-edited. "Typically deepens an octave" and "gradually becomes richer" are unquantified encyclopedia wording, not measurements; prefer the longitudinal and morphological notes for numbers.
