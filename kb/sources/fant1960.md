# Acoustic Theory of Speech Production

## What it says
- Sets out the source–filter theory: the glottal source spectrum is shaped by the vocal tract transfer function, whose resonances (formants) depend on the tract shape and length.

## Bearing on voice training
- The frame behind every indicator in Koenami: pitch and voice quality belong to the source, 響き (formant spacing) to the filter.

## Notes
- Book; the standard reference for the model. Claims here are the book's thesis, not page-level citations.
