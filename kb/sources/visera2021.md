# 【女声講座】裏声も高音もいりません。必要なのは発声と『倍音』だけ

A lesson that rejects both of the moves Japanese practice usually starts with — falsetto and high pitch — and puts overtones in their place.

## What it says
- Platform metadata, 2026-09-17: title 「【女声講座】裏声も高音もいりません。必要なのは発声と『倍音』だけ【バーチャル雄嬢様】」, channel ヴィセラ, published 2021/11/28, 17,006 views.
- The claim is a substitution: 「裏声も高音もいりません」, what is needed is 「発声と『倍音』」 — phonation and harmonics, the latter set off in quotation marks by the uploader.
- The presenter's billing is 「バーチャル雄嬢様」 (a virtual male young-lady persona), which places the channel in the VTuber-adjacent performance scene rather than trans care.

## Bearing on voice training
- The overtone route to brightness is the same thing the English-language wiki describes as restoring high-frequency harmonic content, and the same thing the wiki's own practitioners add through "edge" into falsetto [[sumi-new-terminology]] [[sumi-glossary]] [[note-akagikanon202607]].
- Rejecting both 裏声 and high pitch, it argues against the two instructions a Japanese beginner meets first [[note-nuu]] [[note-ippanmtf]] — which makes it a useful marker of the disagreement inside the Japanese corpus itself [[harmonics-and-spectrum]] [[vocal-registers]].

## Limits
- Platform metadata only (oEmbed for author and title, the watch page for date and view count); the method was not examined, and the claim that overtones suffice is the uploader's.
