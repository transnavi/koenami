# 一側輪状甲状筋麻痺に対する甲状軟骨形成術Ⅳ型の効果

## What it says
- What the operation does, in the abstract's words: 「甲状軟骨形成術Ⅳ型は、外科的に輪状軟骨と甲状軟骨を接近させることで声帯の緊張を高め、話声位のピッチを上昇させる術式である。」
- The case: a patient whose complaint was that he could not produce high notes, diagnosed with unilateral cricothyroid muscle paralysis by 「喉頭ストロボスコピーおよび喉頭筋電図検査」 and operated on with type 4 thyroplasty.
- Outcome: 「術後、話声位、声域ともに改善し、最低音は維持した。」
- The mechanism claimed: 「高音発声、ピッチ調節においては、輪状甲状筋のみではなく、甲状披裂筋による stiffness の増減が大きく関与していると考えられた。」
- Keywords listed with the article: 上喉頭神経麻痺, 輪状甲状筋麻痺, 甲状軟骨形成術Ⅳ型, 甲状披裂筋, 高音発声.

## Bearing on voice training
- The non-trans mirror of the same operation: it reports the outcome that matters for training — pitch and range up, floor unchanged ([[nishimura2025]] reports the same distinction on a larger series).
- The claim that thyroarytenoid stiffness matters as much as cricothyroid tension is the physiological counterpart of the community's insistence that pitch alone does not make a voice, and that weight and range move separately ([[vocal-weight]], [[sumi-pitch]]).
- The diagnostic route it uses — stroboscopy plus laryngeal electromyography before deciding on surgery — is the level of examination a voice complaint about high notes gets in Japanese ENT practice, and it is the sort of assessment online voice training cannot replace ([[speech-language-pathology]]).

## Notes
- Single case, abstract only. The patient's occupation and any listener assessment are not in the abstract, and the diagnosis of unilateral cricothyroid paralysis is not a trans-related indication.
