# Anterior Glottic Web Formation for Voice Feminization: Experience of 27 Patients

## What it says
- Nonrandomized prospective cohort at a university hospital: 27 cases of androphonia treated with endoscopic anterior glottic web formation. The indication is described as "needed for male-to-female transsexuals, males with testicular feminization, and females with constitutional androphonia" (abstract, "Objective").
- Advantages claimed for the technique: "endoscopic surgery without skin incision and scar, outpatient surgery, potential reversibility, and low risk for vocal fold and airway damage".
- "Their pre- and postoperative mean F0 and speaking F0 were 152 and 158 and 195 and 200 Hz, respectively" — the wording of the abstract gives pre- and postoperative mean F0 as 152 → 195 Hz and speaking F0 as 158 → 200 Hz, with the differences statistically significant (P < 0.001).
- "Their pre- and postoperative acoustic analysis results were not significantly different (P > 0.05)" — jitter, shimmer and noise-to-harmonic ratio did not change significantly, unlike the laser reduction series by the same group.
- Patient self-evaluation: 20 feminine, 2 masculine and 5 neither, a patient satisfaction rate of 74%; medical students rated 85% of postoperative voice samples as feminine.
- "Seven patients (26%) needed laser reduction glottoplasty for voice feminization because they were not satisfied with the voice result."

## Bearing on voice training
- The smallest pitch gain of the Turkish glottoplasty series in this vault — about +43 Hz sustained and +42 Hz speaking — with acoustic quality left statistically unchanged, i.e. the money is bought without the perturbation cost seen elsewhere [[yilmaz2021]] [[fundamental-frequency]].
- A 26% revision rate into a different technique, and a 74% self-rated satisfaction figure, are the baseline numbers against which later techniques in the same centre are measured [[yilmaz2021]] [[yilmaz2024]].
- The indication list is a reminder that pitch-raising surgery has non-transgender indications (constitutional androphonia, testicular feminization), which the vault's framing of these operations as gender-affirming care can obscure [[voice-surgery]].

## Notes
- Prospective but nonrandomized, single centre, no untreated comparison group; satisfaction is self-rated and partly rated by medical students, not by trained listeners alone.
- The abstract's phrasing of the F0 sentence is ambiguous in the source; the reading given here (152 → 195 Hz for sustained, 158 → 200 Hz for speaking) is the one consistent with the numbers as printed.
