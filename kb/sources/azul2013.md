# Die Theorie des doing gender – eine Bereicherung für die Stimmarbeit mit Transgendern?（Azul 2013）

## What it says
- The position it sets out to criticise, in the author's German: «Therapeutisch-medizinische Ansätze im Bereich Stimme und Geschlecht basieren auf der Sichtweise, dass die menschliche Stimme von Natur aus entweder weiblich oder männlich ist, und dass sie bei KlientInnen mit Geschlechtsidentitätsstörungen mithilfe von chirurgischen, hormonellen und/oder therapeutischen Maßnahmen umgewandelt werden kann» — that a voice is naturally one sex and can be converted by surgical, hormonal or therapeutic means (abstract).
- The contradiction it names: «dieses widersprüchliche Verständnis eines einerseits biologisch determinierten und andererseits mit kulturellen Mitteln transformierbaren Stimmgeschlechts» — a voice gender that is at once biologically determined and culturally transformable.
- The theoretical move: from doing-gender theory, «Geschlecht keine biologische Gegebenheit ist, sondern ein individuell nicht kontrollierbarer Effekt kommunikativen Handelns» — gender is not a biological given but an effect of communicative action that the individual does not control (abstract). The English summary puts the consequence directly: "According to this theory gender is no longer considered a biological fact but the result of communication patterns that are beyond individual control."
- What follows from it: «ergibt sich eine Neubestimmung der theoretischen Fundierung und praktischen Umsetzung der Stimmarbeit mit Transgendern, deren Grundzüge jeweils skizziert werden», and "This approach also has an impact on the theoretical foundations and practical implementation of voice treatment with transgenders. The author outlines basic principles for voice therapy." The paper's contribution is that re-founding, not new data.

## Bearing on voice training
- The German-language statement of the critique David Azul is known for in English [[azul2015]], [[azul2018]]: the client is not carrying a voice of the wrong sex to be converted, and the listening situation that decides what a voice "is" lies outside the speaker's control. The practical consequence for training is that the target cannot be a fixed acoustic profile, because the judgement being trained against is other people's, made in context ([[gender-perception]], [[voice-impressions]]).
- It is the theoretical counterweight inside this vault to the surgical and instrumental literature: the same German-language clinical world that measures F0 and operates on the vocal folds also published this argument about what those measurements are measuring ([[voice-surgery]], [[european-languages-voice]]).
- Its «individuell nicht kontrollierbarer Effekt» is the sharpest version of the finding the perceptual studies keep producing — listeners' attributions vary by listener, context and language, and the speaker cannot see the criterion they are being scored against ([[leung2018]], [[hancock2017]]).
