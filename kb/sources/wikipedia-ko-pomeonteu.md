# 포먼트（한국어 위키백과）

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17: 포먼트(formant)는 음성과학 및 음성학에서 인간 성도의 음향 공명으로 인해 발생하는 광범위한 스펙트럼 최대값이다 — a formant is the broad spectral maximum arising from acoustic resonance of the human vocal tract; the article also notes the two definitions (production mechanism vs spectral peak in the generated sound).
- Interlanguage titles: en Formant · ja フォルマント · zh 共振峰 · th ความสั่นพ้องเด่น · es Formante.

## Bearing on voice training
- Terminology anchor for [[formants]]; 포먼트 is a loanword in Korean, so Korean learners meet the same international term — but the clinic pages usually speak of 공명 (resonance) instead ([[yeson-voice-center]]), matching the resonance-vs-formant vocabulary split in the glossary.
