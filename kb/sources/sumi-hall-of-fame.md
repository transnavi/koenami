# Hall of Fame (Voice Resource Project wiki)

## What it says
- The page's whole content is a list: 「This is a big list of really interesting or amazing voices, in no particular order.」
- The five entries read are Kiseki, Super Idol, June Banoon, Shohreh Aghdashloo and annapantsu, each an embedded YouTube player with no description text and no tags.
- Shohreh Aghdashloo is the one name that also appears in the wiki's working list ([[sumi-voice-examples]]), where she is tagged 「very low pitch / FVF constriction / vocal fry」 and 「A quite out of the ordinary voice that's really interesting and beautiful」.

## Bearing on voice training
- The page has no teaching content at all: it is the wiki's collection of voices kept for interest rather than for a training goal, which is why the entries carry no vocal weight, resonance or pitch tags ([[sumi-voice-examples]] is where the tagged, usable list lives).
- It is included here because it documents the aesthetic side of the community's listening — voices admired rather than imitated ([[voice-impressions]]).

## Limits
- Wiki page; no text beyond the list, no stated selection criteria, and the videos were not played for this fetch.
