# Clinical diagnosis and treatment of pitch-related disorders（Zhuang et al. 2025）

## What it says
- The category this paper opens with: 音调异常是多种嗓音疾病的常见表现 — pitch abnormality is a common manifestation of many voice disorders (Chinese abstract).
- The mechanisms it names as the cause: 其病理机制复杂，涉及声带张力、质量的变化以及喉神经肌肉功能的异常等 — changes in vocal-fold tension and mass, and abnormal laryngeal neuromuscular function.
- Its stated aim: 本研究旨在探讨音调异常相关疾病的生理机制及诊疗方法，为临床音调异常相关疾病的诊疗提供参考 — to examine the physiological mechanisms of pitch-related disorders and their diagnostic and therapeutic approaches, as a clinical reference.
- Authors and setting: Department of Voice Medicine, Xiamen University Zhongshan Hospital and the Xiamen Key Laboratory of Voice Medicine; a second centre in Fujian.

## Bearing on voice training
- The Chinese clinical heading a pitch-feminization request arrives under in a mainland voice clinic is 音调异常 (abnormal pitch) — the category that also holds 男声女调, the persistently high voice of a cisgender man ([[yao2025]]), and the pitch-lowering surgeries for it [[wang2009]].
- The mechanism list — tension, mass, neuromuscular function — is the ordinary voice-pathology account of pitch, with no space in it for the speaker's gender; that absence is the reason the trans-specific vocabulary in this vault (TWVQ, gender perception, [[gender-perception]]) has to come from elsewhere even when the acoustic target is identical ([[chinese-voice]], [[fundamental-frequency]]).
- It complements the general assessment framework the same journal published the same year [[xu2025a]]: one article on the framework, one on the pitch category inside it.
