# 一口气带你彻底入门伪音（吱吱吖-, 2026）

## What it says
Timestamps refer to the Bilibili upload. The quoted wording is the machine transcript, with homophone errors corrected (伪声 for 尾声/维生, 挤嗓子 for 几嗓子, 秘诀 for 坟诀, 变声 for 恋生).

- Male and female voices differ by ear as low/thick versus high/thin/bright; narrowing the voice alone or raising it alone does not produce a female voice (00:54–01:41).
- The first rule is to stop using the throat muscles: pushing with the throat is "很错误的一种发声方法", and "十个人里面有九个" male learners fail there (01:53–02:46).
- Breath drives the folds, the lungs cannot push air by themselves, and the diaphragm is "肺部下方的一个膜状肌肉" that supplies the force; coughing, blowing the nose, crying and laughing are given as everyday actions that recruit it (02:50–04:36).
- Drill 1: push a low, strong 哼 from the diaphragm with the throat relaxed, soften it, then raise it to a female height. The height is the learner's choice: higher sounds sweeter and brighter, lower more natural or mature (04:43–06:31). The result is a light, whisper-like neutral voice that has lost the male heaviness (06:36–07:26; 08:43–08:53).
- Drill 2, for breath: inhale through the nose and release a slow, even hiss; uneven speed and force are normal at first (07:29–08:32).
- About half a month of practice with the method is said to give clear results (08:33–08:42).
- Drill 3: produce 气泡音 somewhat higher than usual, like the hum of crying, then compress it upward into a thin, sharp, bright core. The fallback trigger is the sound made when clutching the stomach with a stomach ache (09:14–11:36).
- Do not merge the thin core with the soft neutral voice before each is stable, or the male voice returns (11:40–11:59).
- Resonance: add nasal resonance by voicing 嗨, and a smile that lifts the cheek muscles (抬笑肌) to brighten the voice. All of it depends on not squeezing the throat; learners who squeeze at this step are told to go back to the basics (13:18–14:30).
- A voice made entirely of 实音 sounds like a robot. The fix is 虚实结合: add the feeling of a sigh while holding the female position, without dropping back to the male voice (14:35–16:02).
- Stabilise the voice on scales, then on song melodies without lyrics, then add articulation. Speech should stay close to everyday speaking and keep the 虚实 mix: "该实的时候实，该虚的时候呢，就虚一点" (16:07–19:41).
- Early on it asks viewers to comment 666 for tips and a voice-changer app membership (00:43).

## Bearing on voice training
- The ordering (release throat effort, then breath, then register, then resonance, then breath mix, then speech) puts throat tightness first, the same hazard the community handbook ties to injury [[aogi2021]].
- The breathing account overstates the diaphragm's role: speech exhalation is regulated by the rib cage and abdominal muscles [[breathing-and-support]].
- The raised 气泡音 likely lies above fry's perceptual upper threshold of about 70 Hz [[herbst2020]]; its F0 was not measured. The target may be a lighter setting such as mix, whose open quotient falls between chest and falsetto [[lee2023]] [[vocal-registers]].
- The smile step matches lip spreading, which raised F1–F3 in a small resonance-therapy study [[carew2007]] [[formants]].
- 虚实结合 names a mix of breathy and firm phonation; see [[voice-quality]].
