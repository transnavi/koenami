## What it says
- What the record establishes: Welzel C, Röpke E, Hanson S, Berghaus A and Neumann K published a nine-page article titled above in Sprache · Stimme · Gehör, volume 26, pages 125–133, in 2002, DOI 10.1055/s-2002-34653.
- What the title asserts, and no more: the article is about the effect of cricothyroidopexy on gender-specific voice qualities in transsexualism — an outcomes question about a specific pitch-raising operation, from the group whose modified technique is reported in the Halle dissertation [[neumann2005]].

## Bearing on voice training
- It is the journal-article report of the cricothyroidopexy programme whose doctoral write-up the vault holds as [[neumann2005]]: Berghaus and Neumann are co-authors of both, and the technique is named after them («Cricothyroidopexie nach Berghaus/Neumann»).
