# Frecuencia fundamental en hombres transexuales en diferentes etapas de tratamiento hormonal

## What it says
- Chilean study measuring median speaking fundamental frequency (Mf0) in transsexual men at different stages of hormone treatment, plus minimum, maximum and range values. Praat was used on an automatic word series (abstract).
- Groups were split by dose count: 「Grupo 1: sujetos con 11 o menos dosis de testosterona. Grupo 2: sujetos con 12 o más dosis.」
- Result: 「Los valores encontrados son comparables con los esperados para hombres no transgéneros」 — the values matched what is expected for non-transgender men. And no significant differences in Mf0, Mf0min, Mf0max or range between groups 1 and 2.
- The correlation claim: 「Existe una correlación baja entre el número de dosis y la Mf0」 — a low correlation between dose count and Mf0.
- Conclusion: 「Existe una asociación entre el tratamiento hormonal y la masculinización de la voz, sin embargo, no existe evidencia que señale que a mayor número de dosis de hormonas exista una mayor masculinización de la voz」, with a call for larger studies.

## Bearing on voice training
- A precise negative result against dose-response: testosterone masculinises the voice, but more doses do not masculinise it more ([[hormones-and-voice]], [[voice-masculinization]]). That matters for anyone deciding whether an unsatisfactory voice justifies raising the dose — this study offers no support for it, on n small enough that it cannot rule it out either.
- The mean-frequency values landing in the cis-male expected range duplicates the group-mean finding of [[nimura2015]] and the pooled result of [[ziegler2018]]; the individual spread, which the abstract does not give, is where the clinical problem lives ([[irineu2025]]).

## Notes
- Sample size is not stated in the abstract, and the dose-count split (11 vs 12) is an arbitrary threshold; no randomisation, no longitudinal follow-up. Graded medium for design and instrumentation, weak for power.
- Claims taken from the abstract; the Spanish full text's tables were not read.
