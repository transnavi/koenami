# 跨性別者嗓音治療方法與評估工具之文獻回顧（林子軒、蔡昆瀛 2024）

## What it says
- From the abstract (Airiti, 2026-09-17): the review covers therapy and assessment for trans voice, because 跨性別女性（男性跨女性）由於在嗓音轉換上經歷困難…可能需要嗓音治療的介入 — MtF speakers face the harder voice transition and may need therapy; changing voice serves 心理健康與生活品質.
- Therapy methods it groups for trans women: 嗓音保健運動 (vocal hygiene exercises), 音高攀升運動 (pitch-raising exercises) and 共鳴治療 (resonance voice therapy), which 可以提升基頻和共振峰頻率 ("can raise F0 and formant frequencies"); surgery's 主要目的則在於提升基頻.
- Assessment tools it lists: 視覺類比量表 (visual analogue scales) and 跨性別女性嗓音問卷 (trans woman voice questionnaire, TWVQ) among clinically usable self-report instruments.
- Its gap statement: 國內目前文獻仍然稀少 ("domestic [Taiwan] literature is still scarce").
- Keywords: 跨性別者, 嗓音治療, 評估工具, 基頻.

## Bearing on voice training
- The first Chinese-language review note in this vault from Taiwan's SLP association journal: it confirms, in Traditional Chinese clinical vocabulary, the same method triad (hygiene, pitch, resonance) the international literature supports ([[voice-feminization-therapy]]) and the same instrument family as [[wang2022]]'s simplified-Chinese TWVQ — the two sides of the strait share a translation lineage and little else in the literature.
- The "domestic literature scarce" claim is itself evidence for [[chinese-voice]]'s gap list.

## Limits
- Narrative review (初探, preliminary) in a national professional-society journal, not a systematic review; graded medium. Only the abstract and metadata were read (doi.org negotiation + Airiti abstract page); full text behind Airiti access.
- No effect sizes extractable from the abstract.
