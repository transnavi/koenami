# 성대（한국어 위키백과）

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17: 성대의 길이는 바이올린 현처럼 목소리의 음높이에 영향을 미친다 — vocal-fold length affects pitch "like a violin string"; the folds are a pair of mucosal infoldings across the larynx, open in breathing and vibrating in speech and song, innervated by the recurrent laryngeal branch of the vagus nerve.
- Interlanguage titles: en Vocal cords · ja 声帯 · zh 声带 · th เส้นเสียง · es Cuerdas vocales.

## Bearing on voice training
- Korean terminology anchor for [[vocal-folds]]: 성대 is the term used in the clinic pages (성대단축술 "vocal fold shortening", 성대 떨림 "fold tremor", [[yeson-voice-center]]) and the length–pitch framing is the same one the surgery pages rely on.
