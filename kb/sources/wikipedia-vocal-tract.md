# Vocal tract (English Wikipedia)

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17: "The vocal tract or speech apparatus is the cavity in mammals and in birds where the sound produced at the sound source is filtered."
- Short description: "Sound-making organs in animals."
- The definition is the source–filter split in one sentence: the tract is defined by what it does to a sound made elsewhere, not by producing sound itself ([[source-filter-model]]).
