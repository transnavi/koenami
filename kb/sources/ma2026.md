# Evaluation of the Effects of Testosterone on Voice in Transmasculine Adolescents and Young Adults

## What it says
- Prospective study: adolescents and young adults assigned female at birth, naïve to testosterone and to voice therapy, enrolled before hormone initiation and followed for 12 months (abstract, "Methods").
- Fifteen enrolled; 14 (mean age 18.43 years, SD 4.31) completed the 12-month follow-up. Measured monthly: F0 in sustained vowel and connected speech, pitch range and smoothed cepstral peak prominence; plus VHI-10, Transgender Voice Questionnaire (TVQ) and PROMIS Anxiety and Depression scales.
- Timing: "Speaking F0 decreased at 3 months (mean −32.9 Hz, p = 0.033) and plateaued by 9 months (p < 0.01). Sustained vowel F0 decreased earlier at 2 months (p = 0.036)."
- "Semi-tone pitch range showed no significant change across 12 months (p = 0.47)." CPP in connected speech "decreased significantly from baseline at 3 months (p = 0.047) and improved but remained significantly lower than baseline at nine (p = 0.010) and 12 months (p = 0.003)."
- VHI-10 "transiently worsened at 3 months (+3.14, p = 0.033) but returned to baseline by 9 months (p = 0.42)"; TVQ improved from 51.3 at baseline to 26.5 at 12 months (p = 0.002); PROMIS anxiety decreased (p = 0.018) while depression did not (p = 0.56).
- Conclusion: F0 lowering begins at month 2 and stabilises by month 9, with improved voice-gender congruence and anxiety; described as "the first prospective study with a substantial adolescent cohort".

## Bearing on voice training
- Gives the timetable a young person starting testosterone can expect: the first change at about two months, most of the drop in the first three, settling by nine; pitch *range* does not grow [[hormones-and-voice]] [[puberty-voice-change]].
- The transient VHI-10 rise at three months — handicap up while F0 is falling — is the awkward middle of the change, and it resolves without intervention by month nine [[patient-reported-outcomes]].
- CPP staying below baseline at 12 months means the voice that comes back is not the voice that left: a voice-quality change accompanies the pitch change [[voice-quality]] [[voice-masculinization]].

## Notes
- n = 14 completing 12 months, single US centre (UCSF), no control group; monthly within-person measurement is the strength, small size the limit. The abstract reports no participants who started voice therapy during the year.
- Claims taken from the PubMed abstract.
