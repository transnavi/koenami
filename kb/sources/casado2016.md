# Glotoplastia de Wendler y tratamiento logopédico en la feminización de la voz en transexuales

The Spanish-language report from the Marbella group whose English-language study is in the vault as [[casado2017]]; this one is the earlier, single-arm description of the same technique plus therapy.

## What it says
- Ten trans women who underwent Wendler glottoplasty followed by voice therapy, assessed by the ENT service at Hospital QUIRÓN-Marbella with the logopedia programme at the Universidad de Málaga and the psychology faculty (abstract, author affiliations).
- The operation as described: 「de-epithelialization of the anterior third of both vocal folds; this area was sutured and the surface of both vocal folds was vaporised with laser diode」.
- Assessment before and after: F0, maximum phonation time, the transgender self-assessment questionnaire (TSEQ), and perceptual voice assessment by inter-rater agreement.
- Result: 「All the male transsexuals who wish to become women patients significantly increased their Fo (106 Hz on average) after the treatment」, with significant improvement in self-reported satisfaction and degree of voice feminization. No improvement in maximum phonation time.
- Conclusion: Wendler glottoplasty 「is a surgical procedure to contribute to feminising the voice, with good medium-term results and without noteworthy medical complications」, with the increase observed across several pre- and post-surgery control measures and with voice therapy.

## Bearing on voice training
- A 106 Hz mean gain is the largest figure in the vault's glottoplasty material, well above the 47.9 Hz sustained-/e/ gain in the Brazilian series [[aires2023a]] — different measures, different populations, and a reminder that "F0 rose" statements are not comparable across studies without the task ([[voice-surgery]], [[fundamental-frequency]]).
- The intervention is surgery **plus** therapy, and the group's own follow-up study isolates therapy's contribution by comparing patients with and without it ([[casado2017]]). Together with [[cielo2021]] and [[ferreira2025]] this is the Spanish–Brazilian–Portuguese consensus that the operation is half the treatment.

## Notes
- n = 10, no control arm, no randomisation; satisfaction and feminisation rated by inter-rater agreement but the listeners were not blinded to condition in any way the abstract states.
- Claims taken from the PubMed abstract (the article is published in both English and Spanish); the Spanish full text's tables were not read.
