# Covert Practice (Voice Resource Project wiki)

## What it says
- Premise: "At times, we may be in an environment where we can't do all the usual exercises, but still want to work on our voice. Here are some alternatives that are either quiet or don't sound like we're changing vocal gender."
- **Humming**, listed as good for "vocal weight", "clarity / breathiness", "FVF constriction", "stability", "pitch range", "reducing strain". "In general, we can just replace a pitch slide with a humming pitch slide."
- For reducing weight: "we can start low and heavy then go up in pitch, letting the voice become lighter." For adding weight: "start moderately low, slide down letting the voice get heavier to the lowest note we can do before going into fry / M0. At this point, we can 'layer' on more vocal weight by going up again without letting the voice become lighter and repeat the process. It's important to not make the voice intentionally louder in this process, since we want vocal weight to naturally get heavier rather than going into pressed phonation."
- Also: "hold a note for a while, making it less strained / effortful over time ... We can also listen out for signs of constriction or breathiness. Both have a noisy quality that interrupts the light, hollow, 'sinewave' type sound."
- **Whispering**, listed as good for "larynx height / resonance", "speech patterns", "mouth space resonance", "microbehaviours" — with a warning block: "should only be done for a few minutes a day"; "should only be quiet and light, not constricted"; "should be avoided if having FVF issues".
- The stated reason for the limit: "Whispering engages the false folds slightly, and engages them more if we make the whisper louder. This is why it's important to use a quiet, light, airy whisper. Be careful not to restrict airflow to make it quieter. Another issue is that it will dry out the vocal folds. It's generally safe to whisper for a few minutes a day however, especially if we're doing so quietly by retracting the false folds."
- Use of whispering in practice: "It can be quite hard to change the gender of a whisper since it misses components like vocal weight, meaning we rely on other markers like speech patterns and different resonance components (i.e. mouth space) to gender the sound."

## Bearing on voice training
- Supplies the quiet alternatives for people who cannot practise aloud, which is a real constraint that clinical protocols assume away [[community-training]] [[voice-health]].
- The whispering limits — a few minutes a day, quiet, light, no airflow restriction, avoid with false-fold problems — match the mechanism the wiki's own hyperadduction and questionable-exercises pages describe for false-fold constriction [[sumi-hyperadduction]] [[vocal-fatigue]].
- The observation that a whisper cannot be gendered by weight, so only mouth space and speech patterns carry it, is a neat isolation experiment for which cues a learner actually controls [[voice-impressions]].
