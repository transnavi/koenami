# Effect of Wendler glottoplasty on voice and quality of life of transgender women

## What it says
- Prospective interventional cohort of trans women who underwent Wendler glottoplasty at a Recife university hospital: seven patients, mean age 35.4, mean postoperative follow-up 13.7 months (abstract, "Results").
- Post-operative gains: sustained /e/ F0 rose by a mean 47.9 ± 46.6 Hz (p = 0.023) and speaking F0 by a mean 24.6 ± 27.5 Hz (p = 0.029).
- Everything else was flat: no significant pre/post difference in maximum phonation time, formant frequencies (F1, F2), frequency range, jitter, shimmer, or the GRBAS grade, roughness, breathiness, asthenia and strain ratings.
- Self-report moved much further than acoustics: the Trans Woman Voice Questionnaire dropped from 98.3 ± 9.2 to 54.1 ± 25.0 (p = 0.007), and mean self-perceived femininity of the voice rose from 2.8 ± 1.8 to 7.7 ± 2.4 (p = 0.008).
- Complications: one patient (14%) had a post-operative granuloma and one (14%) a premature suture dehiscence. Conclusion: 「Glottoplasty is safe and effective for feminizing the voice of transgender women. There was an increase in fundamental frequency, without aggravating other acoustic parameters or voice quality.」

## Bearing on voice training
- A precise statement of what glottoplasty does and does not do: it moves F0, it leaves formants and voice quality where they were, and the questionnaire score improves by twice what the acoustics would predict ([[voice-surgery]], [[formants]]). The filter is untouched, which is the argument for therapy alongside surgery ([[casado2016]], [[cielo2021]], [[ferreira2025]]).
- The 47.9 Hz standard deviation on sustained F0 is as important as the mean — with n = 7 a ±47 Hz spread means some individual results were close to nothing.
- The same first author's meta-analysis of glottoplasty outcomes is in the vault as [[aires2023]]; the position paper by the Brazilian laryngology academy is [[aires2026]].

## Notes
- n = 7, single centre, no control arm (a within-subject before/after design), so regression to the mean is uncontrolled; graded medium for the prospective design, not for the evidence strength.
- Claims taken from the PubMed abstract; the surgical technique details and the individual trajectories were not read.
