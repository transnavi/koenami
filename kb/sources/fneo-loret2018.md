# Concours 3 minutes - Alice - L'orthophonie pour féminiser la voix

## What it says
- An entry in the 2018 edition of the French orthophonie students' competition "3 minutes pour garder l'orthophonie en mémoire", organised by the FNEO (Fédération Nationale des Étudiants en Orthophonie), uploaded 2018-10-15. The description names the entry: "Alice Loret - L'orthophonie pour féminiser la voix", and points to the competition site fneo.fr/concours-2018/ for voting.
- The format is fixed — three minutes to present one orthophonie topic to a general audience — so the video is an explanation of what voice feminization is and what the profession does with it, aimed at fellow students and the public rather than at clients.

## Bearing on voice training
- The student-competition layer of the French field, and a direct predecessor of the practice report by the same author one year later [[loret2019]]: the same orthophoniste, Alice Loret, first presenting féminisation de la voix to her profession's national student body and then publishing a five-patient protocol trial in Glossa.
- Documents that French voice feminization entered orthophonie training as a named subject by 2018, the year before the French TVQ adaptation was published [[morsomme2019]].

## Limits
- A competition entry, not a study; no outcome data. The video was not transcribed for this note, so what is recorded here is the entry's subject and occasion, not its argument. Graded community.
- The competition site and its current hosting were not checked; the entry is attested through the video and its description.
