# 女声ボイトレ「進捗レポート：2026年７月23日」

A self-measured training log: five voice configurations the writer can now produce, each recorded, judged by an app, and described with its spectrum.

## What it says
- Six months of training (started the previous year, with the previous month showing progress), driven by voice dysphoria from a life spent barely speaking and a narrow range. The five configurations named: 「イケボ声（地声、低め、脱力：本来の私の声）」, 「カエル声（地声、中間、エッジ：昔の普段の声、難しい事を考えるとこれになる）」, 「フニャオス声（地声、高め、脱力：最近のマイブーム）」, 「お姉さん声（裏声、低め、脱力：テンションが上がるとこの声になる）」, 「ロリ声（裏声、高め、エッジ：裏声にエッジ入れるの出来るようになった）」.
- Method of judgement: 「声の判定には最近人気の『こえ、はかる』を使わせて頂きました」 — an app that judges masculine versus feminine using F0 and formants — and Audacity for spectral analysis.
- The writer's own history with the voice: 「声自認が声変わり前のまま思春期を迎えたので、低い声が鬱で、エッジを利かせてF1を強調して高めに聞こえる声を長年使ってきました」 — years of using a pressed, F1-emphasised voice to sound higher, which a teacher then told them had to be undone first (「一旦声をリセットして癖を抜かないと、声の開発が出来ない」).
- On the M2 material: 「私の裏声は、そのままだと倍音が少ない正弦波に近い音になります。しかし、女性の声は豊かな倍音が響く明るい声です。そこで、裏声にエッジを効かせて倍音を入れてみたらどうだろうと練習してみたら、ある程度それっぽくなってきました。しかし、この発声法では本来のCT筋だけでなく、TA筋も使ってる感じで持久力が低いです。２～３分使うと声がおかしくなります。」
- On the app's limits: even with a high F0 the AI did not return a female judgement for the お姉さん声 (「F0が高くてもAIにはバレてますね、女性判定になりませんでした」).
- An AI voice-changer experiment (RVC) is reported with FFT comparisons: with a converted sample the writer found F0 near 200 Hz in both real and converted voice, the real voice emphasising around 400 Hz, and 「最も違うのは600Hz以上の高音域で12dbくらいの差があります。これは音量で言うと４倍に相当します。このあたりが声の印象を決めるのかもしれません」. The writer's conclusion is that the converted voice is useful as 「フォルマントやトーンの参考データ」 and that 「話してる本人の声自認がボイチェンに引っ張られて、話し方やトーンが影響を受けます」.

## Bearing on voice training
- A rare Japanese account that publishes its numbers: the configs are labelled by register (地声 / 裏声), weight-like quality (脱力 / エッジ) and pitch, which is the community's own vocabulary for the same axes the clinical sources call register and vocal weight [[vocal-registers]] [[vocal-weight]] [[japanese-community]].
- The "edge" account is the community's standard route to brightness: adding pressed closure to a thin M2 to restore harmonics — and the writer's own report that it uses the TA muscle and lasts only two to three minutes is the community's strain warning in practice [[voice-health]] [[vocal-fatigue]].
- The AI-voice-changer section is the clearest Japanese write-up of what such tools do to the speaker rather than the listener: the writer says the user's own voice self-perception is pulled along by the converted output [[machine-gender-scoring]] [[tools]].

## Notes
- One practitioner's log. Judgements come from a consumer app (「こえ、はかる」) rather than a controlled rating, and the acoustic readings come from Audacity FFT screenshots; the writer explicitly marks the voice labels as rough and unintentional (「狙ってそういう声を出せてる認識は全く無いです」). The account says the writer has been training since the previous year, apparently with a teacher.
