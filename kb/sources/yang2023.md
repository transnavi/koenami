# The fundamental frequency (f0) distribution of Korean speakers in a dialogue corpus using Praat and R

## What it says
- Measured the f0 distribution of 2,740 Korean speakers in a dialogue speech corpus with Praat and R, trimming extreme values by the interquartile f0 range of each speaker's intonational phrases (abstract).
- Across all speakers the mean f0 was 185 Hz and the median 187 Hz; "the pitch values of daily conversations varied in the range of 238 Hz" (abstract).
- By sex: "distinct median f0 values: 114 Hz for males and 199 Hz for females", with a significant t-test between groups; male-group skewness 1.24 and kurtosis 5.21 (leptokurtic), female skewness 0.58 and kurtosis 3.88 (abstract).
- Regression of median f0 on age gave a slope of 0.15 for the male group and –0.586 for the female group — "a divergent relationship"; the author concludes a normative f0 distribution by Korean age and sex group is now examinable, but "more rigorous data might be required to define a relation between age and f0 values" (abstract).

## Bearing on voice training
- The largest recent Korean-language F0-by-sex reference found for this vault: Korean cis men's conversational median ~114 Hz and cis women's ~199 Hz give Korean learners the domestic numbers to set targets against, alongside the English-language norms in [[fundamental-frequency]] sources such as [[hillenbrand2009]].
- The gap (≈85 Hz between medians) is comparable to the male–female spacing reported for other languages [[titze1989]].

## Notes
- Corpus speech (dialogue), not sustained vowels or read passages; medians of intonational phrases, so values are not directly interchangeable with reading-passage speaking F0; KoreaScience full text unreachable in this session — claims are from the abstract.
