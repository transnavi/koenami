# Belting (music) (English Wikipedia)

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17: "Belting is a technique of singing in which a singer carries their chest voice above their break or passaggio with a proportion of head voice. Belting is sometimes described as \"high chest voice\" or \"mixed voice\". It is often described as a vocal register, but is rather a descriptive term for the use of a register."
- So the encyclopedia refuses to count belting as a register: it is a way of using chest/mix above the break.

## Bearing on voice training
- Carrying chest above the break is the opposite of the usual trans-feminine task (lightening as pitch rises). It is the CCM skill [[saldias2019]] compared with hyperfunctional dysphonia. The article's "proportion of head voice" is the mix claim, not a measured TA/CT ratio.
