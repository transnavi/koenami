# 음성여성화수술의 최신지견

## What it says
- The article is a 특집 (feature) review by 김형태 of 예송이비인후과 음성센터 / 공연예술의학음성연구소 ([[yeson-voice-center]]), published in 임상이비인후과 21(2) 184–190, 2010, open access under CC BY-NC, with a published-online date of 2020-05-31 on the journal's site.
- Keywords listed with the article: 음성여성화수술; 윤상갑상접근술; 성대단축술, 성대적응술; 성문횡격막 — voice feminization surgery, cricothyroid approximation, vocal-fold shortening and adaptation, glottal web.
- The opening of its 요약 frames the subject through singing-range history: 「일반적으로 남성이 낼 수 있는 가장 높은 음역을 테너(Tenor)라고 하며, 가장 낮은 음역의 소리를 베이스(Bass), 테너와 베이스 사이의 음역을 바리톤(Baritone)이라고 하며, 여성 또는 어린이의 가장 높은 음역을 소프라노(Soprano)라고 한다.」 It then notes that because women were barred from singing in medieval churches, 「남성이 여성의 목소리인 알토와 메조소프라노 소리를 내야 했으므로 이는 카스트라토에 의해서 가능하였고, 현대에서는 카운터 테너에 의해서 가능하다」, and that the castrato voice was described as 「영혼의 목소리 또는 신의 목소리, 천상의 목소리」 and 「여성이 낼 수 없는 여자의 목소리」.

## Bearing on voice training
- The earliest Korean-language review of voice feminization surgery in this vault, by the surgeon whose later work ([[kim2017]], [[kim2020]], [[kim2024]]) the vault holds in English; this one documents the frame the Korean field started from in 2010.
- Its keyword list is the useful part: 윤상갑상접근술 (cricothyroid approximation) alongside 성대단축술 and 성문횡격막 (glottal web) — the operations that were on the table, of which the vault's other Korean material now centres on VFSRAC ([[kim2017]], [[yeson-voice-center]]).
- The singing-historical opening is the argument that a male body producing a female voice is an old practice rather than a new medical one, which is the same point the 両声類 material makes for Japan ([[kitahara2017]], [[wikipedia-ja-ryouseirui]]) and the community makes for itself ([[singing-and-voice]]).

## Limits
- A 2010 feature review; the 요약 is a narrative summary of the field with no systematic search, and only its opening paragraphs were readable on the page fetched. The body of the article was not read, and nothing here is a clinical claim from it.
