# 声带（中文维基百科）

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17: 聲帶或稱聲腔、嗓子，是位于喉部的两瓣左右对称的膜状解剖結構，主要功能是振動以發聲。聲帶肌肉受迷走神经的控制，可以調整聲帶的張力，以改變振動頻率。 — two symmetric membranous structures in the larynx whose vibration produces voice; vagus-nerve-controlled muscles adjust tension and so vibration frequency.
- The extract states the open/closed cycle plainly: 呼吸时聲帶張開…憋氣時聲帶關閉。说话，唱歌等發聲动作时，聲帶通过与空气的相互作用产生振动， and that vocal-fold vibration is the sound source of voiced speech sounds (語音中浊音的声源).
- Interlanguage titles: en Vocal cords · ja 声帯 · ko 성대.

## Bearing on voice training
- The plain-language entry point for Chinese readers to [[vocal-folds]]; the fold–nerve–tension wording matches what the 伪声 tutorials call 声带闭合 (glottal closure) and what the surgery pages call 声带截短 ([[mtfwiki-vocal-surgery]]).
