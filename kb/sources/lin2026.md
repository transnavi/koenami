# Intensive telepractice voice feminization for trans women — Taiwan pilot (Lin CA et al. 2026)

## What it says
- A Taiwan-based pilot (University of Taipei speech-language pathology program with Changhua Christian Hospital and Mackay Memorial Hospital ENT departments; abstract, PubMed, 2026-09-17): 10 trans women enrolled, 9 completing 12 sessions of resonant voice therapy combined with Vocal Function Exercises and voice hygiene, all within 4 weeks, delivered via telepractice.
- Outcomes: significant post-treatment improvements in acoustic measures "such as fundamental frequency and semitones", in TWVQ scores, in auditory-perceptual ratings, and in self-rated voice femininity.
- The authors' own limitations: single-group design without a control, small sample; they call for randomized, follow-up trials with larger samples comparing treatment modalities.

## Bearing on voice training
- The first Mandarin/Taiwan-context therapy trial note in this vault: it packages the same components the Taiwan review names (共鳴治療 + 嗓音保健, [[lin2024]]) with Vocal Function Exercises ([[stemple1994]]) into an intensive 4-week telepractice format — evidence that intensive delivery works in a Chinese-speaking setting, complementing the Western-series in [[voice-feminization-therapy]].
- TWVQ as an outcome measure again ties to [[patient-reported-outcomes]] and [[wang2022]]'s translation lineage.

## Limits
- Pilot, single-group, n = 9 completers; no control group; no follow-up beyond post-treatment; the language of the therapy sessions (Mandarin or Taiwanese-accented Mandarin) is not stated in the abstract.
