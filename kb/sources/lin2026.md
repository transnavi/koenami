# Voice Outcomes of Intensive Voice Feminization Therapy for Transgender Women Delivered via Telepractice: Pilot Study

## What it says
- Pilot study of an intensive voice feminization program delivered entirely via telepractice: 10 transgender women enrolled, 9 completed (abstract).
- The protocol: "12 sessions of resonant voice therapy in combination with Vocal Function Exercises and voice hygiene … completed within 4 weeks" (abstract, methods) — a compressed, intensive course rather than the usual weekly cadence.
- Significant post-treatment improvements in acoustic measures including fundamental frequency and semitones, in Trans Woman Voice Questionnaire (TWVQ) scores, in auditory-perceptual ratings and in self-rated voice femininity (abstract).
- Authors' conclusion: preliminary evidence that transgender women benefit from intensive voice therapy by telepractice, "with a more feminine voice and better self-perceived voice-related quality of life"; limitations are the single-group design without control and the small sample (abstract).

## Bearing on voice training
- A worked example of what a course of therapy looks like — 12 sessions in 4 weeks, resonant voice therapy plus VFE plus hygiene — and that the whole course can be delivered remotely with measured acoustic and questionnaire gains ([[voice-feminization-therapy]], [[speech-language-pathology]]).
- The 1-of-10 dropout is the only attrition datum the pilot offers; bigger courses' drop-out rates remain a gap.

## Notes
- Taiwanese team; single group, no control, n = 9 completers; TWVQ used as the patient-reported outcome.
