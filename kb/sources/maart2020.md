# Non-binair | Logopedie om mijn stem te verlagen

## What it says
- A Dutch-language vlog uploaded 2020-11-01 by Maart, who introduces themself in the description: "Mijn naam is Maart, 22 jaar en ik ben non-binair. Op mijn kanaal post ik video's over hoe ik het ervaar om non-binair te zijn en waar ik allemaal tegen aan loop. Mijn voornaamwoorden zijn: die, hen of hij."
- The episode is about speech-language therapy with the goal of *lowering* the voice: "Deze week ga ik het hebben over logopedie." The title states the direction plainly — "Logopedie om mijn stem te verlagen" — and no testosterone is mentioned as the route.
- The description links a voice app on Google Play and a documentary about being non-binary that the creator took part in, along with a document explaining non-binary identity.

## Bearing on voice training
- The vault's first Dutch-language community account of behavioural masculinization without hormones, the direction [[papeleu2026]] later tested in a Dutch-speaking randomized trial — a non-binary person speaking in the first person about the same work those results describe in aggregate.
- A non-binary target, not a passing one: the goal stated is a lower voice of one's own rather than a man's, which is the gap [[nonbinary-voice]] records for outcome studies. The Dutch register it uses is the everyday one — logopedie, stem verlagen — while the clinical papers say masculinisatie [[leyns2023a]] and the German guides say Stimmanpassung [[dgti-stimmanpassung]].

## Limits
- One person's vlog; no measures, no follow-up, no clinician named, and the video was not transcribed for this note. Graded community.
- Uploaded 2020, before the Ghent masculinization trial was published; nothing here reports an outcome.
