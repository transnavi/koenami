## Bearing on voice training
- 少年音 is the middle rung of the male 声线 ladder the dataset builds: 正太音 (pre-pubescent, [[bili-ningyunwenhua]]), 少年音 (adolescent, here), 青年音 [[bili-wuming]], 叔音 [[bili-xiacanglan]], 攻音 [[bili-baishu]]. On the male side the rungs differ mainly in pitch, weight and formant placement rather than in vocal-fold anatomy ([[vocal-registers]], [[vocal-weight]]).
- A 少年音 target is also the one a female speaker reaches most easily, which is why it recurs as an entry point in 女伪男 teaching, e.g. 轶-响's 从0到1 series [[bili-yixiang2024]].
