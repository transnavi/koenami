# 通常発話の声質に関連した日常表現語：聴取評価による抽出

## What it says
- Selected 20 well-understood everyday Japanese voice-quality words from 137 candidates and, by listening tests and factor analysis, found three factors: 明瞭性 (clarity), 迫力性 (power) and 高さ・若さ (pitch/youth) — as summarised on Koenami's method page.
- Listeners could not distinguish 「鼻にかかった」 from 「鼻の詰まった」, so nasality forms one axis (method page).

## Bearing on voice training
- Source of the Japanese vocabulary for Koenami's listening-review scales (澄み, 張り, 太さ, 鼻声, 明るさ).
