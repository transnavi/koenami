# 후두（한국어 위키백과）

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17: 후두(喉頭, larynx)는 기관 입구의 특수한 구조 — the larynx is the specialized structure at the tracheal entrance, part of the airway and a closing device that keeps food out of it; 인간의 발성기가 위치하며 — the human phonation organ sits here; sound arises when the glottis narrows and vibrates as air passes.
