## What it says
- Observational, descriptive-correlational cross-sectional study of 30 trans women in Chile's Región Metropolitana (Santiago), using the Trans Woman Voice Questionnaire (TWVQ) after a clinical-history questionnaire; ethics approval GP-70/2018, Facultad de Medicina Clínica Alemana – Universidad del Desarrollo.
- Opens by stating that no Chilean baseline existed: "En Chile no se cuenta con antecedentes sobre esta temática" — the paper presents itself as the first Chilean study of the question.
- Scores were analysed as a total and by three sub-items (ansiedad y evitación / anxiety and avoidance; función vocal / vocal function; identidad vocal / vocal identity).
- Self-perception was affected most by vocal function, then anxiety and avoidance, then vocal identity, in order of perceived difficulty.
- Reports "evidencia preliminar de que la autopercepción de la voz podría asociarse con la asistencia al especialista de la voz y con el tiempo de terapia hormonal cursado" — preliminary evidence that self-perception may be associated with having seen a voice specialist and with time on hormone therapy.

## Bearing on voice training
- Supplies the three-factor reading of the TWVQ (function / anxiety-avoidance / identity) in Spanish, a finer grain than the single total score used in [[mora2018a]] and [[casadomorente2021]].
- The hormone-duration association is an association in a cross-section, not evidence that oestrogen changes a trans woman's voice; [[ziegler2018]] and [[nygren2016]] cover the asymmetry between oestrogen and testosterone.

## Notes
- n = 30, one metropolitan region, convenience sample; no acoustic measures and no listener ratings.
- The three-item structure is the authors' proposal applied to their own data, not an independently validated factor solution.
