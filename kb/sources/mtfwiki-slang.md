# 通俗用语表（MtF.wiki）

## What it says
- Self-description: 以下解释均为一般化理解，仅供参考 ("the explanations are generalized understandings, for reference only").
- 药娘: 该称呼可能具有冒犯意味，尤其是在被"外人"使用时。指通过HRT治疗使自身女性化的人群。药娘通常是跨性别女性，但也有非二元性别、伪娘等使用激素。MtF不都是药娘，药娘不一定是MtF。 — possibly offensive, especially from outsiders; people feminizing through HRT; overlapping with but not equal to MtF. (Contrast the neutral Baidu Baike entry [[baike-yaoniang]].)
- 伪娘/女装大佬: 指打扮、着装、行为女性化的男性，一般指女装男子 — feminine-presenting males, usually crossdressers; the page notes some who call themselves 伪娘 are trans women who can accept a male body.
- 木桶饭, 飞天猫, 满天星: homophone/pinyin-rebus slang for M t F (Mù Tǒng Fàn) used to dodge censorship; parallel coinages exist for the other directions.
- Pass: 形容词。指性别表达取得第三者视角认同 — being gendered correctly by a third party.
- GD: Gender Dysphoria, 性别不安/性别焦虑. 糖: slang for the hormone medications themselves.
- Social-type compounds: 家长党 (supported by parents), 年龄党 (started HRT in early puberty), 天赋党 (先天身体条件更靠近性别认同 — naturally closer to one's identified gender) versus 天残党 (先天条件差距大), 手术党/术娘 (post-SRS), 中奖党 (deemed offensive — those who "won a lottery" of rare intersex traits found on testing; the page says 不建议使用).
- 跨儿 appears throughout as the community's affectionate umbrella ("trans folks"), used the same way in bilibili tags ([[bili-luna2023]]).

## Bearing on voice training
- The lexicon the Chinese trans community actually uses for the topics this vault covers: "pass" is the goal that 嗓音女性化 serves ([[mtfwiki-chenzhen]] uses pass directly), 天赋党/天残党 encode the belief that anatomy (vocal-tract length, fold length) limits outcomes — the same belief 陈臻 states clinically (共振峰结构主要受制于声道的解剖结构).
- Censorship-driven coinages (木桶饭) explain why searching for this material in Chinese needs the right keywords.

## Limits
- Community-edited slang list; connotations shift and the page itself flags several terms as offensive or not recommended; entries are quoted as the page states them.
