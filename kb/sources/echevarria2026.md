# Preliminary Analysis of Candidate Items for an Abbreviated 11-Item Trans Woman Voice Questionnaire

## What it says
- Retrospective analysis of TWVQ (formerly Transsexual Voice Questionnaire Male to Female) responses from 26 trans women presenting for initial evaluation at Mayo Clinic; each of the 30 items scored on a four-point Likert scale (abstract, "Methods").
- Items were ranked by how often they drew a severe response ("usually or always") and by item–total Pearson correlation.
- "Twelve items were endorsed as severe by at least 40% of respondents, with the highest frequencies observed among statements related to vocal identity."
- Item–total correlations ranged r = 0.21 to 0.81; items with low correlations were dropped "even if they had moderate endorsement frequency". Full questionnaire Cronbach's α = 0.95.
- The proposed 11-item version held α = 0.89 "while retaining representation across vocal identity, anxiety and avoidance, and vocal function domains".
- The authors are explicit about the limit: "Further validation in larger independent cohorts is required before clinical implementation, and a more detailed analysis of a larger patient cohort may yield a different short-form questionnaire."

## Bearing on voice training
- Names which complaints carry the burden in a clinical population: the items most often rated severe are those about vocal identity, not about vocal function [[patient-reported-outcomes]].
- A short form aimed at "screening and monitoring in time-limited clinical settings", with the full TWVQ kept for comprehensive assessment — practical for anyone tracking progress weekly [[voice-feminization-therapy]].

## Notes
- n = 26 from one US tertiary centre, exploratory, no external validation; item selection on the same sample that would need to confirm it, so the α = 0.89 is optimistic. The paper's own title calls the analysis preliminary.
- Claims taken from the PubMed abstract.
