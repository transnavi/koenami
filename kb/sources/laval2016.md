# Féminisation/masculinisation de la voix et le rôle de l'orthophoniste

## What it says
- A teaching capsule from the Clinique d'enseignement en orthophonie at Université Laval, produced "en partenariat avec le CIUSSS de la Capitale-Nationale", uploaded 2016-02-24 on the university's medicine faculty channel. It is one of a series in which the clinic explains "les causes et conséquences de diverses problématiques de communication et le rôle de l'orthophoniste dans l'évaluation et l'intervention auprès des personnes qui présentent ces problématiques" (video description).
- The presenter, as the description names her, is "Isabelle Savard, orthophoniste et chargée d'enseignement au programme d'orthophonie".
- The subject is placed in the same series as aphasia, stuttering in adults and children, dementia, dyslexia, hyperfonction vocale and language delay — that is, as one communication problem among the ordinary caseload of a Québec orthophonie teaching clinic, not as a specialty apart.

## Bearing on voice training
- The Québec end of the francophone field, and the only Canadian source this vault holds on gendered voice: it attests that a Québec university clinic taught voice feminization and masculinization, and the orthophoniste's role in them, before any French-language TVQ existed [[morsomme2019]] and three years before the French practice report in Glossa [[loret2019]].
- Its framing of the orthophoniste as the professional who evaluates and intervenes is the same division of labour the French community and professional pages describe [[wikitrans-voix]] [[alloortho-voix]].

## Limits
- A teaching capsule; the description states the topic and the presenter but not the capsule's content, and the video itself was not transcribed for this note, so no clinical claim is recorded from it.
- The Laval faculty page and the CIUSSS de la Capitale-Nationale site both failed to load on 2026-09-17, so the clinic's own written description of this problematique could not be checked.
