# A VOZ NO PROCESSO DE CONSTRUÇÃO DA IDENTIDADE DE GÊNERO DE TRANSEXUAIS

## What it says
- The premise: even when a trans person's appearance is read as their gender, 「essa imagem é frequentemente desfeita ao falar」 — speaking undoes it. Hence the interest in vocal adaptation.
- Method: a questionnaire applied at a travesti and transsexual association in Alagoas, covering 「satisfação vocal, desconforto ao falar e experiências de preconceito relacionadas à voz」. Of 30 participants, 11 answered the questionnaire adequately (abstract).
- Result: most reported discomfort with a voice that 「não corresponde à identidade de gênero」, causing dysphoria and social difficulty. 「Nenhum participante realizou procedimento médico ou terapia fonoaudiológica para adequação vocal.」 Not one of the respondents had had any medical procedure or speech therapy for voice.
- The authors' conclusion: 「A terapia fonoaudiológica é essencial para adequar a voz à identidade de gênero」 — and they pair it with a call for 「políticas públicas inclusivas e o acesso a serviços de saúde adequados」.

## Bearing on voice training
- The number is the finding: eleven respondents, none of whom had had any voice intervention, all reporting voice-related dysphoria and prejudice. It is the access side of the same problem [[nemr2026]] describes from the clinical floor and [[rosa2026]] reviews from the literature ([[transgender-care-context]], [[voice-health]]).
- Methodologically it is a small local survey in a lightly-reviewed journal, but "no participant had had treatment" is the kind of population fact the vault's outcome studies cannot supply — every therapy trial is by construction done on people who got therapy.

## Notes
- 11 usable responses out of 30; a single association in one state; questionnaire, no acoustic measures, no comparison group. Graded low.
- Claims taken from the Portuguese abstract; the questionnaire items and the response distribution were not read.
