# Investigating Validity and Reliability of the Turkish Version of Transsexual Voice Questionnaire (Male to Female)

## What it says
- Turkish validation of the TVQMtF, carried out entirely at Hacettepe University, Ankara — Speech and Language Therapy, Psychiatry and Otolaryngology departments together (author affiliations, PubMed record). The abstract notes the TVQMtF "was previously translated into 12 languages".
- 41 trans women completed the TVQMtF-TR, a self-perceptions-of-voice-femininity questionnaire and the WHOQOL-BREF-TR; 58.5% repeated the TVQMtF-TR after two weeks (abstract, "METHOD").
- Cronbach's α = 0.972; item–total correlations 0.323–0.876; intraclass correlation coefficient = 0.931 (abstract, "RESULTS").
- Strong negative correlation between TVQMtF-TR score and self-perceived voice femininity; significant negative correlations with the psychological and environmental WHOQOL domains, none with the social and physical domains.
- Concludes the Turkish version is "a valid and reliable tool".

## Bearing on voice training
- Completes the vault's TVQ translation set alongside [[dacakis2013]] (original), [[mora2018a]] (Spanish), [[salm2020]] (German), [[robotti2022]] (Italian), [[schwarz2017]] (Brazilian Portuguese) and [[morsomme2019]] (French): Turkish-speaking clients can now be scored on the same instrument.
- The same weak-to-absent correlation with generic quality-of-life domains seen in [[mora2018a]] — voice-related distress is its own construct.

## Notes
- Single-centre sample (n = 41) recruited where the Hacettepe gender programme operates; test–retest on 24 participants.
- No acoustic or listener-perception measures in this study.
