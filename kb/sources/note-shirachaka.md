# イェソン耳鼻咽頭科 声帯短縮術 体験レポ 男声→女声

A long first-person account of travelling from Japan to Seoul for 声帯短縮術 (vocal-fold shortening) at the Yeson ENT clinic in Gangnam. Most of the free text is travel logistics; the clinical detail is deliberately held back.

## What it says
- The stated motive is that the information did not exist in one place: 「韓国の病院を調べても、予約方法、航空券、ホテル、術前検査、手術当日の流れ、術後の生活について、情報がない。」 The post covers 「渡韓前の下準備から手術、術後、帰国、術後の3ヶ月おき経過まで」.
- The current schedule at Yeson, per the writer: 「今現在は入国(1日)－初診(1日)-手術(1日)-再診＆帰国(1日)で最短4日間の滞在で手術が行なえます」 — the clinic's own website still saying eight days is described as out of date.
- Pre-operative day (2日目): blood tests, nothing to eat or drink for four hours beforehand (「検査の4時間前から絶飲食」), full payment of the surgical fee in advance (via Wise), passport for identity confirmation, and 「アクセサリー・ネイルオフは必須です」. Hormone medication is normally to be stopped two weeks before, which the writer says they did not do, adding 「自己責任でお願いします」 and that they would have stopped for a more invasive operation.
- Post-operative restriction, given as the hardest part: 「声オペ後に話しかれても2か月は発声禁止という制約と誓約」 — no phonation for a period the writer puts at two months, and soft food (お粥) recommended afterwards. They also warn against carrying anything heavy after the operation because 「オペ後に重いものを運ぶと呼吸が乱れて声帯近辺の筋肉が拘縮するからです」.
- Cost and logistics given as figures: return airfare 60,230 yen in the September peak season, and a one-month-out Tuesday-night booking pattern; the writer's own advice is not to use low-cost carriers because the cancellation policy is too painful (「オペをキャンセルした場合、2年間は手術予約が取れません」).
- The clinical sections — the details of the day-2 tests, the operation itself and the day-4 re-examination — are explicitly behind a paid section: 「イェソンでの2日目検査の詳細等は有料記事ゾーンにて本音で語りますね」, and the same for the surgery day and the follow-up.

## Bearing on voice training
- Records the practical shape of medical travel for voice surgery from Japan: a four-day round trip, a fee paid up front, no phonation for weeks, and a two-year booking penalty for cancelling [[voice-surgery]] [[transgender-care-context]].
- The author's own post-operative caution — heavy lifting disrupting breathing and 「声帯近辺の筋肉」 — is the patient-side version of the voice-rest advice the clinical sources give after glottoplasty [[voice-health]].
- Names the destination clinic and its district (Yeson ENT, Gangnam, Cheongdam-dong), the Korean centre this community most often travels to; the vault already has a note on it [[yeson-voice-center]] and on the Thai alternative [[kamol-hospital]] [[korean-voice]].
