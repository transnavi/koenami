# Chinese Expert Consensus for Assessment of Vocal Function (2024) (Xu W et al. 2025)

## What it says
- The first expert consensus on vocal-function assessment in China (abstract, PubMed, 2026-09-17), produced by the Chinese Medical Association's voice subspecialty group and the 中华耳鼻咽喉头颈外科杂志 (Chinese Journal of Otorhinolaryngology Head and Neck Surgery) laryngopharyngology group through literature search, quality evaluation, two meetings and two rounds of questionnaire voting.
- Scope of assessment it standardizes: baseline patient information, subjective and objective assessment of voice quality, voice-related quality-of-life instruments, laryngoscopy and vocal-fold vibration evaluation (stroboscopy), aerodynamic analysis, and laryngeal neuromuscular electrophysiological function evaluation.
- Intended audience: voice specialists, otolaryngologists and primary-care practitioners in China.

## Bearing on voice training
- The measurement backbone that mainland 跨性别 voice care plugs into: the same assessment domains mtf.wiki tells surgery seekers to expect (嗓音检查: 高/低音, 音域, 发声方式, 最长音时间, 基频; 喉镜检查, [[mtfwiki-vocal-surgery]]) and the same journal whose series report the glottoplasty outcomes ([[zhang2023cn]], [[sun2025]], [[chen2024cn]]).
- Not a transgender-specific guideline — it says nothing about gender-affirming voice — but it is the instrument standard any Chinese-language trans-voice study or clinic will be read against.

## Limits
- The consensus is published in English in J Voice; the Chinese-language original in the Zhonghua journal was not fetched. Graded high as a validated national guideline; abstract-level reading only, so the specific recommended instruments and cutoffs are not recorded here.
