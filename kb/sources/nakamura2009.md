# 痙攣性発声障害症例の手術方法の検討―性同一性障害による音声障害を有する症例について―

One patient with two voice diagnoses at once — adductor spasmodic dysphonia and a gender-related pitch complaint — treated with two operations in sequence, with the numbers reported at each step.

## What it says
- The case: a 32-year-old woman with GID as an underlying condition, who had had gender-affirming surgery in March 2005 and had already changed her legal registration. She had long been troubled by a low speaking F0 (SFF) and had developed 「声のつまり」 — a squeezed, blocked voice — over several years (abstract).
- Measurements at first presentation (August 2007): 「モーラ法にて11/21，SFF 133.3 Hz，最低音124.2 Hz，最高音418.2 Hz，MPT 21秒」 — 11 of 21 morae affected on the mora method, SFF 133.3 Hz, lowest 124.2 Hz, highest 418.2 Hz, maximum phonation time 21 s.
- The plan, chosen to address both complaints at once: 「TA切除術はつまりの改善とピッチ上昇どちらも期待できる。まずTA切除術を先行させ，ピッチ上昇効果不十分の際には甲状軟骨形成術4型 (4型) を追加するという手術を予定した」 — thyroarytenoid resection first (it can help both the squeeze and the pitch), with type IV thyroplasty held in reserve.
- Step one (October 2007): at six months 「モーラ法では0/21でつまりは取れたが，SFF 200.1 Hz，最低音151.1 Hz，最高音364.1 Hz，MPT 12秒」 — the squeeze was gone, SFF up to 200.1 Hz, but maximum phonation time down from 21 s to 12 s. She asked for more pitch.
- Step two (May 2008, type IV added): at one month 「モーラ法は0/21，SFF 244.8 Hz，最低音213.0 Hz，最高音322.4 Hz，MPT 8秒」 — SFF 244.8 Hz, but MPT down to 8 s and the top of the range narrowed from 418 Hz to 322 Hz.
- Conclusions: 「TA切除術はSDの改善に対しては有効であったが，GIDのための低音化音声障害に対しては効果不十分であった。そのため4型を追加し良好な結果が得られた。TA切除術と4型は併施可能な術式であった。」

## Bearing on voice training
- The clearest Japanese illustration that every gain in speaking F0 here was paid for elsewhere: maximum phonation time fell 21 → 12 → 8 s and the upper range narrowed by roughly 96 Hz as SFF rose 133 → 245 Hz ([[fundamental-frequency]], [[voice-health]]). It is the case-level version of what the cohort studies report as "no significant change in maximum phonation time" ([[aires2023a]]) or do not report at all.
- The mora method as an outcome measure — 11/21 to 0/21 — is a Japanese dysphonia severity index that the vault's English sources do not use ([[voice-quality]], [[japanese-voice]]).
- Combining a spasmodic-dysphonia operation with a feminisation operation in one patient is a genuine edge case; the authors' claim is only that the two are compatible.

## Notes
- n = 1, no blinding, and the follow-up intervals are short after each step (six months and one month); graded low.
- Claims taken from the Japanese abstract on the J-STAGE article page.
