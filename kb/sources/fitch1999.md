# Morphology and development of the human vocal tract: a study using magnetic resonance imaging

## What it says
- MRI of vocal tract morphology across ages shows clear male–female differences in overall vocal tract length and in the relative proportions of the oral and pharyngeal cavities.
- These sex differences are absent in children and arise at puberty, as part of the male vocal remodelling at puberty.

## Bearing on voice training
- Formant differences between sexes come from tract length (larynx descent), which is why larynx height and pharynx width are training targets for 響き.
