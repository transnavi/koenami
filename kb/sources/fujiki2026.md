# Factors Influencing Auditory Perception of Masculinity and Naturalness in Transmasculine Voices

## What it says
- Recordings from 6 transmasculine speakers on hormone replacement therapy and 12 age-matched cisgender speakers; 105 listeners of varying age, gender and sexual orientation rated masculinity, naturalness and attributed a gender (abstract, "Method").
- Acoustic predictors tested: F0, smoothed cepstral peak prominence, F0 range, Dysphonia Severity Index and perturbation measures, alongside speakers' own masculinity/naturalness ratings and VHI.
- "Ratings of voice naturalness and masculinity were predicted by F0 on connected speech (p < .01)." Masculinity correlated negatively with F0, r = .79; naturalness "significantly increased when F0 was within normative range for cisgender speakers".
- The transmasculine speakers' voices were "acoustically similar to cismale voices", yet both speakers and listeners rated them "less masculine and less natural than cis male voices"; transmasculine speakers were "21% more likely to be misgendered than cisgender speakers (p < .01)".
- VHI scores were significantly higher in transmasculine participants (p < .01) despite the acoustic similarity.
- Listener age mattered: older listeners rated transmasculine voices as significantly less natural than cisgender voices (p < .01), and the masculinity–naturalness link was stronger for older listeners (r = .41) than younger ones (r = .18–.23).

## Bearing on voice training
- An acoustic match is not a perceptual match: voices indistinguishable on the measures still read as less masculine and less natural, and the speakers know it (higher VHI) [[voice-masculinization]] [[gender-perception]] [[voice-impressions]].
- "Naturalness" is a listener variable, not a voice property — it moves with the age of the person judging [[voice-quality]].

## Notes
- n = 6 transmasculine speakers is small; 105 listeners. US academic recruitment (Indiana University, Wisconsin). The abstract does not report which language or which connected-speech task was recorded.
- Claims taken from the PubMed abstract.
