# Protocolo de abordagem da voz para afirmação de género: revisão sistemática

## What it says
- European Portuguese systematic review (PRISMA 2020) of PubMed and Google Scholar publications in English, Portuguese and Spanish, "que contemplem a avaliação clínica, tratamento médico e/ou cirúrgico da voz em doentes transgénero"; 10 publications included (abstract, "Material e Métodos").
- The protocol it derives: audio-perceptual assessment (GRBAS and CAPE-V), acoustic analysis (frequência fundamental, formant frequencies, vocal range, maximum phonation time), laryngostroboscopy at low, mid and high pitch, and the questionnaires TWVQ and VHI-10.
- Therapy pathway: "Numa primeira fase da abordagem terapêutica, o indivíduo transgénero deve ser encaminhado a um terapeuta da fala habilitado", with surgery weighed only when this is insufficient.
- On trans men: "a maioria dos homens transgénero fica satisfeito com a voz obtida com o tratamento hormonal, existem ainda poucos estudos acerca da abordagem cirúrgica nesta subpopulação".
- For trans women, "a glotoplastia de Wendler é o procedimento de eleição".

## Bearing on voice training
- The first European-Portuguese voice protocol this vault holds; Portugal's pathway mirrors the stepped therapy-then-surgery model of [[nhse1719]] and [[casado2017]], stated in Portuguese clinical vocabulary (terapeuta da fala = speech-language pathologist).
- The measure set (F0 + formants + TWVQ) matches what [[leung2018]] found carries gender perception.

## Notes
- Published in the Portuguese national otorhinolaryngology society's journal; graded low because a 10-study narrative protocol synthesis in a national journal carries less weight than a full meta-analysis, and the included studies are not appraised in the abstract.
- Brazilian Portuguese and European Portuguese differ in clinical terminology (fonoaudiologia vs terapeuta da fala); this is the European form.
