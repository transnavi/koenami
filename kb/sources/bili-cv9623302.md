# 伪音每天必练基本功（Bilibili 专栏）

## What it says
- The author introduces himself as 大鑫老师，拥有播音，配音从业经验10年 ("ten years in broadcasting and dubbing") and the article doubles as an advertisement for a 播音 course sold through a WeChat account (公众号：HD0310seo).
- The course it lays out is breath-led. The claim about scale-climbing is qualified: 有些人爬音阶可以直接爬成女生，注意：那是在已经会伪声的情况下才可以的，爬音阶只要达到及格线就行(一个半八度) — climbing scales can take you straight to a female voice only if you already have 伪声, and the passing standard is a range of about an octave and a half. The prohibition follows: 如果怕不上去一定不要变成假音，伪声是在真音的基础上建立的 ("if you cannot reach it, do not switch to falsetto; 伪声 is built on modal voice").
- Breath is divided into three components: 胸式呼吸 (chest breathing), 抬软腭 (raising the soft palate) and 抬舌根 (raising the tongue root). On chest breathing: it is found by running until out of breath, and it 只是平时练习才会用，不要试着将他变成日常呼吸，会缺氧的 ("is for practice only; do not make it your daily breathing, you will get light-headed").
- 抬软腭 is taught through the beginning of a yawn or a sob, with the explicit instruction 记住不要抬小舌头 ("do not raise the uvula") and a list of what raising the uvula feels like instead: 舌头不由自主往回缩，呼噜声，猪叫，犯恶心，嗓子干 ("the tongue retracts, snoring or pig noises, nausea, a dry throat"), plus 有卡痰的感觉, 发出喝的声音, 吐气沙哑，吸气嗓子眼凉.
- 抬舌根 is taught as a temporary aid: 他可以嗓子眼堵住，使气息无法通过嗓子…然后会使软腭上抬 ("it blocks the throat so the breath cannot pass, which forces the soft palate up"), and the instruction is to drop the habit once the sensation is learned 因为抬着软腭就没有办法说话了 ("you cannot speak while holding the soft palate up").
- The practice list is then: 呼吸的练习 (deep breath held in the abdomen, then a slow even release), opening the oral cavity to the width of an egg (口腔做个打哈欠的动作), moving the sound back by imagining it as a sweet swallowed into the throat (发一个“呜”音，把声音想象成一个糖，吞进嗓子里), chest-resonance sensing by humming or 金鱼吐泡泡, and 气泡音 described as 声音挂在声带上的感觉 ("the feeling of the voice hanging on the vocal folds"), produced by 由胸部缓缓升起一股微弱的气流.
- Its 三、伪音的发声原理 lists six cavities in two groups — 发音区 (喉腔, 咽腔) and 调音区 (头腔共鸣, 鼻腔共鸣, 口腔共鸣, 胸腔共鸣) — states the aim as 组合腔体…这样才叫伪装，而不是模仿 ("combining cavities — that is disguise, not imitation"), and draws a boundary against singing: 声乐是唱，伪声是说，一定要区分开 ("singing is singing, 伪声 is speaking — keep them apart").

## Bearing on voice training
- The article is the clearest example of the layer's internal disagreement about breath. It prescribes chest breathing for 男伪女 on the grounds that most women breathe that way, and warns that it must not become the daily pattern; the 2024 book course, by contrast, rejects both chest-only and abdominal-only and requires 胸腹联合 breathing [[bili-cv9417414]], which is also what the vault's [[breathing-and-support]] note describes as supported speech breathing.
- The 抬软腭 and 抬舌根 drills are the folk origin of a real mechanism: raising the soft palate closes the nasal port and contributes to oral resonance release, while a raised tongue root narrows the pharynx. The article's own error list (raising the uvula rather than the palate, producing snoring, nausea and dryness) is the community's practical version of what the handbook warns about when it says high-larynx work must be paired with open-throat work [[aogi2021]], and [[moegirl-weisheng]]'s harm rating of 抬软腭 at 5★ 伤嗓 belongs to the same drill.
- 别抬成假音，伪声建立在真音上 is the register point three other sources make [[bili-cv460384]], [[bili-cv9417414]], [[bili-cv7530256]]; the 一个半八度 range standard and the ban on making chest breathing habitual are the kind of concrete numbers the layer rarely supplies.
