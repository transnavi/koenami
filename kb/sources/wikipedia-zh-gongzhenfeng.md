# 共振峰（中文维基百科）

## What it says
- Summary endpoint, 2026-09-17 (extract in traditional Chinese): 共振峰（formant）是用來描述聲學共振現象的一種概念，在語音科學及語音學中，描述的是人類聲道中的共振情形。常用的量測方法是由頻譜分析或聲譜圖中，尋找頻譜中的峰值。 — formants describe acoustic resonance of the human vocal tract, measured as spectral peaks from spectrum analysis or spectrograms.
- The extract notes a measurement caveat that matters for high-pitched voices: 假如說話者，用比較高的基頻發出元音，例如小孩或女性的聲音，則頻譜上看起來比較像是寬帶狀，比較無法看出明顯的峰值 — at higher F0 (children, women) the spectrum looks broadband and the peaks are less distinct.
- Interlanguage titles: en Formant · ja フォルマント · ko 포먼트.

## Bearing on voice training
- The Chinese term 共振峰 is the exact equivalent the vault's [[formants]] note uses; the high-F0 measurement caveat is the same one Koenami's method page states for its 響き measure ([[koenami-method]]).
- In community teaching the word usually heard is 共鸣 (resonance) rather than 共振峰; [[aogi2021]] is the Chinese guide that deliberately teaches the 共振峰/F1/F2 vocabulary.
