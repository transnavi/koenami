# TransVoiceTech: Ear Training for X-Y Resonance vs Pitch

## What it says
- oEmbed title as above, TransVoiceLessons. Watch-page date text: 2019/02/15. Chapters: Introduction; Ear Training and Demonstrations of Voice Modulation; Summary.
- Description (Japanese translation on this fetch): the video is "耳のトレーニングのデモンストレーション"; "共鳴とピッチは互いに依存しているという考えが、ようやく解消されたことを願っています." Untrained speakers "誤ってそれらを結びつけてしまう" because of "制御不足."
- Acoustic aside in the description: brighter resonance can make high pitches easier because of "建設的干渉と脱建設的干渉" (constructive and destructive interference) and acoustic feedback on the folds; a larger tube does the reverse. "これらのパラメータをある程度独立してコントロールできるようになることが重要です." One isolation method named: a pitch tracker, holding a single pitch while changing resonance.

## Bearing on voice training
- Early TVL statement that pitch and resonance are independently controllable — the X–Y frame later extended to weight in [[erose2022a]]. Using a pitch tracker as a training tool is the community's main measurement habit ([[voice-tools-devextras]], [[transvoice-app]]).
