# 認定施設一覧（日本GI（性別不合）学会）

## What it says
- The page is a bare list headed 認定施設一覧, with no criteria, dates or notes attached. The facilities named, in the order the page gives them: 岡山大学病院, 社会医療法人 光生病院, 山梨大学医学部附属病院, 名古屋大学医学部附属病院, 札幌医科大学附属病院, 沖縄県立中部病院, 医療法人 明理会 行徳総合病院, 札幌中央病院, 国立大学法人 富山大学附属病院, and 独立行政法人国立病院機構 東京医療センター. Ten institutions.
- The society's site carries the other directories alongside it — 認定医一覧, 医療系コーディネーター一覧, 教育系コーディネーター一覧, メンタルヘルス専門職所属施設, エキスパート研修会, 研究大会, GI診療ガイドライン.
- The society's name changed on 2024-03-18, according to the 理事長声明 page fetched in the same pass: 「本学会は，長年にわたり「GID（性同一性障害）学会」との名称を使用してきましたが，ICD-11の発効により，GID（性同一性障害）という名称は，今後，使用されなくなります」；the new name 日本GI（性別不合）学会 was reported to the 総会 on 2024-03-17. The same page holds statements on 特例法 revision (2021-05-21), support for the UN joint statement against forced sterilisation (2021-03-25), testosterone depot preparations (2014-04-12) and mastectomy cases (2013-04-01) — none of them about voice.

## Bearing on voice training
- This is the facility layer a Japanese reader has to pass through: gender care in Japan is organised around designated institutions and コーディネーター ([[gi-society]], [[transgender-care-context]]), and the society's own guideline is the document that governs what those facilities do ([[jspn-guideline2024]]).
- The two highest-volume Japanese voice-surgery clinics the vault holds — the Kyoto ENT clinic and Metropolitan ENT Surgery Clinic Tokyo ([[kyoto-ent-clinic]], [[met-ent-tokyo]]) — do not appear on this list, and the list says nothing about voice services; voice is reached through ENT and 音声外来 rather than through this directory ([[kyoto-ent-rehab]], [[kobayashi2023]]).
- None of the society's published statements concerns voice, which is consistent with a field where the clinical guideline covers the surgical and hormonal pathway and leaves voice to other specialties ([[clinical-guidelines]]).

## Limits
- A list without criteria: it does not say what accreditation requires, when the entries were last confirmed, or whether the facilities offer voice services. Fetched once on 2026-09-17; the 理事長声明 page was read in the same pass and the statements listed above are quoted from it.
