# 音声治療に抵抗した変声障害に対する甲状軟骨形成術III型の経験

A one-patient report on the boundary case: two years of voice therapy without benefit, then operation.

## What it says
- The standard sequence, stated as background: 「In general, the primary therapy for mutational voice disorder has been voice therapy. However, we sometimes see cases in which voice therapy does not decrease the high pitch of mutational voice disorder.」
- The case: type III thyroplasty in a patient with mutational voice disorder who had already had two years of voice therapy 「without remarkable improvement of his voice」.
- The outcome: 「His voice pitch became much lower after the Isshiki type III thyroplasty surgical procedure. The patient's fundamental frequency (F0) decreased from 276 Hz before surgery to 126 Hz after surgery.」 A 150 Hz drop.
- Conclusion: 「Isshiki type III thyroplasty was found to be useful as surgical treatment for higher pitch and resulted in a voice that was satisfactory to the patient with mutational voice disorder.」

## Bearing on voice training
- The failure of therapy that motivates it is the interesting part: two years of treatment, F0 still at 276 Hz, and the operation took it to 126 Hz — inside the ordinary male range in a single step ([[voice-therapy-techniques]], [[voice-surgery]]). Compare the same clinic group's three cases in [[nakamura2011]], where therapy-resistant mutational dysphonia moved from ~175 Hz to ~88–115 Hz.
- It is the Japanese statement of the sequence the vault's guidelines formalise elsewhere: therapy first, surgery for non-responders ([[clinical-guidelines]], [[sato2007]]).
