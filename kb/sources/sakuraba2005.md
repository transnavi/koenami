## Bearing on voice training
- It is the missing middle of the Sakuraba line in this vault: after the 2003 acoustics conference abstract [[imaizumi2003]] and before the 119-speaker listener study [[sakuraba2009]], this is the case where the training itself was documented. Together with [[sakuraba2007]] it shows the same group moving from one treated case (2005) to a classification scheme for untrained voices (2007) to a large listening experiment (2009) ([[voice-feminization-therapy]], [[japanese-voice]]).
- Its co-author 峯松信明 is a speech-engineering researcher, which is why the Japanese early work on trans voice came out in acoustics and IEICE venues rather than in clinical journals — the same pattern as [[kawahara2014]] ([[voice-across-languages]]).
