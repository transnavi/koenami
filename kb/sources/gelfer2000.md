# Comparison of acoustic and perceptual measures of voice in male-to-female transsexuals perceived as female versus those perceived as male

## What it says
- 15 trans women plus 6 cis women and 3 cis men read the Rainbow Passage and produced /a/ and /i/; 20 listeners rated them.
- Speakers perceived as female had a higher mean speaking fundamental frequency and a higher upper limit of SFF than those perceived as male; the upper limit of SFF correlated with femininity ratings.

## Bearing on voice training
- The top of the pitch range, not only its average, relates to being heard as female.

## Notes
- Small sample; formant differences were measured but the abstract reports significance only for SFF.
