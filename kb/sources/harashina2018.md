# SRS事始め

A first-person history of how gender-affirming surgery became legal medical practice in Japan, published by the patient association on the twentieth anniversary of the first operation. It contains the earliest Japanese clinical anecdote in this vault about voice.

## What it says
- The turning point named: 「1998年10月16日、埼玉医科大学総合医療センター（川越市）で医療行為としておおやけに認められた国内初の性別適合手術（FTM）が行われた。」 The background was the ブルーボーイ事件: 「1969年、ある産婦人科医が性転換手術を行ったことに対し、懲役2年、執行猶予3年、罰金40万円の重い判決を受けた。」 The author notes the judgment did not ban the operation outright — 「しかるべき手順をもって行えば許されるものとしていた」 — but the public read it as a ban, and 「それ以来医師はこの問題を避けて通り、医療、法的サポートを必要とする当事者にとって『暗黒時代』が続き」.
- The voice passage, in his account of the 1992 patient who first approached him: 「しかしその患者はどこから見ても男性なのに、衣服を脱げばその体は完全に女性で、しかもどうしても自分は気持ちの上では男としか思えない、女の声がいやで焼き鳥の金串をのどに突っ込んで声を低くしたとの告白に驚愕し、そのことについて学ぶことを約束した。」 — a trans man who had pushed a yakitori skewer into his own throat to lower his voice.
- Timeline: 1995 埼玉医科大学倫理委員会へのFTM 2例の申請 and the press leak; 1996-07 the committee's 答申 recognising the surgery as legitimate medical practice; 1996-09 a ジェンダークリニック委員会; 1997-05 日本精神神経学会's first 「性同一性障害に関する答申と提言」; 1999-03 第1回性同一性障害研究会 (a society from 2006); 2003-07 the 特例法; 2004-07 its entry into force.
- On the guideline's lineage the article is explicit: the ethics-committee answer 「それが「性同一性障害の診断と治療のガイドライン」につながり、特例法ができて性別の取扱いの変更が可能となり」 ([[jspn-guideline2018]]).
- A note appended by the publisher: 「※ 本文中、現在使用されていない用語が含まれておりますが、作者の意向を尊重し原文のママを記載いたしました。」 The vocabulary is period vocabulary.

## Bearing on voice training
- The skewer anecdote is the only pre-clinical Japanese voice datum in the vault: before any Japanese guideline existed, a trans man's response to dysphoria about voice was self-injury to the larynx. It positions voice as a motive force in the same history that produced the surgical framework, in a document that otherwise never mentions voice.
- Read against the guideline note: the framework this memoir describes went on to have no voice chapter ([[jspn-guideline2018]], [[jspn-guideline2024]]), so the motive recorded here was not matched by a treatment category.

## Limits
- A memoir by the surgeon who performed the first operation, published by an advocacy organisation on its own site; it is a primary account but not a checked historical source, and its dates and characterisations are his. Period terminology is retained in quotations.
- Twenty years old as of 2018; nothing in it describes current practice.
