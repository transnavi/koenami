## What it measures/teaches
- The clinic states its own mechanism: an transoral endoscopic approach with no neck incision, stripping and suturing the mucosa and the anterior third of the lamina propria, with permanent sutures shortening and thinning the vocal folds. The claimed result is a 女性化音质 and durability of effect — these are the clinic's claims, not independent findings.
- The channel's evidence format is the before/after clip plus patient testimony, both of which select for success. The vault's surgical concept note and the reviews behind [[voice-surgery]] describe pitch elevation in the range these procedures aim at and the irreversibility and loudness-loss caveats the community repeats ([[mtfwiki-vocal-surgery]]); nothing in this channel's records addresses failures.
- Its function is patient recruitment in Chinese, and the intermediation accounts that tag it — [[bili-weimengxiangxingdong]], [[bili-talita]] — complete the funnel.
