- The three-point design (day 10, day 13, six months) is the same follow-up pattern as [[bili-neroblack]] and more complete than the single-clip testimonials; the half-year video reports on singing and on how training interacted with the surgery.
- The half-year item is titled in English (Voice Report) while the day-10 and day-13 items are Chinese — a VTuber-style packaging of a clinical experience, and the only item in the dataset that pairs 手术 with 嗓音训练的效果和方法 explicitly.
- The channel stops after the half-year report, so no longer-horizon outcome is recorded here.

## Limits
- Metadata-only verification: title, description, upload date, plays, duration and tags from the Bilibili search API (wbi); the view API is blocked from this machine (412) and no audio was transcribed, so the reported voice changes cannot be heard or verified.
- Personal record account, not a clinic; no procedure name (e.g. 声带缩短) is stated in the records, and no complication is reported.
