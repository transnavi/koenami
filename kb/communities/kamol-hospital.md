# Kamol Cosmetic Hospital — VFS / VMS

## What it offers
- A Bangkok gender-affirming-surgery hospital (site in 12 languages including 한국어 and 日本語) offering Voice Feminization Surgery (VFS) under Male-to-Female and Voice Masculinization Surgery (VMS) under Female-to-Male, alongside SRS, FFS and Adam's-apple revision.
- The VFS page describes the procedure as modifying "the pitch and resonance of your voice", names three common techniques — cricothyroid approximation (CTA), anterior glottal web formation, and vocal cord shortening — and says the surgeon discusses "the most suitable voice feminization technique for your individual needs and goals".
- VFS candidate criteria as listed: transgender woman or gender-diverse individual, completed hormone therapy (if applicable), non-smoker, good health, realistic expectations.
- VFS recovery as listed: strict voice rest, soft diet for a few days, medications, follow-ups; "most individuals can return to normal activities within a few weeks", "a complete vocal recovery may take several months"; risks include temporary or permanent hoarseness/breathiness, infection, bleeding, scarring.
- The VMS page targets "a masculine voice … around 100-125 Hz", performed "if the patient is not satisfied with his voice despite the use of male hormone therapy", with preoperative Video Laryngostroboscopy and spectrum-acoustic study, a 2–3 cm neck incision, reduction of the laryngeal diameter and shortening of the folds; 1–2 nights admitted, silence until first follow-up.
- The hospital also posts patient-journey video content (e.g. "Nong Peach's Voice Feminization Journey at Kamol Hospital: One-Month Post-Surgery Update", found via the site's search API).

## What it measures/teaches
- Feminization: pitch and resonance as the two named goals, with a menu of the three technique families the reviews catalogue [[song2017]] [[catani2024]]; masculinization: an explicit 100–125 Hz target range for a male-frequency voice [[voice-masculinization]].
- VMS framing treats testosterone as the default masculinizer and surgery as the remedy for an insufficient response — the mirror of the transfeminine "hormones do not change the voice" premise [[kim2020]] [[son2026]].
