# Yanhee International Hospital — Voice Change Surgery

## What it offers
- A Bangkok international hospital's voice-change page: surgery "designed to transform the voice box (larynx) as a way to change or improve the voice pitch", offered to MTF patients (low-to-high, "the goal is to raise the pitch to the fundamental frequency of a female voice") and to FTM patients or high-pitched cisgender men (high-to-low) — "whether they identify as male, female, or somewhere in between" (page, fetched 2026-09-17).
- The page prices it: Voice Change Surgery, 2 nights in hospital, 121,000 THB; the consultation includes stroboscopy (4,900 THB) (price table).
- Post-operative instructions: complete voice rest for one week including whispering, since "there are very few sutures in there and until your body's own scar tissue helps support the procedure, the sutures could pull out"; speaking resumes gradually after a week, aerobic activity after two weeks, weight lifting after a month.
- Risks as listed: infection, bleeding, anesthesia reaction, and "sub-optimal result consisting of a weak voice".
- The Pride Center page (also fetched) names the ENT specialist Dr. Ornouma Sriwanishvipat as "Expert in Voice Change for MTF and FTM", part of a trans-care team spanning SRS, FFS, psychiatry and hormone management; the hospital advertises the center as "Thailand's First Comprehensive Transgender Healthcare Hub" (news headline found via the site's search API).
- The page links out to the surgeon's own site for before-and-after videos ([[dr-ornouma]]).

## What it measures/teaches
- Pitch as the headline metric, in both directions; no acoustic series or targets in Hz published on the page; outcome evidence is before/after videos on the surgeon's site.
