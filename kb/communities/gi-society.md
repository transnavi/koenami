## What it offers
- The Japanese academic society for gender-incongruence medicine: the body that, with 日本精神神経学会, writes the national clinical guideline and certifies the clinicians and facilities that follow it. Office at 岡山大学大学院保健学研究科中塚研究室; contact jsgi@gi-soc.jp.
- Renaming notice on the site, dated 2024-03-18, signed by 理事長 中塚幹也: 「本学会は，長年にわたり「GID（性同一性障害）学会」との名称を使用してきましたが，ICD-11の発効により，GID（性同一性障害）という名称は，今後，使用されなくなります．」 The new name was reported at the 総会 held during GID学会第25回研究大会 (沖縄大会), 2024-03-17.
- Guidelines server, which is the practical service this vault uses: `guideline.html` links the PDFs of the 第3版, 第4版 (2014-05-17, 2017-05-20 and 2018-01-20 版), the 第5版 (2024-10-25 file), the 2025 補正版, and the two reporting forms added by the 補正. Announcement of 2024-08-29: 「性別不合に関する診断と治療のガイドライン第5版を公表いたしました！　第５版からは、日本精神神経学会と日本GI（性別不合）学会との合同ガイドラインとなっております。」
- Ongoing activity on the site as of 2026-09-17: 第25回エキスパート研修会 (2026-10-25, Zoom), 第28回研究大会・総会 (岡山, 2027-02-20/21, 大会長 松本洋輔 — the author of [[matsumoto2022]]), 認定医一覧, 医療系・教育系コーディネーター一覧, 認定施設一覧.

## What it measures/teaches
- Its guidance is diagnostic and treatment-framework material, not voice material: the site's own menu has no voice page, and the guideline's treatment chapter covers hormone therapy, mastectomy and gender-affirming surgery ([[jspn-guideline2018]], [[jspn-guideline2024]]).
- The 認定施設 (certified facilities) list is the operational link to the insurance rule: surgery performed at these facilities in line with the guideline has been covered since FY2018 ([[jspn-guideline2024]]).

## Limits
- A professional society's own site: self-description, event notices and PDF hosting. Membership, certification and guideline content are not independently described here.
- The 認定施設一覧 page could not be parsed in this pass (its content did not render as text through the fetch), so the facility list itself is not recorded in this note.
- The society is not the same body as the patient association gid.jp ([[gidjp-opinion2023]]); the two have different domains and different roles.
