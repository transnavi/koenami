## What it offers
- Gender-affirming voice teacher based in Montréal (they/them). About page, fetched 2026-09-17: "a queer, nonbinary, and disabled gender-affirming voice teacher" teaching "since 2010," with "B.Sc. in Physics," "B.Mus. in Jazz Voice," "M.A. in Songwriting," and "Somatic Voicework™️ Levels 1, 2, and 3."
- Homepage headline: "Transition from voice dysphoria to a voice you love with gender-affirming voice courses." Named courses: Mindful Voice Feminization; Mix & Match! Designing Your Nonbinary Voice; Masculinize Your Voice Without Testosterone; Trans Voice Practice Club; plus private coaching, teacher training and scholarships.
- About page distinguishes the work from speech-language pathology: "de-medicalizing this aspect of gender-affirming care can be really helpful. The medical model is generally built to treat people like 'patients with disorders.' But you are not a problem to be solved!"
- Free webinar advertised on the homepage: "Change the Gender of Your Voice (No Hormones or Surgeries Required!)." YouTube channel `@reneeyoxon`.

## What it measures/teaches
- Homepage definition: "Voice dysphoria is feeling unhappy or uncomfortable because the sound of your voice doesn't reflect who you are or illicit the reaction you want from others." Claim: "you can change the sound and perceived gender of your voice through practice" "without hormones or surgery."
- About page lists speaking at the Philadelphia Trans Wellness Conference and "the American Speech and Hearing Association Conference," among others.
- Individual exercises live in separate notes ([[yoxon2023]] Big Dog Little Dog; [[yoxon2025]] vocal-weight exercises). The site itself does not publish Hz or formant targets.

## Limits
- Commercial site: courses, coaching, newsletter. Testimonials on the homepage are student quotations, not outcome data.
- The about page's degrees and conference list are self-reported.
- "No hormones or surgeries required" is a claim about behavioural change being possible, not a claim that hormones never change the voice ([[ziegler2018]]).
