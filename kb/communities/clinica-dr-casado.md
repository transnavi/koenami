# Clínica Dr. Casado (Marbella)

## What it offers
- A private ENT and voice clinic in Marbella (Av. Ramón y Cajal 4), run by Juan Carlos Casado Morente, who is also the first author of the vault's Spanish glottoplasty studies [[casado2016]] and [[casado2017]]. His credentials as the site states them: doctor in medicine and surgery, ENT specialist, head of the ENT service at Hospital Quirónsalud Marbella and Campo de Gibraltar, and president of the voice and phoniatrics commission of the Sociedad Española de Otorrinolaringología since October 2015.
- The feminisation service is presented as four stages: 「Etapa 1: Examen vocal / Etapa 2: Cirugía / Etapa 3: Logopedia post-operatoria / Etapa 4: Revisiones post-operatorias」.
- Surgery is Wendler glottoplasty, described as endoscopic through the mouth: 「Sin incisiones en el cuello, nuestro procedimiento endoscópico acorta y tensa las cuerdas vocales」, 45–75 minutes, under general anaesthesia, with hospital admission, and 「reposo absoluto de voz durante 15 días」 afterwards. Patients are told they can generally travel the day after surgery.
- Included with the operation as listed: a home logopedia kit, three reviews (one month, three months, six months, in person or virtual), and surgeon-and-therapy fees covered if a second intervention is needed. A free assessment consultation is offered, also by video.
- Logopedia is also offered 「sín cirugia」, and the clinic states that 「En muchos casos, la voz puede trabajarse mediante sesiones de logopedia especializada, sin necesidad de pasar por quirófano」. The post-surgical programme is stated as a minimum of ten sessions, with more for professional voice users.

## What it measures/teaches
- The therapy components are listed under seven headings: 「Resistencia · Flexibilidad cordal · Kinestesia · Resonancia · Articulación · Melodía · Vocabulario」.
- The stated rationale for each maps onto the vault's concept set: resistance so the surgically raised pitch holds under daily use without the larynx straining ([[vocal-fatigue]]); vocal-fold flexibility so laughing, coughing and shouting stay in the new range; resonance work so the sound concentrates 「en las cavidades más altas」 ([[formants]]); 「una articulación algo más abierta y hacia delante」 ([[articulation]]); melodic work 「incluyendo finales de frase ascendentes」 ([[intonation]]); and communication style work for those who want it.
- Sessions 「realizamos un seguimiento de la evolución vocal … podemos grabar diferentes muestras de voz y comparar los avances obtenidos, valorando tanto el tono alcanzado como la estabilidad, la naturalidad y el confort al hablar」 — before-and-after recordings assessed for pitch, stability, naturalness and comfort.
- The clinical promise made to patients is stronger than the vault's sources support: 「Tu voz será bonita y quedará estereotipadamente femenina, siempre y cuando se respete los días de reposo, realice lo que se solicita y se lleve a cabo la terapia logopédica tal y como describe la terapeuta vocal」.

## Limits
- A commercial clinic page: self-description and marketing, with no published case numbers, no outcome data and no prices on the pages fetched. Patient testimonials appear as first-name-plus-initial quotes.
- The glottoplasty pages make the claim of stereotyped femininity conditional on adherence, which places responsibility for the result on the patient; the vault's outcome sources report means and variances, not guarantees ([[aires2023a]], [[ballestas2018]]).
- No separate Spanish, Mexican, Argentine or Chilean clinic page with published prices could be reached in this pass; the search engines available to this session were blocked or degraded, which is why this note stands alone among the Spanish clinical resources.
