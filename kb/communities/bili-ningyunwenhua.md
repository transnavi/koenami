## What it measures/teaches
- The channel's speciality is 正太音 (the pre-pubescent boy's voice line) and 压音, taught in one-to-five-minute clips — a fine-grained taxonomy of non-adult 声线 rather than the 御姐/少女 pair the larger channels favour.
- It also teaches the 伪声 direction system explicitly (女伪女, 男伪女, 女伪男), which is the same grid the 萌娘百科 entry uses ([[moegirl-weisheng]]) and the article taxonomy at [[bili-cv5924045]] names as 声音位置 differences.
- 气泡音 is treated as the entry point to 女声 in the 2022 clip; [[moegirl-weisheng]] rates 气泡音 as the least harmful of the four named methods, so the claim is within the genre's own safety folklore.
