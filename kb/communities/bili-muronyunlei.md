## What it measures/teaches
- 男伪女 by 真音 (modal-voice) route, plus a separate male 攻音 line — the channel treats the masculine and feminine ends as the same skill applied at different 共鸣 placements.
- The 2017 合集 is the entry point most cited in later Chinese tutorials; 塞德曲黑's series [[bili-saidequhei2021]] and 建站-community course notes assume viewers arrive from this kind of compilation.
- No acoustic measurements or software use are described; teaching is by demonstration.
