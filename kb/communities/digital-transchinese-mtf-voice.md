## What it offers
- A free self-study PDF guide ("嗓音学习指南", 3rd edition), 13.9 MB, dated 2024-11, originally hosted at voice.cntt.uk (live as of verification) and mirrored on the Trans Chinese Digital Archive. Author credited as Catherina Grace, produced with a "跨性别言语训练研究小组" (Transgender Speech Training Research Group).
- Per its own abstract: written for transgender women starting from zero experience, distinguishing its method from common "假声" (falsetto) teaching and singing-technique approaches, aiming for a method requiring little natural talent so "nearly everyone" can reach a feminized pitch. States its method takes longer than some alternatives but is meant for sustained daily use without vocal-fold damage.
- States it was compiled by the group from medical materials, publicly available internet resources, and multiple learners' personal experience reports, with a training approach "basically consistent with" methods used in most hospital voice-training programs, intended for transgender women unable to access hospital-based voice therapy.

## What it measures/teaches
- A two-phase curriculum per its abstract: pitch training first (约3周, roughly 3 weeks, described as mandatory/core), followed by timbre ("音色") refinement (3 weeks or longer, described as elective, offering 3 alternative methods).
- States a concrete pitch target/pass threshold of 185–220+ Hz for the pitch phase; states there is no equivalent fixed "pass" threshold for timbre, since timbre preference is individual.
- States the document favors quantifiable practice benchmarks throughout so learners can self-train without in-person guidance.

## Limits
