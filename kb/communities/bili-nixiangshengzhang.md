## What it measures/teaches
- Both items are voice-change documentation rather than training: one tracks three-plus years from 初一 to 高一 (ages ~12 to ~16) using recordings of songs the person sang, the other is a forwarded record (代发) from another person. The 公鸭嗓 caveat in the first description is the unedited middle of a testosterone-driven voice change, which is what the vault's puberty and hormone notes describe ([[puberty-voice-change]], [[hormones-and-voice]]).
- Singing recordings as the measurement instrument is a practical choice for anyone without acoustic software: the song is the fixed reference, so the pitch difference between years is audible without Praat, in the same spirit as the fixed-word rule in [[formants]].

## Limits
- Metadata-only verification: title, description, upload date, plays, duration and tags from the Bilibili search API (wbi); the view API is blocked from this machine (412) and no audio was transcribed, so the changes described cannot be heard or measured.
- Amateur record accounts; no hormone regimen, timeline in doses, or acoustic data is given, and the second video is third-hand.
