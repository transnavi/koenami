## What it measures/teaches
- The channel's thesis, stated in its own titles across three years: folk 伪声 practice is 不科学 (unscientific), the failures it produces (发不出女声) have identifiable causes, and the fix is method rather than effort. That is the same rupture the MtF.wiki infrastructure and the 2021 handbook [[aogi2021]] were built around ([[chinese-community]]).
- 变声期 (voice-change) safety is the second recurring thread: two videos in 2023 are built entirely around whether training during puberty is permissible (变声期到底能不能伪音？！ and 揭秘！为什么变声期前不能学伪声), and a third covers the harm of self-teaching — each framing the topic as a hazard.
- The 2025 alt-account series teaches the deliberately exaggerated 夹子音 register in three minutes or less; the 2026 main-account videos shift to 30-minute multi-声线 courses.
