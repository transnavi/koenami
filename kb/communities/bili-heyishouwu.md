## What it measures/teaches
- The channel is an early instance of the 科学伪声 (scientific 伪声) wave that the 2023 manifesto video [[bili-zhizhiya2023]] and the 避坑指南 account [[bili-weiyinchenhai]] later front — here as a self-organised study group (何忆伪声学院) whose stated goal is 不毁音教学, teaching that does not damage the voice.
- Its route to 男为女 is 戏腔 (opera-style voice) through 咽腔共鸣, so the channel belongs with the 小嗓 strand — the theatrical register borrowed as the 伪声 target [[bili-liujiubushi69]], [[bili-jingjupan]] — rather than with the acoustics-based handbooks.
- The 气泡音 video's premise (there is a correct and an incorrect vocal fry) matches the broadcast-voice treatment at [[bili-xuyinglun]], and the two are the only places in the dataset where 气泡音 is presented as something that can be done wrongly.
