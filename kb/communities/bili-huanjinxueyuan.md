# 幻音学院（Bilibili）

An organisation account rather than an individual creator: it advertises a website (huanyinxueyuan.com), a WeChat public account, a Weibo account, a 喜马拉雅 channel and eleven QQ groups split by topic, and runs free public classes twice a week.

## What it offers
- BV1Tf4y197MN (2020-08-08, 58.0K plays, 138 min 20 s) 伪声伪音零基础初学者入门教程教学合集【幻音学院】 — "含23个教程视频，分13个伪音视频，10个番外视频。视频内大部分为基础理论教学…（每个人学习到一定程度由于声带、嗓音、喉位不同，适用的方法都是差异的，不推荐大家盲目练习）". The collection is mirrored into the group's file-share (伪音合集群798470339).
- BV1uM4y1M71e (2021-07-07, 14.2K plays, 2 min 15 s) 萝莉音与少萝音的区别 — taught by an instructor credited as 泫漓; the entry point to the channel's 声线 taxonomy.
- BV1TQ4y1e76Q (2021-12-04, 3.5K plays, 2 min 48 s) 伪声伪音如何稳定声音声线
- BV1234y1i7Zi (2022-01-22, 5.0K plays, 2 min 48 s) 伪声伪音气泡音讲解
- Related: the channel published an article, 初学伪音你需要避免踩的坑(女生篇) [[bili-cv8487280]], listing 压音, 端腔 and 盲目女伪男 as the three beginner traps and naming 毁嗓 outcomes.

## What it measures/teaches
- The channel's stated position is that methods are individual and that blind practice is not recommended (不推荐大家盲目练习) — a rare caution inside the 伪声 teaching market, and the same warning the article version makes explicit about 压音 causing a permanent 异物感.
- Taught targets are the 女声 声线 grid: 萝莉音 vs 少萝音 as a named distinction, plus 正太音, 御姐音 ([[bili-cv8487280]] tags), i.e. the ACG character-voice classification rather than a gendered-speech model.
- It runs on the group-and-livestream model: free open classes, topic-specific QQ groups (伪音, 音色, 声控, 配音, 戏腔, 声乐, Beatbox), and a paid-teaching funnel.
