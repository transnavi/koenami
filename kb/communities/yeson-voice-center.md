# 예송이비인후과 음성센터 (Yeson Voice Center)

## What it offers
- A private Seoul voice clinic ("목소리의 토탈케어" — total care for the voice) run by 김형태 (Kim Hyung-Tae, founding director) and 이지욱 (Lee Ji Wook); its clinics include 음성여성화수술 (voice feminization surgery), 보툴리눔클리닉, and a 음성관리프로그램 (site navigation and 본원소개 page, fetched 2026-09-17).
- The 성대단축술/전유합후진술 page describes VFSRAC endoscopically: 점막과 성대고유층의 1/3 지점을 제거한 후 영구적인 실을 사용하여 꿰매는 방법 — removing the front third of the mucosa and lamina propria and suturing with permanent thread, through the mouth with no neck incision, a 3–5 mm surgical field.
- Outcome tables on the same page (labelled 12년 연구 자료, 2003~2014, i.e. drawn from [[kim2017]]): total group 144.1 → 207.4 Hz at 6 months; 성전환자 그룹 (transgender group) 134.6 → 208.2 Hz; androphonia group 153.5 → 206.5 Hz.
- A second table reports by 목젖제거 (Adam's apple shaving): without shaving +79.9 Hz versus with shaving +67.3 Hz mean pitch increase, and the page states 92%의 만족도 with 6–12 months needed to reach a full female-frequency voice.
- The Q&A page (2_4_2) states postoperative training ("기능성음성훈련") should continue 적어도 8~12개월, frames it as storing a new phonation pattern in the brain over 6–8 months, recommends working with a 후두질환전문가나 음성언어치료사, and explains adjunctive 보툴리눔톡신 (botulinum toxin) treatment for 성대 떨림 (vocal-fold tremor).
- Staff page lists 1급 음성언어치료사 (first-class voice-language therapists) 강선정, 조인수, 이다비 plus 발성치료사 (vocal coaches) — the Korean SLP tier inside the clinic.
- The homepage news list shows a 2026-07-29 item 예송이비인후과, 강동성심병원 LGBTQ+센터와 진료 협력 강화를 위한 업무협… (business agreement with the Gangdong Sacred Heart Hospital LGBTQ+ center) — a domestic care-network link.

## What it measures/teaches
- The clinic's own framing matches [[kim2020]]: surgery raises F0 by shortening/tensioning/thinning the folds, and the 음성재활훈련 afterwards rebuilds the phonatory pattern and 여성공명주파수 (female resonance frequency) generation.
- Teaches, per the Q&A page, that resonance remains trainable after surgery: 소리의 공명은 음성치료와 음성재활을 통하여 본인 스스로 인두강의 모습을 변형시킬 수 있으므로 — "resonance can be changed by the patient through voice therapy and rehabilitation" (2_4_1 page).

## Limits
- Provider marketing pages: the 92% satisfaction figure and the comparison table (which rates competing methods — 윤상갑상고정술 CTA, 갑상연골축소술, glottal-web formation, laser — as 가성 "falsetto" or 거친음 "rough" voiced, singing 불가능 "impossible") are the clinic's own presentation of its rivals, not independent comparisons; peer-reviewed counterparts are [[kim2017]] and [[kim2024]].
- The claim that voice training alone risks functional voice disorders (Q&A item 1) is the clinic's argument for surgery; it is attributed, not established here.
- Site in Korean; a separate international-patient track (국제환자진료안내) exists but was not fetched.
