## What it offers
- A paid voice-training service founded by Zheanna erose (self-described as a transwoman with a 15-year background in music, audio production and acoustics, BA in Music Composition from Indiana State University). Site copy: "internationally renowned as a master of voice feminization and has directly taught hundreds of others... while reaching millions with her free voice education materials."
- Team of four listed instructors as of the 2026-09-17 fetch: Zheanna erose (founder), Ffionn (Clover) Grigsby (voice teacher since 2017, postgrad diploma in Voice Pedagogy, University of Wales Trinity Saint David / Voice Study Centre), Vivienne Snow (Berklee-trained singer/producer, pursuing a master's in Choral Music at USC), and Gabby G. (started as a TransVoiceLessons student, now teaches; also runs a volunteer voice workshop with the Brooklyn Pride Center).
- Three paid formats: private lessons (1 hour, personalized, "2-5 weeks between lessons" recommended cadence); group courses (12 sessions of 100 minutes, up to 27 students with 3 instructors, lecture + ear-training + small-group practice, "~10 minutes of personal time with an instructor" per student); consultations (20-minute assessment of where to head next / which program fits).
- Also maintains a free YouTube channel and free voice-education materials referenced on the site, though the specific YouTube "about" text could not be retrieved during this fetch (returned no description via scrape).

## What it measures/teaches
- Voice and speech feminization technique: resonance, pitch, intonation, vowel length, and — per the group-course description — combining these skills into sustained control of a feminized voice.
- No acoustic-measurement tooling of its own is described on the pages fetched; teaching is demonstration- and coaching-based ("All exercises are demonstrated by Christella"-style modeling by instructors, ear training, and personalized correction), not app-based pitch/formant feedback.

## Limits
- Paid service (private lessons, group courses, consultations); pricing not listed on the pages fetched.
- Site copy is self-description/marketing, not third-party verification of outcomes; no published case data or acoustic outcome measures found on the fetched pages.
- Only the homepage, /aboutus, and /services pages were fetched; the YouTube channel's own "about" text was not successfully retrieved in this pass.
