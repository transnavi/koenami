## What it offers
- Public Discord invite landing titled "Join the TransVoice Discord Server!" Meta description fetched 2026-09-17: "Check out the TransVoice community on Discord - hang out with 9753 other members and enjoy free voice and text chat."
- The r/transvoice wiki index (Wayback 2025-01-09) advertises the same invite (`https://discord.gg/BPPMkDu`) as "Join us on discord for fun conversation and instant voice feedback."

## What it measures/teaches
- The public landing page states only that the server offers "free voice and text chat." It does not describe a curriculum, Hz targets, or which channels exist.
- Teaching content, if any, lives behind the invite; this note records only the public claim that the server is used for "instant voice feedback" ([[r-transvoice]]).

## Limits
- Discord's invite page is a thin Open Graph card. Channel lists, rules, bot tools and the actual feedback practice were not visible without joining, which was not done.
- Member count (9,753) is Discord's figure at fetch time; it is not an outcome measure.
- Other English trans-voice Discords (Seattle Voice Lab, SumianVoice, TransVoiceLessons Patreon) have their own invites; this note is only the invite named on the r/transvoice wiki.
