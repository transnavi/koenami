## What it offers
- iOS app by Murphy Labs, LLC, titled "Genderfluent: voice training." Listing's own description: "Genderfluent is your personal voice training app. Get real-time feedback on pitch, formants / resonance, gender, and volume as you speak. Whether you're practicing for gender affirmation, building confidence, or are just curious, Genderfluent gives you feedback anytime and anywhere."
- Free-plan limits stated in the listing: "Up to 1-min real-time formants and 5-min real-time gender estimates per day with the free plan; unlimited if you upgrade to Plus."
- Additional listed features: record & replay (15-min free storage after signup, unlimited with Plus paid tier), sharing recordings with a voice coach/friends/family, curated practice cards (words, phrases, stories), and daily goals with reminders.

## What it measures/teaches
- Real-time pitch, formants/resonance, an estimated "gender" classification of the voice, and volume, while the user speaks.
- Practice structure is card-based (curated words/phrases/stories) plus goal-setting and reminders, rather than a fixed multi-week course (no course structure described in the listing, unlike TransVoice.App or Christella VoiceUp).
