## What it offers
- iOS app by developer "Kailar." Listing's own framing: "designed for transgender, non-binary, and gender-diverse people who want to explore, practice, and better understand their voice," combining "guided exercises, a structured course, and visual tools for pitch, loudness, spectrograms, and other voice characteristics."
- A step-by-step course covering, per the listing: pitch and pitch variation; voice quality and articulation; pronunciation and clarity; prosody, speech melody, and expression; applying exercises to everyday speech; building a consistent practice routine.
- Explicitly supports multiple goals, not only feminization: "a more feminine voice presentation," "a more masculine voice presentation," "androgynous or non-binary vocal expression," or simply "better understanding their speaking voice." Rated 4.77/5 from 22 ratings at time of fetch; genres Health & Fitness / Education.

## What it measures/teaches
- Live tools: real-time pitch monitor with Hertz display, spectrogram, loudness meter, voice stability/pitch tracking, and recording/comparison tools for tracking practice over time.
- Listing is explicit about the limits of its own measurements: "The measurements can help you observe changes and experiment with different speaking styles. They are not an assessment of your voice or gender." And separately: "Displayed measurements are estimates and may vary depending on your device, microphone, environment, and speaking style."

## Limits
- Listing states the app "does not guarantee specific results" and frames itself as "a learning and practice app for independent voice exploration," not a clinical or diagnostic tool.
- Listing advises stopping an exercise "if it feels uncomfortable" — no built-in safety/vocal-health screening beyond that instruction.
- iOS only per the listing found; monetization model (free vs. paid tiers) not detailed in the description text retrieved.
