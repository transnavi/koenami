## What it offers
- The Japanese professional society for speech-language-hearing and voice medicine, and the publisher of the journal this vault's Japanese perception study comes from ([[sakuraba2009]]).
- From the UMIN society record (A00064, updated 2025-04-23): 日本音声言語医学会 / The Japan Society of Logopedics and Phoniatrics, 設立年 1956, 会員数 1,700人, 理事長 梅野博仁 (久留米大学医学部 耳鼻咽喉科・頭頸部外科学講座 主任教授), 事務局 in 飯田橋, Tokyo.
- 機関誌: 「音声言語医学（年間4回発行、1,800部、10,000円／年間）」 — the journal in which [[sakuraba2009]] and the Japanese testosterone time-course papers ([[nimura2015]], [[yanagi2015]]) were published.
- Credentials, added recently: 「1．音声言語認定医　2021年開設　2．音声言語認定士　2021年開設」. Both started in 2021; the society's certification system is thus newer than the Japanese gender-medicine certification ([[wikipedia-ja-gi-society]], which records 認定医 from 2016 and コーディネーター from 2021).
- Its clinical guideline: 「音声障害診療ガイドライン2018版」, published through 金原出版.
- Upcoming meeting at the time of the record: 第71回日本音声言語医学会総会・学術講演会, 2026-10-23/24, 渡邊雄介 (国際医療福祉大学 東京ボイスセンター), 東京赤坂キャンパス. Parent body listed: 日本耳鼻咽喉科頭頸部外科学会.

## What it measures/teaches
- Its subject matter is voice and speech disorders generally — 「音声障害診療ガイドライン2018版」 is a dysphonia guideline, not a gender guideline. Gender-related voice work appears in its journal as individual papers, not as a society programme.
- The 言語聴覚士 (speech-language-hearing therapist) profession this vault's [[speech-language-pathology]] concept covers is adjacent: the society is a medical-society body of physicians and researchers, and its 認定士 scheme is how the clinical voice field credentials non-physician practitioners in Japan.

## Limits
- Recorded from the UMIN society database rather than the society's own site (jslp.org did not respond to the fetch in this pass); figures are the society's own registration data as of 2025-04-23.
- No page of this society was found that addresses trans voice care; its relevance here is the journal and the credentialing picture, not a policy or a training offering.
