## What it offers
- The team site of 王棨德 (ENT attending and voice-center director at 亞東紀念醫院, New Taipei) and its speech-language pathologists — a hospital-based Taiwanese service covering both surgery and training for gender-related voice change, listed in the TAPCPR friendly-provider directory as 聲帶手術，可轉介語言治療師 ([[tapcpr-friendly-2024]]).
- The 變聲/調音/跨性別 surgery page (dated 2026-01-28) states numeric targets: for (birth-assigned) males raising pitch, 音頻調整至女性音域的目標為提高到180赫茲以上; for females lowering, 降至140赫茲以下; it explains the mechanism as adjusting 聲帶的長度、厚度、以及張力, notes puberty drops girls' pitch 約半個八度 and boys' 超過一個八度, and posts audio case pairs (e.g. 語言訓練 115→191 Hz and 117→166 Hz; 玻尿酸注射 lowering 117→101 Hz; 開放式嗓音重置 128→107 Hz).
- It posts prices: 手術費用依手術方式決定，約8萬到12萬之間，麻醉費用另計，約1~3萬 (surgery NTD 80,000–120,000 plus anesthesia NTD 10,000–30,000), and repeats that 手術的主要目標是"調整音高"，音質在手術後可能會有些許改變, recommending post-operative 語言治療 to learn 不同性別的發聲特質.
- The 2026-08-24 news post advertises the center's 跨性別聲音訓練: 不需要開刀，透過音高、共鳴位置及嗓音功能訓練，慢慢找到更符合自己的聲音 ("no surgery needed: pitch, resonance placement and voice-function training to gradually find a voice that fits you").

## What it measures/teaches
- Pitch in Hz as the headline metric (180 Hz up / 140 Hz down targets), with resonance placement and vocal function as the training pillars — the same triad the Taiwan review summarizes (音高攀升運動、共鳴治療、嗓音保健運動, [[lin2024]]).
- Demonstrates outcomes via paired before/after audio rather than published acoustic series.

## Limits
- Provider marketing pages (evidence: community): the case audio and targets are the team's own presentation, not peer-reviewed outcomes; the 180/140 Hz targets are service-defined, though close to the ranges in [[fundamental-frequency]] sources.
- Fee and case data drift; fetched pages listed above only.
