## What it offers
- A public Reddit community created 23 February 2012. The 2025-01-09 Wayback capture of the wiki index lists 116,772 subscribers and the tagline "A place to share your transgender vocal training related recordings for constructive criticism by the community."
- Sidebar copy on that capture: "This is a place to share your voice recording for constructive criticism by the community, as well as gather and share resources for vocal training."
- Rules recorded on the capture: no bigotry; keep posts trans-voice-training related; solicitation unrelated to voice training is barred (coaches may offer services but "should not be done excessively or as ones only contribution"); no NSFW even if voice-related; surveys need moderator approval against the American Anthropological Association code of conduct.
- Points members to a wiki (feminization, masculinization, other resources), a Discord invite (`https://discord.gg/BPPMkDu`), and recording hosts Clyp, Soundcloud and Audioboo.

## What it measures/teaches
- The capture's "starter tips" are attributed, not presented as the subreddit's own research.
- `/u/fnvt` on feminization: "Stay out of falsetto! Falsetto is only useful in finding your head voice"; "Work on your resonance! You should feel no vibrations in your chest when speaking. The target you're looking for is your 'head voice'"; "try pushing your larynx (the thing your adam's apple is attached to) up as far as you can"; "Talk to yourself in your female voice when no one else is around, so you can build up muscle memory."
- `/u/Makecab` on masculinization: humming vowels after sirens ("Mmmm-may…") to "anchor your voice lower"; "ng" sounds ("bing-bong, ding-dong, king-kong") at a low comfortable pitch, later with the chin raised, "helpful for both pitch and resonance."
- No Hz targets, formant numbers or app recommendations appear on the index page itself.

## Limits
- Reddit blocks live fetches; this note is the 2025-01-09 Wayback capture of the wiki index, not the live feed.
