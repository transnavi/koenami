## What it offers
- The YouTube channel of Prof. Dr. Kürşad Yelken. The channel's own description: "Prof. Dr. Kursat Yelken is a Professor of Otolaryngology, Head&Neck Surgery and a Fellow of the European Board of Otolaryngology, Head&Neck Surgery. He received Laryngology training in Harvard Medical School, MEEI, Boston, USA. He offers Voice & Facial Aesthetics treatments out of his clinic in Istanbul."
- Videos confirmed by oEmbed: «Ses feminizasyon terapisi/Voice Feminization Therapy» (uploaded 2022-09-22) and "Voice Feminization Surgery, Voice Lift Surgery"; the Turkish search listing also surfaces further material under his name.
- The same surgeon is the author of the Turkish textbook chapter «Ses Estetiği» [[yelken2022]] and a co-author of the tensioning-glottoplasty case series in this vault [[birkent2026]], so the channel is one arm of a documented Turkish phonosurgical practice.

## What it measures/teaches
- The description of the therapy video makes an explicit argument against treating pitch as the whole task: «Ses frekansını yükseltmek, seste feminen algısını yaratmak için tek başına yeterli olmayabilir» — raising the frequency alone may not be enough to create a feminine perception — and lists what else must be addressed: «Rezonans, Entonasyon, Jest ve mimikler, Vurgu, Konuşma hızı, Kullanılan sözcükler» (resonance, intonation, gestures and facial expression, stress, speech rate, word choice). The English half of the same description repeats the list as "Resonance / Intonations / Gestures and facial expressions / Accent / Speech speed".
- It states the surgical side plainly: experienced surgeons can bring frequency to the desired level, but patients may still report that the voice is not feminine enough, and it concludes by recommending "voice therapy before and after" surgery.
