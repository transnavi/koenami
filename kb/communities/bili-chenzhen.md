## What it measures/teaches
- The channel's three titles state a complete clinical position: training works (5-week results), it needs a professional rather than self-study (专业人员的引导), and it is assessed on 音高 plus 音色 rather than pitch alone. The last point matches the meta-analytic finding that pitch explains under half of perceived gender [[leung2018]] and the multi-cue view in [[voice-quality]].
- The account name 陈臻谈嗓音 ("Chen Zhen on voice") and its content line up with the mtf.wiki protocol page that documents the same trainer's F0-first method and his anatomy-bound view of formants [[mtfwiki-chenzhen]], though the identification is by name and content rather than by the channel's own statement — the existing note [[bili-chenzhen2025]] records the same inference.
