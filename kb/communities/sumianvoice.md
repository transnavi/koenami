## What it offers
- Coaching practice of Sumi, self-described on the 2026-09-17 homepage as "Gender voice modification coach" offering "Voice training for trans and non-binary people and anyone else with an interest in changing the gender presentation of their voice." Public and private lessons, recorded for review; Discord with "practice sessions, Q/A, workshops" and "two free lessons every week."
- Open wiki at `https://wiki.sumianvoice.com/` ("Voice Resource Project"), started by Sumi and Emma. Front page mission: "approachable information and where possible, direction, so that people have what they need to at least get started on changing their voice." GitHub: `SumianVoice/TransVoice-Wiki`. The front page states it is "still mostly feminisation oriented" with masculinisation "in the works."
- Wiki table of contents (fetched 2026-09-17) names the community's working vocabulary as page titles: Vocal Weight, Resonance, Clarity, Breathiness, Onsets, Creak, Nasality, Speech Patterns, Pitch Naturalisation / PIPM, Base Pitch, Hyperadduction, Questionable Exercises, Questionable Terms.

## What it measures/teaches
- The vocal-weight wiki page (fetched 2026-09-17) is the fullest English-community definition in this pass; see [[sumi-vocal-weight]].
- Resonance page: "We hear this quality as low and high resonance or as brightness"; "It's best not to think about resonance as formants or physical space changes but just as how the voice sounds." Named brightening tools include whispers, "vampire slides," larynx height, vowel shape, OPC (oropharyngeal closure), OPE (oropharyngeal expansion), mouth space and twang.
- Homepage also links a "voice map" at `https://map.sumianvoice.com/` (not fetched).

## Limits
- Wiki banner: "This site is under construction! Stuff be missing, beware!" Several linked glossary and getting-started URLs 404'd on 2026-09-17.
- Coaching site is commercial (Ko-Fi, paid lessons). The wiki is volunteer-edited pedagogy, not a trial.
