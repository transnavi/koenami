## What it measures/teaches
- The 哈音 drill, given a physiological rationale in the description: an unconstricted 哈 has low 声带 tension, so it lets the learner feel the difference between relaxed and pressed phonation before any pitch is attempted. That is the same diagnostic use of a breathy onset the 2021 manual calls 男虚音 and the essay course calls 气音 [[bili-cv460384]].
- The 咬字 video gives the progression explicitly — 字 → 词 → 句 → 段 → 篇 — which is the practice ladder [[bili-cv9417414]] sets out as 单音-延长单音-短词-延长短词-短句-长句.
