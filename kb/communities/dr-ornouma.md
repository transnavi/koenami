# Dr. Ornouma Sriwanishvipat — Voice Change Surgery

## What it offers
- The personal site of Ornouma Sriwanishvipat, MD, an otorhinolaryngologist practising at Yanhee International Hospital, Bangkok; the site carries a dedicated Voice Change Surgery section with 中文 and 한국어 page versions, plus a before-and-after page (dr-ornouma.com/voice-change-surgery-before-and-after/).
- Corrections of low pitch (patients "who wish to acquire a feminine voice (common in 'Male-To-Female' Transsexuals)"): 2–3 hours under general anesthesia, a 1.5–2 cm incision along natural skin lines, sutures out at 10 days, 2 nights in hospital.
- Corrections of high pitch ("Male patients or 'Female-To-Male' transgender with high voice pitch pattern who wish to acquire a masculine voice"): 2–3 hours, 2–3 cm incision, 1 week to suture removal, 2 nights in hospital — i.e. an open, external approach in both directions.
- The page frames the stakes in the site's own words: "The alteration of the voice pitch frequency is an important step in the transsexual change of an individual to make a person blend in the gender of his or her preference."
- Post-operative care: no talking for one week (communication in writing or sign language), coughing discouraged, soft diet, and "a follow up check up after 10 days for suture removal alongside with the speech therapy" — speech therapy appears at suture removal.

## What it measures/teaches
- Pitch direction and incision-based protocol detail; outcome evidence is the before-and-after media page (not analysed numerically here); speech therapy positioned as part of the package.
