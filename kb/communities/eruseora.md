# 이러서라

## What it offers
- A Korean speech-coaching channel and site (eruseora.com). Its channel description (fetched 2026-09-17) presents the coach as 연세대학교 언어병리학 석사 (MA in language pathology, Yonsei University) and 사단법인 언어재활사협회 정회원 (full member of the Korean Association of Speech-Language Pathologists), coaching actors (윤계상, 이시영, 유태오, 강유석 are named) and clients with 발음/음성 문제.
- The description lists target users: professionals needing announcer-like voices, actors needing character voices and 발성/발음 coaching, and people with long-standing pronunciation and voice problems from habit or tension.
- One video in the training oeuvre addresses transgender voice: "매력적인 목소리 만들기, 예쁜 목소리 만들기, 멋있는 목소리 만들기, 목소리 성형, 발성연습[이러서라]transgender's voice" (VdlA0aH_gso, published 2020-04-12, verified by oEmbed 2026-09-17) — the title itself uses 목소리 성형 ("voice plastic surgery") as the colloquial label for voice change.

## What it measures/teaches
- Korean-language speech and voice coaching (발성, 발음, 말하기) by a credentialled 언어재활사-track coach; the transgender-voice video sits inside a general catalogue rather than a dedicated trans-voice curriculum.
