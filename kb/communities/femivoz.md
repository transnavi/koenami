# FEMIVOZ — FEMINIZACIÓN DE LA VOZ

## What it offers
- A voice-coaching practice run by Mariela Astudillo, self-described in the channel description as "Vocóloga y entrenadora vocal LGBTQIA+" with "20 años de experiencia", based in Spain (WhatsApp contact with a +34 number, site femivoz.es, contacto@femivoz.es).

- Online sessions by videoconference: "SESIONES ONLINE DE FEMINIZACIÓN DE LA VOZ EN ESPAÑOL PARA TODOS LOS PAÍSES", with a free first appointment advertised.
- The YouTube channel carries exercise videos in Spanish and French (the French-language "EXERCICES DE FÉMINISATION DE LA VOIX" series verified via oEmbed on 2026-09-17).

## What it measures/teaches
- Feminization, masculinization, androgynization and neutralization of the voice, steroid-related and de-transition voice work, and "identity and emotional voice development"; a free-video model with paid one-to-one coaching behind it.
- The French videos walk through articulation, respiration, posture, vocalises and chant (singing), and warn viewers about exercises found on the internet ("attention aux exercices trouvés sur le net", video title verified via oEmbed).

## Limits
- A commercial coaching business; "vocóloga" is a self-designation, not a licensed clinical title in Spain; the site femivoz.es was not fetched in this pass and the claims are the channel's own marketing copy.
- Androgynization and neutralization are offered without published outcome data anywhere in the field [[nonbinary-voice]].
