# Measurement tools

Software used to measure or display voice: [[praat]], [[voicelab]], [[phonia]], [[informant]], [[acoustic-gender-space]], [[voice-tools-devextras]], [[christella-voiceup]]. Koenami's own method is on its method page.

## What is known
- General-purpose analysis: Praat measures pitch, formants, intensity, jitter, shimmer and voice breaks and is the reference against which other tools are validated [[praat]]; VoiceLab automates the same measurements over many files with fixed parameters [[voicelab]]; Phonia is a free browser-based workstation "validated against Praat" [[phonia]]. Koenami's own measures are Praat's autocorrelation F0 median, a ΔF-type formant-spacing average, Praat's HNR and a 1–4 kHz to 0.1–1 kHz energy ratio, each with the caveats its method page states [[koenami-method]].
- Live feedback for practice: InFormant shows pitch and formant tracks while speaking [[informant]]; the Acoustic Gender Space viewer places recordings on a pitch-by-resonance map with reference clips and states that "there are no sharp cutoffs" between gendered regions [[acoustic-gender-space]]; Voice Tools (iOS) shows pitch, an associated "voice gender" and volume in real time and states that it does not alter the voice [[voice-tools-devextras]]; TransVoice.App combines a guided course with pitch, spectrogram and loudness tools and disclaims being an assessment of the voice or its gender [[transvoice-app]]; Genderfluent adds a "gender" estimate on a freemium plan [[genderfluent]]; Christella VoiceUp packages a three-stage feminisation course with a free pitch graph [[christella-voiceup]].
- Evaluated feedback software: TruVox's pitch module (five structured exercises with real-time pitch display) scored 79.8 on the System Usability Scale with 21 transfeminine participants, and exercise performance improved within a single session in all but one exercise; the authors say the module "should not be considered a complete GAVT package" [[weese2025]]. Its resonance module shows vocal-tract resonances as peaks of a real-time linear-predictive spectrum with built-in English vowel targets; in-lab usability scored 75.25 and 8 of 10 participants trended in the expected direction on the most-practised vowel within one session [[mcallister2025]].
- Short visual feedback on F2 lets speakers move F2 up or down on demand, and higher F2 was rated more feminine [[kawitzky2020]]; a telepractice voice-training programme produced results similar to in-person delivery in 82 student teachers [[grillo2021]]; a virtual-reality pilot with eleven trans women raised willingness to communicate in the VR group, with modest and variable voice-related outcomes [[leyns2025]].
- Automatic gender scoring: a 2007 Japanese master's thesis trained an estimator of perceptual 女声度 on listener labels and reached model–listener r = 0.86 against listener–listener r = 0.88 [[maruyama2007]]; in 2026 an AI classifier trained on a large low-quality corpus matched human listeners' binary gender judgements at 92% (100% for the listeners) and beat a model trained on a smaller high-quality set [[simon2026]]. The wider scoring literature is under [[machine-gender-scoring]].
- A Japanese practitioner who has built formant-measuring programs writes that formant readings shift with vowel, vocal-tract shape, noise, microphone, sampling rate and LPC settings, that browser audio pipelines damage F3–F4, and that formant "gender verdict" apps are fun to play with but 「科学的に正確な性差判定ツールではない」 [[note-formant-app]].
- A free, ad-free Japanese practice app built by a learner offers synthetic model voices, in-app recording and comparison without external storage, and deliberately neutral branding so that using it does not out the user [[note-kirkas]].

## Contested or unclear
- How much a real-time display helps beyond a coach's ear: the single-session gains in the TruVox studies are usability and short-term performance results, with no comparison against training without the display [[weese2025]] [[mcallister2025]].
- Whether formants measured by consumer apps are stable enough to train against, given the measurement problems a practitioner lists [[note-formant-app]] and the vowel and mouth-shape dependence Koenami's own page states [[koenami-method]].

## Open questions
- No published trial compares feedback software with unaided practice on listener-rated outcomes.
- No Japanese-language feedback tool has been evaluated with users.
