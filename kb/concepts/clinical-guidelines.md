# Clinical guidelines

Professional standards that frame assessment, training and surgery.

## What is known
- WPATH Standards of Care version 8, voice and communication chapter [[coleman2022]].
- Clinical review and good-practice recommendations under SOC7 [[davies2015]].
- ASHA's practice portal page gives SLPs a central role, frames its assessment areas by SOC8, lists the technique repertoire, and states that clinicians serving this population should be specifically trained and may not refuse services on the basis of gender identity [[asha-gender-affirming-voice]].
- The UCSF guideline chapter assigns behavioural change to specialty-trained SLPs and phonosurgery to laryngologists, and calls for a comprehensive evaluation by both, including videostroboscopy, before treatment starts [[ucsf2016]].
- In the UK the RCSLT competency framework defines who is qualified: core voice competencies plus supervised Levels A–C set by caseload; it went through a profession-wide consultation in 2017 and predates SOC8 [[rcslt2019]].
