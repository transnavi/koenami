# Singing and the speaking voice (歌唱と話声)

Singing pedagogy is the other half of the technique literature trans voice work borrows from: how to cross a passaggio, what belting is, what "mix" measures as, and which studio words (chest, head, twang, cry) isolate a structure rather than a gender. The speaking task is not the singing task — a speaking pitch lives in a few tones, a sung line crosses registers on purpose — but the mechanisms are the same larynx.

## What is known
- "Vocal pedagogy is the study of the art and science of voice instruction. It is used in the teaching of singing and assists in defining what singing is, how singing works, and how singing technique is accomplished" [[wikipedia-vocal-pedagogy]]. Voice therapy is the speech-language pathologist's field; pedagogy is the related field "to alter voice for the purpose of singing" [[wikipedia-voice-therapy]]. The Japanese interlanguage title of the pedagogy article is ボイストレーニング, a word Japanese community use also applies to speech work.
- Register vocabulary in the studio is not the speech-pathology four. "Head voice" and "chest voice" may each mean a range, a resonance area, or a timbre, and "there is no consistent opinion among vocal music professionals" [[wikipedia-head-voice]] [[wikipedia-chest-voice]]. A review found more than 100 scholarly terms for registers [[herbst2020]]. Henrich traces the confusion to García and proposes laryngeal mechanism as the way "to specify at least the laryngeal nature of a given singing voice register" [[henrich2006]].
- **Passaggio.** The transition area between registers; the Italian school names a primo and secondo passaggio and, in the male voice, a zona di passaggio, and treats even timbre through that zone as a training goal [[wikipedia-passaggio]]. Untrained men break with irregular fold vibration [[echternach2010]]; professional tenors who carry stage voice across C4–A4 open the jaw and lower the larynx relative to falsetto for most vowel conditions, with vowel-dependent pharynx width [[echternach2014]]. A sung scale can contain a laryngeal transition and a later resonatory one that teachers do not agree in naming [[herbst2020]]. In elite sopranos above C6, no exclusive register-defining resonance strategy was found [[koberlein2021]].
- **Mix.** Singers' mix, produced on instruction, is measurable: lower pressure than chest, lower flow than falsetto, open quotient and H1–H2 between the two, at the same F0 [[lee2023]]. TA/CT activity is gradual in trained singers, "likely leading to intermediate scenarios" [[herbst2020]]. Belting is "sometimes described as 'high chest voice' or 'mixed voice'"; the encyclopedia treats it as "a descriptive term for the use of a register", not a register of its own — "a singer carries their chest voice above their break or passaggio with a proportion of head voice" [[wikipedia-belting-music]].
- **Belting and load.** On CT, belting shares a high larynx and a narrowed epilarynx with hyperfunctional dysphonia, and differs by a wider lip and jaw opening and a larger oral cavity ("megaphone shape"); the authors ask about the "price of decibels" [[saldias2019]]. Belting's pressed sound "has led to the hypothesis that belting may be strenuous", while singers and teachers "do not regard belting as particularly strenuous"; that disagreement is what the study set out to image [[saldias2019]]. Water-resistance therapy as a CCM warm-up has measurable aerodynamic effects [[saldias2020]]; tube phonation in singers is associated with comfort, projection and economy [[mendes2018]].
- **Twang, Estill, CCM qualities.** Twang is mostly a resonance manoeuvre (higher F1 and F2) that also raises closed quotient [[sundberg2010]]. Estill partitions production into controllable structures and trains qualities as combinations of those options [[wikipedia-estill-voice-training]] [[steinhauer2019]]. EVT-certified CCM singers showed better control of perturbation and spectral energy on a set task than otherwise-trained CCM singers [[fantini2017]]; Slack/Thick/Thin/Stiff after training are distinct on aerodynamic and perturbation measures [[barone2021]]. Six Estill phonation types in one singer were distinct on 3D MRI for fold thickness, contact and open quotient, with supraglottic posture covarying [[traser2026]]. Listeners trained on the model heard laryngeal and epilaryngeal configurations at above chance [[zhang2026]]. A practitioner essay argues that anatomy-first, "gender bias-free terminology" makes EVT usable for non-binary singers [[accetta2022]]. Student teachers trained with Estill figures inside a prevention model improved acoustics and fatigue measures, in person and by telepractice [[grillo2021]].
- **Acoustic registers in trained voices.** Once the laryngeal binary is smoothed, remaining "register" events can be harmonics passing through formants at vowel-predictable points, historically named "turning over, turning, tipping, or covering" [[bozeman2022]].
- **Singing handicap in trans speakers.** In 61 trans women seeking gender-affirming voice care, Singing VHI-10 was 19 ± 13 and correlated only weakly with TVQ (R = 0.35); "trans women report perceived voice handicap in speaking and singing, which appears separate from gender-related voice/communication concerns" [[young2021]].
- **Transfer into speech work.** Semi-occluded exercises, resonant voice, twang and sirens/glides used in trans training come from this studio and clinic literature ([[titze2006]], [[verdolini1998]], [[sundberg2010]], [[adabi2026]], [[yoxon2023]]). Community curricula that teach them for speech include [[transvoicelessons]].
- **Binding and singing.** In a survey of 38 singers who wear chest binders while singing, participants reported mental-health benefits alongside difficulty with inhalation, loss of singing stamina and decreased voice volume, and preferred to keep binding while singing [[dolquist2025]].

## Contested or unclear
- Whether belting is sustainable or strenuous is exactly the disagreement [[saldias2019]] images and does not settle (one singer, four patients).
- "Head" versus "falsetto" as a sexed pair of words is a pedagogical habit, not a physiological split [[herbst2020]] [[wikipedia-falsetto]].
- How much of a singing-trained mix setting a speaker can hold in connected speech at a conversational loudness is not measured in this vault.

## Open questions
- Listener gender ratings of the same speaker in sung mix versus spoken mix versus spoken M2, at matched F0.
- Whether singing training (passaggio work, mix, twang) changes speaking-voice outcomes in trans adults beyond what speech therapy already does.
