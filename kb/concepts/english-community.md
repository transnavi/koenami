# The English-speaking trans voice community

Who teaches gendered voice in English, in which vocabulary, on which platforms. The teaching runs from a 1990s personal guide through a subreddit and Discord to coach businesses with their own jargon; the vocabulary the whole ecosystem now shares — pitch, resonance/size, vocal weight — was largely fixed by TransVoiceLessons in 2019–2022 and is reused with credit by other coaches.

## What is known

**The historical root.** Melanie Anne Phillips' "How to Develop a Female Voice" teaches that pitch is secondary ("the difference between the average male voice and the average female voice is only about 1/2 octave"), names Resonance as the seventh, decisive "tool", rejects falsetto, and adds strain limits ("never even approach the point at which you might get hoarse") [[phillips2010]]. Her mechanism — clamping the "voice box" down to half size — is her own model, not the tract-length account [[formants]] [[phillips2010]].

**The forum layer.** r/transvoice was created in 2012 as a place to post recordings "for constructive criticism by the community" [[r-transvoice]]; its wiki index (last revised 2016, capture 2025) links feminization, masculinization and other-resources pages that could not be fetched [[transvoice-wiki]]; its starter tips already treat resonance/larynx height as the work and falsetto as the trap [[r-transvoice]]. The associated Discord server is advertised for "instant voice feedback" [[transvoice-discord]].

**TransVoiceLessons.** A paid coaching business with four listed instructors and free YouTube material [[transvoicelessons]]. Its videos fix the community vocabulary in stages: resonance and pitch as separable X–Y axes with a pitch tracker as the isolation tool [[erose2019]]; R1 as "THE GENDER CONTROL KNOB" [[erose2020]]; ear training that hears R1, R2 and weight separately [[erose2021]]; the same source contrast first as thick/thin fold mass [[erose2020a]], then as "vocal weight" drilled at constant pitch [[erose2022a]], then the weight–size "fullness" model with its buzzy (overfull) and hollow (underfull) failure modes [[erose2022]]. One DSFD co-author is affiliated with TransVoiceLessons, which is the closest the community vocabulary has come to a measured dataset [[netzorg2025]].

**Sumi / SumianVoice.** A coaching practice with a free weekly-lesson Discord and the open Voice Resource Project wiki, whose table of contents is itself a map of the community's working vocabulary (Vocal Weight, Resonance, Clarity, Breathiness, Onsets, Creak, Hyperadduction, Questionable Exercises, Questionable Terms) [[sumianvoice]]. The wiki's weight page is the fullest English definition in the vault [[sumi-vocal-weight]]; the pitch-slide lesson adds the loudness caveat [[sumi2021]].

**Renée Yoxon.** A Montréal teacher framing the work as de-medicalized ("you are not a problem to be solved!") with voice dysphoria as the starting complaint [[renee-yoxon]]; originator of the widely circulated Big Dog Little Dog resonance drill, whose wording collapses brightness and lightness into one sentence [[yoxon2023]], and of a 2025 vocal-weight exercise video [[yoxon2025]].

**Seattle Voice Lab.** A remote coaching business marketing feminization, masculinization and androgyny in "6–8 lessons" via a "Trans Voice Certified™" coach; its YouTube titles use the same weight/resonance vocabulary ("Husky Feminine Voices (Weight vs. Resonance!)") [[seattle-voice-lab]].

**Independent creators.** A popular beginner larynx tutorial that publicly retracted its swallow-to-find-the-larynx cue on safety grounds [[lucy2023]]; ear-training videos reusing TVL's size/weight concepts with credit [[dasilva2022]]; "whisper sirens" documented as a named drill [[sutherland2025]].

**The numbers the community quotes as targets.** A 2026 guide records the slogans and bands in circulation: "Just raise your pitch to 200 Hz and you'll sound feminine"; "female-typical above ~180 Hz, male-typical ~100–140 Hz, with an ambiguous range between—commonly narrowed to ~145–165 Hz"; "F0 explains only 41.6% of perceived gender" [[lucero2026]]. Each number traces to research already in the vault: the 41.6% is the meta-analysis's F0 variance figure [[leung2018]], the 145–165 Hz window is the connected-speech ambiguity range [[gelfer2013]], and the F0-plus-formants point restates [[hillenbrand2009]]; the guide itself warns against precise-looking breakdowns of the remaining variance [[lucero2026]]. The same 180 Hz floor appears on the Wikipedia therapy article per [[lucero2026]] and as a clinic target in the Chinese-speaking world [[voicedoctor-tw]]. The community's own measurement habit is the pitch tracker [[erose2019]], now packaged as apps that estimate pitch, formants and a "gender" score in real time — while stating "they are not an assessment of your voice or gender" [[transvoice-app]] [[genderfluent]] [[tools]].

**Outcome words.** "Passing" — being perceived as a cisgender person of one's gender — is the listener-outcome word, with its desirability debated inside the community [[wikipedia-passing-gender]]; "clocked" is its failure case, as in a voice that "clocks" as male [[erose2022]]; "voice dysphoria" names the starting complaint [[renee-yoxon]] [[wikipedia-transgender-voice-therapy]].

**How the community model maps onto research.** The pitch/size/weight split is a learner-facing restatement of the source–filter division: weight and spectral slope belong to the source, size to the tract [[source-filter-model]] [[bozeman2022]] [[herbst2020]]. The community's R1-first claim ([[erose2020]]) ranks differently in listener research, where F0 comes first and formants second [[leung2018]] [[gelfer2013]] — and in the TVL-adjacent DSFD study ΔF was the strongest predictor of perceived gender [[netzorg2025]]. The community's falsetto-avoidance heuristic [[phillips2010]] [[r-transvoice]] has a mechanism-level counterpart: the M2 register is available to any larynx and by itself says nothing about how the voice is gendered [[herbst2020]].

## Contested or unclear
- Speed: "in the span of 6–8 lessons you'll have found your feminine voice" [[seattle-voice-lab]] against 8–12 sessions in the largest prospective study [[sodersten2026]] and a 12-session, 4-week intensive pilot [[lin2026]] — no community curriculum has published outcome data at all.
- R1 as "the most important element of voice feminization" [[erose2020]] against F0-first, formants-second rankings [[leung2018]] [[gelfer2013]] [[netzorg2025]].
- "Changing vocal weight alone can in some cases result in an androgynous voice" [[sumi2021]] — an author's claim with no listener test behind it.
- Big Dog Little Dog's equation of brighter with lighter and more feminine in one sentence [[yoxon2023]]; brightness (tract) and lightness (source) are separable [[bozeman2022]].

## Open questions
- The r/transvoice wiki's resource lists (femsources, mascsources, othersources) are linked but unfetchable (Wayback 429 on this pass), so the forum's own canon of guides is unverified [[transvoice-wiki]].
- "L's Voice Training Guide", widely referenced in English written guides, could not be located or fetched in this pass (search endpoints empty or blocked, Internet Archive CDX offline); it has no note and no claim here.
- Most teaching now happens on commercial platforms and private Discords whose curricula are invisible without paying or joining [[transvoicelessons]] [[seattle-voice-lab]] [[transvoice-discord]].
- Whether the community's ear-training pipeline produces better outcomes than clinic protocols is untested; the only measured artifact is the 45-minute DSFD from one teacher [[netzorg2025]].
