# Gendered voice in the other European languages

A hub for the languages outside this vault's main English/Japanese/Chinese/Korean/Spanish coverage: Portuguese, German, French, Italian, Dutch, Swedish, Turkish, Russian, Polish. Each statement carries its source.

## Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal)
- Brazilian fonoaudiologia: the SUS Trans Outpatient Clinic's PAV-trans/PRV-trans protocol stages individual source work and a 12-parameter group filter programme, aiming at "passabilidade" [[dornelas2021]]; self-perception of voice in trans people [[dornelas2020]]; TVQ adaptations [[schwarz2017]] [[schwarz2024]]; a mapping of assessment and therapy procedures used by Brazilian SLPs [[viana2025]].
- Portugal: a PRISMA review in the national ENT society's journal sets the stepped protocol — audio-perceptual (GRBAS, CAPE-V), acoustics (F0, formants, range, MPT), laryngostroboscopy, TWVQ and VHI-10; therapy first, Wendler glottoplasty for trans women when insufficient, and the observation that most trans men are satisfied with the hormonal result [[ferreira2025]].
- Community: Brazilian tutorial series teach altura, ressonância and peso vocal — pitch, resonance, vocal weight [[ganda2025]]; pt Wikipedia's trans-voice article is titled Treinamento vocal and includes crossdressers in its audience [[wikipedia-pt-treinamento-vocal]].
- European and Brazilian clinical registers differ: terapeuta da fala versus fonoaudiólogo(a) [[ferreira2025]] [[dornelas2021]].

## German
- Instruments: German TVQ MtF validated (α = .97 range reported in the study) [[salm2020]].
- Care structure: a Cologne/Düsseldorf expert-interview paper finds no German guideline and recommends early Logopädie before irreversible surgery, with speaking-F0 adaptation as the central treatment goal [[heydrich2023]].
- Surgery: the Würzburg glottoplasty series' telephone test — 7 of 18 operated speakers passed as female on the band-limited channel, and formants stayed between the sexes [[meister2017]].
- Community: peer Stimmtraining videos in German ("Stimmfeminisierung ohne OP") [[leonie2025]]; de Wikipedia covers Feminisierende Laryngoplastik but holds no therapy article [[wikipedia-de-feminisierende-laryngoplastik]].
- Vocabulary: Stimmfeminisierung, Logopädie/Logopädin, Kopfstimme/Bruststimme, Falsett, Stimmlippen, Kehlkopf [[heydrich2023]] [[meister2017]]; note Herbst's objection to reserving Falsett for men and Kopfstimme for women [[herbst2020]].

## French
- Instruments: the French TVQ MtF (Liège + Aix-en-Provence, n = 36 Belgian and French trans women) is usable in clinic, with six concern areas [[morsomme2019]].
- Practice: a Glossa protocol trial with five trans women — F0, formant values and vocal range rose "parfois", interindividual variability prevented firm conclusions, patient satisfaction rose [[loret2019]].
- Encyclopedic: fr Wikipedia titles the field Féminisation de la voix and names orthophonie as the route [[wikipedia-fr-feminisation-de-la-voix]].
- Québec sources could not be verified in this pass (see the report); vocabulary attested here is metropolitan/Belgian: féminisation de la voix, orthophonie/orthophoniste, prise en charge, plainte [[loret2019]].
- French exercise videos exist from a Spain-based coach [[femivoz]].

## Italian
- Instruments: I-TVQMtF, the Italian adaptation of the TVQ MtF, translated and preliminarily validated [[robotti2022]].
- Community: a 2023 video frames the work as logopedia with daily exercises "per circa un anno, seguiti da un logopedista esperto" [[iula2023]].
- No Italian-language outcome study beyond the instrument was verified in this pass.

## Dutch (Belgium and the Netherlands)
- The Ghent group dominates: systematic review of speech-therapy effects [[leyns2021]]; short-term listener/self-perception results of a feminization programme [[leyns2023]]; long-term follow-up [[leyns2024]]; a virtual-reality delivery pilot [[leyns2025]]; a randomized trial of intensive intonation training for a more masculine-sounding voice in Dutch speakers (23 participants, listeners rated voices more masculine) [[papeleu2026]]; glottoplasty outcomes [[dhaeseleer2023]].
- Dutch register vocabulary: the nl Wikipedia's M2 article is titled Kopstem, folding falset(stem) in as a synonym — a naming by felt location rather than mechanism [[wikipedia-nl-kopstem]].

## Swedish
- Karolinska lineage: acoustic outcomes of gender-affirming voice training and their association with listener perceptions [[sodersten2026]]; longitudinal testosterone effects in trans men [[nygren2016]].
- Umeå's qualitative line: clients' motives and barriers — "a voice of my own choice" against treatments "developed from views of vocal gender as perceived by cisgender persons" [[holmberg2023]].
- Swedish care framing is person-centred rather than pass-oriented, the contrast drawn against Brazil's "passabilidade" [[dornelas2021]].

## Turkish
- Instruments: TVQMtF-TR validated at Hacettepe (α = 0.972, ICC = 0.931) [[taskin2022]]; the reworked trans-male a-TVQFtM validated in Kocaeli (α = 0.975), with better scores in the hormone-treated group [[sirin2021]].
- Access: of 31 Istanbul respondents none had received speech and language therapy and 54.8% did not know the service existed; those who knew learned online [[oguz2021]].
- Surgery and acoustics: Wendler glottoplasty left F0 female-like but formants between the sexes in 20 Turkish-speaking trans women [[paltura2019]]; the Hacettepe laser reduction glottoplasty series [[yilmaz2021]].
- Turkish grammar is gender-neutral (no he/she distinction), a structural fact speakers in the cited studies navigate; the studies themselves do not analyse it.

## Russian
- The ru Wikipedia article Голосовая терапия для трансгендерных людей names both голосовая терапия and голосовой тренинг [[wikipedia-ru-golosovaya-terapiya]].
- No Russian-language peer-reviewed study of trans voice could be verified in this pass (Crossref, OpenAlex, PubMed and DOAJ searched 2026-09-17).

## Polish
- No Polish-language source on gendered voice was verified in this pass; the pl Wikipedia holds only the general anatomy/register articles (pl Falset, Krtań, Fałd głosowy; langlinks retrieved 2026-09-17). Listed here so the gap is visible.

## Open questions
- German and French care pathways are documented only through an expert-interview paper and a five-patient protocol — no controlled trial in either language.
- The Turkish studies do not separate Turkish-specific phonetic factors (vowel inventory, pitch-accent behaviour) from universal ones [[voice-across-languages]].
- Russian, Polish and Italian communities: YouTube/forum material exists but was not verifiable in this pass beyond [[iula2023]]; collecting it needs a dedicated search with better access to VK/Telegram and Italian forums.
