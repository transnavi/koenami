# Sex differences in voice: anatomy, puberty and culture

Why adult voices differ by sex, and how much of that is anatomy versus learned style.

## What is known
- Vocal-fold length drives the F0 difference; larynx size drives power and airflow [[titze1989]]; tract length and cavity proportions diverge at puberty in males [[fitch1999]]; developmental F0 and formant patterns are age-, sex- and vowel-specific [[whiteside2001]].
- Formant-based size cues are weak within a sex [[pisanski2014]]; taller, higher-testosterone men have lower F0 and ΔF [[cartei2014]].
- Phonetic sex differences include learned components [[simpson2009]]; Japanese–Dutch pitch differences are cultural [[vanbezooijen1995]]; creaky voice changed its gendered meaning within a generation [[yuasa2010]]; speakers exaggerate the dimorphism at will [[cartei2012]].
- Voice-actor "boy" voices follow a convention rather than real boys' acoustics [[hayashi2026]].

## Open questions
- Normative F0/formant ranges for Japanese adults by sex are not yet collected in this vault; Koenami's reference distributions are not population norms.
