# The Japanese scene

Japanese has two separate teaching traditions for producing a voice of the other sex, and they share a vocabulary. One is the performance tradition that grew up on ニコニコ動画 from 2007 and calls its practitioners 両声類; the other is trans voice practice, which reaches learners through ENT clinics, commercial voice schools, note.com posts and YouTube channels, and for which the national clinical guideline has no section. Reading a Japanese source on voice requires knowing which of the two is speaking, because the words are the same and the goal is not.

## What is known
- **The word.** 両声類 is the scene's own term, defined as 「性別を逆転させたような声で歌唱する人達への敬称」, a term of respect for singers who sing in a voice that sounds like their sex reversed [[nicodic-ryoseirui]]. The ニコニコ大百科 article, first written 2008-05-26 and last revised 2015-01-06, gives that definition as its first line [[nicodic-ryoseirui]]; the word remains a 俗語 with no interlanguage link on Japanese Wikipedia [[wikipedia-ja-ryouseirui]], and the pixiv encyclopedia's entry reproduces the same headword line [[pixiv-ryoseirui]].
- **What it labels.** The tag is applied 「主に"声質"だけをタグの対象とするため、(ある程度なら)音痴でもタグがつく」 — voice quality, not pitch accuracy, and not identity; a second sense covers anyone who commands both a 男声 and a 女声 [[nicodic-ryoseirui]]. Judgement is explicitly the listener's: 「ある人には女声に聞こえるが、別の人は男の裏声にしか聞こえない」 [[nicodic-ryoseirui]].
- **Where it lives.** On ニコニコ動画 the corpus is large and durable: 7,349 videos carrying 両声類 as of 2026-09-17, from a 2007-05-27 first entry to uploads in the same week as the query, with 女声 at 5,318, 男声 at 2,347 and 多声類 at 1,396 [[nicovideo-ryoseirui-tag]]. The most-viewed item is a 2009 two-singer cover at 3,263,956 views, and the tag there was hung on the performance by the audience, not claimed by the singer [[nicovideo-akapiko2009]]; a 2007-12-11 upload applies it to a single performer switching between the parts of a duet, credited as 声色 work [[nicovideo-ribbonmatch2007]]. On ニコニコ生放送 the article says the 両声類 are more often 声真似練習者 than singers [[nicodic-ryoseirui]].
- **What a 女声 is made of, in the scene's words.** The training vocabulary is the singing school's: 地声 against 裏声, 換声点 for the boundary [[brushvoice-joseigoe]], [[wikipedia-ja-seiku]], [[wikipedia-ja-uragoe]]. The register list Japanese Wikipedia itself uses is ボーカルフライ、表声、裏声(ファルセット)、ホイッスル [[wikipedia-ja-koe]]. A commercial singing school's guide states the method's core as the opposite of bel-canto practice — 「喉を広く開くというよりは、喉を狭くして発声する」 — to be done 「あくまでも地声のまま」, and points learners at two body signs, the folds stretched and 喉仏 risen and hidden [[brushvoice-joseigoe]].
- **The school version.** A 声優 vocational school teaches 両声類 as a career skill and names three methods for men, all defined against falsetto — メラニー法, ささやき法 (whisper method) and 濁り抽出法 (muddy-extraction method) — with メラニー法 described there as 「地声のまま高い声を出す技法」 done 「のどを細めるようにして、のど仏を使わずに声を出します」 [[anime-academy-ryoseirui2021]]. The same page supplies the resonance set チェストボイス・ミドルボイス・ヘッドボイス・ウィスパーボイス, placing ミドルボイス as 「地声と裏声の中間の声」 [[anime-academy-ryoseirui2021]], which is where the singing schools put ミックスボイス [[brushvoice-joseigoe]]. It also states the only practice-safety rule found across the community guides alongside the commercial school's own warning — 「「負荷がかかっている」と感じた場合は迷わずに休む」 [[anime-academy-ryoseirui2021]], [[brushvoice-joseigoe]].
- **The two vocabularies collide.** The same page gives 「のどを開く」 as one of three fundamentals, defined as 「あくびの状態を作りましょう」 [[anime-academy-ryoseirui2021]], while the trans-facing guide's central instruction is 「喉を狭くして発声する」 [[brushvoice-joseigoe]]; neither source reconciles them, and no Japanese study located here measures laryngeal posture during 女声 production.
- **The word メラニー法.** Japanese Wikipedia names the American self-teaching guide by Melanie Anne Phillips as *the* named method, and says 「この方法においては高い声を出すことを目的とせず、女声らしい響きをつけることで、相手に女性として認識させようというものである」 [[wikipedia-melanie-ho]]. The commercial guide treats メラニー法 as a generic label for 「男性が女声を出す発声方法」, which is looser than the encyclopedia's attribution [[brushvoice-joseigoe]]. The same school states its method's three uses in one sentence — アフレコ, 歌のパフォーマンス and トランスジェンダーの方の日常 [[brushvoice-joseigoe]].
- **Channels and videos.** On YouTube the trans-trainer lane and the entertainment lane are distinct. A trans woman voice teacher bills herself as 現役ボイトレ講師・両声類 and teaches 抑揚 (intonation), long-duration maintenance and upper-range work under the 【女声】 label [[satsukipon]]. A 2025 女声講座 with 112,121 views sells a character target — お姉さん声 — under the 両声類 tag [[fujimine2025]], and the most-watched lesson found in this pass, at 5,495,516 views and published 2022-06-17, is marketed as the shortest one, from a channel whose description advertises 女声釣り配信 [[jura2022]]. The reverse direction is taught the same way: a woman's 「私流！男声の出し方講座」, published under the disclosure convention 「彼は女性です」 [[noroda2020]].
- **Tools.** A Japanese web app for 女声 practice offers model voices, immediate self-recording and everyday example sentences, and was built by a person not out in daily life who designed it so that its icon, colours and copy carry no LGBT marking and it shows no ads, to avoid outing its user [[note-kirkas]]. Its author describes practice as 「孤独で苦しい」 [[note-kirkas]]. Against the measurement tools, a practitioner who has built formant software argues that reliable formant measurement is hard, that browser audio pipelines 「通話向けに最適化」 damage F3–F4, that most apps decide from 基礎ピッチ plus F1–F2 when 「人間の耳は F1〜F4のバランスで母音を聞き分けている」, and that F0 feedback is the usable part for practice while a formant-based gender verdict is not [[note-formant-app]].
- **What Japanese practice does with the research.** The Japanese study that sets a training target found 「70%以上女性に聴こえる発話の声の基本周波数（F0）は母音で平均270Hz，朗読で217Hz」 against cis women's means of 243 Hz and 217 Hz, and then that 「生物学的女性と同じF0値の範囲にあっても，女性と判定されない声が7割近くあり」 [[sakuraba2009]]. That is the same pitch ceiling the English-language literature reports [[gelfer2013]], [[leung2018]]; the other Japanese data measure different endpoints — naturalness of 両声類 voices, highest at 200–240 Hz in one contest [[hasegawa2017]], and attractiveness — not perceived-as-female judgement — peaking at 280–290 Hz [[tanaka2022]]. The community's own material works by feel and imitation rather than by measurement [[brushvoice-joseigoe]], [[note-kirkas]].
- **Where trans people actually get voice surgery in Japan.** Two ENT clinics publish the operations in patient-facing terms: 甲状軟骨形成術Ⅳ型, attributed in one to 一色信彦 in the 1970s, and ウェンドラー声帯短縮術, attributed to Wendler in 1990 [[kyoto-ent-clinic]], [[met-ent-tokyo]]. The Kyoto clinic reports 87 type-IV cases (2018.1–2022.4) moving from a pre-operative mean of 162 Hz to 240 Hz and notes F0 drifting back down for six months, and a 14-case 声帯短縮術 series in which F0 instead drifts up as post-operative swelling resolves; it also publishes totals of 1,056 喉頭形成術 (2011-10 to 2023-12) and 404 声帯短縮術 (2023 to 2024-10) [[kyoto-ent-clinic]]. Its post-operative ボイストレーニング is self-funded at 30 minutes for ¥6,600, and a fallback route of repeated 声帯注射 is offered where surgery is not possible [[kyoto-ent-clinic]]. The Tokyo clinic offers the lower-pitch direction too, 甲状軟骨形成術Ⅲ型, for 変声障害 and for people who want a lower voice [[met-ent-tokyo]], [[nakamura2021]].
- **What the guideline says, and does not.** The Japanese national guideline's treatment chapter is 「身体的治療（ホルモン療法（二次性徴抑制療法を含む）、乳房切除術、性別適合手術）」 — there is no voice section in the 目次 of the 第4版改 (2018) or the 第5版 (2024), and voice is not among the 第5版's listed 改訂点 [[jspn-guideline2018]], [[jspn-guideline2024]]. Voice in Japan is thus organised outside guideline care: ENT and laryngology venues for the surgery [[matsumoto2022]], [[nakamura2022]], [[nakamura2021]], a speech-language-hearing society for the perception research [[sakuraba2009]], [[jslp]], and commercial schools and YouTube for the training [[brushvoice-joseigoe]], [[satsukipon]].
- **Money and legal order.** Japan's psychiatric society states the split plainly: 「2018年から性別適合手術への保険適用が認められる一方で、ホルモン療法にはいまだに保険が適応されていません」 [[jspn-guideline2024]], and the patient association repeats that psychotherapy and surgery are covered while hormone therapy is not [[gidjp-opinion2023]]. In practice the surgery route is described as unusable through insurance, because the prohibition on 混合診療 makes a course that includes self-paid hormones entirely self-funded [[gidjp-opinion2023]], and because 声の女性化手術 is reported to require the 戸籍 to have been changed to female first — which itself requires the surgery the law demands [[note-maru2blog]], [[wikipedia-ja-tokureiho]]. The 2023-10-25 Supreme Court decision struck down the 生殖腺 requirement unanimously and sent the 外観 requirement back to the high court, without settling either insurance question [[gidjp-opinion2023]], [[wikipedia-ja-tokureiho]]. The association's own figures put 戸籍 changes at 11,919 over 2004–2022 [[gidjp-opinion2023]].
- **Where the scene's material comes from.** Not from the guidelines: the 2022 喉頭 panel that treated voice for gender incongruence is a conference session, not guidance [[matsumoto2022]], [[nakamura2022]], and the paper that establishes a Japanese F0 target was published in 音声言語医学 in 2009 [[sakuraba2009]].
- **Two views on what to attend to.** A note.com essay whose own text identifies it as a Gemini Notebook argues that anatomy-led 女声 instruction backfires, because the intrinsic laryngeal muscles are near-impossible to move deliberately and the brain responds by over-driving the extrinsic muscles around them [[note-shinkyo]]. At the other end, a training log names five configurations by register (地声/裏声), weight-like quality and pitch, and its sequel publishes F0 with F1–F4 for two 地声 configurations and F0 with F1–F3 for two 裏声 ones [[note-akagikanon202607]] [[note-akagikanon202609]].
- **Japanese machine scoring has a longer history than the current apps.** A 2007 University of Tokyo master's thesis built an automatic estimator of 知覚的女声度 — how female an MtF speaker's voice sounds to listeners — reaching r = 0.86 against listener ratings where listeners agreed with each other at r = 0.88, with an interface the author reports was used clinically [[maruyama2007]]. The same research group had been publishing listener-judged acoustic work on Japanese MtF voices since at least 2003 [[imaizumi2003]], [[sakuraba2009]].

## Contested or unclear
- **Whether pitch or 声質 is the target.** The community says 声質, and the listening data agree that the two dissociate [[nicodic-ryoseirui]], [[sakuraba2009]] — but the Japanese surgery the clinics sell is measured in Hz, and the guideline-adjacent papers argue their case in 話声位 and QOL terms [[kyoto-ent-clinic]], [[nakamura2022]]. No Japanese source found in this pass states how much F0 change is enough after surgery, and the drift in both directions is clinic-reported only [[kyoto-ent-clinic]].
- **「喉を狭くする」 against resonance widening.** The commercial guide's central instruction is to narrow the throat [[brushvoice-joseigoe]]; the English-language practice literature the vault holds treats resonance work as tract shaping and often as pharyngeal widening ([[voice-feminization-therapy]], [[carew2007]], [[kawitzky2020]]). No Japanese study located here measures laryngeal height or pharyngeal width during 女声 production, so the disagreement cannot be resolved from Japanese sources.
- **How much of the 両声類 method transfers to trans life.** The 両声類 material is stage and mimicry work with a listener as judge [[nicodic-ryoseirui]], [[nicovideo-ryoseirui-tag]]; the trans material is about being read correctly in daily interaction, including on the telephone and when coughing or sneezing [[kyoto-ent-clinic]], [[met-ent-tokyo]]. The same words carry both, and no source in this pass examines whether the training differs.
- **The surgery-versus-training sequence.** A Japanese community account reports that pre-operative training is sometimes withheld because some techniques risk fold injury, and that post-operative training aims at stability rather than at a feminine voice [[yomedanchii-voice]]; that account declares itself non-expert, and no Japanese clinical source in this pass states the same sequencing rule.

## Open questions
- Whether voice is absent from the national guideline because it is delegated to ENT and speech-language practice, or because it was never proposed; the guideline's history section records the 1997 origins, and voice appears in that history only as part of the record [[jspn-guideline2018]], [[jspn-guideline2024]], [[harashina2018]].
- What Japanese listeners actually use when F0 is inside the cis range and the voice is still not judged female [[sakuraba2009]]; the Japanese perceptual vocabulary exists in the research ([[kido2001]], [[yasuda2023]], [[kanato2016]]) but has not been applied to trans or 両声類 voices in the sources found here.
- Whether the 両声類 corpus's methods — 喉を狭くする, 地声のまま高い声, 換声点 control — have ever been measured acoustically; no such Japanese study was located.
- Whether the insurance reading in the community accounts is correct as a matter of billing rules; no official statement was found, and the two sources here are a patient association and a personal blog [[gidjp-opinion2023]], [[note-maru2blog]].
- What Japanese trans people use instead of a validated instrument: no Japanese TVQ/TWVQ translation was located, and the outcome measures in the Japanese clinical papers are 話声位 and QOL stated in the abstract [[nakamura2022]], [[sakuraba2009]].
- Whether the 2003–2007 Tokyo work was ever carried into practice in Japan; the 2003 abstract's full text is not online and the 2007 estimator's clinical use is reported only by its author [[imaizumi2003]], [[maruyama2007]].
