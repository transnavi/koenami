# Voice surgery: glottoplasty, cricothyroid approximation, thyroplasty

Procedures that raise pitch by shortening the vibrating folds (Wendler glottoplasty, vocal-fold shortening with anterior commissure retrodisplacement), tensing them (cricothyroid approximation, type IV thyroplasty) or reducing their mass (laser reduction). Outcomes are larger F0 gains than training, with trade-offs in roughness and loudness.

## What is known
- Shortening techniques give the largest F0 increases in meta-analysis [[song2017]]; weighted gains: VFSRAC +73.3 Hz, Wendler +54.1 Hz, CTA +30.3 Hz, LAVA +26 Hz, therapy +39.9 Hz; Wendler + therapy gives the best TWVQ improvement; adverse events (granuloma, fatigue) are commoner with surgical combinations [[chowdhury2025]].
- Glottoplasty versus CTA (n = 53): glottoplasty +27 Hz more, stable over time, but roughness rose and one severe dysphonia occurred; CTA gains decayed [[mora2018]]; CTA alone leaves voices rated between male and female groups [[vanborsel2008]]; after CTA the intraoperative pitch settles about 112 Hz lower by six months [[maeda2025]].
- Laser reduction glottoplasty: F0 132→198 Hz, VHI and TVQ improved, but jitter, shimmer and NHR increased and 32% wanted revision [[yilmaz2021]]; simulation shows anterior fixation raises F0 while loudness falls beyond ¼ fixation [[titze2020]].
- Wendler glottoplasty F0 gains range 20–112 Hz; postoperative therapy and voice rest are stressed [[catani2024]]; Beijing series: F0 152.7→207.7 Hz, TVQMtF 89.9→50.4 [[zhang2023cn]]; Nanjing series (n = 296): +57.4 Hz, 94.3% satisfied [[sun2025]]; a Colombian series of 36 with post-operative rehabilitation reports a mean +112 Hz at six months and a fall of about 30 points on the TSEQ [[ballestas2018]].
- A Recife series of seven trans women after Wendler glottoplasty gained a mean 47.9 ± 46.6 Hz on sustained /e/ and 24.6 ± 27.5 Hz in speaking F0, with maximum phonation time, F1–F2, frequency range, jitter, shimmer and every GRBAS parameter unchanged, while the TWVQ fell from 98.3 to 54.1 and self-rated femininity of the voice rose from 2.8 to 7.7 [[aires2023a]].
- Japan: type IV thyroplasty has been the dominant procedure, tuned intraoperatively under local anaesthesia; SFF 133.8→237.8 Hz in 32 patients [[nakamura2007]], and F0 rose in all 31 patients of a 1999–2006 series, from a mean 135 Hz to 236 Hz [[sanuki2007]]; paired with therapy [[kobayashi2022]]; anterior glottoplasty now compared with it, both exceeding 200 Hz [[hiroshiba2024]].
- A 2025 continuing-education feature for ENT surgeons is titled 「甲状軟骨形成術Ⅳ型と声帯短縮術」 under the heading 《トランスジェンダーに対する音声女性化手術》, naming those two operations; its body sits behind a login [[hiroshiba2025]]. A two-page 研修ノート item in 耳鼻咽喉科臨床 is titled 性同一性障害に対する甲状軟骨形成術4型, and its PDF was not fetched [[nakamura2020]].
- Type III thyroplasty, which shortens the anteroposterior diameter of the thyroid cartilage and so relaxes the folds, works against a speaking F0 that is too high: in 19 Japanese cases operated between 2001 and 2006, bilateral operations took the mean from 196.0 Hz to 127.8 Hz and unilateral from 198.4 Hz to 149.3 Hz, with the AC/DC ratio rising from 36.7 % to 52.1 % [[nakamura2008]]; an FtM patient who declined both hormones and genital surgery had her pitch lowered by type III alone [[kojima2008]].
- Type IV thyroplasty raised speaking F0 from 186.1 to 323.7 Hz and from 178.3 to 251.1 Hz in two cis women whose pitch had fallen with a female-hormone abnormality, the pitch tuned intra-operatively to what each patient was satisfied with [[nakamura2009a]]; in eight non-trans patients with a high-pitch disorder (four after thyroid surgery, one traumatic, three idiopathic) it raised speaking F0, highest pitch and range and improved mean VHI, while the lowest pitch was not significantly raised (p = 0.363) [[nishimura2025]].
- Combining thyroarytenoid resection with type IV: in one patient with spasmodic dysphonia and a gender-related low-pitch complaint, the resection cleared the 「声のつまり」 and raised SFF from 133.3 Hz to 200.1 Hz, and adding type IV took it to 244.8 Hz, while maximum phonation time fell from 21 to 12 to 8 s and the top of the range narrowed from 418.2 Hz to 322.4 Hz [[nakamura2009]].
- Type IV thyroplasty's reversibility and late complications: a patient operated on elsewhere under general anaesthesia developed bilateral fold abduction impairment with airway narrowing, and cutting the approximating nylon suture did not release it, so a tracheal fenestration was performed [[kurita2011]]; 13 years after another operation the GORE-TEX bolus and traction suture had become an infected granuloma, and removing both left the raised speaking pitch unchanged [[mizawa2022]]; and in a patient asking for the operation to be undone the cricoid and thyroid had fused, so removing the traction suture left speaking F0 at 206 Hz before, 201 Hz during and 202 Hz after [[nishiyama2023]].
- Tensioning glottoplasty, which combines anterior glottic shortening with anteriorly directed tensioning, raised mean F0 from 126.2 Hz to 241.2 Hz in 20 trans women measured one month after surgery [[birkent2026]].
- Wendler glottoplasty held at a mean 18.63 months: F0 gains persisted and were comparable to cisgender female controls, while listeners still attributed the wrong gender significantly more often than for those controls, and, apart from a lasting improvement in the emotional domain of the VHI, voice-related quality of life scores fell below their short-term values [[rogalska2025]].
- A Japanese patient reports about a month of near-total voice rest after Wendler glottoplasty at a Kyoto clinic, and a 230 Hz average two years later that still reads as male when she uses a masculine tone of voice [[note-eri-kyoto]].
- Masculinizing surgery is uncommon and thinly evidenced: the literature on type III thyroplasty for vocal masculinisation is four studies covering 23 patients, with techniques and outcome measures varying substantially [[kalra2026]] [[rendon2026]].
- A Seoul clinic's Korean-language video markets VFSRAC under the tag 음성여성화 alongside vocal-fold botulinum toxin and voice examination, in a series asking viewers to 「트렌스젠더의 목소리를 찾아보세요!」 [[yeson2021]].
- Wendler glottoplasty in ten Italian trans women aged 25–40 raised mean F0 from 151.4 ± 10.8 Hz to 231.6 ± 13.5 Hz, with the Italian trans-specific and generic questionnaires both falling (I-TVQMtF 71.6 → 24.9, I-VHI 59.2 → 19.8), a perceptual femininity visual analog scale improving, laryngostroboscopy confirming 90% glottic closure and 80% symmetrical vocal-fold vibration, and transient dysphonia in one patient resolving with conservative management [[scarpa2025]].
- Wendler glottoplasty in 31 patients raised mean F0 from 135.8 to 206.3 Hz overall, and to 213.8 Hz in the under-40 group, while estimated subglottic pressure rose significantly and the dysphonia grade worsened in the group aged 40 and over; three patients needed a revision procedure, two of them after a suture-line breakdown because the postoperative voice-rest recommendations had not been followed [[mastronikolis2013]].
- The Würzburg modification of Wendler's glottoplasty, in 21 patients, elevated the fundamental frequency into the typical female range with the lower frequency limit rising without any reduction of the frequency range, and left the Dysphonia Severity Index unchanged; about a third of the patients felt affected by the restricted dynamic range and elevated Voice Handicap Index scores indicated that in everyday life they continued to feel handicapped by their voice, while subjective satisfaction correlated strongly with each individual's pitch gain; the authors' conclusion is that a better multidisciplinary therapy concept for voice feminization is necessary [[meister2017a]].
- The German cricothyroidopexy line: the Halle Cricothyroidopexie mit Miniplatten, derived from Isshiki's type IV thyroplasty, took 67 trans women from no female speaking pitch preoperatively to a mean speaking pitch 5–6 semitones higher, with 28% in the female range and 38% reaching at least an indeterminate one, and one-year and long-term observation showing the results durable [[neumann2005]]; the peer-reviewed report of the same technique in 59 patients operated between October 1993 and December 2000 gives about 30% in the female range and 32% in an indeterminate one, records range and dynamic range still restricted at about half a year, and finds no significant pre/post difference in the formants, including the F3 treated as a key frequency for gender recognition [[welzel2002]].
- The Würzburg IZSS centre page lists «Transgender-Stimmerhöhungen» as one entry among the conditions it treats, inside an interdisciplinary voice-and-swallowing centre staffed by ENT/phoniatrics with speech therapists and psychologists, appointments only by arrangement and no price published [[ukw-izss]]; the phoniatric assessment preceding such an operation was already being called for in 1999, when the authors noted that the standards of care for transsexuals covered neither a diagnostic approach nor surgical therapy for voice and asked medical scientific societies to offer phonosurgery as an optional treatment [[rosanowski1999]].
- German routine care places the surgical involvement and the training in one treatment list of six disciplines for trans patients, «die HNO-Mitbetreuung mit Phonochirurgie» being a separate entry from «die logopädische Behandlung inkl. Stimmtherapie» [[athanasoulia2020]].
- Guidance on indications sits in [[coleman2022]] and [[davies2015]]; Japanese providers are listed on transnavi.jp/voice/.

## Contested or unclear
- Whether the extra F0 from glottoplasty is worth the roughness risk is a patient decision; the trials are retrospective (level 4) [[mora2018]].
