# Mandarin-speaking transgender voice

Chinese-language research and resources on trans voice.

## What is known
- Case–control acoustics of 15 Mandarin-speaking trans women: vowel-specific formant shifts and lower f0 than cis women, with fatigue risk [[huang2024]].
- TWVQ-SC validated (α 0.97, ICC 0.84) [[wang2022]]; narrative review from West China Hospital [[chen2024cn]].
- Surgery series: Wendler glottoplasty in Beijing (F0 152.7→207.7 Hz, TVQMtF 89.9→50.4) [[zhang2023cn]]; vocal-fold shortening with anterior commissure retrodisplacement in Nanjing (n = 296, +57.4 Hz, 94.3% satisfied) [[sun2025]].
- Community guides: [[digital-transchinese-mtf-voice]], MtF.wiki and the あおぎ葵 handbook (cited on Koenami's method page).
- Taiwan: a speech-language-pathology society review of trans-voice therapy and assessment tools finds domestic literature sparse (國內文獻仍然稀少) [[lin2024]]; a 12-session intensive telepractice pilot of resonant voice therapy with vocal function exercises (n = 9) improved F0, TWVQ scores and self-rated femininity [[lin2026]]; most of 140 Taiwanese SLPs surveyed accepted trans voice work as in scope but felt insufficiently skilled [[litosseliti2019]].
- Mainland voice medicine files the persistently high-pitched male voice under 男声女调 (mutational falsetto): a Guangzhou therapy series (n = 26) [[liang2017]], a Shenyang relaxation-laryngoplasty series (n = 30, F0 276.5 → 127.5 Hz) [[wang2009]] and a nursing-journal discussion of the training workflow [[yao2025]], a vocabulary adjacent to, and disjoint from, trans feminization.
- Vocal-function assessment in China follows the 2024 Chinese Medical Association expert consensus (subjective and objective quality, quality-of-life instruments, laryngoscopy, aerodynamics, electrophysiology) [[xu2025]].
- Community infrastructure: MtF.wiki's price-listed provider directory, with an explicitly unlicensed tier [[mtfwiki-vocal-confirmed]], its surgery overview naming 声带截短术 as the mainland default [[mtfwiki-vocal-surgery]], its 通俗用语表 slang glossary [[mtfwiki-slang]], and a protocol page for the most-referenced trainer, whose F0-first method re-contours Mandarin tones 3 and 4 to hold F0 up [[mtfwiki-chenzhen]]; see [[chinese-community]].
- Chinese Wikipedia has a 跨性别语音疗法 article whose Japanese interlanguage is メラニー法 [[wikipedia-zh-kuaxingbie-yuyin-liaofa]]; eleven wikipedia-zh-* notes record the Chinese terms for larynx, folds, falsetto, formant, F0 and voice change.
