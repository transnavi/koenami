# Non-binary and androgynous voice

Voices that are meant to be, or are heard as, neither clearly masculine nor feminine. Research is recent and thin; the consistent finding is "in between" perception.

## What is known
- Gender-diverse voices are frequently perceived or acoustically represented as falling between binary categories [[rosi2025]]; non-binary and trans speakers were rated toward the mid-point [[taylor2026]].
- A three-way task (man/woman/androgynous) found F0 alone changed the odds of "androgynous" versus "man" [[hardy2020]].
- Gender-expansive listeners categorised sibilants differently from cisgender listeners, most for a non-binary synthetic voice [[hope2023]].
- A large survey of TGD adults (40% non-binary) found hormone therapy and counselling associated with better voice-related outcomes [[shen2026]].
- Scoring femininity and masculinity as separate scales, rather than one axis, is how Koenami and the 2025 review frame this [[rosi2025]].

## Contested or unclear
- Whether "androgynous" is a stable perceptual category or the absence of a decision.

## Open questions
- Training goals for an androgynous target are undescribed in the clinical literature in this vault.
