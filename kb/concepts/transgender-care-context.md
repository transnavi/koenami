# Transgender care context — where voice work sits

Voice and communication services are one component of gender-affirming care: defined internationally by clinical guidelines, commissioned differently in each health system, and reached by far fewer people than want them.

## What is known
- WPATH's Standards of Care version 8 is the international protocol; its voice and communication chapter covers assessment, training by qualified clinicians and voice surgery [[coleman2022]].
- The Endocrine Society's 2017 guideline places hormone treatment inside multidisciplinary assessment — diagnosing clinicians required, mental health provision required for adolescents and recommended for adults — and recommends no hormone treatment before puberty [[hembree2017]].
- In England, NHS England service specification 1719 requires "expertise in gender-specific voice and communication development" in the specialist multidisciplinary team and gives voice and communication its own lane in the non-surgical pathway: SLT assessment, then GIC-specialised 1:1 or group intervention or referral to a local speech and language therapy service [[nhse1719]].
- Voice is where dysphoria concentrates and where access fails most: median voice dysphoria 7/10 against 4/10 for jawline/chin, 23% of transgender and nonbinary survey participants had ever sought care with a speech-language pathologist, and want-versus-had gaps ran 52%/23% (feminization-seeking) and 35%/8% (masculinization-seeking) for voice therapy [[ziltzer2023]].
- The highest-grade synthesis of therapy outcomes is a PRISMA systematic review of 14 studies, positive for pitch, resonance, self- and listener perception but limited by small samples, short follow-up and inconsistent measures [[leyns2021]].
- No Cochrane review covers voice or communication interventions for transgender people: a PubMed search restricted to the Cochrane Database returned zero hits on 2026-09-17, leaving [[leyns2021]] as the top of the evidence pile.
- Controlling for age and volume of voice use at work, transgender participants scored 12.4 points higher than cisgender participants on the 32-point WorkHoarse voice-related work-productivity impairment measure [[chang2026]].
- Of 187 TGD survey respondents, the 68% reporting vocal-gender incongruence scored lower on quality of life (70 vs 76) and were far likelier to have been misgendered because of their voice in the past year (85% vs 33%) [[stryker2025]].
- Voice ranked second only to upper body among the features contributing to gender dysphoria, and that incongruence is attributed mainly to how others perceive the speaker rather than to self-perception [[pu2025]].
- At a travesti and transsexual association in Alagoas, most of the 11 respondents who answered the questionnaire adequately reported discomfort with a voice that 「não corresponde à identidade de gênero」, and 「nenhum participante realizou procedimento médico ou terapia fonoaudiológica para adequação vocal」 [[sosthenes2025]].
- Trans women in São Paulo reported using hormones without supervision 「due to the urgency of aligning with their gender identity」, seeking passability to avoid harassment, and valuing vocal therapy for working on vocal potentialities, self-perception and self-acceptance, with peer support and positive trans references named as sources of confidence [[sebastiao2022]].
- Japanese gender care is organised around ten 認定施設 accredited by the 日本GI（性別不合）学会, alongside directories of 認定医 and of 医療系 and 教育系 コーディネーター; neither the facility list nor the society's 理事長声明 concern voice [[gi-soc-ninteishisetsu]].
- A Belgian survey co-designed with the population it studied documents need and access together: 48.2% of 197 TGD respondents were dissatisfied with their voices, 57.4% had engaged in voice and communication training with self-reported positive outcomes concentrated among (trans)feminine respondents, and non-binary participants, especially those presumed female at birth, faced more barriers to accessing it [[papeleu2025b]].
- A German university service presents voice feminisation as one entry among the conditions of an interdisciplinary voice-and-swallowing centre, reachable only by arrangement and with no published price [[ukw-izss]], while the German specialist literature puts the non-surgical route — functional voice therapy and hormone treatment — first and phonosurgery in a specialised centre as the addition [[pieper2025]].
- In a Russian cohort of 1117 transgender patients, 53.6% were already taking hormones and only 8.6% of them had obtained the prescription from an endocrinologist, 76.7% of trans women against 32.3% of trans men [[makarova2021ru]].
- Among 926 Russian patients presenting with gender-related complaints in 2014–2021, 75.2% had no mental illness other than the gender-related one and the suicide rate was 12.5%, with outward correspondence to the associated gender named among the modifiable risk factors [[kremenitskaya2022ru]].
- Turkish insurance coverage for gender-affirming procedures runs through genetic consultation, and of 217 applicants karyotyped for it 98.2% were congruent with the sex assigned at birth [[bagcaz2019]].
- In a Vietnamese four-province study of 141 transgender people every participant had used hormones and more than 73% bought them from someone already using them on word-of-mouth advice, and of the 32 who had had surgery half received no post-operative care [[pham2020vn]].

## Contested or unclear
- Where voice services belong: inside the gender clinic (the NHS GIC-specialised lane) or in general speech-language pathology services (the NHS referral-on lane) — both models run in parallel [[nhse1719]]; a Brazilian survey of 52 SLPs describes session content without recording the setting [[viana2025]].
- How people should enter the pathway: the NHS specification protects self-referral alongside GP referral [[nhse1719]], yet in survey data most transgender and nonbinary adults had never seen a speech-language pathologist at all [[ziltzer2023]] — referral routes and self-referral rights are not, by themselves, producing access.

## Open questions
- What a course of therapy costs a service and how many people drop out: session counts come from single-site series ([[adessa2023]], [[lin2026]]) and completion from only one of them ([[lin2026]]).
- How waiting-time pressure in commissioned systems (England's GIC model) interacts with the voice lane's capacity [[nhse1719]].
