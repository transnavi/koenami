# Thai voice and gender

What is known about gendered voice in Thai: an early domestic phonosurgical series, a medical-tourism clinic landscape offering pitch surgery in both directions, and the Thai terminology learners meet.

## What is known
- A Ramathibodi Hospital (Mahidol University) series of 6 trans women pioneered thyroid-cartilage and vocal-fold reduction: composite resection shortening and tensing both folds, mean pitch 147→315 Hz, with patients pleased by voice softness and the simultaneous Adam's-apple reduction; 6-year follow-up [[kunachak2000]].
- Bangkok hospitals market voice surgery to international patients: Yanhee's voice-change page prices MTF and FTM surgery at 121,000 THB with stroboscopy, one week of absolute voice rest and a generic risk list, inside a Pride Center offering the full transition pathway [[yanhee-voice-surgery]]; its ENT specialist's own site details an open 1.5–2 cm (MTF) / 2–3 cm (FTM) technique in 2–3 hours with speech therapy at the 10-day suture removal, in English, Chinese and Korean [[dr-ornouma]].
- Kamol Hospital offers VFS and VMS pages in twelve languages; VFS is described as pitch-and-resonance surgery with a menu of CTA, glottal-web formation and fold shortening [[kamol-hospital]].
- The masculinization route is framed as the fallback for an insufficient testosterone response, with an explicit masculine-voice target of 100–125 Hz and preoperative stroboscopy and acoustic study [[kamol-hospital]].
- A Thai-affiliated laryngologist (Khon Kaen University) co-authored the UCSF cohort giving GAVT dosage: mean 5.7 sessions in the first year, 62% satisfied — data from US patients, included as the closest Thai-author voice-therapy evidence found [[laohakittikul2025]].
- Terminology anchors in Thai Wikipedia: เสียงพูด (voice) [[wikipedia-th-siangphut]], กล่องเสียง (larynx) [[wikipedia-th-klotsiang]], เส้นเสียง/สายเสียง (vocal cords) [[wikipedia-th-sensiang]], ความสั่นพ้องเด่น (formant — a native descriptive coinage, not a loanword) [[wikipedia-th-khwamsanphongden]], คนข้ามเพศ (transgender) [[wikipedia-th-khonkhamplet]] and หญิงข้ามเพศ (trans woman, with the community terms ที-เกิร์ล/T-girl) [[wikipedia-th-yingkhamplet]].

## Contested or unclear
- Clinic pages name technique families but not which method they perform, and publish no acoustic series — outcomes appear as before/after media [[yanhee-voice-surgery]] [[dr-ornouma]] [[kamol-hospital]].
- The 147→315 Hz of the 2000 series is surgeon-reported on 6 patients with pre-2001 methods; it is not comparable with modern glottoplasty outcomes [[kunachak2000]].

## Open questions
- No Thai-language or Thailand-cohort study of voice feminization outcomes or of gender-affirming voice therapy was found in the reachable literature; what exists is one 2000 series plus clinic marketing.
- Thai is a tone language with five lexical tones; how raising average F0 interacts with tonal targets appears in none of the sources here.
- No Thai validation of a trans-voice questionnaire and no Thai listener study of gender perception were found.
