# Spanish-speaking transgender voice

Spanish-language research, communities and terms on gendered voice, across Spain and Latin America.

## What is known
- Patient-reported outcomes: the authorised Spanish TVQ MtF (30 items, α = 0.976, ICC = 0.885, Madrid) [[mora2018a]]; a 10-item short form derived from 51 surgical patients (α = .79, correlation with the long form > .90) [[casadomorente2021]].
- Surgery and therapy: Wendler glottoplasty with post-operative voice therapy beat surgery alone in a Marbella series (n = 18) [[casado2017]]; VFSRAC+LAVA raised F0 more than plain glottoplasty (+69.7 vs +47.8 Hz), with postoperative speech therapy recommended either way [[casadomorente2024]].
- Adolescents: Spanish trans girls blocked early in puberty had F0 71.5 Hz higher and lower TWVQ/VHI-30 scores than a late-block group, with the sources' own hedging ("seem", "could") [[dominguezriscart2025]].
- Perception: Chilean self-perception studies [[orellana2021]]; a cross-sectional study of the voice's role in gender affirmation [[ramirez2022]]; a Colombian psychological/fonoaudiología overview [[munoz2025]]; an integrative therapy-versus-thyroplasty review [[malebran2021]].
- Reviews written for readers where the field is little known: a Costa Rican narrative review naming F0, formants and quality of life, and the research gap in prosody, articulation and non-verbal communication [[viales2025]]; an Argentine fonoaudiología reflection on "voz migrante" (bodily transition does not always imply vocal transition) [[mendez2025]].
- Legal context: Spain's Ley 4/2023 sets self-determination from 16 and general LGBTI health protection but never names voice therapy, leaving provision to regional and private services [[ley4-2023]]; Argentina's Ley 26.743 frames identity rights the same way [[ley26743]].
- Communities: FEMIVOZ (Mariela Astudillo), a Spain-based vocóloga offering online feminization, masculinization and neutralization coaching in Spanish for all countries, with French exercise videos too [[femivoz]]; peer tutorials such as Julieta Longi's exercise-sharing video [[longi2021]]; a Barcelona radio interview documenting the private FemVeu clinical unit [[tornero2019]].
- Encyclopedic coverage is surgery-only: es Wikipedia has Laringoplastia de feminización but no article on transgender voice therapy (searched 2026-09-17) [[wikipedia-es-laringoplastia-feminizacion]].
- Vocabulary: resonancia, formantes, frecuencia fundamental, feminización de la voz, masculinización de la voz, terapia del lenguaje (fonoaudiología o logopedia); community phrasing "feminizar la voz" [[longi2021]] [[viales2025]].

## Open questions
- No Spanish-language randomised trial of behavioural voice therapy was found in this pass; the therapy evidence is surgical-adjacent [[casado2017]] or narrative [[viales2025]] [[malebran2021]].
- No published Spanish-language listener study of which acoustic changes move gender perception in Spanish.
