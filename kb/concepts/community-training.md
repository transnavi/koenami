# Community training resources

The hub over every non-clinical community resource in the vault: who teaches gendered voice, in which language, on which platform. The narratives live in the language hubs — [[english-community]], [[chinese-community]], [[japanese-community]], [[korean-voice]], [[spanish-voice]], [[european-languages-voice]]; this page indexes the individual resources and what each one claims about itself.

## What is known

**English-language coaching businesses and creators.**
- TransVoiceLessons: paid private and group teaching plus free video material; the origin of the size/weight vocabulary [[transvoicelessons]] [[english-community]].
- SumianVoice: coaching practice with a free weekly-lesson Discord and the open Voice Resource Project wiki [[sumianvoice]].
- Renée Yoxon: de-medicalized one-to-one and course teaching, Big Dog Little Dog, weight exercises [[renee-yoxon]] [[yoxon2023]] [[yoxon2025]].
- Seattle Voice Lab: remote coaching business marketing feminization, masculinization and androgyny [[seattle-voice-lab]].

**Forum and instant-feedback spaces.**
- r/transvoice: recordings posted for community criticism since 2012, with a wiki and Discord [[r-transvoice]] [[transvoice-wiki]].
- The TransVoice Discord server: public invite advertising "free voice and text chat" and "instant voice feedback" [[transvoice-discord]].

**Apps and measurement tools for self-training.**
- TransVoice.App: guided course plus real-time pitch, spectrogram and loudness tools, with an explicit "not an assessment of your voice or gender" disclaimer [[transvoice-app]].
- Genderfluent: real-time pitch, formants and a "gender" estimate on a freemium plan [[genderfluent]].
- The wider measurement stack (Praat, Christella VoiceUp, Informant and others) is indexed under [[tools]].

**Chinese-language resources.**
- The 2024 self-study MtF voice-training guide mirrored on the Trans Chinese Digital Archive, with a stated 185–220+ Hz pitch-phase benchmark [[digital-transchinese-mtf-voice]].
- A New Taipei hospital team's (亞東紀念醫院) public teaching and surgery pages, community-grade provider material with 180 Hz-up / 140 Hz-down targets [[voicedoctor-tw]].
- The wider 伪声 and MtF.wiki layer is mapped in [[chinese-community]].

**Japanese-language lessons.**
- A YouTube 女声 lesson with one of the larger audiences in the 2026 sweep (about 115,000 views) tells learners in its title to copy the demonstration and set theory aside, 「真似するだけ」 [[kuzutetsu2025]]; the wider 両声類 and 女声講座 layer is mapped in [[japanese-community]].

**Korean-language lessons.**
- The most-watched Korean female-voice tutorial found in the 2026 sweep is an all-in-one course whose opening video is an orientation rather than a lesson: instructor background, goal setting, a stated difference from other teaching and the curriculum [[cocochang2023]].

**Vietnamese, Indonesian and Thai channels.**
- A Vietnamese YouTube series on converting the voice from male to female builds from «âm vực cơ bản» (basic vocal range) upward rather than from pitch targets, and mixes voice instruction with hormone and transition topics on one channel [[yt-senem]].
- An Indonesian peer tutorial presents a male-to-female routine as «cara latihan aku» — how I practise — with practice frequency as its instruction and a pointer to the presenter's Instagram for questions [[yt-niningnnur-2022]].
- Thai tutorials in the «ทำเสียงผู้หญิง (trap)» genre teach a female character voice to male speakers; the one channel documented invites viewers into a gaming Discord and publishes no vocal-health guidance on the pages read [[yt-hananeji]].

**Editorial and practice rules the English-language wiki states.**
- An exercise is included if it works most of the time and has a fairly long track record, then cleared through a risk gate: where a risk exists and a beginner-usable mitigation does not, the method is scrapped, and otherwise the mitigation is printed with the exercise [[sumi-pedagogy]].
- The daily budget given is 5–10 minutes of exercises and no more than a few hours of a trained voice, with strain or added effort as the hard limit at any time and for any reason [[sumi-how-to-practice]].
- For practice that cannot be done aloud, humming substitutes for a pitch slide and whispering for resonance and speech-pattern work, the latter for only a few minutes a day and only while quiet and light [[sumi-covert-practice]].
- The feminisation curriculum the wiki recommends runs in order — lower vocal weight and raise pitch by pitch naturalisation, raise resonance and brighten as needed, clean the voice up, practise speech patterns, get more pitch range, then start mimicry and microbehaviours — and states its position on pitch in the first paragraph: 「Pitch is not that important! Well, it's actually really important but not in the way you might think. It's very useful for training, intonation and a few other things, but it's not a gender factor in and of itself.」 [[sumi-getting-started]].
- Mimicry is the wiki's stated route to microbehaviours and speech patterns and, explicitly, not to vocal weight, pitch range, resonance or clarity [[sumi-mimicry]].

**What all of these have in common.**
- Each note records the resource's own claims about itself; none of these curricula has published outcome data, and the only community method adjacent to measurement is the single-teacher DSFD dataset [[netzorg2025]].
- Where the vocabulary overlaps research constructs (weight vs closed quotient, size vs formants), the mapping and its limits are worked through in [[vocal-weight]] and [[formants]].

## Contested or unclear
- Commercial claims of speed ("6–8 lessons" [[seattle-voice-lab]]) sit against clinical schedules of 8–12 sessions [[sodersten2026]] and 12-session intensives [[lin2026]]; no independent verification exists for any community curriculum.
- Volunteer infrastructure is fragile: the Sumi wiki banners itself "under construction" with missing pages [[sumianvoice]], and the r/transvoice wiki's resource lists were already unreachable in 2026 [[transvoice-wiki]].
- Safety teaching varies in form: one YouTube tutorial retracted a swallow-based larynx cue on safety grounds [[lucy2023]], while 萌娘百科 rates each method on a throat-harm scale and caps daily practice [[moegirl-weisheng]].

## Open questions
- Which community drills survive measurement — no study tests pitch slides, whisper sirens or Big Dog Little Dog against acoustic or listener outcomes [[sumi2021]] [[sutherland2025]] [[yoxon2023]].
- How the app-delivered "gender" scores relate to listener perception; TransVoice.App disclaims assessment [[transvoice-app]] while Genderfluent shows a "gender" estimate [[genderfluent]]; see [[machine-gender-scoring]].
