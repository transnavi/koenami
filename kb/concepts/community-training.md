# Community training resources

The hub over every non-clinical community resource in the vault: who teaches gendered voice, in which language, on which platform. The narratives live in the language hubs — [[english-community]], [[chinese-community]], [[japanese-community]], [[korean-voice]], [[spanish-voice]], [[european-languages-voice]]; this page indexes the individual resources and what each one claims about itself.

## What is known

**English-language coaching businesses and creators.**
- TransVoiceLessons: paid private and group teaching plus free video material; the origin of the size/weight vocabulary [[transvoicelessons]] [[english-community]].
- SumianVoice: coaching practice with a free weekly-lesson Discord and the open Voice Resource Project wiki [[sumianvoice]].
- Renée Yoxon: de-medicalized one-to-one and course teaching, Big Dog Little Dog, weight exercises [[renee-yoxon]] [[yoxon2023]] [[yoxon2025]].
- Seattle Voice Lab: remote coaching business marketing feminization, masculinization and androgyny [[seattle-voice-lab]].

**Forum and instant-feedback spaces.**
- r/transvoice: recordings posted for community criticism since 2012, with a wiki and Discord [[r-transvoice]] [[transvoice-wiki]].
- The TransVoice Discord server: public invite advertising "free voice and text chat" and "instant voice feedback" [[transvoice-discord]].

**Apps and measurement tools for self-training.**
- TransVoice.App: guided course plus real-time pitch, spectrogram and loudness tools, with an explicit "not an assessment of your voice or gender" disclaimer [[transvoice-app]].
- Genderfluent: real-time pitch, formants and a "gender" estimate on a freemium plan [[genderfluent]].
- The wider measurement stack (Praat, Christella VoiceUp, Informant and others) is indexed under [[tools]].

**Chinese-language resources.**
- The 2024 self-study MtF voice-training guide mirrored on the Trans Chinese Digital Archive, with a stated 185–220+ Hz pitch-phase benchmark [[digital-transchinese-mtf-voice]].
- The Taipei hospital team's public teaching and surgery pages, community-grade provider material with 180 Hz-up / 140 Hz-down targets [[voicedoctor-tw]].
- The wider 伪声 and MtF.wiki layer is mapped in [[chinese-community]].

**What all of these have in common.**
- Each note records the resource's own claims about itself; none of these curricula has published outcome data, and the only community method adjacent to measurement is the single-teacher DSFD dataset [[netzorg2025]].
- Where the vocabulary overlaps research constructs (weight vs closed quotient, size vs formants), the mapping and its limits are worked through in [[vocal-weight]] and [[formants]].

## Contested or unclear
- Commercial claims of speed ("6–8 lessons" [[seattle-voice-lab]]) sit against clinical schedules of 8–12 sessions [[sodersten2026]] and 12-session intensives [[lin2026]]; no independent verification exists for any community curriculum.
- Volunteer infrastructure is fragile: the Sumi wiki banners itself "under construction" with missing pages [[sumianvoice]], and the r/transvoice wiki's resource lists were already unreachable in 2026 [[transvoice-wiki]].
- Safety teaching is uneven: one YouTube tutorial retracted a swallow-based larynx cue on safety grounds [[lucy2023]], while folk material elsewhere still rates harmful drills without caveats [[moegirl-weisheng]].

## Open questions
- Which community drills survive measurement — no study tests pitch slides, whisper sirens or Big Dog Little Dog against acoustic or listener outcomes [[sumi2021]] [[sutherland2025]] [[yoxon2023]].
- How the app-delivered "gender" scores relate to listener perception; the apps themselves disclaim assessment [[transvoice-app]] [[genderfluent]] [[machine-gender-scoring]].
