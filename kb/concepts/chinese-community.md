# The Chinese-speaking voice community

Who teaches gendered voice in Chinese, in which words, on which platforms, and how the 伪声 (fake-voice) subculture's method relates to the clinical literature. Three layers: an ACG performance subculture on Bilibili and wikis; trans-community infrastructure (MtF.wiki, archives, translated guides); and hospital or clinic services on the mainland and in Taiwan.

## What is known

**The 伪声 layer.** 伪声 means disguising one's voice as another's; in the ACG subculture it mostly means cross-gender voice, and it is codified as a 萌属性 (moe attribute) with cavity-based theory (男伪女 = shift to 咽腔 resonance, reduce 喉腔, remove 胸腔), star-rated methods (音阶法, 气泡音, 抬软腭, 学猫叫) and harm folklore [[moegirl-weisheng]]; the spelling 伪音 appears in video titles and tags [[bili-liujiu2023]] [[bili-zhizhiya2023]]. Its mass-market teaching lives on Bilibili: canonical tutorials with millions of plays date back to at least 2019 ([[bili-jiangfeng2019]]) and 2020 ([[bili-qiancang2020]]); its target register is often named with Chinese-opera vocabulary, 小嗓 ([[bili-liujiu2023]]); by 2023 the market's own creators were titles like 拒绝不科学伪声, acknowledging in their titles why folk methods fail ([[bili-zhizhiya2023]]). The singing-practitioner end of the same culture is explicitly the Chinese branch of the Japanese 両声類 concept [[moegirl-weisheng]].

**The Bilibili tutorial ecosystem.** The platform's 伪声 teaching runs in two separate markets with separate vocabularies. 男伪女 is the larger: its surviving line begins with a 2016 production that exported the Baidu 伪声吧 forums' authority onto video [[bili-caomeiweitian2016]] and a 2017 omnibus [[bili-muronyunlei2017]], continues through the 2019 pharyngeal-cavity flagship [[bili-jiangfeng2019]] and the 2020–2021 compilations [[bili-laxiaojiao2021]] [[bili-zhishengpeiyin2024]] [[bili-qiancangmonster2025]], and lands in 2026 short-video accounts that mix 伪音 tags with 科学发声 and 嗓音女性化 [[bili-pusuxueweisheng]] [[bili-tonghuaqiuye]]. 女伪男 is served by fewer and longer courses — a 2015 recorded group class [[bili-baishu2015]], the most-played 压音 tutorial in the corpus [[bili-qiyaomichiyo2018]], and a modern three-step format [[bili-wuming]] [[bili-yizhiyaoyan]] — with 压音 as its core drill and its named injury risk [[bili-weiyinjiaolianershu]], and with hormone-driven change rather than training as the durable route [[voice-masculinization]]. Voice lines are named after characters and archetypes rather than described acoustically: 萝莉音, 少女音 and 御姐音 on the female side, 正太音, 少年音, 青年音, 叔音 and 攻音 on the male, plus 戏腔 and imitations such as 熊二音 [[bili-jiangfeng2017]], defined through a position-and-breath grid [[bili-cv5924045]] and given per-line 共鸣 ratios and semitone offsets [[bili-cv9417414]]. 大嗓 and 小嗓 are ordinary Chinese vocal classification before they are 伪声 vocabulary: the pair draws millions of plays as a listening comparison [[bili-kaitekaixi2026]] and is taught by both a vocal pedagogue [[bili-hebingxin]] and an ENT professor [[bili-meixiangsheng]]; in Peking opera 小嗓 is the high register of the 小生 and 旦 roles [[bili-liujiapeng2020]] [[bili-jingjupan]] [[bili-hanwen2022]], and the subculture borrows the word for its target register [[bili-liujiubushi69]]. The market began attacking itself from 2019 under the label 科学伪声: a 不毁音 study group [[bili-heyishouwu]], a 2020 argument against learning and teaching 伪音 at all [[bili-xiuchangdejiucai2020]], the 2022 and 2023 declarations against unscientific practice [[bili-zhizhiya2022]] [[bili-zhizhiya2023]], and an account whose name is the warning [[bili-weiyinchenhai]], all resting on community self-report of harm — 变声期 practice, 压音, a base voice that did not come back [[bili-lele2021]] [[bili-mena]] [[bili-cv8487280]] [[bili-cv8757762]] — and sharing the wiki's practice caps and its 紧嗓子 prohibition [[moegirl-weisheng]]. Clinicians appear on the same feed: a mainland trainer marketing 跨性别嗓音训练 directly [[bili-chenzhen]], an ENT professor teaching 女声男调 through an outpatient funnel [[bili-meixiangsheng]], and a Korean voice clinic recruiting Chinese patients with its own surgical description [[bili-yisongvoice]]. Surgery and testosterone circulate as vlogs: the before/after testimonial is the standard format, whether a single success clip [[bili-luna2023]] or a serial post-operative record at day 10, day 13, 25 days, one month and six months [[bili-talita]] [[bili-neroblack]] [[bili-zhaoruoxi]], with one post-operative clip headlining failure [[bili-surfdog2026]] and lifestyle accounts carrying the decision to operate to an entertainment audience [[bili-ccjiang2024]]; the testosterone side reaches far with little detail [[bili-quanqiudamowang2018]] [[bili-oumeishigequan2025]] and is documented closely only inside the community [[bili-yiyi]] [[bili-nixiangshengzhang]] [[bili-keke]] [[bili-cv8358321]].

**The trans-community layer.** The 2021 handbook MTF声音女性化练习手册 re-teaches feminization in acoustic vocabulary (源-滤波器, 共振峰, F0/F1/F2, 喉位) and names the 伪声 video genre as the problem it replaces [[aogi2021]]. MtF.wiki holds the community's clinical map: training belongs to 言语治疗, train first and treat surgery as an add-on, software only monitors pitch [[mtfwiki-vocal-therapy]]; a price-listed provider directory spanning hospital ENT, rehabilitation and one psychiatric-centre trainer, plus an explicitly unlicensed tier [[mtfwiki-vocal-confirmed]]; a surgery wiki whose complication framing (响度损失, irreversibility, 效果抽奖) mirrors the clinical reviews [[mtfwiki-vocal-surgery]]; and a detailed protocol page for the most-referenced trainer, 陈臻, whose F0-first method, anatomy-limits view of formants and Mandarin tone-contour workaround are the clearest Chinese-language description of clinical practice [[mtfwiki-chenzhen]], and a Bilibili channel that appears to be the same clinician reaching clients directly, an identification inferred from the channel name and content [[bili-chenzhen2025]]. The community's slang (药娘, 伪娘, 木桶饭, pass, 天赋党/天残党) is codified with its own offensiveness warnings [[mtfwiki-slang]], and 药娘's Baike definition documents the self-medication context in which oestrogen-only users still need voice work [[baike-yaoniang]]. A free self-study guide circulates through the 跨性别中文数字档案馆 (Trans Chinese Digital Archive) as a mirrored PDF [[digital-transchinese-mtf-voice]]; masculinization content includes an authorised Chinese translation of an English community video, with small reach [[bili-fledence2025]]; surgery outcomes circulate as medical-tourism case videos, such as one patient's glottoplasty in Korea at about 60,000 RMB [[bili-luna2023]].

**The clinical layer.** Mainland journals publish trans-voice work in 中华耳鼻咽喉头颈外科杂志 ([[zhang2023cn]], [[sun2025]], [[chen2024cn]]) against a national assessment standard [[xu2025]], and treat the high-pitched male voice (男声女调) with therapy [[liang2017]] and relaxation laryngoplasty [[wang2009]] — a diagnosis adjacent to, and vocabulary-disjoint from, trans feminization [[yao2025]]. Taiwan has an SLP-society literature review [[lin2024]], a telepractice therapy pilot [[lin2026]], a survey showing most of 140 SLPs accept trans voice work as in-scope but feel insufficiently skilled [[litosseliti2019]], hospital teams advertising 變聲/調音 surgery with 180 Hz-up / 140 Hz-down targets and post-op therapy [[voicedoctor-tw]], and an advocacy-organisation directory of voice providers island-wide [[tapcpr-friendly-2024]]. Client-side narratives document what therapy actually contains for a Taiwanese trans man (8 sessions, habits over pitch) [[protalk-gabby2024]].

**How the layers relate.** The 伪声 subculture supplies the vocabulary (共鸣, 喉位, 气泡音, 小嗓) and the platform [[moegirl-weisheng]] [[bili-liujiu2023]]; the trans community supplies the science translation [[aogi2021]] and the directories [[mtfwiki-vocal-confirmed]] [[tapcpr-friendly-2024]]; the clinics supply the assessment and surgery [[mtfwiki-vocal-surgery]] [[voicedoctor-tw]] [[xu2025]]. The handbook's critique — folk tutorials are vague and single-method [[aogi2021]] — is the exact rupture the wiki infrastructure was built to bridge, and the mainland trainer the community treats as default states his knowledge base is 康复学和嗓音学, at odds with 民间伪声教程 and with singing pedagogy [[mtfwiki-chenzhen]]. Both community infrastructure and clinics converge on the same ordering (training before surgery, therapy after surgery) [[mtfwiki-vocal-therapy]] [[mtfwiki-vocal-surgery]] [[voicedoctor-tw]], matching the systematic reviews behind [[voice-feminization-therapy]] and [[voice-surgery]].

## Contested or unclear

- Whether 咽腔共鸣 and 喉位 drills are safe without supervision: the community itself rates 抬软腭 5-star harmful and caps practice ([[moegirl-weisheng]]), while the handbook argues high larynx per se is not the injury, throat tightness is [[aogi2021]].
- Whether formants can be trained far: the mainland trainer holds formant structure is anatomy-bound and only 微调-able, most trainees landing at a neutral +50–60 Hz voice [[mtfwiki-chenzhen]], while the resonance-therapy literature reports measurable formant gains and femininity shifts [[carew2007]] [[kawitzky2020]].
- How much of the 伪声 teaching ever reaches a gender-affirming goal at all — its own framing is performance and ACG convention, and the MtF guides treat it as entertainment [[aogi2021]] [[digital-transchinese-mtf-voice]].

## Open questions

- No study measures outcomes of any 伪声-derived self-training method; the community's expectations rest on testimonials and one trainer's reported +50–60 Hz [[mtfwiki-chenzhen]].
- No mainland Chinese-language outcome study of trans voice therapy (as opposed to surgery) was findable in the indexes reachable from here; Taiwan's pilot is single-group with 9 completers [[lin2026]]. Whether 中国听力语言康复科学杂志 or CNKI hold such work remains unverified (CNKI unreachable).
- Chinese-language FTM voice-training material found here is one translated video [[bili-fledence2025]] and one clinic narrative [[protalk-gabby2024]]; no Chinese-language FTM outcome data.
- Knowledge supply is unstable: censorship-driven slang ([[mtfwiki-slang]]) and archive-mirrored PDFs ([[digital-transchinese-mtf-voice]]) mean sources vanish; how the ecosystem will be documented in five years is open.
