# Voice-therapy techniques (訓練技法)

The exercises speech-language pathologists, singing teachers and community trainers actually do: semi-occluded vocal tract work (straws, tubes, lip trills, humming), resonant voice, vocal function exercises, twang, sirens and glides, and the Estill figures that isolate one structure at a time. Trans voice programmes borrow this toolkit; almost none of the trials below include trans speakers.

## What is known
- Voice therapy, on the encyclopedia's definition, "consists of techniques and procedures that target vocal parameters, such as vocal fold closure, pitch, volume, and quality", is "provided by speech-language pathologists", and is used "to aid in the management of voice disorders, or for altering the overall quality of voice, as in the case of transgender voice therapy"; vocal pedagogy is the related singing field, and therapy "may also serve to teach preventive measures such as vocal hygiene" [[wikipedia-voice-therapy]].
- **Semi-occluded vocal tract (SOVT).** Lip trills, tongue trills, bilabial fricatives, humming and phonation into tubes or straws are "hailed by clinicians, singing teachers, and voice coaches as efficacious for training and rehabilitation" [[titze2006]]. A self-oscillating fold model with a 44-section tract found that a semi-occlusion at the lips "heightens source-tract interaction by raising the mean supraglottal and intraglottal pressures. Impedance matching by vocal fold adduction and epilarynx tube narrowing can then make the voice more efficient and more economic (in terms of tissue collision)" [[titze2006]].
- A PRISMA meta-analysis of SOVTEs for dysphonia (eight trials, none at low risk of bias, GRADE "very low") found SOVTEs more effective than control interventions at changing F0 (ES −14.42 Hz) and at lowering subglottic pressure, but not significantly superior on shimmer, jitter, perceptual grade or self-assessment; the authors conclude SOVTEs are "not to consider significantly superior if compared to other treatments, even if a favorable trend was detected" [[pozzali2021]].
- Restricting inclusion to free-end-tube (straw-in-air) work left three studies (127 participants) with high risk of bias; the review still reports "beneficial effects … in improving acoustic characteristics and glottal performance" [[asadollahpour2025]].
- In singers, a systematic review of six studies (141 singers) found phonation into glass tubes in water, straws and LaxVox tubes associated with "more comfortable phonation, better voice projection, and economy in voice emission"; electroglottography was the commonest assessment [[mendes2018]].
- Water-resistance therapy as a 15-minute warm-up in 22 CCM singers changed aerodynamics; self-perceived resonant quality was similar to an open-tract warm-up, and the authors note that "vocal warm-up strategies might produce a placebo effect" [[saldias2020]]. A water-resistance ventilation mask designed so the patient can *speak* against water resistance, in 20 women with dysphonia, improved comfort, effort and self-rated quality after one session without moving the acoustics, except for a rise in lowest frequency in both groups [[kissel2023]].
- **Resonant voice therapy.** Singers and actors producing "resonant voice" used "a barely adducted or barely abducted laryngeal configuration" distinct from pressed and breathy (but not from normal) [[verdolini1998]]. In 13 women with nodules, confidential voice therapy and resonant voice therapy both showed benefit over roughly two weeks, and "the likelihood of benefiting from therapy directly covaried with compliance scores … but not with therapy type" [[verdolinimarston1995]].
- **Vocal function exercises.** In 35 women with objectively normal voices, four weeks of VFEs (sustained /i/, glide up, glide down, sustained low-note series) changed phonation volume, flow rate, maximum phonation time and frequency range relative to placebo-exercise and no-treatment controls [[stemple1994]].
- **Twang.** In one professional singer, "twang" versus neutral tended to have higher subglottal pressure and always had higher SPL; the source showed a greater closed quotient and a weaker fundamental, while F1 and F2 were higher and F3 and F5 lower. "The formant differences … were more important than the source differences for the perception of 'twanginess.'" The authors suggest the resonatory strategy "may reflect a vocal strategy that is advantageous from the point of view of vocal hygiene" [[sundberg2010]].
- **Humming, sirens, glides, yawn-sigh, fry onset.** The mutational-falsetto review found most of eleven studies used an eclectic mix of yawn-sigh, glottal fry, laryngeal manipulation, inspiratory phonation and humming, with "nearly all parameters of voice quality improving" [[adabi2026]]. Community resonance work uses panting and barking to change tract size ("Big Dog Little Dog"), presented as the author's favourite rather than as a trialled protocol [[yoxon2023]].
- **Estill Voice Training.** The programme "analys[es] the process of vocal production into control of specific structures in the vocal mechanism" so that "consciously move[ing] each structure" increases "the potential for controlled change of voice quality" [[wikipedia-estill-voice-training]]. The organisation's own overview is an essay on history, features, goals and teacher training [[steinhauer2019]]. Four Estill source conditions (Slack, Thick, Thin, Stiff) produced after training were differentiated with 88% accuracy by aerodynamics and perturbation: Thick had high pressure and stability, Thin lower pressure than Thick, Stiff high airflow, Slack low F0 and high instability [[barone2021]]. EVT-certified CCM singers outperformed otherwise-trained CCM singers on a quality-control task of pitch, perturbation and spectral-energy distribution [[fantini2017]]. In 82 vocally healthy student teachers, a Global Voice Prevention and Therapy Model using Estill as the stimulability component improved acoustic and perceptual measures in person and by telepractice, and decreased voice-related quality-of-life impact and vocal fatigue during student teaching [[grillo2021]]. Dynamic 3D MRI of one singer producing six Estill phonation types showed distinct fold thickness, contact and open quotient for each type, with open quotient tracking thickness and with supraglottic posture covarying [[traser2026]]. Listeners trained on physiology and sound identified laryngeal and epilaryngeal Estill configurations at well above chance, though some combinations remained hard to hear [[zhang2026]]. A practitioner essay argues that EVT's anatomy-first, "gender bias-free terminology" and "unlimited vocal options" make it usable for non-binary singers; that is the author's claim from interviews with her own students [[accetta2022]].
- **Trans-specific programmes that use these tools.** A systematic review of 14 studies of speech therapy for trans women found positive effects on pitch, oral resonance, self-perception and listener perception, with small samples, no blinding and short follow-up [[leyns2021]]. A crossover RCT (n = 30) found pitch elevation and articulation–resonance training each helped, and the combination scored highest on listener femininity [[leyns2023]]. Oral resonance therapy (lip spreading, forward tongue, five sessions, n = 10, no control group) generally raised F1–F3 and F0, with only the F3 rise significant [[carew2007]]; F2 biofeedback shifted F2 and perceived femininity [[kawitzky2020]]. Clinical maps of the same techniques under SOC7 and SOC8 are [[davies2015]] and [[coleman2022]].
- Across 35 studies of harmonisation programmes for trans women, the typical intervention was weekly, individual and in person, averaged 12.49 sessions, used resonance-based methods in 88.6% of cases, and had no follow-up in 54.3% [[brasil2026]].
- SOVT in trans speakers, single session only: in 40 trans people aged 18–40 one session of semi-occluded vocal tract therapy produced immediate change in local jitter, jitter APQ5, harmonics-to-noise ratio and F0, F0 being the change that made the voice higher [[fuenzalida2021]]; in eight untreated trans participants aged 13–24 the immediate effect was on jitter local and jitter RAP, with the harmonics-to-noise ratio approaching the normal value [[sandoval2019]].
- A Brazilian integrative review of voice and the transexualisation process screened 51 articles and found seven that addressed vocal therapy, six of them discussing the speech-language pathologist's part, with studies directly on the professional's role still scarce [[rosa2026]].
- Clients identified oral/forward resonance work as one of the most helpful voice training methods, while the frequency with which it was worked on did not predict voice satisfaction after training [[harris2026]].
- A coach's action research names mouth shape/oral posture, placement and felt vibration as the three practical routes into resonance [[conroy2025]].
- The English-language wiki states that hard onsets have a tendency to bring out heavy vocal weight, that soft onsets sound cleaner, and that aspirate onsets encourage breathiness [[sumi-onsets]].
- A 2023 JOHNS special issue on non-surgical treatment of voice, articulation and language carries a 性同一性障害 sub-section by 小林礼佳, the 言語聴覚士 whose account of voice therapy around voice surgery at a Japanese clinic is also in the vault ([[kobayashi2022]]); the article body sits behind a login [[kobayashi2023]].

## Contested or unclear
- Which named exercise produces which acoustic change is documented for resonance ([[carew2007]], [[kawitzky2020]]) and, in singers, for mix versus chest versus falsetto ([[lee2023]]). Pitch-elevation protocols vary and are not unpacked in the trans-woman trials [[leyns2021]].
- SOVT feels useful after one session even when the acoustics do not move [[kissel2023]]; warm-up methods can share a self-report effect while differing physiologically [[saldias2020]]. Felt ease is not a substitute for measurement.
- Estill's isolation of structures is a training model. Independent comparisons exist ([[fantini2017]], [[barone2021]], [[grillo2021]]), but several of the authors have organisational or commercial ties to Estill or to the assessment app used, and none of those trials recruited trans speakers.

## Open questions
- A trial of straw phonation, resonant voice, or VFEs *in trans speakers*, with a named protocol and a listener-gender outcome.
- Whether Estill Thick/Thin maps onto the "weight" dimension community trainers already teach, at a speaking pitch rather than on a sung quality.
