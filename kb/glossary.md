# Glossary

Each entry gives the English term, the concept note that holds the sourced statements, a one-line gloss, and the term's equivalents in other languages. Unless a source note is cited on the line, an equivalent is the title of the corresponding Wikipedia article (interlanguage links retrieved 2026-09-17 with `scripts/kb-wiki.mjs`); Wikipedia titles are a `community`-grade check of usage, not a dictionary. Glosses are plain-language summaries: the evidence is in the concept note behind the link. Community jargon is marked with the source that uses it.

## Anatomy

- **Larynx** [[larynx-anatomy]] — the voice box: a cartilage frame in the neck that holds the vocal folds; the thyroid cartilage in front shows as the laryngeal prominence.
  ja 喉頭 · zh 喉 · ko 후두 · es Laringe · de Kehlkopf · fr Larynx
- **Vocal cords** [[vocal-folds]] — the paired folds inside the larynx whose vibration makes voiced sound; "vocal folds" is the term voice science prefers.
  ja 声帯 · zh 声带 · ko 성대 · es Cuerdas vocales · de Stimmlippe · fr Corde vocale
- **Glottis** [[vocal-folds]] — the gap between the vocal folds; open for breathing, closing and opening once per cycle in phonation.
  ja 声門 · zh 声门 · ko 성문 · es Glotis · de Glottis · fr Glotte
- **Vocal tract** [[vocal-tract-anatomy]] — the air passage above the larynx (pharynx, mouth, nose) whose shape and length set the formants.
  ja 声道 · zh 聲道 · ko 성도 · de Vokaltrakt · fr Canal vocal
- **Pharynx** [[vocal-tract-anatomy]] — the throat cavity between larynx and mouth; its size changes with larynx height and tongue root position.
  ja 咽頭 · zh 咽 · ko 인두 · es Faringe · de Rachen · fr Pharynx
- **Soft palate** [[vocal-tract-anatomy]] — the velum, which opens or closes the nasal passage and so controls nasality.
  ja 口蓋 · zh 软腭 · ko 연구개 · es Velo del paladar · de Gaumensegel · fr Voile du palais
- **Thyroid cartilage** [[larynx-anatomy]] — the largest laryngeal cartilage, the shield that forms the laryngeal prominence; the vocal folds attach inside its front angle.
  ja 甲状軟骨 · zh 甲狀軟骨 · ko 방패연골 · es Cartílago tiroides · de Schildknorpel · fr Cartilage thyroïde
- **Cricoid cartilage** [[larynx-anatomy]] — the ring below the thyroid cartilage; the cricothyroid joint between the two tilts to stretch the folds.
  zh 環狀軟骨 · ko 반지연골 · es Cartílago cricoides · de Ringknorpel · fr Cartilage cricoïde
- **Arytenoid cartilage** [[larynx-anatomy]] — the paired cartilages at the back of the folds that swing them together (adduction) and apart.
  zh 杓狀軟骨 · es Cartílago aritenoides · de Stellknorpel · fr Cartilage aryténoïde
- **Cricothyroid muscle** [[larynx-anatomy]] — the muscle that tilts the thyroid cartilage forward, lengthening and tensing the folds; the main pitch-raising muscle.
  ja 輪状甲状筋 · zh 環甲肌 · ko 반지방패근 · es Músculo cricotiroideo · de Musculus cricothyroideus · fr Muscle cricothyroïdien
- **Thyroarytenoid muscle** [[larynx-anatomy]] — the muscle inside each fold (its inner part is the vocalis); its contraction shortens and thickens the folds and is central to chest register.
  ja 甲状披裂筋 · zh 甲杓肌 · ko 방패모뿔근 · es Músculo tiroaritenoides · fr Muscle thyro-aryténoïdien
- **Adam's apple** [[larynx-anatomy]] — the laryngeal prominence, the visible angle of the thyroid cartilage, larger on average after a testosterone puberty.
  ja 喉頭隆起 · zh 喉结 · ko 후두융기 · es Nuez de Adán · de Adamsapfel · fr Proéminence laryngée du cartilage thyroïde
- **Hyoid bone** [[larynx-anatomy]] — the bone the larynx hangs from; tongue and strap muscles move it, and with it the larynx height.
  ja 舌骨 · zh 舌骨 · ko 목뿔뼈 · es Hueso hioides · de Zungenbein · fr Os hyoïde
- **Epiglottis** [[larynx-anatomy]] — the leaf-shaped flap above the folds that covers the airway when swallowing.
  ja 喉頭蓋 · zh 会厌 · ko 후두덮개 · es Epiglotis · de Kehldeckel · fr Épiglotte
- **Thoracic diaphragm** [[breathing-and-support]] — the breathing muscle under the lungs; inhalation is active, exhalation for speech is regulated by the rib cage and abdominal muscles.
- **Breathing** [[breathing-and-support]] — the airflow that drives the folds; subglottal pressure sets loudness far more than it sets pitch.
  ja 呼吸 · zh 呼吸 · ko 호흡 · es Ventilación pulmonar · de Lungenventilation · fr Ventilation pulmonaire

## Acoustics and measurement

- **Fundamental frequency** [[fundamental-frequency]] — F0, the rate of vocal-fold vibration in hertz; heard as pitch. Koenami reports its median as 高さ. The Francophone work writes it **fréquence fondamentale** [[arnold2015]] [[remacle2018]], abbreviated fo in the Liège results [[gillot2020]].
  ja 基本周波数 · zh 基本頻率 · ko 기본 주파수 · es Frecuencia fundamental · pt Frequência fundamental · de Grundfrequenz · fr Fréquence fondamentale
- **Pitch (music)** [[pitch-and-scales]] — the perceived height of a sound; equal perceived steps, equal musical intervals and equal hertz are three different rulers.
  ja 音高 · zh 音高 · ko 음높이 · es Altura · de Tonhöhe · fr Hauteur
- **Semitone** [[pitch-and-scales]] — one twelfth of an octave, a frequency ratio of about 1.059; 12 semitones double the frequency. The German surgical series report their pitch gains in Halbtöne, a mean 5–6 of them [[neumann2005]] [[welzel2002]].
  ja 半音 · zh 半音 · ko 반음 · es Semitono · de Halbton · fr Demi-ton
- **Harmonic** [[harmonics-and-spectrum]] — a component of the voice source at a whole-number multiple of F0; formants are not harmonics but shape which harmonics come through.
  ja 高調波 · zh 谐波 · ko 고조파 · es Armónico · de Harmonische · fr Harmonique
- **Overtone** [[harmonics-and-spectrum]] — any partial above the fundamental; in the voice these are the harmonics.
  ja 倍音 · zh 泛音 · ko 배음 · es Sobretono · de Oberton · fr Partiel
- **Formant** [[formants]] — a resonance of the vocal tract; F1 and F2 carry vowel identity, and the overall spacing tracks tract length. Koenami's 響き is a spacing measure.
  ja フォルマント · zh 共振峰 · ko 포먼트 · es Formante · de Formant · fr Formant
- **Resonance** [[formants]] — the tract's reinforcement of certain frequencies; in training vocabulary "resonance" usually means the brightness that comes with a shorter tract.
  ja 共鳴 · zh 共振 · ko 공명 · es Resonancia · de Resonanz · fr Résonance
- **Timbre** [[voice-impressions]] — the quality that distinguishes voices at the same pitch and loudness; set by the spectrum and its change over time.
  ja 音色 · zh 音色 · ko 음색 · es Timbre · de Klangfarbe · fr Timbre
- **Spectrogram** [[harmonics-and-spectrum]] — a picture of frequency against time; harmonics appear as horizontal lines, formants as darker bands across them.
  ja スペクトログラム · zh 时频谱 · ko 스펙트로그램 · es Espectrograma · de Spektrogramm · fr Spectrogramme
- **Loudness** [[breathing-and-support]] — perceived intensity; driven by subglottal pressure and glottal closure, measured as sound pressure level.
  ja 音の大きさ · zh 響度 · ko 음량 · es Sonoridad · de Lautheit · fr Sonie
- **Mel scale** [[pitch-and-scales]] — a perceptual pitch scale used in speech technology; equal steps sound equal to listeners.
  ja メル尺度 · zh 梅尔刻度 · ko 멜 척도 · es Escala Mel · fr Échelle des mels
- **Praat** [[tools]] — the free phonetics program whose pitch and HNR algorithms Koenami reuses.
  ja Praat · zh Praat语音学软件 · ko 프라트 · es Praat · de Praat · fr Praat
- **MDVP / Multi-Dimensional Voice Program** (en) — an acoustic analysis package named beside Praat in the Rome hormone-therapy protocol, the second instrument in that battery rather than an Italian term [[caruso2026]]. → [[patient-reported-outcomes]] · [[tools]]
- **Prosogram** (en) — Mertens's prosody stylisation tool, built at KU Leuven and used at Liège to draw a melodic profile where F0 and formant averages show nothing about intonation [[blanckaert2019]] [[gillot2020]]. → [[intonation]] · [[tools]]
- **VoxLog** (en) — the ambulatory dosimeter that logged F0 and delivered the vibrotactile alert in the Liège biofeedback case, «Le BF programmé sur le dosimètre VoxLog» [[remacle2018]]; the journal report describes the same device without naming it [[morsomme2022]]. → [[voice-therapy-techniques]] · [[tools]]
- **Femininity VAS** (en) — the perceptual femininity rating on a visual analogue scale in the Salerno glottoplasty cohort, moving from 4.4 to 1.9 after surgery [[scarpa2025]]. → [[gender-perception]] · [[patient-reported-outcomes]]
- **Jitter** [[voice-quality]] — cycle-to-cycle variation of the period; with shimmer (amplitude variation) a perturbation measure used in clinical voice assessment.
  ja ジッター · zh 抖动 · ko 지터 · es Jitter · de Jitter · fr Gigue
- **Cepstrum** [[voice-quality]] — a spectrum of the log spectrum; cepstral peak prominence (CPP) is a robust measure of periodicity used alongside HNR.
  ja ケプストラム · zh 倒頻譜 · es Cepstrum · de Cepstrum · fr Cepstre
- **Vocal range** [[vocal-registers]] — the span from the lowest to the highest pitch a voice can produce; not the same as the speaking range.
  zh 人声音域 · ko 성악 음역 · fr Voix en musique classique
- **Vibrato** [[singing-and-voice]] — a regular pitch oscillation in trained singing.
  ja ビブラート · zh 颤吟 · ko 비브라토 · es Vibrato · de Vibrato · fr Vibrato

## Phonation, registers and qualities

- **Phonation** [[phonation]] — sound production by vocal-fold vibration; the myoelastic-aerodynamic theory explains the self-sustaining cycle.
  ja 発声 · zh 發聲態 · ko 발성 · es Fonación · fr Phonation
- **Vocal register** [[vocal-registers]] — a range of pitches produced with one vibratory pattern; the laryngeal-mechanism model names them M0 to M3.
  ja 声区 · ko 성구 · de Gesangsregister
- **Modal voice** [[vocal-registers]] — the ordinary speaking register (M1, "chest"), with the whole fold body vibrating and a longer closed phase.
  es Voz modal
- **Falsetto** [[vocal-registers]] — the register above modal (M2), with thinner folds, a reduced mucosal wave and a higher open quotient; not the same thing as a feminine voice.
  ja ファルセット · zh 假聲 · ko 가성 · es Falsete · de Falsett · fr Fausset
- **Head voice** [[vocal-registers]] — a sensation-based name for the upper register; the vibration felt in the head is feedback, not the sound source.
  ja 頭声 · ko 두성
- **Chest voice** [[vocal-registers]] — a sensation-based name for the lower register (M1); chest vibration is felt only below about 300 Hz.
  ja 胸声 · ko 흉성 · es Voz de pecho · de Bruststimme · fr Voix de poitrine
- **Vocal fry register** [[vocal-registers]] — M0, the lowest register: slow, damped pulses heard one by one; called creak in phonetics and 気泡音 in Chinese pedagogy.
  zh 氣泡音 · es Laringalización · fr Laryngalisation
- **Whistle register** [[vocal-registers]] — M3, the highest register, with full closure and resonatory rather than laryngeal differences from falsetto.
  zh 哨音 · es Voz tiple · de Pfeifregister · fr Voix de sifflet
- **Passaggio** [[vocal-registers]] — the transition between registers; laryngeal and resonatory transitions can happen at different pitches.
  es Pasaje · de Passaggio · fr Passaggio
- **Belting (music)** [[singing-and-voice]] — carrying a chest-like production high in the range; loud, bright and effortful; the wiki defines it as adding weight and volume to keep high pitches in M1 and warns that it tends to encourage hyperadduction [[sumi-belting]].
  ja ベルト · es Belting · de Belting · fr Belting
- **Twang** [[voice-therapy-techniques]] — a bright, nasal-sounding quality used as a resonance exercise in singing and voice therapy; mostly a resonance manoeuvre (higher F1 and F2) that also raises closed quotient and loudness; studied in one professional singer [[sundberg2010]]; the wiki writes it AES constriction and says it "adds the impression of high resonance" at the cost of buzziness rather than weight [[sumi-glossary]] [[sumi-resonance]].
  de Twang
- **Breathy voice** [[voice-quality]] — incomplete fold closure lets air noise through; acoustically a steeper spectral tilt and larger H1–H2.
  ja 息もれ声 · zh 气声 · ko 숨섞인소리 · es Voz jadeante · fr Murmure
- **Creaky voice** [[voice-quality]] — low, irregular, constricted phonation; several kinds exist and no single acoustic measure catches all of them.
  ja きしみ声 · zh 嘎裂声 · ko 삐걱거리는 소리 · es Voz ronca · fr Voix craquée
- **Whispering** [[phonation]] — speech without fold vibration; the tract shapes noise instead of harmonics.
  ja ささやき声 · zh 耳语 · ko 속삭임 · de Flüstern · fr Chuchotement
- **Humming** [[voice-therapy-techniques]] — voicing with the mouth closed; a semi-occluded exercise that eases fold vibration.
  zh 哼鸣 · ko 콧노래 · es Tarareo · de Summton · fr Fredonnement
- **Hoarse voice** [[voice-health]] — rough, breathy or strained voice; when it persists, a laryngologist should look at the larynx.
  ja 嗄声 · zh 声音嘶哑 · ko 쉰목소리 · es Disfonía · de Dysphonie · fr Dysphonie
- **Voice change** [[puberty-voice-change]] — the pubertal deepening of the voice as the larynx grows: late and abrupt in boys, smaller and gradual in girls.
  ja 変声 · zh 變聲 · ko 변성기 · es Mutación · de Stimmwechsel · fr Mue
- **Intonation (linguistics)** [[intonation]] — the pitch movement over a phrase; Koenami's 抑揚 is the 10–90 percentile F0 span in semitones.
  ja イントネーション · zh 語調 · ko 억양 · es Entonación · de Intonation · fr Intonation prosodique
- **Prosody (linguistics)** [[intonation]] — rhythm, stress and intonation together. The Liège work calls its assessment «l'évaluation de la prosodie», «un parent pauvre» beside the F0 measures, and proposes an automatic prosodic profile to replace the listener's impression [[blanckaert2019]].
  ja 韻律 · zh 韵律 · ko 운율 · es Prosodia · de Prosodie · fr Prosodie
- **Sibilant** [[articulation]] — the hissing consonants (s, sh); in English studies their spectrum is heard through the listener's assumption about the speaker's gender.
  ja 歯擦音 · zh 咝音 · ko 치찰음 · es Consonante sibilante · de Zischlaut · fr Consonne sibilante
- **Vowel** [[articulation]] — an open sound whose identity is carried by F1 and F2.
  ja 母音 · zh 元音 · ko 모음 · es Vocal · de Vokal · fr Voyelle
- **Nasalization** [[articulation]] — sound passing through the nose when the velum is lowered.
  ja 鼻音化 · zh 鼻音化 · ko 비음화 · es Nasalización · fr Nasalisation

## Training, therapy and health

- **Voice therapy** [[speech-language-pathology]] — behavioural treatment of voice by a speech-language pathologist or phoniatrician.
- **URICA-Voice** (en) — the stage-of-change instrument used to stage a patient's readiness for voice training; one Guangzhou study takes its stages as the outcome and relates sex, income, occupation and travel distance to them [[liuc2025]]. → [[voice-therapy-techniques]]
- **VCT** (en) — the Belgian survey's term, not expanded in its abstract: 57.4% of 197 trans and gender-diverse respondents had taken it up, and non-binary people presumed female at birth reported the most barriers to reaching it [[papeleu2025b]]. → [[voice-feminization-therapy]] · [[speech-language-pathology]]
- **Vocal pedagogy** [[singing-and-voice]] — the teaching of singing; the source of much training vocabulary (registers, support, placement).
  ja ボイストレーニング · es Pedagogía vocal · de Gesangspädagogik
- **Vocal warm-up** [[voice-health]] — gentle exercises before demanding use, such as humming, lip trills and straw phonation.
  ko 목풀기
- **Speech–language pathology** [[speech-language-pathology]] — the profession that delivers voice therapy.
  ja 言語聴覚療法 · zh 言語治療 · ko 언어병리학 · es Terapia del lenguaje · de Logopädie · fr Orthophonie
- **Transgender voice therapy** [[voice-feminization-therapy]] — gender-affirming voice and communication training; the Japanese article title, メラニー法, names one community method.
  ja メラニー法 · zh 跨性别语音疗法 · fr Féminisation de la voix · pt Treinamento vocal · ru Голосовая терапия для трансгендерных людей [[wikipedia-pt-treinamento-vocal]] [[wikipedia-ru-golosovaya-terapiya]]
- **Transgender voice surgery** [[voice-surgery]] — pitch-raising procedures such as glottoplasty and cricothyroid approximation, and pitch-lowering type III thyroplasty.
- **Vocal cord nodule** [[voice-health]] — small swellings on the folds from repeated collision.
  ko 성대결절 · es Nódulo · de Stimmlippenknötchen
- **Muscle tension dysphonia** [[voice-health]] — voice strain from excess laryngeal muscle tension without structural damage, treated by a laryngologist and a speech-language pathologist together.
  es Disfonía por tensión muscular
- **Puberphonia** [[voice-health]] — a persistently high, falsetto-like voice after puberty in a cisgender man; its literature is used by analogy in masculinization surgery reviews.
  zh 变声障碍性假声
- **Laryngitis** [[voice-health]] — inflammation of the larynx.
  ja 喉頭炎 · zh 喉炎 · es Laringitis · de Laryngitis · fr Laryngite

## Care and identity

- **Transgender** [[transgender-care-context]] — people whose gender differs from the sex assigned at birth.
  ja トランスジェンダー · zh 跨性別 · ko 트랜스젠더 · es Transgénero · de Transgeschlechtlichkeit · fr Transgenre
- **Trans woman** [[female-voice]] — a woman assigned male at birth; voice feminization work concerns her.
  ja トランス女性 · zh 跨性別女性 · ko 트랜스여성 · es Mujer trans · de Trans Frau · fr Femme trans
- **Trans man** [[male-voice]] — a man assigned female at birth; testosterone lowers his voice, training and surgery exist for the rest.
  ja トランス男性 · zh 跨性別男性 · ko 트랜스남성 · es Hombre trans · de Trans Mann · fr Homme trans
- **Non-binary** [[nonbinary-voice]] — a gender outside the man/woman binary; voice goals are often an in-between or flexible voice.
  ja ノンバイナリー・ジェンダー · zh 非二元性別 · ko 논바이너리 · es Género no binario · de Nichtbinäre Geschlechtsidentität · fr Non-binarité
- **Gender dysphoria** [[patient-reported-outcomes]] — distress from the mismatch between gender and body or social perception; voice is one common source.
  ja 性別違和 · zh 性別焦慮 · ko 성별 불쾌감 · es Disforia de género · de Geschlechtsdysphorie · fr Dysphorie de genre
- **Gender-affirming hormone therapy** [[hormones-and-voice]] — estrogen-based or testosterone-based hormone treatment; only testosterone changes the adult voice.
  ja 性別適合ホルモン療法 · zh 性别肯定激素治疗 · ko 트랜스젠더 호르몬 치료 · es Terapia hormonal transgénero · fr Transition hormonale
- **Estrogen** [[hormones-and-voice]] — the feminizing hormone; it does not raise the pitch of an adult voice [[leyns2021]] [[wikipedia-feminizing-hormone-therapy]].
  ja エストロゲン · zh 雌激素 · ko 에스트로겐 · es Estrógeno · de Estrogene · fr Œstrogène
- **Testosterone** [[hormones-and-voice]] — the masculinizing hormone; it lowers the pitch of trans men's voices over the first year, with wide individual variation [[nygren2016]] [[ziegler2018]], and a testosterone puberty leaves a lower voice that later estrogen does not undo [[ciancia2026]].
  ja テストステロン · zh 睾酮 · ko 테스토스테론 · es Testosterona · de Testosteron · fr Testostérone
- **Puberty blocker** [[hormones-and-voice]] — GnRH analogues that pause puberty; trans women whose suppression began at earlier Tanner stages had higher adult F0 and more satisfaction with their voice [[ciancia2026]] [[boogers2025]].
  ja 二次性徴抑制剤 · zh 青春期阻滞剂 · ko 사춘기 억제제 · es Bloqueadores de pubertad · fr Inhibiteur d'hormones

## Koenami's own metric names

The app's five measures and the verdict, as its method page defines them [[koenami-method]]:

- **高さ** — median F0 of voiced frames (Praat autocorrelation, 65–500 Hz). → [[fundamental-frequency]]
- **響き** — a formant-spacing (ΔF) measure: the mean of F1/0.5, F2/1.5, F3/2.5, F4/3.5 over measurable frames; it changes with vowel and mouth shape. → [[formants]]
- **質感** — median HNR (Praat cross-correlation); affected by breath, hoarseness and noise, and not a direct measure of vocal weight. → [[voice-quality]] · [[vocal-weight]]
- **明るさ** — energy of 1–4 kHz relative to 0.1–1 kHz in dB; depends on vowel, phonation, microphone. → [[voice-quality]]
- **抑揚** — the 10th–90th percentile span of F0 in semitones; compare the same sentence only. → [[intonation]]
- **滑舌** — articulatory clarity, rated by listeners in the sample review, not measured acoustically. → [[articulation]]
- **話す速さ** — from the transcript, in mora (ja), characters (zh), syllables (ko) or words (en); not comparable across languages.
- **声の判定 / 女性度** — a linear-discriminant projection of the five measures, scaled 25 = masculine samples' median, 75 = feminine samples' median; uncalibrated against listeners. → [[machine-gender-scoring]]

## Community and pedagogy terms

Terms as the communities and teachers use them, with the note that attests the usage.

- **両声類** (ja, りょうせいるい) — on ニコニコ動画, an honorific for singers who perform in a voice that sounds like the other sex, judged by 声質 rather than pitch accuracy; also someone who commands both a 男声 and a 女声 [[nicodic-ryoseirui]]; the tag also appears in YouTube 女声講座 titles as the billing of the two-voice skill [[aikun2020]] [[fujimine2025]], and a channel named 両声類大学 addresses trans learners with the same vocabulary [[ryoseiruiuniv2021]]; the term reaches academic use too, as the subject of an Acoustical Society of Japan paper on 「男性両声類」 that asks which features carry the impression of a female voice [[kitahara2017]]. → [[japanese-community]]
- **女声 / 男声** (ja) — "female voice / male voice" as produced voices, the tags paired with 両声類 [[nicodic-ryoseirui]]. → [[female-voice]] · [[male-voice]]
- **裏声** (ja) — falsetto; in the 両声類 article, what one listener may hear where another hears a 女声 [[nicodic-ryoseirui]]; Japanese training accounts treat it as the material a 女声 is built from — stabilise it, find where it flips, then press down into that break [[note-ippanmtf]] — while one lesson rejects it outright as unnecessary [[visera2021]]. → [[vocal-registers]]
- **Big dog / little dog** (en) — a resonance exercise: pant like a small dog for a small, bright tract, like a big dog for a large, dark one, then keep that shape when speaking; described by voice teacher Renée Yoxon, who credits no originator [[yoxon2023]]. → [[voice-therapy-techniques]] · [[formants]]
- **Chest voice / head voice** (en; ja 胸声 / 頭声; ko 흉성 / 두성; es voz de pecho) — sensation-based register names; the felt vibrations are feedback, not the sound source, and chest vibration is felt only up to about 300 Hz [[herbst2020]]. → [[vocal-registers]]
- **Chest resonance** (en, clinical) — a resonance target rather than the register name above: a case report on transmasculine therapy lists it among the goals worked on and reports it as part of what made the voice read male [[santos2020]]. → [[formants]] · [[voice-masculinization]]
- **Closed quotient** (en) — the share of each vibratory cycle during which the folds are closed; a longer closed phase gives stronger high harmonics and a brighter, heavier sound [[herbst2020]]. → [[vocal-weight]] · [[voice-quality]]
- **H1–H2** (en) — the amplitude difference between the first two harmonics; a more open glottis strengthens the low harmonics relative to the high ones [[hanson1997]], listeners hear changes in it as breathiness [[garellek2013]], and it is one of the measures behind "vocal weight". → [[vocal-weight]]
- **Creak / vocal fry** (en; ja きしみ声; zh 嘎裂声 / 气泡音) — several distinct kinds of low, constricted or irregular phonation; fry proper is periodic but heavily damped [[keating2015]]. → [[voice-quality]] · [[vocal-registers]]

- **Laryngeal mechanisms M0–M3** (en) — the four vibratory regimes shown by electroglottography: M0 fry, M1 chest/modal, M2 falsetto, M3 whistle; M1 and M2 overlap in pitch [[roubeau2009]] [[herbst2020]]; the wiki gives the same four as M0 fry / M1 normal speech / M2 falsetto / M3 whistle, adding mix and head voice as further names for M2 and flageolet and flute register for M3 [[sumi-mechanisms]]. → [[vocal-registers]]
- **Mix / mixed voice** (en; ja ミックスボイス; ko 믹스보이스) — a production measured in singers as low-pressure and low-flow, with open quotient and H1–H2 between chest and falsetto at the same pitch [[lee2023]]. → [[vocal-registers]]
- **Kopfstimme / Bruststimme** (de) — head and chest voice; some German-language authors reserve Falsett for men and Kopfstimme for women's upper register, a split Herbst rejects on anatomical grounds [[herbst2020]]. → [[vocal-registers]]
- **SOVT / straw phonation / LaxVox** (en) — semi-occluded vocal-tract exercises (straws, tubes, lip trills, humming) that raise pressure above the folds and make phonation more efficient in simulation [[titze2006]]; a meta-analysis of eight trials rates the evidence very low [[pozzali2021]]; the wiki's usage runs humming and lip trills together under the heading and prescribes very light SOVTEs, humming kept almost to the point of failing, while a hyperadduction habit clears [[sumi-glossary]] [[sumi-creak]] [[sumi-hyperadduction]]. → [[voice-therapy-techniques]]
- **Resonant voice** (en) — the barely adducted, barely abducted target of resonant voice therapy [[verdolini1998]]. → [[voice-therapy-techniques]]
- **Vocal function exercises / VFE** (en) — Stemple's four-exercise conditioning set with trial evidence in healthy voices [[stemple1994]]. → [[voice-therapy-techniques]]
- **Estill figures: Slack, Thick, Thin, Stiff** (en) — Estill Voice Training's named vocal-fold conditions, aerodynamically distinct after training [[barone2021]] [[wikipedia-estill-voice-training]]. → [[voice-therapy-techniques]]
- **Vocal hygiene** (en) — education about voice use, listed as indirect therapy for muscle-tension dysphonia [[vanhoutte2011]]. → [[voice-health]]
- **ボイストレーニング** (ja) — Japanese Wikipedia's title for the vocal-pedagogy article; in community use the word covers speech work too [[wikipedia-vocal-pedagogy]]. → [[singing-and-voice]]

The Voice Resource Project wiki's own working vocabulary, as its pages define it:

- **Vocal fold vibratory mass · VFVM** (en community) — how much of the vocal folds is moving; the quantity the wiki takes as the likely cause of vocal weight and says cannot easily be measured, so it is estimated from the sound qualities associated with it [[sumi-glossary]] [[sumi-new-terminology]]. → [[vocal-weight]] · [[vocal-folds]]
- **prekt · welit · rezion · sezion · lun · sul · brint · dern · lendis · prefol** (en community) — the Voice Resource Project wiki's invented vocabulary, kept on a signed page so the definitions cannot drift: prekt is F0, the cycles per second of the true vocal folds and not the subharmonics; welit the estimated VFVM, with adduction, twang, volume and creak excluded by rule; rezion the assumed average level of F1 and F2, anchored to the F1; sezion the impression of the speaker's size, where a voice sounds sul small or lun large; brint and dern hearing a voice as buzzy-bright or dull-dark; lendis the effect of airflow and closure on overall amplitude; and prefol, in the older signed version, high adduction with only the pressure voice needs and low airflow, which can be creaky or squeezed [[sumi-new-terminology]]. → [[vocal-weight]] · [[fundamental-frequency]] · [[formants]] · [[voice-quality]] · [[breathing-and-support]] · [[phonation]]
- **Closure** (en community) — the amount of air speech uses against the amount let through, laid out as one scale from glottal stop through pressed phonation, a slight adduction that adds brightness, flow phonation, darkening abduction, breathy and overblown to whisper [[sumi-breathiness]]. → [[phonation]] · [[voice-quality]]
- **Onset · hard / soft / aspirate onset** (en community) — the attack of the sound: a hard onset starts the folds pressed and tends to bring out heavy weight, a soft onset starts them lightly closed and sounds cleaner, an aspirate onset starts them open and can encourage breathiness [[sumi-onsets]] [[sumi-breathiness]]. → [[voice-therapy-techniques]] · [[phonation]]
- **Flow phonation** (en community) — the balanced middle of the closure scale, the state soft onsets encourage [[sumi-breathiness]]. → [[phonation]]
- **Overblown · airplaning** (en community) — the rung past breathy on the closure scale, short of no closure at all [[sumi-breathiness]]. → [[breathiness]]
- **Cover · covers** (en community) — anything that disguises part of a voice or modifies the whole of its quality; the wiki names nasality, tongue-root retraction and "M2 or too low weight" [[sumi-clarity]]. → [[voice-quality]]
- **Knodel** (en community) — the wiki's word for tongue-root retraction, which it also calls the Stitch voice and which harmonic-to-noise ratio does not catch [[sumi-clarity]]. → [[articulation]]
- **Mouth space** (en community) — one of the wiki's named controls on resonance, beside larynx height, vowel shape and OPC/OPE: corner tension with the expression removed [[sumi-resonance]]. → [[vocal-tract-anatomy]] · [[formants]]
- **Macrovowel · microvowel** (en community) — keeping the mouth in one vowel posture to colour speech, against modifying single vowels darker or brighter without changing which vowel they are heard as [[sumi-glossary]]. → [[articulation]]
- **Base pitch** (en community) — the pitch a voice returns to after intonation, which the wiki ties to vocal weight and fold mass [[sumi-glossary]] [[sumi-pipm]]. → [[fundamental-frequency]]
- **Subharmonics** (en community) — extra harmonics appearing halfway between the real ones when every second cycle is interrupted; the wiki reads them as a possible sign of false-fold constriction and as part of a "white noise" quality [[sumi-glossary]]. → [[voice-quality]]
- **Strohbass · pulse** (en community) — two of the names the wiki lists for M0 beside fry [[sumi-mechanisms]]. → [[vocal-registers]]
- **Mechanism break · voice crack** (en community) — the flip into M2 when a pitch is not being carried with enough weight or volume; the wiki reads a break in belting as a sign that the weight is there, not as something to force past [[sumi-belting]]. → [[vocal-registers]]
- **TA · CT · LCA** (en community) — thyroarytenoid, cricothyroid and lateral cricoarytenoid; the wiki puts pitch with CT, weight with TA (marked with its own question mark) and closure with the arytenoids and LCA, and one Japanese log corrects its own "edge" from TA to LCA on the same grounds [[sumi-voice-anatomy]] [[note-akagikanon202609]]. → [[larynx-anatomy]]
- **FVF** (en community) — the false vocal folds; their constriction is listed as a source of low harmonic-to-noise ratio and of subharmonics, and as bad for vocal health in the long term [[sumi-glossary]] [[sumi-clarity]]. → [[voice-health]] · [[vocal-folds]]
- **Microbehaviours · voiceprint** (en community) — the features too small to explain or train individually, which the wiki offers as the reason a voice matched on pitch, weight and resonance can still read feminine; the definition gives "voice print" as its own synonym, and the level is placed below onsets, the smallest thing the wiki otherwise trains [[sumi-microbehaviours]]; ranked second among gendered factors, with mimicry over years as the stated route [[sumi-vocal-gender]] [[sumi-microbehaviour-list]]. → [[voice-impressions]] · [[gender-perception]]
- **Out-of-place factors** (en community) — the wiki's separate list of features that are not gendered but make a voice sound unnatural: hollowness, breathiness, FVF constriction, overdone speech-pattern or accent modification, tongue-root retraction, nasality [[sumi-vocal-gender]]. → [[voice-impressions]]
- **PIPM / Pitch Naturalisation** (en community) — the wiki's weight-reduction method, whose instruction is to stop controlling single features and let the voice adjust by itself, with the goal given as a sound rather than a physical feeling; the page does not expand the acronym [[sumi-pipm]]; the wiki's front door recommends it as the way in for feminisation, crediting it with "probably 70% of the skills and changes you will need to learn" [[sumi-getting-started]]. → [[vocal-weight]] · [[voice-therapy-techniques]]

- **Mimicry** (en community) — imitating a chosen clip, which the wiki gives as the only route to the layer below onsets: "Some parts of the voice are so small and hard to consciously modify that mimicry is the only way we know of"; the method is a 2–5 second clip, copy the speech patterns rather than analyse them, and dub over it or mouth it before speaking [[sumi-mimicry]]; the wiki states it is for microbehaviours, speech patterns, sibilants, tempo and consistency, and explicitly not for weight, range, resonance or clarity [[sumi-mimicry]]; it is the last step of the wiki's plan, to be started once the target voice's pitch and resonance are reachable [[sumi-getting-started]]. → [[community-training]] · [[voice-impressions]]
- **Low pitch brightness · high resonance deepness** (en community) — the two entries the wiki's microbehaviour list holds: a low-pitched voice that still sounds bright and higher than it is, and a deep-sounding voice that is high in resonance without being bright; the list itself concedes the first "might not be a microbehaviour and instead just be something as simple as adding twang", and says no terms exist yet for most of what it is describing [[sumi-microbehaviour-list]]. → [[voice-impressions]] · [[vocal-weight]]
- **Pitch floor** (en community) — a minimum pitch set in the wiki's own in-site spectrogram, which "will cause the program to beep annoyingly whenever the sound drops below the set value"; it is the only measurement the tool advertises, a threshold to keep above rather than a verdict on the voice [[sumi-in-site-spectrogram]]. → [[fundamental-frequency]] · [[tools]]
- **"becoming an ear"** (en, in a Korean STS thesis) — the first stage of vocal transition as a Seoul National University thesis describes it: "developing sensitivity to voice through experience and training", the first stage of what the thesis, adapting Latour's "becoming a nose", calls "becoming a voice"; it is set before the search for a default everyday voice [[yun2025]]. → [[voice-impressions]] · [[community-training]]
- **Transnormativity · depathologisation** (en) — transnormativity is "the idea that there is a correct way to transition, or that transgender identity is in conflict with gender non-conforming identities", which a NIME instrument paper names as what its design refuses, adding that gender-based voice training in clinical settings "can still perpetuate transnormativity" [[kuhn2026]]; the Korean thesis argues the same way from the other side, saying that widening what counts as transition "challenges the pathologization of trans bodies in medicine" [[yun2025]]. → [[transgender-care-context]]

- **伪声 / 伪音** (zh, wěishēng) — "disguised voice"; in the ACG subculture, a cross-gender performed voice, codified on 萌娘百科 with a cavity-based resonance model and star-rated methods; its singing end is named as the Chinese branch of the Japanese 両声類 [[moegirl-weisheng]]. → [[chinese-community]]
- **男伪女 / 女伪男** (zh) — the male-to-female and female-to-male directions of 伪声 [[moegirl-weisheng]] [[bili-qiancang2020]]. → [[chinese-community]]
- **咽腔 / 喉腔 / 胸腔 / 头腔 / 鼻腔共鸣** (zh) — the cavity model of resonance in 伪声 teaching: 男伪女 means more pharyngeal (咽腔), less laryngeal (喉腔) and no chest (胸腔) resonance [[moegirl-weisheng]] [[bili-jiangfeng2019]]. → [[formants]]
- **喉位** (zh) — larynx height; a higher larynx shortens the tract and raises F2 in the handbook's account [[aogi2021]]. → [[formants]] · [[vocal-tract-anatomy]]
- **气泡音** (zh) — vocal fry, used as the "first sound" exercise and rated least harmful by the community [[moegirl-weisheng]]; the Chinese Wikipedia title of the vocal-fry article. → [[vocal-registers]]
- **咽音** (zh) — the usual Chinese rendering of twang, per the handbook [[aogi2021]]; in 伪声 videos also a loose name for a pharyngeal exercise. → [[voice-therapy-techniques]]
- **小嗓** (zh) — the opera-derived name for the high register that 伪声 targets [[bili-liujiu2023]]. → [[vocal-registers]]
- **夹子音** (zh) — a pinched, cutesy extreme voice, named partly in parody [[bili-zhizhiya2023]]. → [[vocal-registers]]
- **中性音** (zh) — the neutral intermediate voice trained before a cross-gender one [[moegirl-weisheng]]. → [[chinese-community]]
- **男声女调** (zh clinical) — the persistently high-pitched male voice, mutational falsetto, in mainland voice medicine [[yao2025]] [[wang2009]] [[liang2017]]; it sits inside 音调异常, the abnormal-pitch category whose stated mechanisms — 声带张力、质量的变化以及喉神经肌肉功能的异常 — contain no gender at all [[zhuang2025]]. → [[voice-health]]
- **嗓音女性化 / 嗓音男性化** (zh) — voice feminization, the term of the community wiki and clinics [[mtfwiki-vocal-therapy]] [[voicedoctor-tw]], and voice masculinization as a translated video titles it [[bili-fledence2025]]. → [[voice-feminization-therapy]] · [[voice-masculinization]]
- **声带截短术 · 环甲接近术** (zh) — vocal-fold shortening (Wendler-type glottoplasty), the mainland default, and cricothyroid approximation [[mtfwiki-vocal-surgery]] [[zhang2023cn]]. → [[voice-surgery]]
- **药娘** (zh community) — a person feminizing through hormones, often self-administered; MtF.wiki's slang table marks it as possibly offensive from outsiders [[mtfwiki-slang]] [[baike-yaoniang]]. → [[chinese-community]] · [[hormones-and-voice]]
- **跨女 / 跨男 / 跨儿** (zh community) — trans woman, trans man, and an affectionate umbrella term [[mtfwiki-slang]]. → [[chinese-community]]
- **pass · 天赋党 / 天残党** (zh community) — being gendered correctly by others; those whose anatomy starts close to the goal versus far from it [[mtfwiki-slang]]. → [[gender-perception]]
- **言语治疗 / 語言治療 / 言語治療** (zh, by region) — speech-language pathology on the mainland, in Taiwan and in Hong Kong [[wikipedia-zh-yanliao-zhiliao]]. → [[speech-language-pathology]]
- **變聲期** (zh) — the pubertal voice-change period, which the community treats as off-limits for training [[wikipedia-zh-biansheng]] [[moegirl-weisheng]]. → [[puberty-voice-change]]

- **嗓音显微外科技术** (zh) — phonomicrosurgery, one of the four operations the 2025 assessment survey reports as yielding 「改善发声及吞咽功能取得良好效果」 [[xu2025a]]; the standardisation editorial counts 嗓音显微外科手术技术的创新 among the advances of the field [[xu2023cn]]. → [[voice-surgery]]

- **主观听感知评估 / 客观声学检测 / 嗓音相关生活质量评估** (zh) — subjective audio-perceptual assessment, objective acoustic measurement and voice-related quality-of-life assessment: the three-part assessment vocabulary the standardisation editorial names first [[xu2023cn]], matching the 主观听感知、客观声学、生活质量量表 multi-dimensional framework of a 2025 editorial in the other national ENT journal [[xu2025a]]. → [[patient-reported-outcomes]]
- **依从性** (zh) — adherence; 嗓音训练依从性 is adherence to voice training, which one Guangzhou study stages with the URICA-Voice scale and relates to sex, income, occupational voice demand and distance to the hospital [[liuc2025]], and which remote delivery raised for out-of-town patients in another [[chenw2026]]. → [[voice-therapy-techniques]]

### Japanese (ja)

Terms met in the Japanese sources added in the 2026-09-17 pass, with the note that attests the usage. The 両声類 scene's words and the ENT clinics' words are listed separately because a reader meets them in different places.

- **地声** (ja) — the natural speaking voice, defined in the encyclopedia only by opposition: 裏声 「地声（表声）に対する語」 [[wikipedia-ja-uragoe]]; the training guides set it as the material to work from, 「あくまでも地声のまま高い声を出す」 [[brushvoice-joseigoe]]; one self-taught speaker keeps a high 地声 alongside the low one and reports its cost, 「高い声を裏声ではなく女の地声で出そうとすると、音域がかなり狭くなるため歌えない」 [[note-ippanmtf]]. → [[vocal-registers]]
- **表声** (ja) — the term the 声 article uses for the same register in its list ボーカルフライ、表声、裏声(ファルセット)、ホイッスル [[wikipedia-ja-koe]]. → [[vocal-registers]]
- **頭声区** (ja) — head register, the register the 裏声 article says 裏声 generally comes from [[wikipedia-ja-uragoe]]; en head voice, ko 두성. → [[vocal-registers]]
- **換声点** (ja) — the register break, given as the thing to learn to control because 「低くなりすぎると本来の男声に切り替わってしまったりします」 [[brushvoice-joseigoe]]; en passaggio / break. → [[vocal-registers]]
- **喉を開く ／ 喉を狭くする** (ja) — open the throat (the ordinary singing instruction) versus narrow it (the メラニー法 instruction): 「喉を広く開くというよりは、喉を狭くして発声するということが最大のポイント」 [[brushvoice-joseigoe]]. → [[voice-feminization-therapy]]
- **喉仏** (ja) — the visible Adam's apple; the guides say to raise and hide it and not to press it down [[brushvoice-joseigoe]], [[wikipedia-melanie-ho]]; learners call holding it high 「女声を出すまでの第一関門」, the first hurdle, reached by swallowing, by the tongue root or by the hyoid [[note-nuu]], and a 2026 course gives throat position a whole instalment [[mitama2026b]]; en Adam's apple, ko 목젖 (also used for uvula). → [[larynx-anatomy]]
- **声質** (ja) — voice quality, the property the 両声類 tag targets: 「主に"声質"だけをタグの対象とするため」 [[nicodic-ryoseirui]]; en voice quality. → [[voice-quality]]
- **声色** (ja) — voice colour, what an uploader praises when a performer switches parts: 「声色を巧みに変えて一人で歌いきる男性」 [[nicovideo-ribbonmatch2007]]; the 声 article lists it first among the things 声 varies in [[wikipedia-ja-koe]]. → [[voice-quality]]
- **多声類** (ja) — a many-voice performer, the wider category in the tag set (1,396 videos on 2026-09-17) [[nicovideo-ryoseirui-tag]], named as a related article by both encyclopedias [[nicodic-ryoseirui]], [[pixiv-ryoseirui]]. → [[japanese-community]]
- **声真似** (ja) — voice mimicry; the ニコニコ大百科 article says 生放送 performers are more often 声真似練習者 than singers [[nicodic-ryoseirui]], and the tag carries 11,348 videos against 両声類's 7,349 [[nicovideo-ryoseirui-tag]]. → [[community-training]]
- **女声講座** (ja) — "female-voice course", the YouTube genre name, used by entertainment channels and by trans trainers alike [[fujimine2025]], [[jura2022]], [[satsukipon]]; the reverse genre is 男声講座 [[noroda2020]]. → [[community-training]]
- **男性両声類** (ja) — a male 両声類, the group one Japanese study sampled for naturalness ratings [[hasegawa2017]]. → [[japanese-community]]
- **声パス** (ja, community) — voice passing, the goal label in a trans woman's voice-training category, paired with ボイトレ as 「声パス・ボイトレ」 [[yomedanchii-voice]]; one practitioner argues the bar is lower than learners fear, since listeners do not suspect 両声類 and read 言葉遣い and 振る舞い as much as the voice [[note-kyanaru-voicepass]]; en passing [[wikipedia-passing-gender]]. → [[gender-perception]]
- **メラニー法** (ja) — the Japanese name for Melanie Anne Phillips' method; the encyclopedia keeps the attribution 「自身もトランスジェンダーであるアメリカのメラニー・アン・フィリップス…によって開発された」 [[wikipedia-melanie-ho]], while a commercial guide uses it loosely for any 「男性が女声を出す発声方法」 [[brushvoice-joseigoe]]. → [[voice-feminization-therapy]]
- **話声位, SFF** (ja) — speaking fundamental frequency, the Japanese clinical measure named in paper titles and keywords [[sakuraba2009]], [[nakamura2021]], [[nimura2015]]; type 3's series spells the abbreviation out, 「話声位（speaking fundamental frequency：SFF）」 [[nakamura2008]]; it is the figure single-case reports quote [[mizawa2022]], [[nishiyama2023]] and the number clinics publish in Hz [[kyoto-ent-clinic]]; the 声 article's figure is 成人男性で120Hz前後、成人女性で240Hz前後 [[wikipedia-ja-koe]]; en speaking F0 / SFF, pt frequência fundamental, es Mf0. → [[fundamental-frequency]]
- **音声女性化手術** (ja) — voice feminization surgery, the clinics' patient-facing name for the pitch-raising operations [[kyoto-ent-clinic]]; zh 嗓音女性化, ko 음성여성화수술. → [[voice-surgery]]
- **声帯短縮術 ／ ウェンドラー声帯短縮術** (ja) — vocal-fold shortening, attributed to Wendler's 1990 glottoplasty [[kyoto-ent-clinic]] and described by a Tokyo clinic as 「世界的に主流となってきている手術」 [[met-ent-tokyo]]; the clinic's own page gives the mechanism, 「声帯の前3分の1を縫合することで振動する部分が短くなり」 [[kyoto-ent-wendler]], followed there by 「術後およそ2週間は発声できません」 and a minimum of six months before the voice stabilises [[kyoto-ent-wendler]]; patients reach it at a Kyoto clinic, followed there by about a month of voice rest [[note-eri-kyoto]], or by travelling to a Seoul clinic that quotes a four-day stay [[note-shirachaka]]; a 2025 feature teaches it beside 甲状軟骨形成術Ⅳ型 as the standard pair [[hiroshiba2025]], the same comparison the clinic prints for patients [[kyoto-ent-clinic]]; zh 声带截短术, ko 성대단축술. → [[voice-surgery]]
- **甲状軟骨形成術Ⅳ型 ／ Ⅲ型** (ja) — type 4 thyroplasty, which tightens fold tension to raise 話声位 — 「牽引糸を用いて甲状軟骨と輪状軟骨を近接させ話声位を上昇させる術式である」 [[mizawa2022]] — and type 3, which shortens the cartilage's front-to-back dimension so the folds relax and the pitch falls [[nakamura2008]]; the two are the operations the Japanese clinics offer for pitch [[kyoto-ent-clinic]], [[met-ent-tokyo]], [[nakamura2021]], type 4 under the alternative name 輪状甲状軟骨近接術, credited in Japanese practice to 一色信彦 in the 1970s [[kyoto-ent-clinic]] and written 輪状甲状軟骨接近術 [[umemiya2003]] and 甲状輪状軟骨接近術 [[tanaka1981]] in the older literature, which reports the founding pair — type III for five men whose voices were too high, type IV for nine women whose voices were too low [[tanaka1981]]; type 3 lowers SFF further when done bilaterally than unilaterally and raises the AC/DC ratio with it [[nakamura2008]], while type 4's pitch change is credited to cricothyroid tension plus thyroarytenoid stiffness rather than tension alone, so the lowest note need not rise [[ri2018]] [[nishimura2025]]; the abbreviation TP4 appears in Japanese abstracts [[mizawa2022]]; zh 环甲接近术. → [[voice-surgery]]
- **喉頭枠組み手術 ／ 音声外科手術 ／ 喉頭形成術** (ja) — laryngeal framework surgery, the category name for the type 3/4 operations [[nakamura2021]] and the title term of a case series on GID and spasmodic dysphonia [[sanuki2007]]; phonosurgery is the keyword on a type-4 outcomes paper [[nakamura2022]]; a complication report writes the same class 喉頭形成術 and gives the reason the Japanese lineage operates awake — 「覚醒下で患者の音声を確認しながら行う手術である」 [[kurita2011]]. → [[voice-surgery]]
- **声帯注射** (ja) — repeated steroid injection into the folds, offered where surgery is not possible, with 「調節ができず、効果には個人差があります」 [[kyoto-ent-clinic]]. → [[voice-surgery]]
- **喉頭隆起切除** (ja) — shaving the laryngeal prominence, offered by a Tokyo clinic alongside the pitch operations with the warning 「極端な切除を行うと声帯にダメージを与えてしまう」 [[met-ent-tokyo]], and combinable with them; in one report it was done together with type 4 thyroplasty, and the patient presented 13 years later with an infected granuloma around that operation's traction suture and bolus [[mizawa2022]]; en Adam's-apple shave. → [[larynx-anatomy]]
- **音声治療（VT）／ 理学療法（PT）／ 自己トレーニング** (ja) — voice therapy and physical therapy as the Japanese clinical abbreviations, given together with type 4 thyroplasty in one case report [[kunieda2023]], and self-training as what a clinic calls the post-operative work it charges for separately [[kyoto-ent-clinic]]; en voice therapy, physiotherapy. → [[voice-therapy-techniques]]
- **ホルモン音声障害** (ja) — hormonal dysphonia, defined in the Japanese literature as the disorder a woman develops when given virilising drugs — 「男性ホルモン剤や蛋白同化ステロイド剤などの男性化作用のある薬剤を女性に投与した場合に発症する音声障害を指す」 [[miyata2013]] — with the mucosal wave reduced alongside the pitch and range loss [[kusuyama2005]]; one case in a cis woman treated for menopausal symptoms is described as 「話声位の低音化，声域の狭小化」 [[kunieda2023]]; therapy recovered the mucosal wave in three of four cases, but only after the drug was withdrawn [[kusuyama2005]], and a 30-year follow-up of one woman found the pitch drop still there and still worsening despite her own training [[kobayashi2012]]. → [[hormones-and-voice]]
- **性別適合手術 ／ 特例法** (ja) — gender-affirming surgery, the term both the psychiatric society and the GI society use as official [[wikipedia-ja-seibetsutekigoushujutsu]] and one of the keywords of the Okayama comprehensive-treatment review, 「性同一性障害 , 包括的治療 , 性別適合手術」 [[namba2014]]; the same team's 2001 paper uses the English 「SRS」 and lists 「voice change, laryngeal surgery」 among the ancillary procedures after the genital operations [[takahashi2001]]; 特例法 is the everyday shortening of 「性同一性障害者の性別の取扱いの特例に関する法律」 [[wikipedia-ja-tokureiho]], [[gidjp-opinion2023]]. → [[transgender-care-context]]
- **混合診療** (ja) — mixing insured and self-paid treatment in one course, which the community blames for making insured surgery unreachable for anyone already paying for hormones [[note-maru2blog]], [[gidjp-opinion2023]]. → [[transgender-care-context]]
- **乙女塾** (ja) — a study programme for 「女の子らしくなるための学びの場」 founded by the trans singer and voice trainer 西原さつき, whose channel teaches 女声 as 現役ボイトレ講師・両声類 [[satsukipon]]. → [[japanese-community]]
- **ささやき法 ／ 濁り抽出法** (ja) — "whisper method" and "muddy-extraction method", the two named 男声→女声 techniques a voice-actor school lists beside メラニー法; the first trains 「吐息まじりに声を出す」 and builds volume from there, the second starts from a throaty 「ヴゥゥー…」 and thins it [[anime-academy-ryoseirui2021]]. → [[voice-therapy-techniques]] · [[japanese-community]]
- **チェストボイス ／ ミドルボイス ／ ヘッドボイス ／ ウィスパーボイス** (ja) — the four resonance-register names taught for 両声類 work, defined by where the vibration is felt: chest, the midpoint between 地声 and 裏声 with 「鼻の奥にある空洞」, the head (equated on the page with 裏声), and a whispered, folds-open production [[anime-academy-ryoseirui2021]]; en chest voice / mixed voice / head voice / whisper voice. → [[vocal-registers]]
- **知覚的女声度** (ja) — "perceived degree of female voice", a listener-rated score for an MtF speaker's voice, used as the target of an automatic estimator that reached r = 0.86 against listener ratings where listeners agreed with each other at r = 0.88 [[maruyama2007]]; en perceived femininity score. → [[machine-gender-scoring]] · [[gender-perception]]
- **男女識別** (ja) — speaker-sex classification, the keyword for the signal-processing side of this work [[maruyama2007]]. → [[machine-gender-scoring]]

- **エッジ** (ja) — "edge": pressed vocal-fold closure added to a thin 裏声 so harmonics come back into it; one log reports that this configuration uses the TA as well as the CT and 「２～３分使うと声がおかしくなります」, then corrects the muscle it should come from to the LCA [[note-akagikanon202607]] [[note-akagikanon202609]]; en pressed phonation / edge. → [[voice-quality]] · [[phonation]]
- **倍音** (ja) — overtones; named as what a female voice actually needs by a lesson that rejects 裏声 and high pitch, 「必要なのは発声と『倍音』だけ」 [[visera2021]], and as what a plain 裏声 lacks before エッジ is added [[note-akagikanon202607]]; en overtone / harmonic. → [[harmonics-and-spectrum]]
- **音色 ／ 響き** (ja) — timbre, and resonance as it is heard: the two names the Japanese corpus uses for the quality dimension, made chapter one of a 両声類大学 course ahead of pitch [[ryoseiruiuniv2021]]; 響き is also the name of Koenami's formant-spacing measure [[koenami-method]]. → [[voice-quality]] · [[formants]]
- **こえ、はかる** (ja) — a consumer app that judges a recording masculine or feminine from F0 and formants; a training log uses it as its judge and records that a high F0 was still not enough for a female verdict [[note-akagikanon202607]]; the sequel calls it only "the app" [[note-akagikanon202609]]; en (app name, no equivalent). → [[machine-gender-scoring]]
- **雑味** (ja) — "extra flavour": rough or noisy quality in a voice, listed by one practitioner among the things that make it read male [[note-kyanaru-voicepass]]; en roughness. → [[voice-quality]]
- **塊量感 ／ 質量感** (ja) — a sense of mass or heft in the voice, the community's phrasing for what the English scene calls vocal weight; listed with a low pitch as what fails 声パス [[note-kyanaru-voicepass]]; en vocal weight. → [[vocal-weight]]
- **ドス** (ja) — a gravelly low resonance; 「男性的なドスの利いた低音域(100Hz台とその下)」 is one practitioner's name for the register that tips a voice to male [[note-kyanaru-voicepass]]; en gravelly low voice. → [[voice-quality]]
- **イケボ** (ja) — the performance word for an attractive male voice, the target of a 男声講座 sub-genre and the counterpart of カワボ [[natsunoame2022]]; en (no equivalent). → [[male-voice]]
- **カワボ** (ja) — a cute voice, the female term paired with イケボ in the same lesson vocabulary [[natsunoame2022]]; en (no equivalent). → [[voice-impressions]]
- **ショタボ** (ja) — a word in the 男声 lesson vocabulary, listed beside イケボ and ダミ声 [[natsunoame2022]]; the reading "a boyish young-male voice" (ショタ + ボイス) is this glossary's own gloss, not from a note; recorded from a lesson title alone (youtube.com/watch?v=UyEGtIk9Mqk, oEmbed 2026-09-17), the lesson itself not written up. → [[male-voice]]
- **ダミ声** (ja) — a word in the 男声 lesson vocabulary [[natsunoame2022]]; the reading "a hoarse, raspy voice" is this glossary's own gloss, not from a note; the title of a 女声 lesson announces its presenter as 「元ダミ声」, one who came from a hoarse voice (youtube.com/watch?v=fso3KTKUWcw, oEmbed 2026-09-17). → [[voice-quality]]
- **ボイトレ** (ja) — the clipped everyday form of ボイストレーニング (whose entry above records the Wikipedia article title), the genre label on follow-along practice videos and in the logs, where it is paired with the target as 女声ボイトレ [[tanakarin2021]] [[note-akagikanon202607]]. → [[community-training]] · [[singing-and-voice]]
- **脱力** (ja) — releasing effort; the organising instruction in one practitioner's training, labelling three of the five configurations in the July log (and the 脱力裏声 of its sequel) and naming the state the teacher's 「ふくろう」「あくび」 cues aimed at [[note-akagikanon202607]] [[note-akagikanon202609]]; en relaxation / release. → [[voice-therapy-techniques]]
- **あくび ／ ふくろう** (ja) — "yawn" and "owl", the relaxation cues a teacher gave a learner whose 地声 carried too much TA tension, which the learner turned into a descending 「↑ホーッ↓」 exercise and then into long tones [[note-akagikanon202609]]; en (no equivalent). → [[voice-therapy-techniques]]
- **舌根** (ja) — the tongue root; raising it is one of the routes learners use to hold the larynx high, and a beginner reports reaching the position as 「舌根が上顎にくっついてる感じ」 [[note-nuu]]; en tongue root. → [[articulation]] · [[larynx-anatomy]]
- **舌骨 ／ 甲状軟骨** (ja) — the hyoid bone and the thyroid cartilage; one log reports the two feeling fused, 「私は普通の人と違って、舌骨と甲状軟骨が密着してる感じです」, and treats laryngeal massage of the area as the remedy, with the result flagged as an impression [[note-akagikanon202609]]; en hyoid bone / thyroid cartilage. → [[larynx-anatomy]]
- **喉頭マッサージ** (ja) — laryngeal massage; used on the hyoid–thyroid area, after which the writer felt 「喉頭内部がニョーンと伸びる感覚」 and 「厚く重い声帯が少しは軽くなったような気がします」, without measurement [[note-akagikanon202609]]; en laryngeal massage · circumlaryngeal massage. → [[larynx-anatomy]] · [[voice-therapy-techniques]]
- **絶飲食** (ja) — "no eating or drinking": the fast a Seoul clinic required for four hours before the pre-operative tests, in a patient's account of the schedule [[note-shirachaka]]; en nil by mouth. → [[transgender-care-context]]
- **発声禁止 ／ 筆談** (ja) — voice rest and communicating by writing: the clinic's instruction after ウェンドラー声帯短縮術 is 「術後2週間ほどは声を出さず、安静が必要になります。発声は必要最小限にして筆談などを使って声を出さないように心がけてください」 [[kyoto-ent-wendler]], while a patient's account of the same operation gives about a month of near-total rest [[note-eri-kyoto]]; en voice rest / written communication. → [[voice-health]]
- **お姉さん声** (ja) — "older-sister voice", a character target rather than a gender target: the subject of a 2025 lesson and one of a log's five configurations, where it sits in 裏声 at a lower pitch with effort released [[fujimine2025]] [[note-akagikanon202607]]; zh 御姐音, en (no equivalent). → [[japanese-voice]]
- **ロリ声** (ja) — a childlike-girl character voice; in one log it is the 裏声 at a high pitch with エッジ added, the configuration the writer had just learned to make [[note-akagikanon202607]]; zh 萝莉音, en (no equivalent). → [[japanese-voice]]
- **ナレーター ／ 宅録ナレーター** (ja) — narrator, and the home-recording narrator; the trade of the writer of a 2026 essay on what the gender of a voice is for, which ends by treating the voice as a work tool [[note-zunba]]; en narrator / home-studio voice actor. → [[japanese-community]]
- **共鳴 ／ 鼻腔共鳴** (ja) — resonance, and nasal resonance: a Japanese summary of an English livestream warns that 「海外の共鳴＝日本の鼻腔共鳴では一切ない」, because 共鳴 in Japanese practice means nasal resonance and placement, while the English tradition's レゾナンス means brightness and darkness [[note-meimu-yoxon]]; en resonance / nasal resonance. → [[formants]] · [[vocal-tract-anatomy]]
- **アンザッツ** (ja) — the placement zones that Japanese 共鳴 is taken to include, named in the same summary as part of what the word means in practice [[note-meimu-yoxon]]; en (no equivalent). → [[singing-and-voice]]
- **VTuber ／ 新人Vtuber** (ja) — virtual streamer, and one on debut; the frame several 2025–26 女声講座 series publish from, which makes the lessons performance content as much as care [[mitama2026]] [[mitama2026b]]; en VTuber / virtual YouTuber. → [[japanese-community]]
- **ボイチェン** (ja) — voice changer; an RVC conversion whose output one log used as 「フォルマントやトーンの参考データ」, reporting that it pulled the speaker's own voice self-perception along with it [[note-akagikanon202607]]; en voice changer. → [[tools]]
- **RVC** (en) — the AI voice-changer model family behind one log's ボイチェン experiment; the log links a VRChat set-up guide and does not expand the acronym [[note-akagikanon202607]]. → [[tools]]
- **声自認** (ja) — "voice self-image", a writer's phrase for the self-concept of their voice fixed before 声変わり, which they blame for years of a pressed, F1-emphasised high voice [[note-akagikanon202607]]; en voice self-perception. → [[voice-impressions]]

- **変声障害** (ja) — mutational dysphonia, a speaking pitch that stays high past puberty — 「なかには思春期を過ぎても高いままの話声位が持続することがある。それを変声障害という」 [[nakamura2011]]; the sequence is therapy first, with type 3 thyroplasty for non-responders [[otsu2010]] [[nakamura2011]], though the earliest Japanese report argued for operating at once — 「いたずらに時間を費すことは得策とは言えず」 [[tanaka1981]] — and the operation is also used where the folds are atrophic rather than the pitch stuck [[nakamura2008]]; en Wikipedia covers the condition as Puberphonia. → [[puberty-voice-change]]
- **翻転** (ja) — the register break, the voice flipping over into 裏声; named among what a therapy target must avoid — 「絞扼や翻転することなく高音を出させること」 [[sato2007]] — and, in a hormonal-dysphonia follow-up, as the event at onset, 「声は翻転し」 [[kobayashi2012]]; en register break. → [[vocal-registers]]
- **絞扼** (ja) — constriction in the production of high pitch, avoided alongside 翻転 in one therapy programme whose aim is instead 「話声で持続して高音が出せること」 [[sato2007]]; en constriction. → [[voice-therapy-techniques]]
- **気息性発声** (ja) — breathy phonation; the presentation of a patient who came home after pitch-elevation surgery abroad with 「高度の気息性発声」 and was still unsatisfied about five months after the operation abroad, despite granuloma excision [[mesuda2005]]; the compound 気息性嗄声, breathy hoarseness, is one of the states type 3 thyroplasty is indicated for [[nakamura2008]]; en breathy voice. → [[voice-quality]]
- **声のつまり** (ja) — a squeezed, blocked voice quality, developed over years by one patient and named as the complaint her surgery addressed; it cleared after thyroarytenoid resection [[nakamura2009]]; en (no equivalent). → [[voice-quality]]
- **モーラ法** (ja) — the mora method, a Japanese dysphonia severity count of affected morae; one case measured 11/21 before thyroarytenoid resection and 0/21 at both later points [[nakamura2009]]; en (no equivalent). → [[voice-quality]]
- **低音化音声障害** (ja) — pitch-lowering voice disorder, the Japanese term for a voice that has dropped too far; type 4 is offered for it to cis women whose pitch fell with declining female hormone [[nakamura2009a]], and the GID case in one report is labelled 「GIDのための低音化音声障害」 [[nakamura2009]]; en (no equivalent). → [[fundamental-frequency]]
- **包括的治療** (ja) — comprehensive treatment, the Japanese model of care by a multi-department hospital team: the Okayama team was assembled in 1999 from psychiatry, plastic surgery, urology, obstetric/gynaecological surgery and social services [[takahashi2001]], and the word is a keyword of the later review of the same programme [[namba2014]]; en (no equivalent). → [[transgender-care-context]]
- **音声障害診療ガイドライン** (ja) — the clinical practice guideline for voice disorders, 2018 edition, published by 金原出版 through the Japan Society of Logopedics and Phoniatrics [[jslp]]; it carries a classification of voice disorders derived from ASHA's manual with codes for patient-data management, and closes with 12 clinical questions each graded from a literature search [[umeno2022]]; it is a dysphonia guideline and not a gender one, and the corresponding gap — pitch-elevation surgery for MtF patients 「ガイドラインに明確な定義がされていなかった」 — was named by a Japanese case report in 2005 [[mesuda2005]]; en (no equivalent). → [[clinical-guidelines]]
- **専攻医トレーニング講座 ／ 研修ノート** (ja) — two short Japanese journal formats for teaching rather than research, both used by the surgeon whose trans-voice work runs through this line: a four-page training lecture for specialty trainees in the ENT society's journal [[nakamura2024]] and a two-page training note in 耳鼻咽喉科臨床 [[nakamura2020]]; en training lecture / training note. → [[speech-language-pathology]]
- **音声外来** (ja) — a voice clinic or voice outpatient service; it is the route by which a patient reaches the clinic's voice work, and the diagnosis given there is what refers someone to the self-paid training [[kyoto-ent-training]]; en voice clinic. → [[speech-language-pathology]]
- **ボイストレーナー** (ja) — vocal trainer, the self-paid tier rather than the licensed one: a Kyoto clinic runs 「ボイストレーナーによるボイストレーニング」, optional and 自費 at 6,600 yen per 30 minutes [[kyoto-ent-training]]; en vocal trainer / voice coach. → [[voice-feminization-therapy]]
- **保険診療 ／ 自費診療** (ja) — insurance-covered and self-funded treatment, the split that separates the three routes on one Japanese clinic's site: the speech-language pathologist's rehabilitation is 保険診療 at 「1単位(20分)100点」 [[kyoto-ent-rehab]], while the vocal trainer and every pitch-changing operation are 自費 [[kyoto-ent-training]] [[kyoto-ent-wendler]]; the community blames 混合診療 for making insured surgery unreachable [[note-maru2blog]]; en (no equivalent). → [[transgender-care-context]]
- **単位 ／ 点** (ja) — the reimbursement unit, 20 minutes, and the point it is priced in: a clinic's rehabilitation is 「1単位(20分)100点」, quoted as 520 yen a visit at the 3割 copay and 1,120 yen for 3 units [[kyoto-ent-rehab]]; en (no equivalent). → [[voice-therapy-techniques]]
- **認定施設 ／ 認定医 ／ コーディネーター** (ja) — the GI society's directories of accredited facilities (ten are named on the page fetched), certified physicians, and medical and educational coordinators; the society renamed itself from GID学会 on 2024-03-18, and the list carries no criteria and says nothing about voice services, nor does any of the society's published statements concern voice [[gi-soc-ninteishisetsu]]; en (no equivalent). → [[transgender-care-context]]
- **変声 ／ 声変わり** (ja) — the male pubertal voice change; Japanese music education has argued since the 1950s that it arrives earlier, and a review of 18 quantitative surveys finds the claim cannot be settled either way, concluding that singing pedagogy's distance from evidence is the thing to fix [[moriyama2022]]; en voice change. → [[puberty-voice-change]]
- **性同一性障害 ／ GID** (ja) — gender identity disorder, the term used across the Japanese sources read here and still in their titles and keywords [[mizawa2022]] [[sanuki2007]] [[nishiyama2023]]; it was dropped when the GI society renamed itself, on the grounds that 「ICD-11の発効により，GID（性同一性障害）という名称は，今後，使用されなくなります」 [[gi-soc-ninteishisetsu]], and the vault's `jspn-guideline2024` is titled 性別不合に関する診断と治療のガイドライン instead; en (no equivalent). → [[transgender-care-context]]

## Other languages (ru, tr, ar, vi, id, th)

Terms as the Russian-, Turkish-, Arabic-, Vietnamese-, Indonesian- and Thai-language sources use them, with the note that attests the usage. The Russian list was read in Russian clinical writing and one community mini-course; the Turkish in a laryngology textbook chapter and the Turkish Wikipedia; the Arabic in the Arabic Wikipedia surgery article and one Egyptian surgeon's channel; the Vietnamese in a Hanoi survey, a hospital questionnaire study and two community videos; the Indonesian in the Indonesian Wikipedia article, one broadcast segment and one peer tutorial; the Thai in a podcast on the linguistic study of trans women's voices and one tutorial channel.

### Russian (ru)

- **голосовой тренинг** (ru) — voice training; the Russian article opens "Голосовая терапия или голосовой тренинг", giving both the clinical and everyday words [[wikipedia-ru-golosovaya-terapiya]]. → [[voice-feminization-therapy]]
- **феминизация голоса** (ru) — voice feminization; the term the Russian Wikipedia's surgery article uses in its list of feminising operations, «глоттопластику (феминизация голоса: …)» [[wikipedia-ru-genderno-affirmativnaya-hirurgiya]], and the label on the community mini-course «МИНИ КУРС ПО ФЕМИНИЗАЦИИ ГОЛОСА ДЛЯ ТРАНСГЕНДЕРНЫХ ЖЕНЩИН» [[yt-aysl]]; ja 音声女性化手術, zh 嗓音女性化. → [[voice-feminization-therapy]]
- **глоттопластика · крикотироидное сближение** (ru) — glottoplasty and cricothyroid approximation, named together as what the article's «феминизация голоса» consists of: «стандартная лазерная или лазерная редукционная глоттопластика» beside «крикотироидное сближение» [[wikipedia-ru-genderno-affirmativnaya-hirurgiya]]; the Turkish chapter names the same pair in its own spellings, Wendler glottoplasti and krikotiroid yaklaştırma [[yelken2022]]; en glottoplasty · cricothyroid approximation. → [[voice-surgery]]
- **тембр голоса** (ru) — voice timbre; the word a Russian endocrinology review uses for the testosterone-driven change, «Изменение тембра голоса на более низкий … возникают в период до года терапии» [[volkova2019ru]]; de Stimmklang, en voice timbre. → [[voice-quality]]
- **гендерная дисфория** (ru) — gender dysphoria; the diagnosis two Russian clinical articles are titled around [[kremenitskaya2022ru]] and the subject heading of the endocrinologist's review of its management [[volkova2019ru]]. → [[transgender-care-context]]
- **трансгендерное лицо · трансгендерные люди** (ru) — transgender person and transgender people: the register the Russian clinical articles write in, as in the title «…трансгендерными лицами по собственной инициативе» [[makarova2021ru]] and the cohort description «у лиц с гендерной дисфорией» [[kremenitskaya2022ru]], while the review for clinicians addresses «трансгендерным пациентам» [[volkova2019ru]]. → [[transgender-care-context]]
- **нежелательные явления** (ru) — adverse events; what the review says the endocrinologist watches for across lifelong follow-up, «последующее долгосрочное (пожизненное) наблюдение и мониторинг нежелательных явлений» [[volkova2019ru]]. → [[voice-health]]
- **твенг** (ru, community) — twang, kept as an English loan and taught by name in the Russian mini-course syllabus beside the vocal folds, the larynx and the thyroid cartilage, where the syllabus adds it as a resonance strategy [[yt-aysl]]; en twang. → [[vocal-tract-anatomy]]
- **до/после** (ru, community) — before/after; the genre label on the community mini-course's outcome video «Феминизация голоса ДО/ПОСЛЕ», which presents the result as an audio comparison [[yt-aysl]]; en before/after. → [[voice-impressions]]

### Turkish (tr)

- **ses estetiği** (tr) — voice aesthetics, the umbrella a laryngology textbook chapter gives for both directions of pitch surgery: «Ses estetiği ameliyatları, sesin perdesini hastanın yaşı, cinsiyeti ve fiziksel görünümüyle uyumlu hale getirmek amacıyla uygulanan larengeal çatı cerrahileridir» [[yelken2022]]. → [[voice-surgery]]
- **ses inceltme · ses kalınlaştırma** (tr) — voice thinning and voice thickening, the two directions the same chapter's terminology names; the thickening side runs «Ses kalınlaştırma ise mutasyonel falsetto hastaları, kadından erkeğe geçen transseksüeller veya mesleki/estetik nedenlerle daha tok bir ses isteyen kişilere uygulanır» [[yelken2022]]; en voice feminization · voice masculinization. → [[voice-feminization-therapy]] · [[voice-masculinization]]
- **krikotiroid yaklaştırma** (tr) — cricothyroid approximation, one of the feminisation techniques the chapter lists beside «Wendler glottoplasti, VFSRAC, LAVA» [[yelken2022]]; ru крикотироидное сближение. → [[voice-surgery]]
- **mutasyonel falsetto** (tr) — mutational falsetto, one of the indications for the pitch-lowering operation in the same chapter's list [[yelken2022]]; en puberphonia. → [[voice-masculinization]]
- **ses istirahati** (tr) — voice rest; obligatory across all the techniques the chapter describes, «Tüm bu yöntemlerde ortak olarak ameliyat sonrasında sesin iyileşmesini çabuklaştırmak adına belirli bir süre ses istirahati zorunludur» [[yelken2022]]; ja 発声禁止. → [[voice-health]]
- **ses cerrahisi · ses kullanımı** (tr) — voice surgery, and the use of the voice: the Turkish Wikipedia names the first among the gender-affirmation operations that legal and financial barriers keep out of reach, «meme büyütme, yüz feminizasyonu, ses cerrahisi ve vajinoplasti gibi cinsiyet uyum ameliyatları», and files the second under social rather than medical transition, «isim değişikliği, giyim tarzı, saç şekli ve ses kullanımı» [[wikipedia-tr-trans-kadin]]. → [[voice-surgery]] · [[voice-across-languages]]
- **hanımefendi** (tr) — "madam"; in a language with no grammatical gender the address forms carry the gendering that pronouns carry elsewhere: «Türkçede ise "Hanımefendi" gibi kadınlara özgü hitap şekilleri yaygın olarak kullanılmaktadır» [[wikipedia-tr-trans-kadin]]. → [[voice-across-languages]]
- **cinsiyet uyum süreci** (tr) — the gender affirmation process; the heading of a Turkish psychiatry clinic's retrospective study, «Cinsiyet Uyum Süreciyle İlgili Değerlendirmede Kromozom Analizi», which records that Turkish insurance coverage for the procedures runs through a genetic consultation the international guidelines do not recommend [[bagcaz2019]]. → [[transgender-care-context]]
- **kadından erkeğe geçen transseksüeller** (tr) — female-to-male transsexuals; the older register the textbook chapter still uses for one of its pitch-lowering indications [[yelken2022]], against the Wikipedia article's trans kadın [[wikipedia-tr-trans-kadin]]; en female-to-male transsexual. → [[transgender-care-context]]

- **حلاقة القصبة الهوائية** (ar) — tracheal shave; listed beside «جراحة تأنيث الصوت» in the article's feminising procedures [[wikipedia-ar-jirahat-iaadat-tahdid-aljins]]; en Adam's-apple shave. → [[voice-surgery]]

- **giọng nam · giọng nữ** (vi) — male voice and female voice: the two poles of the Vietnamese series title «Luyện giọng từ giọng nam thành giọng nữ» [[yt-senem]], and what the masculinising counterpart addresses in its own title «Cách Luyện Giọng Nói Trầm Như Nam», how to practise a voice as deep as a man's [[yt-truongchuclinh-2020]]. → [[voice-across-languages]]

- **phẫu thuật chuyển đổi giới tính** (vi) — sex reassignment surgery, the term of the Hanoi survey [[pham2020vn]]; the Wikipedia article reserves «phẫu thuật chuyển giới» for the genital operation and warns «Không được nhầm lẫn "phẫu thuật chuyển giới" với phẫu thuật thẩm mỹ ở người chuyển giới» — do not confuse it with cosmetic surgery in trans people [[wikipedia-vi-phau-thuat-chuyen-gioi]]; en sex reassignment surgery. → [[voice-surgery]]

- **trôi nổi · truyền miệng** (vi) — "floating" (unregulated, of unknown origin) and word-of-mouth: the two words the Hanoi study uses for how hormones circulate, «Hơn 73% mua nội tiết tố từ người đang sử dụng và sử dụng theo cơ chế truyền miệng» [[pham2020vn]]; en unregulated · word-of-mouth. → [[transgender-care-context]]
