## What it offers
- iOS app by DevExtras. Listing's own description: "Analyse your voice pitch, associated voice gender, & volume in real-time with the ability to record and replay the last 10 seconds. Essentially a voice analyser for transgender, LSVT, singers, and voice therapy patients to help improve the quality of their voice."
- Explicitly states it does NOT alter the user's voice: "this app does NOT change the pitch of your voice, it only provides visual feedback specifically tailed[sic] for use as part of voice therapy."
- Free version is ad-supported; a paid in-app purchase removes ads. Rated 4.64/5 from 837 ratings at time of fetch; genres Medical / Utilities.

## What it measures/teaches
- Real-time voice pitch and an associated "voice gender" estimate, plus real-time volume, displayed as a graph.
- Provides phonetically balanced sentences to read and reference tones at specific pitches to mimic, plus record/playback (last 10 seconds, not saved to device) without file storage. Listing states no voice recordings are saved or leave the device.
- Listing recommends "about 5 minutes a day over several months" of use, explicitly as a supplement to — not a replacement for — voice therapy: "It does not provide lessons or guidance, it is intended to be used as a tool to assist with voice therapy."
- Listing notes best results require an external microphone; built-in phone mics may pick up background noise and vary results.

## Limits
- No formant/resonance measurement described — only pitch, an associated gender estimate, and volume; the listing itself flags that spectral/phonetic measures are "to be added at a later date," i.e. not present as of this listing.
- Provides no lessons or instructional content; purely a feedback/measurement tool.
- App Store description is developer-authored; the "voice gender" estimate's underlying method (e.g. simple F0 threshold vs. a trained classifier) is not disclosed on the page fetched.
