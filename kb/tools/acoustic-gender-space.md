# Acoustic Gender Space

Browser app, labelled on its own page as a "Research Trial", that plots recordings on a two-dimensional map: the Y axis is pitch, the X axis is resonance ("brighter sounds represented with a higher percentage").

## What it offers
- Example clips placed on the map; pressing play on a point plays its clip.
- Users can add their own recordings; the page states clips are sent to the server for processing but not stored persistently, and data can be exported and re-imported locally.
- Its help text says voices perceived as female typically sit top-right (brighter resonance, higher pitch) and male voices bottom-left, and that "there are no sharp cutoffs at which a voice is guaranteed to be gendered a specific way".

## What it measures
- Pitch and a resonance percentage; the page does not state the resonance algorithm.

## Limits
- English-language examples; the page was showing a high-traffic notice at fetch time. Method and validation are not documented on the page.

## Relation to Koenami
- Direct design ancestor of Koenami's 声の分布 (audible points on an acoustic map); Koenami adds three more indicators, Japanese and other-language references, and the 3D/PCA and 男女差 projections.
