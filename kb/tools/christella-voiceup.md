## What it offers
- iOS app by Speechtools Ltd, built around Christella Antoni, described in the app's own listing as "a Consultant Speech & Language Therapist with over 20 years of experience" and "the UK's leading expert in transgender voice modification."
- Two listings found: "Christella VoiceUp: MTF Voice" (free, with in-app purchases per stage) and "Christella VoiceUp Complete" (all three stages bundled). Both listings state the app "will teach you how to feminize your voice" with a recommended "10 minutes of daily practice."
- Free tier includes one free sample lesson and a free pitch analysis with a graph to "track your pitch change over time." The complete course is "over 2 hours of voice therapy training in 3 stages — beginners, intermediate and advanced," each stage containing five lessons "based on Christella's successful treatment model," increasing in length/complexity through each stage.
- App Store ratings at time of fetch: VoiceUp (base) 4.13/5 from 55 ratings; VoiceUp Complete 5.0/5 from 3 ratings. Listed under genres Health & Fitness / Medical.

## What it measures/teaches
- Teaches modification of resonance, pitch, intonation, and vowel length, with a final lesson in each stage combining these skills "to help you develop control sustaining your feminized voice." All exercises are demonstrated by Christella herself as a voice model.
- Includes a pitch-analysis feature with a graph for tracking pitch change over time (measurement is limited to pitch per the listing text; no formant/resonance measurement tool is described).

## Limits
- Paid app past the free sample lesson (stages sold as in-app purchases in the base version; bundled in the Complete version, price not listed in the fetched metadata).
- App Store description is developer-authored marketing copy plus user-submitted reviews quoted by the developer; not independent verification of clinical efficacy.
- iOS only, per the App Store listings found; no Android listing was checked in this pass.
- One listed review ("I'm using this app as a speech pathologist with my transgender clients...") is a self-reported, unverified third-party quote curated by the developer, not an independent source.
