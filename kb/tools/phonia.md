## What it offers
- A browser-based (also packaged for Windows/macOS/Linux) "phonetics workstation," per the site's own schema.org description: "waveform, spectrogram, pitch, formant, and intensity analysis, and TextGrid annotation. Free and open source, validated against Praat."
- Free of charge (schema.org `offers` field lists price 0 USD); built as a web app (SvelteKit-based per page source).

## What it measures/teaches
- Waveform display, spectrogram, pitch (F0) tracking, formant analysis, intensity analysis, and TextGrid-style annotation — the same measurement categories Praat provides, explicitly claimed as "validated against Praat."

## Limits
- The homepage fetched is a minimal single-page app shell; most functional detail lives in the app itself rather than in static marketing text, so specific validation methodology, accuracy figures, or population tested against Praat could not be confirmed from the page fetched — the claim "validated against Praat" is stated but not substantiated on this page.
