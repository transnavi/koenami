## What it offers
- A browsable map of voice concepts and pedagogy published by the maintainer of the Voice Resource Project wiki, announced from every page of that wiki as 「Voice Map is released! See here for a giant map of (almost) all voice characteristics and pedagogy!」 ([[sumi-getting-started]], [[sumi-communities]]).
- The About node states the purpose and the one thing it is not: 「This is a project by Sumi to put in one place as much voice information as is available. It is more a reference than a guide; you won't learn much of how to change your own voice here, but instead learn about voice itself and how it works, and about voice pedagogy.」
- It carries its own reliability warning: 「Many of these concepts and notes are proven through use, not academics and are just a working theory used by teachers in the field to get the results we need. As with anything some random person on the internet says, question it and do your own research instead of implicitly trusting it. The focus of this project is mapping out currently known voice pedagogy, nothing more.」
- Authorship and licence: 「Copyright (c) 2025 Sumi」, offered under the MIT licence, with source at github.com/SumianVoice/voicemap; it is marked 「Work In Progress」.
- Crediting policy, from the Credit node: tooltips on some notes say 「where the concept was first seen or where the writer (Sumi) got it from or a source of the information or statement, but these are just the earliest known use / source, and are not included if not known」, and 「If a dispute about the origin of a thing is unresolved, the tooltip will be completely removed until it is resolved.」
- Structure, from the data file: roughly 130 nodes in sections for Glottal, Vocal Fold Geometry, Pitch, Vocal Mechanisms, Resonance, Articulation, Nasality, Vocal Health, Training Related and Voice Risks. The nodes read are a mixture of definition, claim and instruction — for example Glottal (「Anything to do with the vocal folds vibrating … covered under the umbrella term glottal behavior」), Pitch to Weight Relationship, Quotient / Open Quotient / Closed Quotient / Speed Quotient, Pressure to Vibratory Mass / CQ, IA CQ Blocking Hypothesis, M0, No Separation Hypothesis, Range Expansion, Macrovowel, Oropharyngeal Constriction, Nasopharyngeal Covers, Compensation, Pitch-Resonance Conflation, Physical Sensations, Overuse of Fry.
- The four 「Important Principles」 on the map: **No Effort** — 「You will never need to put more effort into any speech related training than you would into your untrained and relaxed voice. Ever. No exceptions.」, with the gloss that 「feeling muscle use, a stretching sensation or tension, tightness etc are all unnecessary and should never be tolerated at any point for any length of time; keep it pure and natural, you don't "push through" some difficulty to arrive at some destination, instead you have to build good coordination from the ground up」; **Passive Awareness** — 「The harder you try to control things tightly, the less results you get due to compensation, so use passive awareness instead; listen intently but don't try to control too tightly」; **Understand the "why"** — 「If you're not getting feedback when speaking, change what you're doing so you do get feedback」, where feedback is defined as self-feedback rather than another person's pointers; and **Exploration** — 「we stumble around in the dark until we trip over the correct way or a hint of it」.
- A Data and Research node lists the questions the map would like evidence for: 「is IA activity specifically responsible for mechanism blending along with TA vs CT」; 「is it possible that low IA can allow for partial vocal fold vibration」; 「is only TA responsible for vocal fold vibratory mass and as such vocal weight」; 「is vocal fold vibratory mass actually the cause of the sound, and how specifically (probably need high speed camera footage and skilled vocalist)」; 「can the TA be hypertrophied physically」; 「can the TA be atrophied physically」.
- A Disclaimer node states the content is provided as-is and that 「Nothing in the content is medical advice nor advice of any kind」.

## What it measures/teaches
- It teaches the vocabulary and the causal claims of the community's pedagogy — what quotient means, how pressure relates to vibratory mass, whether creak and fry are separate mechanisms, what happens when pitch and resonance are conflated — rather than a practice routine.
- The separate exercise_fem.js file holds the feminisation exercises, and the map's own Feminisation Guide node gives behavioural instruction rather than technique: 「Never try hard. Never force it. The voice should sound natural at all stages of training. Go a little higher and speak like a girl for a while… If it's right, it will feel effortless.」

## Limits
- The map's own framing: a working theory of practice, not academic evidence, with authors not credited for concepts where the origin is unknown and tooltips withdrawn when disputed — the Data and Research node is a list of unknowns, not of findings.
- It is a JavaScript canvas: fetched as HTML it renders no text, and this note is written from its data and exercise source files. No interactive behaviour (navigation, search, tooltips) was exercised.
- Volunteer project, work in progress, single author, and the disclaimer disclaims advice.
