# 5 · A practice plan the evidence supports

Not a programme, since none has been tested for Japanese, but the shape the trials and the exercise studies give, with the caveats attached. A clinician's assessment is the standard of care where one is available [[coleman2022]].

## How much, how long

The randomised trial used two blocks of 4–5 weeks, one for pitch, one for articulation and resonance, ten weeks in all; both blocks helped and the order did not matter, and many participants still wanted more training at the end [[leyns2023]]. The 74-woman cohort trained for 8–12 sessions [[sodersten2026]]; the oral-resonance study used five [[carew2007]]; the vocal-function-exercise trial ran four weeks [[stemple1994]]. In nodule therapy, benefit tracked how much people practised outside the clinic, not which technique they used [[verdolinimarston1995]]. Gains in self-perception held at one year while listener ratings slipped somewhat by three months, so some maintenance practice is part of the plan [[leyns2024]].

## What to work on

1. **Pitch and its ceiling.** Raise the speaking pitch and, as much, its upper limit: the trans women heard as female had a higher top of their speaking range [[gelfer2000]]. Watch the median and the 90th percentile together in Koenami [[koenami-method]].
2. **Resonance.** Lip spreading and forward tongue carriage raised F1–F3 and, unasked, F0 [[carew2007]]; a visual target for F2 moved it in one session [[kawitzky2020]]. Formant spacing shifts judgements on its own [[cartei2013]]. "Big dog / little dog" panting is one community way to feel the tract size change [[yoxon2023]]. Track 響き on the same sentence each time [[koenami-method]].
3. **Weight and tilt.** Some trained speakers shift their harmonic balance [[sodersten2026]]; a breathier tilt helps a female judgement only when the tract is already short [[neuhaus2026]]. Treat this as the third lever, after the first two.
4. **Intonation and articulation.** Both contribute [[leung2018]]; intonation has its own clinical literature [[hancock2014]]. In Japanese, remember that the span also carries pitch accent [[koenami-method]].
5. **Loudness.** Loudness predicted female-sounding ratings in the largest cohort [[sodersten2026]] and masculinity–femininity ratings in another, where naturalness tracked F0, formants and speech rate [[hardy2020]]; a feminized voice that is also quiet is a common early pattern to correct.

## Targets and what they rest on

For English, above about 165 Hz a male voice stops being heard as male and 145–165 Hz is ambiguous [[gelfer2013]]. The Chinese community guide sets a pass mark of 185–220 Hz and above for its pitch phase [[digital-transchinese-mtf-voice]]. Japanese listeners' attractiveness ratings peak near 280–290 Hz and fall above it [[tanaka2022]], and one 両声類 contest reported naturalness highest around 200–240 Hz [[hasegawa2017]]. None of these is a Japanese norm; there is no Japanese listener study of trans voices yet [[japanese-voice]]. The app's verdict is uncalibrated against listeners [[koenami-method]].

## Keeping the voice healthy

Untrained Mandarin-speaking trans women already showed patterns the authors linked to fatigue risk [[huang2024]]; forcing pitch up is a route into muscle-tension dysphonia, treated by a laryngologist and a speech-language pathologist together [[vanhoutte2011]]. Semi-occluded exercises (straw, lip trill, humming) make phonation more efficient with less tissue collision [[titze2006]] and are a reasonable warm-up and cool-down; vocal function exercises have trial evidence in healthy voices [[stemple1994]]. Persisting hoarseness, pain or loss of range are reasons to stop and get the larynx looked at, since surgery and hormones have their own documented adverse events (fatigue, granuloma) [[chowdhury2025]] and a quarter of trans men on testosterone reported instability or fatigue [[nygren2016]]. See [[voice-health]] and [[vocal-fatigue]].

## Measuring honestly

Record the same sentence each time; the app's own page asks for it because formant averages and intonation spans depend on the words [[koenami-method]]. Keep both a self-rating and, now and then, an outside ear: self-ratings track satisfaction, not listeners [[quinn2021]] [[hancock2011]]. Machine scores correlate with people at about 0.6–0.8 [[chen2021]], which is useful for trend and useless for a verdict on one day.
